# RE-335 — Link motion scripts, specials, Boomerang and Spin Attack weapons

Status: COMPLETE (decomp source, host tests, both PSP builds, PPSSPP golden smoke; Link not reachable in `psp-game`, physical PSP not captured)
Topics: fighter, gameplay, weapon, grab, animation
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/link.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/link_attack.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/weapon.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/status.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/grab.rs`, `tools/gen-anim-table.py`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`

**Question.** What do Link's US motion scripts, special statuses and
weapons do, and where must the port depart from the source structure?

**Source.** `224_LinkMainMotion.c` gives every attack, catch and throw
window. `ftlinkstatus.h`, `ftlinkspecialn.c`, `ftlinkspecialhi.c` and
`ftlinkspeciallw.c` give the status callbacks; `ftlink.h` and `wpvars.h`
their constants. `wplinkboomerang.c` and `wplinkspinattack.c` give the
weapons. The Boomerang's `WPAttributes` are in `226_LinkSpecial1.c`. The Spin
Attack's are the words at offset 0x0C of `225_LinkMain.c`
(`llLinkMainSpinAttackWeaponAttributes`): angle 30, knockback 50/0/30,
5 damage, map diamond 150/0/-150/150. The rapid jab and the jab chain are in
`ftcommonattack1.c` and `ftcommonattack100.c`. The down air's bounce is
Link's branch of `ftCommonAttackAirLwProcHit` (`ftcommonattackair.c`).
`dFTLinkMotionDescs` in `ftdata.c` names the figatrees.

**Findings.**

- `Jab1` sets flag 1 at frame 10 and `Jab2` at frame 14, well before their
  figatrees end (24 and 22 frames). The jab chains on the flag, so Link's
  second and third hits come out mid-animation. Earlier ports read flag 1 as
  the animation end, which holds for their scripts.
- Link needs five A edges (taps and releases) for the rapid jab, Fox four.
  The rapid loop's figatree wraps every 35 frames. Its script pauses after
  the fifth pulse and resumes at the wrap, so the fifth flag 1 and
  `is_anim_end` arrive on the same frame. The first cycle therefore always
  plays five pulses. After that, each pulse without an A edge since the
  previous one ends the loop.
- `ftCommonAttack11SetStatus` loads `FTAttributes::attack1_followup_frames`:
  Mario, Luigi and Link 24, Fox and Samus 30, Donkey Kong 28. The port used
  24 for all. `Attack12` uses the 24-frame default.
- Link has one forward tilt, one forward smash, and `LandingAirF` and
  `LandingAirLw` (`LandingAirD`) motions only. The neutral, back and up airs
  land into `LandingAirNull`.
- `DashAttack`, `FSmash`, `AttackAirN`, `AttackAirF` and `UpSpecial` replace
  live boxes without a clear, so each keeps one hit record across both
  phases. `USmash` and `DSmash` clear between swings.
- Down air: a registered hit clears the boxes, sets Y velocity 40 and ends
  fastfall. Past frame 35 the status restarts at frame 35, and the
  fast-forward skips the frame-5 `MakeAttackColl`. After 30 frames the boxes
  return with a new record only if the frame is below 65. A hit past frame
  35 therefore never refreshes.
- `ftLinkSpecialNProcStatus` reads `hold_stick_x`, not `tap_stick_x`: a
  smash throw needs |stick X| of at least 56 within 8 frames of crossing the
  deadzone. The stick angle is read at the flag-0 frame (26), and clamped to
  ±30 degrees.
- Boomerang: tilt 85 and 160 frames, smash 114 and 190 frames. It slows by
  1.4 a frame to 10 (54 frames for a tilt), then turns back at 0.75 degrees
  a frame. A hit, a shield or a steep surface turns it back at 1.5 degrees a
  frame. On return it speeds up by 1 a frame to 90 and homes on the thrower's
  TopN plus 290. Within 180 units it disappears; the thrower enters
  `SpecialNGet`/`SpecialAirNReturn` only if `is_special_interrupt` is set.
  That flag is cleared by every `ftMainSetStatus` and set by Wait, the slow
  walks, Squat, KneeBend, the jumps, the falls, `FallSpecial` and Link's
  empty-handed Boomerang statuses.
- `wpLinkBoomerangCheckBound` uses `lbCommonSim2D`, which divides the dot
  product by the *sum* of the two magnitudes, not their product. The glance
  threshold is therefore a normal speed of about half the Boomerang's
  speed, not 30 degrees. A 30-degree downward throw still glances off a
  floor.
- Spin Attack weapon: the ground status makes it at frame 0. The aerial
  status makes none (`ftLinkSpecialHiMakeWeapon(fighter_gobj, TRUE)`). Its
  two boxes start 40 units either side and spread at 28 units a frame along
  10 degrees, slowing by 0.4 a frame. Flag 2 sets the radius: 120 at frame
  12, 100 at 20, 80 at 26, off at 30. It follows Link's position four
  physics frames back, 80 units up, stored as `s16`. Its record is never
  cleared and `can_rehit_fighter` is clear, so each target is hit at most
  once.
- Catch (Hookshot): one box on joint 35 from frame 17 to 29. `SetFlag1(12)`
  and `SetFlag2(5)` at frame 17 start `CatchPull` at frame 5, less 5/12 per
  later frame. Both throws release at frame 20. Link's thrown table is
  `ThrownCommon` for every catcher.

**Deviations.**

- The Spin Attack weapon is fighter state, not a weapon-pool entry. Only
  Link's physics callback drives it, and it takes no pool slot. It is
  removed when Link leaves the Spin Attack statuses, since nothing would
  update or destroy it afterwards.
- The Boomerang's off-camera removal (`wpLinkBoomerangCheckOffCamera`) needs
  the camera projection; the port keeps it until its lifetime ends or it is
  caught. Shield and hop callbacks wait on weapon-shield collision.
- The Bomb is an item (`itLinkBomb`). The item system is not ported, so the
  pull motion plays and creates nothing.
- The aerial Spin Attack's ledge catch waits on ledge detection.

**Verification.** Host tests cover every Link move's joint table, the rapid
pulse records, the Spin Attack record, the flag-1 jab chain, the five-edge
rapid jab, the rapid loop's end rule, the aerial landings, the Boomerang
spawn frame, the smash test, the empty throw, the return frame, the catch
and `SpecialNGet`, a hit's return, the glance and the wall return, the Spin
Attack weapon's sizes, spread and single hit, the aerial Spin Attack's
gravity and helpless fall, the down-air bounce and rehit rule, the Bomb
pull, the Hookshot window and pull frame, and the throw descriptors. The
generated animation table adds 29 Link slots and packs his grab and thrown
clips; `romtool anims --verify` agrees on 525 lengths.
