# RE-555 — Weapon map pass, item floor-contact bit

Status: COMPLETE (decomp, two readings per golden; host tests; goldens; no hardware run)
Topics: weapon, item, collision, goldens
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/wpmap.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/map.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/weapon.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/item/map.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/script.rs`

## Question

Do weapons and items run the decomp map pass (`mask_prev`/`mask_curr`, substeps, coll-end), and where did the hand-rolled weapon approximation differ?

## Evidence

- **Weapon map pass**: `crates/ssb-game/src/wpmap.rs` ports `wpMapTestLRWallCheckFloor`, `wpMapTestAllCheckFloor`, `wpMapProcAll`, `wpMapCheckAllRebound`, `wpMapProcAllCheckCollEnd` (`wpmap.c:140`) on `mpProcessUpdateMain` (`mpprocess.c:399-459`). Floor landing (`mpProcessSetCollideFloor`) snaps y only; the sweep continues and `translate` is the substep that met the map.
- **Deleted approximation**: `map_contact`, `MapContact`, `diamond_support_toward_surface`, `swept_coords_intersection`, `refresh_surface` (`weapon.rs`). Moved to coll-end: Fox blaster, boss bullet, charge shot, Yoshi egg, PK Fire, PK Thunder head, sector lasers, monster shots, Pikachu Thunder head, monster rock. Fireball, Boomerang, Kirby Cutter, Samus Bomb use the floor-check passes.
- **Item floor bit**: grounded items raise `MASK_FLOOR` only on a floor-line change (`itMapTestLRWallCheckFloor`), as fighters (RE-554). No reader changed behaviour.
- **PK Thunder wall test**: the head's diamond edges are tested against walls (`mpProcessCheckTestRWallCollisionAdjNew`, `mpprocess.c:1599-1609`; leftward-only gate `mpcollision.c:1650`), not one support point. The old single-point test let the Ness head pass the stage's right-wall top vertex into the stage; the decomp destroys it (`wpNessPKThunderHeadProcMap`, `wpnesspkthunder.c:267-272`).
- **Jibaku route**: `nessjibaku` steer 170 to 175, left steer 191 to 196, capture 227 to 232. Ness never lands on the platform; Up+B is on the main floor; the capture is `SpecialAirHiJibaku` frame 3 launched left.
- **Thunder Jolt rewrite**: air `test_all_check_floor` + wall mask, EdgeU/EdgeD clamp, `GroundMakeWeapon`; ground `GroundProcMap` (`wppikachuthunderjolt.c:413`): Common projection, `GetStatus` crossings, end neighbour, `CheckDestroy` at 100 degrees, lr 2/3 on walls. Past a platform end with no neighbour the jolt is destroyed; N64 trace cpufx-g2: last row el 165 x -984.97, gone el 166.

## Deviations

- Weapon spawn snap (`mpCommonRunWeaponCollisionDefault`, COLLPROJECT) not ported; ground jolt sets `pos_prev = translate` only.
- Jolt initial `rotate` is `(0,1)` (decomp: unwritten struct memory, D-050); ground jolt damage hard-coded to 7.
- `CheckDestroy`: a failed Common query counts as no turn.
- B2 not ported: `mpProcessSetCollProjectFloorID` clears the line id but keeps `floor_angle`; `item.floor: Option<Standing>` would lose the normal, needs a split of line id and angle.
- Goldens rebaselined (native px; two readings: `mpProcessUpdateMain` :441-459 + the weapon `ProcMap`):
  - `f1-vs-cpu` 91488: Fireball floor bounce keeps the full swept x (x 429.80 to 474.07 at tick 686), scene diverges after.
  - `f1-training-pikachu-thunder` 617: Thunder head bursts at the unsnapped substep `translate`.
  - `f1-training-yoshi-explosion` 63618: egg bursts at the unsnapped `translate`; camera follows the egg.
  - `f1-cpufx-jolt-end` 231: jolt dies past the platform end (above).
  - `f1-training-ness-jibaku` 71046: wall-edge destroy and re-steered route (above).
