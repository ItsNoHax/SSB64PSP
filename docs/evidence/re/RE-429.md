## Source and port

`itground/itgbumper.c`, `itpowerblock.c` and `itpakkun.c` supply the item
statuses, callbacks and constants. Their item attributes are decoded from
the USA ROM and transcribed into the host port. The shared item pool now
implements `StageItems`: the controllers create actual items, with handles
that reject a freed slot's stale generation. Saffron's monster requests
remain unimplemented; Pokémon are a separate batch.

- Castle's Bumper follows the moving ground, attacks and swells on a hit.
  It is drawn from the exact GBumper attribute target: file 251's extern
  at 0xCF0 names file 86 + 0x7648; 0xCF4 names its materials at 0x7488.
  NBumper's data extern at 0x69C names the same graph. RE-162's nearby
  0x7BE8 graph was misidentified: selecting it drew a horizontal disc.
  The legacy neighboring-tree material pairing remains in the converter;
  it is not evidence that this graph is either Bumper's runtime model.
- The POW's pop-in, hittable wait, squash and removal drive the stage's
  quake and spawner re-arm through events consumed at the end of the frame.
  The quake excludes the fighter who hit the block, as the source does.
- Both Piranha Plants wait for a clear pipe area, rise and bite, sink,
  launch on sufficient knockback, and wait 1,200 frames to regrow after
  leaving the map. Their attacks and damage boxes grow above the rim.
  Fighter, item and weapon attacks now supply item knockback terms.
  Pipe-entry notification exists and has a host test; no pipe traversal
  code currently calls it.

## Item trees and clocks

`itManagerMakeItem` removes each model's empty descriptor root. Node 1 is
therefore the real item root, including its animation clock; animating
node 0 made the POW immediately hittable. GroundObjects uses the promoted
root for item placement and animation, hides absent items, and keeps each
plant's clocks independent.

The ROM-backed `packed_item_trees_play_on_the_ejected_root` verifies:

| Animation | ROM and packed result |
|---|---|
| POW pop-in, file 155 + 0x13B8 | Idle on play 21; unhittable until then |
| POW squash, file 155 + 0x1288 | Idle on play 23 |
| Piranha rise, file 155 + 0xCC8 | Root Y equals ROM replay bit for bit on every play; 141 plays; maximum 596.39996 |

The item make's initial play counts toward these totals. Pack v80 adds
the three joint clips, both plant material clips and the plant's texture
table; no pack layout changes.

The Piranha descriptor supplies transform kind 0x30 (48), a pitch-locked
camera billboard. `itPakkunAppearProcDamage` changes the secondary matrix
to 0x46 (70), `func_ovl0_800CA194`, and sets Z rotation to PI. This is a
screen-facing spin with no object scale. The draw overrides the packed
node flags and uses the live spin; an ordinary world Z rotation is
insufficient. `itPakkunDamagedProcDead` resets the spin but leaves kind 70
installed. The ROM-backed test covers that persistence after rebirth.

## Capture evidence

The ordinary `traininginishie@240` capture was taken, but neither pipe is
in its camera. Existing Castle/Mushroom Kingdom goldens likewise cannot
prove item appearance; at tick 400 the plants have already sunk.

New scenes `trainingbumper` and `trainingplants` run the same idle Training
simulation to tick 240 with a diagnostic camera. The Bumper camera follows
the item; the plant camera targets its pipe origin plus 400 Y. Eye is
5,000 Z above the target. Each new PSP golden is identical across two
captures. The corrected Bumper has the upright ring and the plant rises
visibly above its pipe.

N64 references reuse RE-422's restored-source warp ROMs, Mario versus
Mario in Training. Mupen64Plus/Rice renders frame-240 captures after the
harness pauses the camera process and writes equivalent eye/at values.
Castle's camera targets the live Bumper at [-422.4541, 3525, 0]; Inishie's
targets the pipe origin [-2756, 1845, 0] plus 400 Y. Screenshot requests
advance an emulator frame. These are qualitative model/orientation
references, not pixel equality: viewport, HUD, initialization and material
phase differ. The N64 plant's mouth is open while this PSP frame's is
closed; exact rise timing is established by the separate ROM replay.
