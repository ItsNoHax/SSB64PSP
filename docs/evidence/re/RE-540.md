# RE-540 — Effect creation-frame play and link-15 creation-order drawing

Status: COMPLETE (N64 GObj/MObj frame traces without screenshots, PSP per-frame dumps, PPSSPP goldens; pack v119; no hardware run)
Topics: effects, animation, draw-order, n64-trace, goldens
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/effect_order.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`

## Question

RE-538 left two open questions. Is the Falcon Punch flame about 2 frames ahead of the N64? Do the anim-joint `efManager` effects (Spin Attack swirl, Sing, Falcon Kick, PSI Magnet) start one animation play ahead? Separately, the PSP drew the fighters' link-15 effects grouped by type, then by port. In what order does the N64 draw them?

## Findings

1. Creation-frame play differs by maker.
   - `efManagerMakeEffect` makes the effect `GObj` with `func_run = efManagerFuncRun` and plays its animation once (`gcPlayAnimAll`), which parses frame 0 without advancing.
   - `efManagerFuncRun` (`src/ef/efmanager.c:1912-1926`) adds `proc_update` only when `gcRunAll` calls `func_run` (`src/sys/objman.c:2125`) on the next frame.
   - An effect therefore shows frame 0 on its creation frame and first advances one frame later.
   - `wpManagerMakeWeapon` adds its process at creation, so a weapon also ticks on its creation frame.
   - `FxSlot::sync` now takes a `PlayRule`. `Effect` starts the slot with `StageAnimator::start_changed` (frame 0 on the first tick, no extra material tick). `Weapon` keeps `start` plus the material creation tick. Weapons: Thunder Jolt, PK Thunder and the Spin Attack weapon graphic.
2. N64 traces (`trace_m.py`, SHOT0=500, no screenshots), with the GObj anim frame and MObj frame logged per fighter anim frame:
   - Spin Attack swirl: Link frame 1 gives GObj frame 0 (PSP was 1).
   - Sing: made at Jigglypuff frame 2 with frame 0 (PSP was 1).
   - Falcon Kick: made at Captain frame 12 with frame 0; wraps to 0 at frame 16 (PSP was 1, wrapped at 15).
   - PSI Magnet: made at status frame 1 with frame 0 (PSP was 1).
   - After the change, the PSP dumps match each row, MObj frames included.
3. The Falcon Punch flame has no lead.
   - On the N64 it is made at Captain frame 42 with MObj frame 0, cycles 0, 1, 2 and is gone at 55. The PSP dump is identical per anim frame.
   - RE-538's ~2-frame offset is a measurement artifact: each screenshot iteration of the old traces ran extra game frames (the old cpufx-n trace repeats rows at el72/73). With a 3-frame flipbook, a 1-frame slip reads as a 2-frame lead.
   - The Fox Reflector already started with `start_changed`; it is unchanged.
4. Scene calibration side finding (not changed). On the clean trace Captain anim frame 1 is el31, and on PSP tick 429. That gives `398 + el` for cpufx-n and cpufx-u, while the scenes use `399 + el`, calibrated on the drifting trace. cpufx-d agrees with 399: Falcon Kick frame 12 is PSP 440 and el41; Ness is PSP 443 and el44.
5. Link-15 draw order.
   - `efManagerMakeEffect` adds the display with `gcAddGObjDisplay(gobj, proc_display, dl_link, 2, -1)`.
   - `gcDLLinkGObjTail` inserts after the last `GObj` of equal or higher priority, so priority-2 effects draw in creation order across fighters.
   - `ssb_game::effect_order` keeps one creation stamp per fighter effect (shield, Yoshi egg shield and egg escape, magnet, sing, spin, punch, kick, reflector) from one counter, and the PSP draws the live ones sorted by stamp.
   - A slot ejected and remade on the same frame takes a new stamp.
   - The Spin Attack weapon graphic (weapon link 14) now draws before every link-15 effect.
6. Residuals:
   - Fighter particles, the damage-fly effects and Thunder Jolt's link-15 displays are still drawn outside the sort.
   - Two fighters making effects on one frame take port order, then kind order. The N64 order also depends on which process made each (`proc_interrupt` before `proc_physics` across fighters).

## Conclusion

Six PPSSPP goldens moved one effect play back toward the N64 and were rebaselined: f1-training-link-spin, -captain-kick, -purin, -ness-magnet; f1-cpufx-down, -up. The creation-order sort changed no golden: no captured scene has two overlapping effects whose creation order differs from the old type-then-port order. `r2-mario-entry-pipe` still fails at base (RE-538 item 8). Open items are in PLAN.md.

## Pause rule (decomp)

- Only `proc_lagstart == ftParamProcPauseEffect` pauses (`ftmain.c:3996`, `ftparam.c:1355`); setters: Link spin, Captain specialn/speciallw, Kirby specialhi, `ftcommonattacks4` (Pikachu). Only `efManagerHaveStructProcUpdate`/`NoEjectProcUpdate` read `is_pause_effect`; Fox reflector, egg lay, shield and bare `gcPlayAnimAll` effects never pause. Pinned by `a_paused_fighters_attached_particle_keeps_running_and_following`.
- Run through hitlag (clocks moved out of `update`, which skips hitlag): PSI Magnet field, Yoshi egg effect, Samus/Kirby charge-shot spin (`wpSamusChargeShotProcUpdate`, no `is_hitlag_weapon` writer), Sing notes, grapple glow. Tests `*_keeps_playing_through_hitlag`.
