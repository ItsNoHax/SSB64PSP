# RE-483 — Magnifier depth masks on a PSP-2000; a kernel call per heap allocation

Status: COMPLETE (PSP-2000, 6.61 ARK, PSPLink v3.2.1, `profile_hw,capture_scene_file,no_audio` builds of `vs4@2400` from `ms0:/PSP/GAME/ssb64/` with pack v108, 18 reports each)
Topics: performance, hardware, toolchain
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-rom/src/depth_mask.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/meshdraw.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`

## Question

While fighters are off-screen, `draw_depth_image` (`mmask`) cost 7.3 ms a
frame over a two-second window of `vs4@2400`, 10.4 ms in the worst
frame (RE-481). Where do those cycles go, and how much can go without
changing a pixel?

## The mask

The interface's magnifier frame (`player_interface::frame`, IA8 expanded
to `[i,i,i,a]`) is grey. Its depth, after the N64 3-point filter, is
`n64_depth::decode` of an RGBA5551 word whose low two bits it drops: the
depth depends only on the filtered intensity `v >> 3`. That makes it a
32-entry table lookup after a one-channel blend, not a four-channel blend
and a decode per pixel (`Tables::grey`; the test
`grey_depths_read_the_top_five_bits` checks every `v` and alpha). At the
game's magnifier scale one mask has 1,089 source quads, 124 of them
mixed, and draws 1,448 row spans, 1,087 of them a single pixel.

## Results

Worst two-second report (the window with three masks a frame); cycles a
mask are that report's per-frame counter averages divided by its
masks a frame:

| Build | `mmask` ms/frame | Cycles a mask | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `0d7b16d` | 7.26 | 843K | 17,469 uncached stores, 987 I- and 1,017 D-cache misses a mask |
| Spans merged into rectangles | 9.08 | — | fewer sprites, ~3 ms more to merge them |
| Grey lookup, plain `Row` state, 16-bit vertices | 5.02 | 581K | |
| + vertices packed into three word stores | 4.53 | 524K | |
| + `rows`' lists reused across calls | 3.21 | 365K | 101 I- and 205 D-cache misses a mask; worst frame 4.59 ms |
| The same, spans computed but not drawn | 2.21 | 253K | submission ≈ 112K cycles a mask, ≈ 77 a span |

- **Each heap allocation is a kernel call.** rust-psp's `SystemAlloc`
  takes every allocation from `sceKernelAllocPartitionMemory` (plus
  `sceKernelGetBlockHeadAddr`) and returns it with
  `sceKernelFreePartitionMemory`. `rows` grew and dropped about 17
  `Vec`s a mask; keeping them in the `Tables` (`Scratch`) saved 159K
  cycles a mask, ~9,400 an allocate-and-free, and cut a mask's I-cache
  misses from 733 to 101 and D-cache misses from 802 to 205: the system
  call path evicts the caller's code and data.
- **Uncached list stores.** A through-mode sprite with `VERTEX_16BIT` is
  two 6-byte vertices, written as three word stores (the frame's
  uncached stores in that window 10,869 → 2,747 a frame).
- The frame's average CPU does not move (19.4 against 19.8 ms; the window
  is short); the worst report's goes from 25.7 to 22.3 ms.
- **No pixel changed.** A debug pass (not committed) painted each pixel
  by the depth the mask's sprites left in the depth buffer (one full
  sprite per distinct depth, depth-tested, no depth writes): PPSSPP
  captures of `vs4@2401`, `@2600`, `@2650` and `@2800` are byte-identical
  to `0d7b16d`'s. The golden `f1-vs-four-magnifier` cannot see a broken
  mask; a deliberately broken build differed under the debug pass.

PRX SHA-256 prefixes: `0d7b16d` `1b7efefe793cbb8f`, final
`11676ee52f3a566b`, spans-only `aeab5fb10db7ec0a`.

## Open

- What else allocates per frame. `docs/memory.md` requires no heap
  allocation in per-frame paths; this one was missed until counted.
  Replacing `SystemAlloc` with an allocator over one partition block
  would remove the kernel calls from every remaining allocation.
- `rows` still costs 253K cycles a mask for 1,448 spans.
