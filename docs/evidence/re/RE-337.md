# RE-337 — Yoshi motions, Egg Lay break timing and knockback resistance

Status: COMPLETE (decomp source, host tests, both PSP builds, PPSSPP Yoshi-fighter smoke; Yoshi not reachable in `psp-game`, physical PSP not captured)
Topics: fighter, gameplay, weapon, grab, capture, animation
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/yoshi.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/yoshi_attack.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/capture_yoshi.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/attack.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/grab.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/weapon.rs`, `tools/gen-anim-table.py`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`

**Question.** How do Yoshi's US motions, Egg Lay victim state and weapons run,
and when does the laid egg release its fighter?

**Source.** `246_YoshiMainMotion.c` supplies normal, catch, throw and special
motion events. `ftyoshispecialn.c`, `ftyoshispecialhi.c`,
`ftyoshispeciallw.c`, `ftcommoncaptureyoshi.c` and
`ftcommonjumpaerial.c` supply callbacks. `247_YoshiMain.c` contains the Egg
Throw and star attack words. `339_YoshiSpecial3.c` contains the Egg Lay
effect's animation joints; `efmanager.c` switches that effect to break index
1. `ftmain.c` and `ftcommondamage.c` give the shared resistance and
zero-knockback damage path. `dFTYoshiMotionDescs` maps motions to figatrees.

**Findings.**

- Yoshi's specials are Egg Lay (B), Egg Throw (up+B) and Yoshi Bomb
  (down+B). There is no Egg Roll special in SSB64.
- Jab 1 sets its chain flag at frame 10. Down air makes 14 one-frame pulses,
  clearing and refreshing its hit record between them. The last refresh at
  frame 32 is cleared on that same frame.
- Egg Throw makes an inert held egg at frame 4, throws it at frame 23, and
  uses the B hold duration for throw force. The thrown egg explodes on a hit,
  map contact or its 50-frame expiry. Yoshi Bomb keeps its descent hitbox
  through its zero-speed falling status, then makes two stars on landing
  frame 3.
- Egg Lay searches on frames 18–23. On a catch, its stage events hide and
  then lay the held fighter. The victim's escape wait starts at 250 frames;
  each mash input removes 12. An egg hit queues half damage and shortens the
  wait by four frames per point of that queued damage. Escape grants 12
  frames of timed intangibility.
- The source leaves `YoshiEgg` only after the effect's break animation ends.
  `dYoshiSpecial3_EggLayBreak_AnimJoint_0x9F8` waits 10 frames. The second
  joint's opcode 12 changes animation length without blocking its clock.
  The port therefore counts 10 break frames after the wait expires.
- `ftMainUpdateDamageStatFighter` subtracts the greater of status and
  passive knockback resistance, clamps at zero, and still calls
  `ftParamUpdateDamage`. For an ordinary uncaptured fighter,
  `ftCommonDamageUpdateMain` selects the damage colour animation when the
  remaining knockback is zero, leaving its status and velocity intact.
  Yoshi's aerial jump sets status resistance to 140.

**Deviations.** The laid egg remains on the port's root-sphere hurtbox rather
than the source's fighter-specific egg hurtbox. Its lay position does not
sweep the map for walls or ceilings, and damaging-floor escape awaits ground
hazards. The break effect is represented by its 10-frame clock, without an
effect object. Yoshi is not selectable in `psp-game`; the Egg Throw and star
weapons are simulated but not drawn. The general weapon-shield and hop
callbacks and aerial Bomb ledge catch await their shared systems.

**Verification.** Host tests cover Yoshi's normal joint mapping, down-air
hit generations, Bomb box and stars, Egg Throw timing and explosion, Egg Lay
grab and victim timing, throw frames, jump turn and resistance. The generated
animation table adds 29 Yoshi slots and fills his grab slots. Workspace tests:
958 passed. `romtool anims --verify`: 561 finite lengths agree. Both PSP
builds pass. Pack v37 rebuilt from the ROM at 22,875,008 bytes; SHA-256
`fcbd4197b33b12f51e98a94eaab3150ddcca07d0e6a6b3bc521ddf61bb8cb539`.
The Yoshi-fighter PPSSPP golden matches. This neutral-pose capture does not
validate Yoshi gameplay rendering or physical PSP behavior.
