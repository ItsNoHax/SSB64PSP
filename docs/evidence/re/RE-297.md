# RE-297 — Shared PSP GE renderer extracted into `psp-runtime`; full-matrix pixel parity confirmed, one real bug found and fixed

Status: COMPLETE
Topics: front-end, toolchain, visual-regression, hardware
Relevant files: psp-runtime/src/gu.rs, psp-runtime/src/meshdraw.rs, psp-asset-viewer/src/main.rs, psp-game/src/main.rs, docs/evidence/re/RE-202.md

**Question.** The PSP runtime refactor plan's Step 4 calls for one shared
GE/GPU renderer implementation (`gu.rs`, `meshdraw.rs`) instead of two
independent copies in `psp-asset-viewer` (formerly `psp/`, RE-nothing-new —
see the Step 1/2/3 rename and extraction commits) and `psp-game`. Step 5
requires proving pixel parity against the established regression-golden
matrix before touching `play.rs`. This entry covers both.

**`meshdraw.rs` was already byte-identical** between the two crates (modulo
one doc comment in `psp-game`'s copy explaining the duplication) and moved
into `psp-runtime/src/meshdraw.rs` verbatim.

**`gu.rs` genuinely diverged**, not just by diagnostics:

* `psp-asset-viewer`'s version is the superset: LB-transition/1P-wallpaper
  framebuffer capture (`request_transition_capture`/`request_wallpaper_
  capture`, real ported ROM behavior per RE-099/100/190/191/193, not
  diagnostics), `debug_text`/`FixedStr` (the RE-202 HUD, gated behind
  `debug_overlay`), `draw_triangles`/`draw_line_strip`, `frame_count`.
* `psp-game`'s version added `draw_rect` plus a `set_viewport_pillarboxed`/
  `set_viewport_fullscreen` toggle that `psp-asset-viewer` never had: the
  viewer always renders 3D content and used to bake the pillarboxed
  viewport directly into `Gpu::init`, while `psp-game` needs to switch
  between a flat 2D intro/menu and a pillarboxed 3D training scene.

**Merge design.** One `Gpu` type, the union of both. `Gpu::init` now
defaults to a full-screen viewport/scissor (`psp-game`'s shape, the more
general starting state); `psp-asset-viewer` calls `set_viewport_
pillarboxed()` to reproduce its old always-pillarboxed behavior.
`begin_frame` takes `Option<Color>` (`psp-asset-viewer`'s shape); `psp-
game`'s four call sites wrap their color argument in `Some(...)`.
`transition_photo_data`/wallpaper capture are shared as real functionality;
`psp-game` simply never calls `request_transition_capture`, so its buffer
stays zero-filled exactly as its own prior dedicated stub did.
`sceGuDebugFlush()` stays unconditional in `end_frame` — RE-202's own root
cause is `sceGuDebugPrint`/`sceGuDebugFlush`'s internal state once glyphs
are actually queued, not a bare `Flush` against an empty buffer, so this
does not reintroduce the hardware crash; `debug_text` itself stays behind
`debug_overlay`, which `psp-game` never enables.

**Bug found and fixed during the merge.** `sceGuViewport`/`sceGuScissor`
calls made outside an open `sceGuStart`/`sceGuSync` display-list block are
silently dropped — they never reach the GE. The first attempt called
`gpu.set_viewport_pillarboxed()` once, immediately after `Gpu::init()`
(before any `begin_frame`), matching the pure "call it once at startup"
reading of the old inline-in-`init` behavior. Under PPSSPPHeadless this
produced an unpillarboxed, horizontally stretched Dream Land —
`AE=2.3e9` against `tests/golden/r0-dream-land-default.png`, not the
expected `AE=0`. Moving the call inside the frame loop, immediately after
`begin_frame` (the same pattern `psp-game` already used for its own
viewport toggle, at `gpu.begin_frame(...); gpu.set_viewport_pillarboxed();`)
fixed it: GE viewport/scissor state persists across frames on real
hardware and in PPSSPP, so a per-loop call still self-corrects to the
right state well before the frozen capture tick, even though the very
first frame's clear briefly uses the previous (full-screen) scissor.

**Regression matrix — full sweep, all byte-identical (`AE=0`).**
PPSSPPHeadless, `--crate psp-asset-viewer`, `--feature <name>`, compared
with `magick compare -metric AE` against each existing golden:

* `regression_capture` vs `r0-dream-land-default.png` (ordinary stage)
* `regression_capture_scene2` vs `r1-mvopeningroom.png` (CI8/untextured)
* `regression_capture_scene3` vs `r1-stage-sector.png` (translucency/clamp)
* `regression_capture_scene4` vs `r1-catch-swirl-flat-color.png`
* `regression_capture_scene5` vs `r2-saffron-city-gate.png` (animated stage)
* `regression_capture_scene6`/`7`/`8`/`9` vs the four `r2-metal-texgen*.png`
  (texgen, including the camera- and object-rotated cases)
* `regression_capture_scene10` vs `r2-peach-castle.png`

  Land, 2 Zebes, 5 Mushroom Kingdom, 10 Hyrule Castle, 16 bonus1-Mario) vs
  their `r2-stage-*.png` goldens

28 scenes total, every one `AE=0`. Every diagnostic/audit Cargo feature on
`psp-asset-viewer` (`addr_diag_probe`, `tri_addr_diag_probe`,
`texgen_normal_diagnostic_1`, `wallpaper_audit_capture`, `debug_overlay`,
`billboard_audit_capture`, `animation_audit_capture`,
`effect_audit_capture`, `effect_animation_audit_capture`,
`effect_material_audit_capture`, `particle_render_audit_capture`,
`transition_audit_capture`, `effect_spawn_audit_capture`,
`wallpaper_sprite_audit_capture`) still builds clean.

`psp-game`'s `regression_capture`+`headless_capture` Training capture
still shows the two-Mario jab-range scene from RE-295/296, correctly
pillarboxed (visually confirmed; no committed golden exists for this
capture yet to diff byte-for-byte).

**Not yet done.** Physical-hardware confirmation of `psp-game` with the
now-shared, always-on `sceGuDebugFlush()` call in `end_frame` — RE-202's
reasoning (a bare `Flush` against an empty glyph buffer is safe) is sound
and matches years of `psp-asset-viewer`'s own shipped behavior, but
`psp-game` itself has never been the subject of that specific hardware
proof. Deferred to the refactor's Step 10 acceptance gate, which already
mandates a physical smoke test of both EBOOTs before F1 work resumes.

**Verification.** `cargo test --workspace`: 425 passed. `cargo psp
--release` builds both EBOOTs and every feature combination listed above.
`cargo fmt --check` inside `psp-runtime` (newly authored files) is clean;
pre-existing formatting drift in `psp-asset-viewer`/`psp-game` (never
covered by CI's root-workspace-only `cargo fmt --all --check`) is
unrelated to this change and left alone.
