# RE-315 — Viewer animators tick per simulation tick, not per render frame

Status: COMPLETE (PPSSPP software captures of all 67 goldens with two padded packs, 27 goldens refreshed; physical PSP not captured)
Topics: animation, visual-regression

Relevant files: `psp-asset-viewer/src/main.rs` (fixed-tick loop, stage
render arm), `tests/golden/`, `TODO.md`

**Question.** RE-314 section 2 found that appending 0x4A940 zero bytes to the
pack changed the render-frame count at the tick-240 capture freeze (242 to
241). `psp-asset-viewer` ticked `stage_anim` and `material_anim` once per
render frame, outside the fixed 60 Hz `for _ in 0..ticks` loop, so the pose
at capture depended on load timing. Can every capture be made to depend on
simulation ticks only?

## Audit of per-frame ticks

| Clock | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `material_anim` (`MaterialAnimator`) | Every render frame | Every sim tick |
| `stage_anim` (`StageAnimator`), load and tick | Every render frame of the stage arm | Every sim tick while the stage arm is the one drawn |
| Effect spawn generator and particle (`effect_spawn_view`, RE-189) | Every render frame | Every sim tick |
| `effect_anim`, `effect_mat_anim` (effect audits) | Every render frame, capped at 4 ticks per selection | Unchanged. The cap makes the final pose independent of frame count; these are windowed audits, not goldens |
| `particle_view` particle | Respawned and ticked 4 times every frame | Unchanged: stateless per frame |
| Fighter skeleton, `play::FighterScene`, `results_transition` | Sim tick | Unchanged |
| `psp-game` `material_anim`, fighters, weapons | One tick per main-loop iteration, which is its sim tick, frozen by `deterministic_capture_frozen` | Unchanged |

The stage animator keeps its old gating: it loads when the stage changes and
ticks only while `stage_view` is set and no earlier render arm
(`effect_spawn_view`, `particle_view`, `billboard_view`, `tex_view`) wins.
A desynchronised script still draws the scenery unanimated
(`stage_anim_ok`). All three clocks stop at the freeze, so they advance on
ticks 1 to 239.

## Verification

All captures use PPSSPPHeadless (software GE). Packs: shipped
(`de9f6c64…`), `pad1` = shipped + 0x4A940 zero bytes (RE-314's size),
`pad2` = shipped + 1,223,104 zero bytes.

**Control, old code.** Stage 35 shipped vs `pad1`: 264 pixels differ at 2x
(max 91). Saffron City (`regression_capture_scene5`) shipped vs `pad1`:
260 pixels (max 193). The padded pack does distinguish the old behaviour.

**New code, all 67 goldens** (38 stage-index, scenes 1 to 10, depth mask,
14 fighters, `mario_entry`, `bonus_platform`, and the two `psp-game`
scenes). For each scene: `pad1` vs shipped 0 pixels, `pad2` vs shipped
0 pixels, and a repeat shipped capture 0 pixels.

**Golden changes.** 27 goldens differ from the new captures: 25 stage-index
goldens, `r2-saffron-city-gate` and `r2-peach-castle` (132 to 15,664 pixels
at 2x). To prove every change is the animator timing, both runtimes were
rebuilt with the stage and material animator ticks disabled. With them
disabled, old and new captures are byte-identical on all 33 scenes that
differ from their golden (these 27 and the six known-failing ones below).
Old-code captures of those 27 match their old goldens, so the pack and tree
were the golden baseline.

Changed: `r2-stage-` `beta-dream-land`, `zebes`, `test-stage`,
`mushroom-kingdom`, `kongo-jungle`, `sector-z`, `yoshis-island`,
`final-destination`, `small-yoshis-island`,
`bonus1-{mario,donkey-kong,samus,luigi,link,yoshi,kirby,purin}`,
`bonus2-{mario,donkey-kong,luigi,link,yoshi,kirby,pikachu,ness}`;
`r2-saffron-city-gate`; `r2-peach-castle`. All 27 were refreshed from the
first new capture, which the repeat capture matched byte for byte.

**Known-failing goldens.** The six `psp-asset-viewer` ones
(`r1-mvopeningroom`, four `r2-metal-texgen*`, `r2-depth-mask-diagnostic`)
are byte-identical between old and new code. They still fail for their own
earlier reasons and were not rebaselined. `psp-game` did not change.

Other checks: `cargo test --workspace` 812 passed. Warnings in the viewer
build are unchanged. The new code has no `rustfmt` differences.

No physical PSP run was made.
