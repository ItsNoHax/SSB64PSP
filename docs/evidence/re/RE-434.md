# RE-434 — Throwable utilities: Motion-Sensor Bomb, Bob-omb, Bumper, Shells, Poké Ball

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, ROM attributes and attack events, N64 Mupen64Plus reference of a Training scene with all six items, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: items, combat, asset-pipeline, rendering, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/item/{msbomb,bombhei,nbumper,shell,mball,mod,map,normal,throwable_tests}.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/{map,wpeffect}.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/tests/containers.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`, `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `tests/golden/`

## Makers and attributes

`make_setup_common` now makes `ITKind` 14 to 19: `itMSBombMakeItem`,
`itBombHeiMakeItem`, `itNBumperMakeItem`, `it{G,R}ShellMakeItem` and
`itMBallMakeItem`. Their `ITAttributes` are file 251 + 0x3BC, 0x424,
0x69C, 0x53C, 0x584 and 0x6E4 (`reloc_data.us.h`); the ROM test decodes
each one and the two explosion tables (0x404, 0x46C). All six are light
throw items with hit status none until a status sets it. Spin speeds are
120% (Motion-Sensor Bomb), 70% (Bumper), 20% (Poké Ball) and 0.

`dITMainProcHoldList`, `...ThrownList` and `...DroppedList` dispatch the
new kinds. `itMainSetThrownSpin` now runs after the release for every kind
with a spin speed, as the source does for all items.

## Motion-Sensor Bomb (`itmsbomb.c`)

Thrown or dropped, its map box shrinks to 30 and `itMapCheckMapProcAll`
sticks it to the first surface: velocity zero, owner cleared, hit status
normal, `rotate.z` along the surface (a floor or ceiling wins over a wall).
`is_attach_surface` with `attach_line_id` is the new `Item::attach_line`;
`itProcessProcItemMain` carries an attached item by its line's speed before
the floor rule. After 100 frames any fighter whose centre (`TopN.y +
map_coll.top / 2`) is within 400 units (squared, unrooted) sets it off.
The loop does not stop: each fighter in range sets it off again, so the
callback can make up to twelve effects. `Emit` grows from 6 to 12. Any
damage also sets it off. If its line disappears it detaches, falls and
keeps watching, without the floor dust.

## Bob-omb (`itbombhei.c`)

At rest, after 180 frames of `multi` it walks at 24 towards the side with
more fighters; a tie draws `rand(2) - 1`. It turns at walls and at floor
edges (`mpCollisionGetFloorEdge*` with `map_coll_width`). After 480 walking
frames it stops, takes `ExplodeWait` for 90 frames, then explodes.
`multi` carries over a pickup, so a Bob-omb dropped after 180 frames never
walks. Thrown or dropped, any surface (`ExplodeMap`) or any hit, shield,
set-off or damage explodes it. The critical colour animation and the walk
display lists are display only and not ported.

## Bumper (`itnbumper.c`)

It hits fighters only. Thrown, walls and ceilings rebound it at 0.8 and the
first floor attaches it for 360 frames with its model laid along the floor.
A hit there swells X and Z (not Y), lights the palette for three frames and
slides it back at 100, stopping at floor edges and when the swell is nearly
over. A hit in the air sends it back at (-400, 200) under gravity 4. It
then blinks for 60 frames and is gone. Its `damage_all_delay` is a `u16`
compared with -1, which never matches; the port keeps the wrap.

## Shells (`itgshell.c`, `itrshell.c`)

A hit on a resting shell launches it at `damage * 8` (Green) or `* 10`
(Red) against the attacker's side, takes the attacker as owner
(`itMainCopyDamageStats`) and starts the slide. The Green Shell clamps to
90, hurts its owner again after 32 frames and stops after 240 sliding
frames; leaving a floor falls rather than slides on (a source oversight).
The Red Shell clamps to 70, steers 1.2 a frame towards the nearest fighter
in link order (a tie goes to the later one), reverses at floor edges and
walls, breaks on its 24th interaction or after 480 frames, and starts a
landing slide at `(lr * -8 - 10) * 0.7`. Both `damage_all_delay`s are `u8`
compared with -1, so the countdown repeats every 256 frames. Both shells
ignore `itMapCheckDestroyDropped`'s despawn result.

## Poké Ball (`itmball.c`)

Its second landing (`times_landed == 2`) opens it; a ball that bounced off
a target opens on its next floor contact. Thirty frames later
`itMainMakeMonster` draws a Pokémon and the ball is gone. A reflected ball
reverts to its holder (`item_vars.mball.owner_gobj`). `MonsterData` ports
`gITManagerMonsterData`: the last two Pokémon are left out, the count runs
12, 11, then 10, and Mew needs a newcomer unlocked
(`ItemPool::unlock_newcomers`, off without save data) and a 1-in-151 draw.
The 13 Pokémon makers make nothing yet, so the draw and the bookkeeping are
all that happen. The rays (`efManagerMBallRaysMakeEffect`) are not made.

## Landing squares the item root

`itMapProcAllCheckCollisionFlag` sets `spin_step` and `rotate.z` to zero
when an airborne item lands. The port did not. A resting Tomato and
Capsule now stand upright. The RE-433 N64 frames show the resting Tomato
upright; the old goldens showed it tilted. This changes three goldens:
`f1-training-utility` (1,632 pixels), `f1-training-capsule` (444) and
`f1-vs-time-up` (12, the corner of an item at the screen edge). Each was
captured twice with no difference.

## Drawing and the Green Shell's palette

`gcAddXObjForDObjFixed(TraRotRpyR)` resets the DObj's rotation
(`gcAddXObjForDObjVar`), and the US makers save and restore only the
translation. The Shells' `rotate.y = 90°` is therefore lost before their
first draw, and kind 0x48 (`func_ovl0_800CAB48`, `R · gGCMatrixPerspF`
with the DObj scale) shows their XY quad face-on. The first draw used 90°
and showed the quad edge-on; the N64 frame shows it face-on.

`itGShellMakeItem` sets `palette_id` 1 and `itRShellMakeItem` 0. The tree
at file 86 + 0x5F88 binds palette 0 (red, 0x55A0). `romtool` now packs the
list at 0x5EC0 a second time under `palettes[1]` (0x5578), keyed (86,
0x5578), as Luigi's Fireball is. The pack grows by 816 bytes; its version
is unchanged.

`psp-game` draws the Bob-omb and the Bumper as kind-0x2E billboards scaled
by the root, the Shells through `model_transform_camera_rotated`, and the
Motion-Sensor Bomb and Poké Ball with their ball as a kind-0x46 billboard
and their armed or open shape under the root's `rotate.z`.

## N64 reference

A temporary decomp build booted Training on Dream Land with two Marios and
made the six items at Mario + (-1100, 500), (-750, 500), (-400, 500),
(450, 500), (800, 500) and (75, 300) at the end of
`sc1PTrainingModeFuncStart`'s fighter loop. Route: A at 330, A at 373.
The patch was reverted and the decomp rebuilt to SHA-1 `e2929e10…`. The
`trainingthrowable` scene places the items the same way and presses A at
ticks 60 and 100. Compared by event: the Bob-omb, Motion-Sensor Bomb,
Bumper and Poké Ball match; both Shells match after the two fixes above.
On the N64 the released Pokémon's attack sends Mario off the stage; the
port releases nothing. The two runs' throws go in different directions
(the port's ball flies left into the Bob-omb, which explodes), so the
later frames were not compared one to one.
