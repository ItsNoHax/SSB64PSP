**Deviations.**
- `DEADUP_TOTAL_WAIT` was `180 × 2 + 45`. The source runs `wait = 1`, then
  `FTCOMMON_DEADUP_WAIT` (180), then `FTCOMMON_DEAD_WAIT` (45): 226 updates.
- `coll_mask_ignore` and `wall_collide_angle` are written but never read in
  the source; they are not kept.
- `ftCommonThrownReleaseFighterLoseGrip` moves a fighter caught mid-throw to
  its joint 4 minus 300 and re-runs map collision; the pair release here only
  drops the links.
- A weapon's reaction still happens as each fighter is searched rather than
  in `wpProcessProcHitCollisions` after all of them (unchanged from RE-333).
- Items (PK Fire pillars) do not clank; `ftMainSearchHitItem` is unported.
- Weapons other than the Boomerang keep their existing shield handling;
  their own `ProcHop` is not ported.

**Pack.** `tools/gen-anim-table.py` adds 25 reaction slots after the existing
446 (`SLOT_WALL_DAMAGE`, `SLOT_COUNT` 471) for all twelve fighters through the
common status → motion pairing. `DownWaitD/U` (motion −2) keep the bounce's
clip and `ReboundWait` (−1) the clanked attack's (`AnyStatus::keeps_motion`).
Samus's `RollF`/`RollB` figatrees are transcribed as a pointer table plus
`_scriptN_M` arrays with unreferenced data disassembled before the table; the
generator now follows the table, which also gave `motion::anim_length` her
roll length (46; it had been an unknown length, so her roll never ended).
`romtool`'s `reaction_slots_and_lengths_match_the_anim_table` checks every
reaction slot name and pins the pack lengths to the motion descs for all
twelve fighters. `psp-runtime` feeds TransN root motion to the rolls, techs
and getup rolls (`reaction::moves_by_transn`). Pack `VERSION` 39.

The first v39 build dropped Samus from the pack (`fighters 26/27`): the ROM
decoder (`anim::decode_length`) and `romtool`'s `joint_table` took the table
length as the first pointer / 4, which for `RollF` is 76 entries past a
24-entry table. `anim::joint_table_len` now also stops at the first word that
cannot be a script pointer. `romtool anims --verify` then decodes all 27
fighters and matches all 1,053 decomp lengths, and the rebuilt pack packs
27/27 fighters and 1,392 clips. Against v38 (version field patched), all
1,092 existing clips are byte-identical (frames, joint table, script bytes);
the 300 new ones are the reaction slots.
