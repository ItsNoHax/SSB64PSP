# RE-522 — Entry effects play at make time and draw lit; lit untextured lists keep prim alpha

Status: COMPLETE (N64 captures of the entry rosters, PPSSPP)
Topics: fighter entry, effects, lighting, alpha, samus, poke ball
Relevant files: `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/mesh.rs`

## Question

Why did the Poké Ball and other entry effects flash a wrong first frame, and why was Samus's capsule glass tube missing?

## Findings

- `efManagerMakeEffect` calls `gcPlayAnimAll` once when it makes the effect, and the effect makers sort after that call, so the effect has already played its first frame on the frame it is made. The port started the effect a tick late. `sync_entry` now starts the effect one tick in and `settle_appear` places the fighter from the first TransN frame on the entry frame.
- Entry effects are lit like items (`configure_item_light` with the stage light angle), not unlit.
- Samus's glass tube (file 349 + 0xAD0) is an untextured lit display list with prim alpha. RE-521's prim-alpha lowering excluded lit lists, so its alpha read 0. The `flat_prim_alpha_untextured` gate no longer requires `!lit`; the lit shade colour is blended by the prim alpha.
- Donkey Kong's AppearR script runs `Effect(BoxSmash)` after the sparkle, which calls `itBoxContainerSmashMakeEffect` (seven staves, 42 RNG draws). The extra draws shift the RNG state for the CPU fight that follows, which moved four VS golden rows.

## Verification

- Roster C: the flash at ticks 202-206 is gone; MAD against the N64 at ticks 218-238 is 9.9-12.6, against 12.2-15.9 before.
- The tube is drawn, lit and colour-matched against the N64.
- Tests: `a_lit_shade_colour_with_prim_alpha_blends_by_the_prim_alpha`, `donkey_kongs_box_smash_makes_the_crate_staves`.
