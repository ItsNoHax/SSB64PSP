# RE-423 — Stage colours: tile-loaded strips, the lighting bit the stream sets, and the DL-link-6 objects' depth state

Status: IMPLEMENTED (ROM display lists and texel data dumped from the relocated files, N64 frame-414 RDRAM display-list walks from the RE-422 warp-boot builds on eight VS stages, new N64 warp-boot captures of Sector Z's battle and Yoshi's Island's stage select, packed-asset ROM tests, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice and broken by each intermediate pack; no physical PSP)
Topics: rendering, stage, texture, lighting, depth, asset-pack, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-rom/src/{mesh,pack,wide_tile}.rs`, `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/tests/stage_colour_fidelity.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `tests/golden/*.png`, `tests/golden/scenes.tsv`

## Questions

1. Yoshi's Island draws its platforms grey and stripes around the smiling
   heart where the N64 draws coloured platforms and fruit, in battle and on
   the stage select. Why?
2. Planet Zebes's tube draws blue where the N64 shows dark teal, and the
   ground near it lacks the N64's red tint. Why?
3. Saffron City's door lights are fainter than on the N64. Why?
4. Do the nine VS stages show other colour differences at a fixed tick?

## Method

- **ROM.** A probe wrote each relocated file (`Archive::load`) and its
  relocation table; a Python F3DEX2 walker disassembled the display lists,
  vertices and TLUTs of files 105, 110–112, 154, 160 and their
  neighbours.
- **N64.** The RE-422 frame-414 RDRAM dumps (`~/ppsspp-test/re422/out2-*`)
  were walked again. Each file was located in RDRAM by matching its
  pointer-free 64-byte blocks, and every `G_VTX` and triangle of its lists
  read with the geometry mode and render mode in force. Two new warp-boot
  builds (`scmanager.c` only): Training on Sector Z, and the stage select
  entered from Training's character select with `maps_training_gkind` on
  Yoshi's Island (`~/ppsspp-test/re423/`). Both patches were reverted; the
  rebuilt ROM's SHA-1 is `e2929e10…`.
- **Port.** Five new capture scenes (`trainingyoster`, `trainingsector`,
  `trainingcastle`, `traininghyrule`, `trainingpupupu`) join RE-422's four,
  so all nine VS stages are captured in Training at tick 400 and compared
  with the N64 at frame 414 (aspect corrected).

## Findings

- **Yoshi's Island's fruit panel is tile-loaded.** Layer 0's list at file
  111 + 0x640 draws the fruit (110 + 0x1030, RGBA32, `G_SETTIMG` width 66,
  66 × 64 texels) in five strips. Each strip `G_LOADTILE`s rows 0–13,
  13–26, 26–39, 39–52 and 52–63 (columns 0–64) into TMEM 0 and draws
  through a tile whose `SETTILESIZE` names the same rows. The converter
  knew only block loads: it read every strip from the image's first texel
  at the tile's 65-texel width (`line` 17 is 34 RGBA32 texels, narrower
  than the tile, so the pitch fell back to the width). Every strip drew the
  image's top rows sheared by one texel per row: the red and green diagonal
  stripes. The tile-loaded strips of files 52, 75, 111, 114 and 118 now
  read their rectangles.
- **Stage lists state their lighting, and the N64 draws them unlit.** Every
  stage list examined starts by clearing `G_LIGHTING` (`d9fdffff
  00000000`, e.g. 111 + 0x49B0, 105 + 0x48A0, 112 + 0x8690) and sets it
  again at its end. In the eight frame-414 traces, all 527 `G_VTX` loads of
  the stage files (104–108, 111–113 and the controller files 152, 154, 155,
  157, 158, 160) run with `G_LIGHTING` off. The packer nevertheless read any
  vertex colour whose bytes form a near-unit vector (`looks_like_unit_normal`,
  RE-021) as a normal and shaded it grey. Yoshi's Island's right platform
  top (227, 227, 132; as signed bytes −29, −29, −124, length 131) drew
  (86, 86, 86); Zebes's tube and the ground beside it lost their teal and
  red vertex colours the same way. The walker knew the list had cleared the
  bit; the packer never asked.
- **Saffron City's gate hid the lights.** The door frame's inner wall (112
  + 0x63E0) carries the seven yellow lights (a CI4 16 × 8 texture at 112 +
  0x1850, repeated to 56 rows). The gate (`grYamabukiMakeGate`, file 160
  + 0x8A0, DL link 6) opens at the battle's start (`gate_wait` 1) and its
  three panels slide to z = ±330 and y = −30 (the frame-414 matrices).
  Their lists set no render mode and do not touch `G_ZBUFFER`; the trace
  shows their triangles under `G_ZBUFFER`, `Z_CMP` and `Z_UPD` (render mode
  0x552078), the state layer 1 set on the same link and its lists restore.
  The port seeded the gate like a layer-0 graph (no depth test), so the open
  panel beside the frame drew over the lights. Mushroom Kingdom's scale
  platforms (155 + 0x5F0, also link 6) had the same seed; the clouds and the
  barrel set their own render modes and already matched.
- **The other stages.** Kongo Jungle, Peach's Castle, Hyrule Castle, Dream
  Land, Mushroom Kingdom and Sector Z (new N64 capture) match the N64 in
  colour after the lighting change, which also removes Peach's Castle's
  grey spire side and gives Sector Z's wall its orange underglow. Dream
  Land's 104 + 0x2A68 was checked the same way: the N64 loads it with
  lighting off (geometry mode 0x200404), after 0x2978 clears it.

## Implementation

- **`G_LOADTILE` rectangles** (`mesh.rs`): `G_SETTIMG` records its width
  and texel size; a `G_LOADTILE` records its TMEM address, source image,
  the byte offset of its corner (`(ult × width + uls) × bits / 8`) and the
  image's row pitch. A texture drawn from that TMEM address and image reads
  from the corner at that pitch (`TextureRef::data_offset`, `source_width`).
  A block load to the same address, an `MObj` sprite or a dropped binding
  forgets the rectangle.
- **Known lighting** (`MeshVertex::lit_known`): set when a geometry-mode
  command that set or cleared `G_LIGHTING`, a `G_MW_LIGHTCOL` or a lit seed
  preceded the `G_VTX`. `pack::add_mesh` and `wide_tile` recover a normal
  from the colour's shape only for a vertex whose lighting is unknown. After
  the change 29 lists still use that fallback: the loading-break aeroplane
  (file 40), the Newcomers movie (61, 62), 52 + 0x1C330, 114 + 0x40B8, 307
  + 0x1BD8, 320 + 0xA968 and 356 + 0x560.
- **DL-link-6 controller objects** (`ground_layer1_graphs`): the gate, the
  clouds, the barrel and the scale strings and platforms take layer 1's seed
  (`GROUND_LAYER1_EXTERNAL`, list 1 under `G_RM_AA_ZB_XLU_SURF`).
- **Pack v75** (34,589,696 bytes, SHA-256
  `dfa1768f6a0b00b2ee6cb009eaf5111412425f18dc6be8d672eea12041130a58`,
  built twice with identical bytes; layout unchanged). The strips add
  134,176 bytes of textures and the lighting change 448 bytes; no texture's
  texels change with the lighting.
- **Scenes.** `trainingyoster`, `trainingsector`, `trainingcastle`,
  `traininghyrule`, `trainingpupupu`: Training on that stage with no input,
  frozen at tick 400.

## Deviations and open items

- **Planet Zebes's upper platforms show a yellow fringe.** Their CI4
  texture (105 + 0x1B28, TLUT 105 + 0x300) keeps a yellow RGB (246, 222,
  57) in its transparent entry 0, drawn under `G_RM_AA_ZB_TEX_EDGE` with a
  zero alpha threshold. Bilinear filtering mixes that colour into the edge
  texels; the GE's cutout gate (`alpha >= 1`, RE-069) keeps them opaque,
  while the RDP scales their coverage by alpha (`CVG_X_ALPHA |
  ALPHA_CVG_SEL`), so they keep little or no coverage and are
  antialiased. The N64 capture shows no yellow
  ring. Changing the cutout gate moves every `TEX_EDGE` surface and the
  texture compensation that trains against the gate, so it is left for its
  own batch (TODO).
- Zebes's platform lamps and the Training HUD differ by animation phase and
  by what the port does not draw, not by colour.

## Conclusion

Three converter faults explain the stage colour differences. A
`G_LOADTILE` rectangle was read from the image's start at the tile's width,
shearing Yoshi's Island's fruit strips; vertices after an explicit
`G_LIGHTING` clear were still reinterpreted as normals whenever their
colours happened to look like one, greying Yoshi's Island's platforms and
Zebes's tube and ground; and the DL-link-6 controller objects were packed
with no depth test, so Saffron City's open gate covered the door lights.
With the rectangle, the stream's lighting bit and layer 1's seed, all nine
VS stages match the N64 in colour at frame 414, except Zebes's cutout
fringe.
