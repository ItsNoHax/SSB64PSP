# RE-478 — The crash after Race to the Finish: Polygons on the ally ports

Status: COMPLETE (decomp source; PPSSPP captures of the campaign through every stage transition after each fixture-cleared stage, at the XMB's memory budget, before and after the fix; host tests with the FPU traps enabled; the PSP was not reachable, so the user's PSP is the hardware check)
Topics: campaign, crash, ui, fpu, hardware, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/spgame/{intro,frontend_tests}.rs`, `psp-game/src/{campaign_screen,main}.rs`, `refs/ssb-decomp-re/src/sc/sc1pmode/sc1pintro.c`

## Question

The user's PSP-2000 (6.61 ARK, started from the XMB, production build of
`a440575`, pack v107) crashed in the 1P campaign "when you leave through
the door at the end of" Race to the Finish, the bonus stage after Metal
Mario. What fails?

## Findings

| Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|
| A Rust panic, not memory, stack or an FPU trap. Under PPSSPP the cleared Race (`onepraceclear`, A every 30 ticks after its capture) panics on tick 871, the first frame of the Fighting Polygon Team's intro, right after the results' A; at the XMB's 47,891,712 bytes and without ballast alike | `rust_panic` in the PPSSPPHeadless log at every capture tick from 871, none at 870 and before; free memory then 36.7 MB, stack peak 243,792 of 524,288 bytes |
| The panic is `intro_text`'s VS name: `NAMES.offsets[players[ally_players[i]].fkind]` for both ally ports, on every stage. The name sprite file has twelve names (Mario to Ness); a Polygon's kind is 14–25 | Temporary capture-log breadcrumbs stop inside `intro_text` |
| The ally ports keep the last battle's opponents. The Race's three Polygons sit on ports 1–3 when the Polygon Team's intro starts; the Polygon Team's on its continue and on Master Hand's intro. Every other intro either has real allies (Mario Bros., Giant Donkey Kong: `sc1PManagerUpdateScene` picks them) or a playable kind left over, or is a bonus intro, which returns before the name | Host campaign walk: Polygons on the ally ports at three intros |
| The source reads the allies' kinds for every stage (`sc1PIntroFuncStart`) but looks up their names only for `sc1PIntroGetAlliesNum(stage)` allies (`sc1PIntroMakeVSName`, the name widths) | `src/sc/sc1pmode/sc1pintro.c` |
| No capture reached this frame: every 1P fixture jumps to its stage's intro from the select, where the ports hold defaults | `capture_fixture` |

So any player who clears the Race and confirms its results crashes there,
whatever the stage's outcome (a failed Race goes to the results too). On
a PSP the panic handler unwinds and ends the game thread; the last frame,
the results, stays on screen.

## Fix

`spgame::intro::named_allies` gives the kinds the VS name reads: the first
`allies_num(stage)` ally ports, as `sc1PIntroMakeVSName`. `intro_text` and
the intro's ally models use it and `allies_num`. Nothing else changes: the
names, widths and positions of the Mario Bros.' and Giant Donkey Kong's
intros are the same values.

## FPU audit of the route

PPSSPP does not trap the FPU (RE-469). The door's completion
(`grBonus3FinishProcUpdate`), the battle's end, `init_bonus_stats`, the
results' ledgers, the intros' entrances and the Polygon Team's battle
start were read for `0/0`, `x/0`, `0×inf`, square roots of negatives and
uninitialised floats. The results are integer arithmetic; the only float
division is Cheap Shot's `count / total`, behind `total != 0`, and the
PSP build tests `total` before the `div.s` (disassembly of
`campaign::finish_battle`, the only `div.s` there), so it is not
speculated.

## Verification

- `frontend_tests::every_campaign_intro_names_only_its_own_allies` walks
  a campaign through the frontend from Link's intro to the ending, A every
  30 ticks (the Race completed, the Polygon Team lost once and continued),
  and checks at every intro that the names read are playable fighters and
  that Polygons were on the ally ports three times. Reading both ports, as
  before, it fails at the first intro; without its count check, at the
  Race's intro, whose ally port holds Metal Mario (the old code returned
  before the name on bonus intros).
- `a_campaign_through_every_stage_raises_no_fpu_exception`: the same walk
  under glibc's divide-by-zero, invalid and overflow traps. An injected
  `0.0 / 0.0` in the results' tick kills it with SIGFPE.
- PPSSPP, `memory_ballast` at 47,891,712 bytes, the capture scenes now
  continue past their capture tick with A every 30 ticks:

| Run | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| `onepraceclear@871`–`@1300` | panic | Polygon Team intro (tick 900), its battle (1000–4000) |
| `onepmetal@2000`–`@6000` | — | Race, a failed Race's results, the Polygon Team, Master Hand (stage 13) |
| `onepzako@3000`–`@6000` | — | the Polygon Team's results, Master Hand's intro and battle |
| `oneptargetclear`, `onepplatformclear` `@1500`, `@2500` | — | Mario Bros.' and Kirby Team's battles |
| `onepgiant@2500`, `@3500` | — | Board the Platforms |
| `onepbossdefeat@2500`, `@4000` | — | the ending, the staff roll |

  No panic in any; deepest stack 295,232 bytes; lowest free memory
  32.4 MB.

## Remaining

The fix has not run on a PSP: the PSP was in USB mass-storage mode. The
production EBOOT is the user's check.
