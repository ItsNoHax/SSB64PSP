# RE-381 — Ness in Training: PK Fire spark, PK Thunder and PSI Magnet

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: fighter, weapon, effect, asset-pipeline, visual-regression
Relevant files: `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/pack.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/{ness,ness_weapon,weapon}.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`, `tests/golden/f1-training-ness*.png`

## Question

What do Ness's PK Fire spark, PK Thunder head and trail, and PSI Magnet
field draw, under which transforms?

## Source

- **PK Fire spark.** `dWPNessPKFireWeaponDesc` (flags 0x00, `Tra` then
  kind 0x2E) and `llNessSpecial1PKFireWeaponAttributes` (file 240 + 0x00)
  name file 336's direct list at 0x168, the MObjSub table at 0x000 and the
  material table at 0x1E0, with no transform animation.
  `wpNessPKFireMakeWeapon` sets `rotate.z = (angle + 90°) * lr`, and both
  `wpNessPKFireProcHop` and `wpNessPKFireProcReflector` negate it.
- **PK Thunder head.** `dWPNessPKThunderHeadWeaponDesc` and the reflected
  head's desc (flags 0x03, `Tra` then kind 0x2E) share
  `llNessMainPKThunderWeaponAttributes` (file 239 + 0x0C). It names file
  335's two-node tree at 0x7C98, the MObjSub table at 0x7AF8, the
  transform table at 0x7D20 (the root's scale pulse, 1.2/1.0/0.755) and
  the material table at 0x7D70 (node 1's texture blink). While steered,
  `rotate.z` is `angle - 90°`. The reflected head
  (`wpNessPKThunderReflectHeadMakeWeapon`) is a new weapon with
  `rotate.z = atan2(vel_air)`.
- **PK Thunder trail.** `dWPNessPKThunderTrailWeaponDesc` (flags 0x02,
  `TraRotRpyRSca`) and `llNessMainPKThunderTrailWeaponAttributes` (file
  239 + 0x40) name file 335's `DObjDLLink` array at 0x8B40 and the MObjSub
  table at 0x89C8. Each update sets `rotate.z` to the direction from the
  previous trail sample, less 90°.
- **PSI Magnet.** `dEFManagerNessPsychicMagnetEffectDesc` (flags
  `0x4 | EFFECT_FLAG_USERDATA`) builds file 352's tree at 0x9A8. The root
  is kind 0x50 (80), a translation to TopN. The children are `Tra` then
  kind 0x2E. The update is plain `gcPlayAnimAll`, with no pause and no
  eject. `ftNessSpecialLwInitVars`, from both Hold setters, makes it once
  (`is_effect_attach`). Hold, Hit and their ground/air switches pass
  `FTSTATUS_PRESERVE_EFFECT`; the End status does not.

## Implementation

- Pack v57 (27,259,232 bytes):
  - The spark (336, 0x168) and trail (335, 0x8B40) become one-node
    objects (`DIRECT_WEAPON_GRAPHS`).
  - The spark, head and trail get their MObjSub pairings, and the spark
    and head get their material tables.
  - All three are packed under the weapon seed.
  - The head's scale pulse is weapon slot 4 (0x7D20..0x7D70, windowed).
  - The head's material script brings file 335 (50,112 bytes) into the
    material-animation blobs whole; this is most of the 75 KB growth.
- `PKFire` gains `rotate_z` and `anim_ticks`. `PKThunder` gains
  `anim_ticks` (reset by the reflect) and `rotate_z()`. `PKThunderTrail`
  exposes `rotation()`. `NessState::magnet_effect` counts the field's
  plays, and `ness::magnet_effect_ticks` returns it only in the Hold and
  Hit statuses.
- `psp-game` draws:
  - the spark as a kind-46 billboard;
  - the head's node 1 as a billboard sized by the root's animated scale;
  - each trail under `Rz(rotation)`;
  - each magnet node as a billboard at TopN plus its composed offset.
- Capture scenes: `ness` (the PK Fire spark in flight), `nessthunder` (the
  head with four trails) and `nessmagnet` (B held in `SpecialLwHold`).

## Deviations

- PK Fire's flames are an item (`itNessPKFire`) and are not drawn, like
  every other item.
- The head's child also carries kind 46. Its own quad is drawn with the
  root's pulse and scale 1 of its own; the composition of two kind-46
  matrices was not traced further.
- The magnet's children are placed in world axes under TopN's position, as
  kind 80 carries no rotation.

## Verification

A host test covers the magnet field: made on the Hold entry with one
play, kept through a Hit, and gone in `SpecialAirLwEnd`. Capture logs show
one spark, one head with four trails, and `SpecialLwHold`, respectively.
`tools/golden.sh verify --twice` matched all 92 scenes. RE-328's head-1
census (`camera_head1_graphs_restore_xlu_mode`) now counts 104 graphs,
the trail's among them, still with 3 mode writers. Workspace tests pass
with an absolute `SSB64_ROM` and one test thread.
