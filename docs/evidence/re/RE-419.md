# RE-419 — Stage wallpapers and the stage select's presentation

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, `reloc_data.us.h`, ROM sprite decode, packed-asset ROM tests, host tests, N64 warp-boot references, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice and broken by disabling the draw, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: stage, sprites, menus, asset-pack, rendering, visual-regression, psp-platform
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-rom/src/{stage,sprite,pack}.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/tests/wallpapers.rs`, `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/{wallpaper,stage_select_layer}.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/meshdraw.rs`, `psp-game/src/{main,stage_screen}.rs`, `tests/golden/f1-*.png`

## Question

No stage drew its wallpaper, and the stage select (both Training's and
VS's, `mnMaps`) drew ten plain rectangles (RE-385). Which sprite does each
stage show behind its geometry, how does it move with the camera, and
what does `mnMaps` draw?

## Source

- **Decision** (`gr/grwallpaper.c`, `grWallpaperMakeDecideKind`). Training
  loads its own sprite (`sc1PTrainingModeLoadWallpaper`) and makes a
  static wallpaper; so do the bonus stages (`gkind >= 17`) and Yoshi's
  Island (5 and 12). Sector Z (1) is the sector kind, Race to the Finish
  (15) a black fill of (10, 10) to (310, 230), Final Destination (16)
  swaps in its own sprite first; every other stage is the common kind.
- **Sprite.** `MPGroundData.wallpaper` at header + 0x48 (between
  `layer_mask` and `fog_color`). In all nine VS `GR*Map` files it is an
  extern relocation to offset 0x26C88 of the stage's own file (88 to 99),
  which is the `dMNMapsWallpaperOffsets` and
  `dSC1PTrainingModeWallpaperHeapOffsets` value both screens subtract.
  Each is a 300 × 220 RGBA16 `Sprite`, opaque in every texel. Training's
  three (`dSC1PTrainingModeWallpaperDescs`) are files 0x1A (black), 0x1B
  (yellow) and 0x1C (blue) at 0x20718, the same shape.
  `dSC1PTrainingModeWallpaperIDs` gives blue to Peach's Castle, Hyrule,
  Dream Land, Saffron and Mushroom Kingdom, black to Sector Z, Kongo Jungle
  and Zebes, and yellow to Yoshi's Island.
  `sc1PTrainingModeInitDisplayVars` also writes the desc's colour over
  `fog_color`: (0, 0, 0), (0xEE, 0x9E, 0x06) or (0xAF, 0xF5, 0xFF).
- **Common** (`grWallpaperMakeCommon`, `grWallpaperCalcPersp`). Made at
  (10, 10), scale 1.004, `SP_TEXSHUF`. Each update takes `dist = eye -
  at` of the battle camera; `scale = 20000 / (|dist| + 8000)` clamped to
  1.004..2; with `dist.z >= 0` the angles are `atan2(dist.x, dist.z)` and
  `atan2(dist.y, dist.z)` (else 0); `x = angle_y / π · 300s − (300s −
  320) / 2` and `y = −angle_x / π · 220s − (220s − 240) / 2`, each clamped
  so the image covers (10, 10) to (310, 230).
- **Static** (`grWallpaperMakeStatic`): (10, 10), scale 1,
  `SP_TEXSHUF | SP_FASTCOPY`, no process.
- **Sector** (`grWallpaperSectorProcUpdate`): `scale = 20000 / (|dist| +
  10000)` clamped to 1.004..2, placed at `10 − 300 (s − 1) / 2`, `10 −
  220 (s − 1) / 2`: scaled about its centre, never panned.
- **Order.** `gmCameraMakeWallpaperCamera` draws link 0 at camera
  priority 80, before the stage camera (50), over the battle viewport.
  The wallpaper's process (priority 3) is made after the battle camera's
  (also 3), so it reads the camera of the same tick. While paused,
  `ifCommonBattlePauseUpdateInterface` runs the camera and then
  `grWallpaperRunProcessAll`; the flag `grWallpaperPausePerspUpdate` sets
  is never read.
- **Stage select** (`mn/mnmaps/mnmaps.c`, US). Cameras, back to front, all
  over (10, 10) to (310, 230):
  - 80: the stone tile (`MNSelectCommon` 0x440) wrapped over 300 × 220 at
    (10, 10).
  - 70: a black fill of (43, 130) to (152, 211) at alpha 0x73; seven tile
    strips (`MNMaps` 0xC728) from x 43 in steps of 16 at y 130; then the
    preview wallpaper at (40, 127), scale 0.37, `SP_FASTCOPY` cleared:
    Training's sprite for that stage in Training, the stage's own in VS;
    or `RandomBig` (0xDE30) at (40, 127) for the random slot.
  - 65: the preview model: each of the stage's four `gr_desc` layers with
    its joint and material animations from frame 0, the root `DObj`
    scaled by `scales[gkind]` (0.5, 0.2, 0.6, 0.5, 0.3, 0.6, 0.5, 0.4,
    0.2). Saffron hides layer 3's root's child's child; Yoshi's Island
    hides layer 0's 15th and 17th `DObj`s in tree order (the root is 1).
    The camera (`dGCPerspDefault`: 30°, 4:3, near 100; far 16384) looks
    from (−3000, 3000, 9000) at `positions[gkind]`. Its thread sets
    `at.y = sin(deg) · 40 + y`, `deg += 2`, where `y` is the target's
    height when the thread first ran: a later stage's own target height is
    overwritten at once. The random slot makes no model and keeps the
    camera.
  - 60: the icons of the unlocked slots at (30 + 50i, 30) and (50i − 220,
    68).
  - 50: the red cursor (0x1AB8) at (50·slot + 23, 23) or (50·slot − 227,
    61).
  - 40: the wooden plaque (0x3840) at (189, 124).
  - 30: fills (160, 128)–(320, 134) in (0x57, 0x60, 0x88) and (194,
    189)–(268, 193) black at 0x33; "Stage Select" (0x26A0) at (172, 122)
    in (0xAF, 0xB1, 0xCC); the name plate: left end at (174, 191), 19
    middles from x 186 to 258 (these keep `SP_FASTCOPY`), right end at
    (262, 191).
  - 20: the stage's series emblem (`FTEmblemSprites`) in (0x5C, 0x22, 0)
    at (189, 124) plus `mnMapsSetLogoPosition`'s offset, and its name in
    black at (183, 196); or the question mark at (223, 144).
  - The US `mnMapsMakeSubtitle` is empty.
- Sprite offsets are `reloc_data.us.h`'s `llMNMaps*` (file 30), their
  formats checked by decoding: the names, cursor and question mark I4,
  "Stage Select" IA8, the plaque and random pictures CI4, the plates,
  icons and tiles RGBA16.

## Implementation

- **Pack v70** (34,283,632 bytes, SHA-256
  `51af4130bc070416e58b6eb4f8b69b3ef93f2f65b4d8e32c0e16b37ec51f847c`, up
  3,573,376 from v69). `stage::GroundData::wallpaper` and
  `stage::wallpaper` read the header's pointer. The nine stage wallpapers
  are `SpriteDesc::ROLE_WALLPAPER` rows whose `costume` is the `GRKind`
  (`Pack::stage_wallpaper`); Training's three are plain rows at their file
  and offset; all twelve are 5551. `sprite::MN_MAPS` (28 sprites) joins
  the 8888 sprite files. No layout change.
- **`ssb_game::wallpaper`.** `decide_kind`, `Wallpaper::make` and
  `update` (the common and sector processes), `training_wallpaper` with
  its fog colour.
- **`ssb_game::stage_select_layer`.** `Layer::new`, `tick` (preview
  remade on a new slot, the camera's bob), `camera`, `model` (scale and
  hidden nodes) and `visit`, which yields the pieces above in camera
  order.
- **`psp-runtime`.** `meshdraw::draw_wallpaper` draws the sprite opaque
  under `G_CC_DECALRGBA`; `draw_stage_preview` draws a stage's layers
  posed by a `StageAnimator`, hiding given nodes with their subtrees.
- **`psp-game`.** `Session::make_wallpaper` runs on every battle entry:
  the kind, the sprite (resolved once, not per frame) and, in Training,
  the fighters' star-KO fog colour. The draw updates the wallpaper from
  the battle camera and draws it right after the clear. `stage_screen`
  draws the select from the layer and keeps the preview's boxed stage and
  material animators, restarted each time the preview is made. The select
  now clears black, as `gcMakeDefaultCameraGObj` does.
- **Scenes.** `stageselectview` and `stageselectyoshi` leave Training's
  select on Hyrule Castle and Yoshi's Island, captured at tick 60;
  `vssector` and `vsyoshi` are `vs`'s countdown on Sector Z and Yoshi's
  Island (tick 300).

## Evidence

- **ROM tests.** `sprite.rs` decodes the 28 `MNMaps` sprites, Training's
  three and the nine stage wallpapers (all 300 × 220 RGBA16 at 0x26C88,
  opaque, nine files). `tests/wallpapers.rs` checks each packed wallpaper
  against its header's pointer, that all twelve are 5551, that every
  `MNMaps` sprite is packed, and that four Dream Land texels read back from
  the pack equal the ROM's.
- **N64 references** (`tools/run-n64-headless.sh` on warp-boot builds of
  the decomp, `scManagerRunLoop` patched and reverted; SHA-1 back to
  `e2929e10…` after). The Training select on Hyrule Castle (frame 243)
  matches the port's `stageselectview` piece for piece: the icons, red
  cursor, stone, "Stage Select" bar, plaque with the Triforce, name plate
  and the blue Training wallpaper under a castle model that spills out of
  the preview box, as it does in the port. On Yoshi's Island (frame 60)
  neither shows the small cloud face that the port draws when the hide is
  removed. VS on Dream Land and on Sector Z (frames 60 to 487) show the
  zoomed Dream Land sky and the Sector Z nebula's flame behind the stage,
  as the port's `vs` and `vssector` do.
- **Negative test.** A build that skips `draw_wallpaper` and the preview's
  hidden nodes changes 49 of the 54 `psp-game` goldens: every battle
  scene, and `stageselectyoshi` by 320 pixels (the cloud face at 2×
  (514, 260)); the menus and `stageselectview` (nothing hidden there) are
  unchanged.

## Deviations

- **Viewport mapping.** The port draws the battle's 3D over the whole
  pillarbox: the stage camera's 38° span the pillarbox's height, so its
  (10, 10) to (310, 230) viewport is scaled by 272 / 220 and its sides
  cropped by about 3 N64 pixels. The wallpaper is mapped by that same
  transform, not by the 272 / 240 N64-screen mapping every other `SObj`
  uses, so it stays registered with the stage and fills the frame as it
  fills the viewport. There is no black 10-pixel border in battle, as
  before.
- **Format and memory.** The twelve wallpapers are 5551 (exact for
  RGBA16), padded to 512 × 256: 262,144 bytes each, 3 MiB in all, where
  the image needs 132,000. A tight 304-texel stride would save about
  1.5 MiB; that is left to `P5`. The GE samples them from main RAM like
  every other packed texture, so they take no VRAM; under a future VRAM
  texture pool one would cost 256 KiB.
- **Filtering.** The static kind's `SP_FASTCOPY` copy mode is drawn with
  the same bilinear rectangle as every other sprite; at the PSP's 1.24
  scale no point-sampled copy exists anyway.
- **Race to the Finish** has no VS stage in the port; its black fill is
  decided but not drawn.
- **Preview model.** Its root scale is applied as a uniform base scale,
  which also scales the root's own rest translation (at most 3.7 units,
  Yoshi's Island). The model is lit by the stage's own light angle, not
  `dMNMapsLights1`. Its material clocks are a separate animator started
  with the preview.
- **Preview timing.** A select opened from another screen draws once
  before its first tick, so the camera's bob starts one frame later than
  the source's.
- **Not compared.** The Yoshi's Island preview's centre texture (the N64
  shows fruit at frame 60, the port the smiling heart) and its platforms'
  colour (striped on the N64, grey in `psp-game` in battle too) differ in
  the stage draw itself, not in this batch (TODO).

## Verification

- **Host tests.** Seven in `wallpaper` (the decision, Training's pick and
  fog, the made `SObj`s, the common kind's centring, zoom, pan clamps and
  linear pan, the sector's scaling) and eight in `stage_select_layer`
  (icons and the lock, cursor, name and emblem by slot, the random slot,
  the preview wallpaper's source, the plate's 21 pieces, the draw order,
  the bob about the first stage's height, the hidden nodes).
- **Goldens.** 46 rebaselined, all identical in both captures: every
  Training and VS battle scene (from `f1-vs-pause`, 63,204 pixels at 2×,
  to `f1-training-rebirthblast`, 273,888), for the wallpaper; and
  `f1-training-stage-select` (126,352) and `-fighter-select` (258,224),
  which go through the select into Training. Four added
  (`f1-training-stage-select-view`, `-stage-select-yoshi`, `f1-vs-sector`,
  `f1-vs-yoshi`), identical twice. The four menu scenes and all 69 viewer
  goldens are unchanged. 123 of 123 match twice.
- **Gates.** Workspace tests with an absolute `SSB64_ROM` and one thread
  (1,527 passed), pinned 1.98.0 Clippy with warnings denied, rustfmt, the
  `no_std` checks and both PSP release builds pass. The release `run` is
  20,288 bytes (20,740 before) with its 18,176-byte frame; the largest
  frame is still `enter_training` at 159,456 bytes. `stage_screen::start`
  builds the boxed preview through a 43,816-byte frame once, when the
  session is made.

## Conclusion

Every Training and VS battle now draws its wallpaper behind the stage:
Training's black, yellow or blue, Yoshi's Island's fixed, Sector Z's
scaled about its centre and the others panned and zoomed with the camera,
from the ROM's own sprites. The stage select draws `mnMaps`' full
presentation, with its animated, bobbing stage preview, and matches the
original's layout.
