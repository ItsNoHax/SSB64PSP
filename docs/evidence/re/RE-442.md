## Port

- `MaterialJoint::clear_script` and `StageJoint::clear_script` model the
  NULL `event32`; `EffectMaterialAnimator` and `StageAnimator` gain
  `clear_scripts`.
- `Item::root_script` (`RootScript { added, cleared }`) records, on the
  `anim_ticks` clock, when a status added and cleared the root script; the
  add's own play counts. It replaces `ItemVars::shell_spin_anim`.
- Pack v90: `romtool` binds the Star's and Ray Gun's tables and the two root
  scripts to their trees' primitives, packs the left walk list (key 0x34C0)
  on the walk's entry, the Green Shell list with the spin frames converted
  under palette 1 (its `MatAnimRef` keyed by that palette, 0x5578), the
  Shells' spin animation (`AnimDesc::ITEM_ANIM_SHELL_SPIN`), and the Bumper's
  lit list (0x7238), attached list (0x7A38) and attached lit list (0x76D8).
- `psp-game` plays the Star's and every held utility's table on the item's
  visual clock (the Ray Gun's no longer replays from tick 0 each draw), the
  root script from its add, clearing it after the recorded play, and draws
  the Bob-omb's list side, the Shells' `rotate.y`, and the Bumper's and
  Castle Bumper's palette and attached lists.

## N64 references

Temporary decomp builds booted Training on Dream Land with two Marios and
made the items at Mario + offset after his `ftManagerMakeFighter`; each patch
was reverted and the decomp rebuilt to SHA-1 `e2929e10…`. Mupen64Plus
screenshots are 960 x 999 (y stretched by about 1.39). Route: A at 60 and
100. Training ticks run about 12 frames ahead of the harness frame count: the
Bob-omb explodes at frame ~246 and port tick ~258.

- Slide: a Green Shell at (75, 300) and a Bob-omb at (750, 500). Mario
  throws the shell left; it slides off past the bush cycling its sprite
  frames, as `trainingslide@130–138` does. The Bob-omb rests, turns to its
  left list with walk frames and smoke and explodes on Mario; port
  `@233/245` match N64 frames 221/233 in list, frames and smoke.
- Bumper: one Bumper at (75, 300), thrown left. It lands flat on the left
  of the floor with its orange top and blue band in both.

The Bumper's lit palette, the Castle Bumper's and the Star's flicker were
not observed on the N64; the ROM test replays the packed Star script and the
Green Shell's sprite frames.
