# RE-370 — The Training grab route faced away from the dummy

Status: COMPLETE (decomp source, PPSSPPHeadless software capture and goldens, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: grab, animation, gameplay, visual-regression
Relevant files: `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `tests/golden/f1-training-grab.png`, `crates/ssb-game/src/grab.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/hurtbox.rs`

## Question

Why does the scripted Training grab whiff in PPSSPP when `romtool jumptest`
reports a catch on the same route (RE-351)?

## Evidence

The decomp's `dMarioMainMotion_Catch` waits six frames, creates a size-290
catch box on joint 28 for one frame, then clears it. `ftMainSearchFighterCatch`
tests that box against grabbable damage boxes. The portable search has the
same frame window. `romtool jumptest` uses the unposed root-sphere fallback;
the PSP runtime samples the joint transforms, so the host catch did not
establish that the posed hand touched the dummy.

In PPSSPP at global tick 114 (`Catch` frame 6), Mario stood at x=-1253.1,
facing right, while the dummy stood at x=-1397.0. His posed joint-28 catch
center was x=-1088.1, away from the dummy, and `catch_touches` was false.
The posed center touched the dummy on frames 4 and 5, before the catch box
became active. The route's comment had claimed Mario was facing the dummy,
but the preceding -30 stick carry never turned him left.

## Change and verification

The grab scene now sends a one-frame left stick tap at tick 98. It enters
the ordinary turn status and completes before Z+A at tick 108. At global
tick 114 Mario faces left, stands at the same x=-1253.1, and the posed
joint-28 catch center is x=-1418.1; `catch_touches` is true. The final
PPSSPP capture at tick 118 reports `CatchWait`, `player_catch=Some(1)`,
`CaptureWait`, and `dummy_capture=Some(0)`.

The `f1-training-grab` golden changed by 305,044 pixels at 2x. The
completed grab puts the two fighters together and reframes the camera,
accounting for the widespread stage shift. Two repeat captures were
byte-identical. The grab and jab goldens both match twice after the
refresh. Workspace tests and both PSP release builds pass; the viewer has
its existing five warnings. A final PPSSPPHeadless grab smoke reaches the
linked capture states. This establishes the software scene, not a
physical-PSP result.
