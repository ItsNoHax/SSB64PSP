# RE-409 — The VS results screen's fighters

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, US build disassembly, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: menus, match, animation, fighter, camera, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/{results,results_scene,results_scene_tests}.rs`, `psp-game/src/{main,results_screen}.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/gu.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`

## Question

The results screen showed only plain slots (RE-400). Pack v64 holds each
fighter's demo clips (RE-408). Where does `mnVSResults` put each fighter,
how big is it, which way does it face, which clip does it play, when does
it appear, and what camera does it use?

## Source

All in `mn/mnvsmode/mnvsresults.c` unless noted.

- **Timing.** `mnVSResultsInitVars` sets `sMNVSResultsInitFightersAllTic`
  (and the wallpaper and results-text tics) to 120, or to 1 after a no
  contest. `mnVSResultsFuncRun` counts the tic first, then calls
  `mnVSResultsInitFightersAll` on that tic. From the next tic,
  `sMNVSResultsCharacterAlpha` rises by 0x16 a tic to 0xFF. It is the
  fighters' fade-in (`scSubsysFighterDrawLightColorGetAlpha`).
- **Making.** `mnVSResultsInitFightersAll` goes through the present
  players in port order. `mnVSResultsInitFighter` makes the fighter with
  the player's kind and costume (`mnVSResultsMakeFighter`), then places
  it, scales it, makes its player tag and sets its status. After a
  contest, `mnVSResultsFaceWinner` then turns every fighter not in first
  place to the winner: `rotate.y = syUtilsArcTan2(xw - x, zw - z)`. The
  default `FTDesc` has `pkind` Demo, so `ftMainSetStatus` never sets TopN's
  facing and it stays 0, toward the camera.
- **Spot.** `mnVSResultsGetSpot` is the row back from the camera: the place,
  plus `aheads[place - ahead]` (`{0, 0, 1, 1}`), plus
  `places[count in that place]` (`{0, 0, 1, 1, 1}`). A shared place sits
  one row further back.
  - With a tie above the player (places 0, 0, 1), `place - ahead` is
    negative, and the C reads before `aheads`. The US object's
    disassembly puts `places` at `sp+44` and `aheads` at `sp+64`, so
    `aheads[-1..-3]` are `places[4..2]`, which are all 1. The port counts
    a negative index as 1.
- **Position.** `mnVSResultsSetFighterPosition` indexes `pos_x_2p`, `_3p`
  or `_4p` by `mnVSResultsGetPlayerDistanceID` and spot. Y and Z come from
  `pos_yz` by spot: (-350, 0), (-450, -2000), (-700, -5000),
  (-900, -9000). The distance ID is the player's order among the present
  players. When one player is alone in first place, that player moves
  inward: the middle of three, or the inner column of its pair of four
  (`mnVSResultsGetPlayerDistanceID`).
- **Scale.** `dSCSubsysFighterScales` by kind, from 1.00 (Donkey Kong) to
  1.33 (Link).
- **Status.** `mnVSResultsSetFighterStatus` gives Lose to everyone after a
  no contest. Otherwise first place gets `mnVSResultsGetStatusWin` and the
  rest get Lose. `mnVSResultsGetStatusWin` picks Win1 to Win3 with
  `syUtilsRandIntRange(3)`, or Win1 and Win2 with `(2)` for Kirby. With
  two players, the switch covers only places 0 and 1. The status goes
  through `ftMainSetStatus(..., 0, 1.0, 0.0)`, which plays the clip's
  first frame at once. None of these demo statuses has a `proc_update`.
- **Random order.** `mnVSResultsFuncStart` first picks the wipe,
  `syUtilsRandIntRange(11)` (`dLBTransitionDescs`), except after a no
  contest. On tic 120, `mnVSResultsMakeConfetti` runs before the fighters,
  then each first-place player draws its Win pick in port order.
- **Camera.** `mnVSResultsMakeFighterCamera` sets the viewport to
  (10, 10) to (310, 230), the eye to (0, 0, 1800), the target to the
  origin and up to +Y. Its projection is `dGCPerspDefault`: 30 degrees,
  aspect 4:3, near 100, far 12800. The default camera behind it clears
  to black. `mnVSResultsFuncLights` lights the fighters from angles
  (10, 10).
- **Translation scales.** `ftMainSetStatus` clears `is_have_translate_scale`
  when a row sets `FTANIM_FLAG_TRANSLATE_SCALES`. Luigi's own demo rows
  set it. His Lose row, Mario's claps, does not, so it plays through his
  scale vectors (`ftParamUpdateAnimKeys`, RE-368).
- **Lead joint.** With `FTANIM_FLAG_TRANSN_JOINT`, `ftMainSetStatus`
  detaches `TransN` from the hierarchy, so its track does not move the
  model. No demo status applies it as velocity.

## Implementation

- **`ssb_game::results`.**
  - `Results` gains the three tics and `character_alpha`.
  - `tick` counts the tic and fades the fighters in.
  - `fighters_due` says whether this tic makes the fighters.
- **`ssb_game::results_scene`.**
  - `Scene::start` draws the wipe. The wipe is picked but not drawn.
  - `Scene::init_fighters_all` ports `mnVSResultsInitFightersAll` with the
    spot, distance, position, scale, status and facing helpers.
  - `CAMERA`, `LIGHT_ANGLE` and `FIGHTER_SCALES` hold the source's numbers.
  - `DemoStatus::skips_translate_scales` handles Luigi's rows.
  - The RNG is `ssb_game::rng`, the shared global seed.
- **`psp-game/src/results_screen.rs`.**
  - Each fighter is a boxed `Model`: the placed fighter, its object and a
    `ssb_rom::skeleton::Skeleton`.
  - On the fighters' tic, the skeleton starts `anim::SLOT_WIN1 + status`
    at frame 0 and plays one frame. Each later tic plays one more frame.
    A Win clip holds its last frame and the claps loop, as the clips do.
  - The skeleton is composed as Training does it, and the runtime lead
    joint stays unbound. Each model is drawn with
    `meshdraw::draw_object_posed` in the player's costume, at its TopN
    position, yaw and `MODEL_SCALE * scale`.
  - `Gpu::set_viewport_n64` maps the camera's N64 viewport into the
    pillarboxed area.
- **`psp-game/src/main.rs`.**
  - `make_results` builds the rankings and `results_screen::start`.
  - `results_frame` ticks them and the fighters, taking kind and costume
    from the battle's `roster`.
  - The screen now clears to black. The plain ranking slots moved to a
    strip along the top, drawn before the fighters.
- **Memory.** `Session` holds `Option<Box<results_screen::Fighters>>`.
  Each fighter's `Model` (with its skeleton) is its own box, made in the
  `#[inline(never)]` `make_model`. Nothing large is on `run`'s stack.
  `run` is 20,800 bytes in release (20,628 before).
- **Capture scene `vsresults`.**
  - A one-stock battle on Dream Land: the player's Luigi against a Kirby
    CPU set to Stand.
  - Held left from "Go" (tick 398 to 520), Luigi runs off the stage and
    the battle ends.
  - At tick 760 the results are up but have no fighters yet. The capture
    at tick 1100 is therefore past the end of Kirby's 161-frame Win clip,
    which ends by tick 1041.

## Deviations

- **Not drawn.** The wallpaper (`mnVSResultsMakeWallpaper` and its
  tints), the confetti, the wipe, the player tags and all results text
  and numbers are not drawn. The rankings stay plain slots.
- **Fade-in.** `character_alpha` is tracked, but the fighters draw opaque
  from their first tic. The source fades them in over 12 tics.
- **Random draws.** The confetti's particle scripts are not ported, so
  their random draws (at tic 120, before the Win picks, and on later
  tics) are not made. Any other scene's unported draws are also missing.
- **Motion scripts.** The demo rows' motion scripts (voices and other
  events) do not run.
- **Same-tic play.** The fighter plays its first frame in the status
  set, as `ftMainSetStatus` does. Whether the fighter's own process also
  plays a frame on its creation tic was not settled. The port does not.

## Verification

- **Host tests** (`results_scene_tests.rs`):
  - no-contest placement, with no random draws;
  - the two-player winner and the loser's turn toward it;
  - the draw order (wipe, then each first place in port order);
  - Kirby's two-way pick;
  - the negative-`aheads` spot, the three-player middle column and the
    four-player inward swap;
  - that only present players with fighters are made;
  - Luigi's translation-scale flag;
  - the tic-120 timing and the 0x16 fade.

  With the negative-index case read as 0, the three-player test fails.
- **Goldens.**
  - `f1-vs-no-contest` was rebaselined (522,232 pixels at 2×, identical
    in both captures). It now shows the red Mario and the yellow Mario
    dummy one row back, both clapping and facing the camera, under two
    level grey slots.
  - The new `f1-vs-results` golden shows Kirby in front in his final Win
    pose, with one arm raised. Luigi, one row back, turns to Kirby and
    claps: Mario's clap played through Luigi's scales. The slots are
    Luigi's grey and Kirby's lit gold.
  - 110 of 110 goldens match twice. The other 108 are unchanged.
- **Gates.** Workspace tests, pinned 1.98.0 Clippy with warnings denied,
  rustfmt and both PSP release builds pass.

## Conclusion

The results screen now poses and places its fighters as
`mnVSResultsInitFightersAll` does. The next batch is the results'
text, numbers and wallpaper sprites.
