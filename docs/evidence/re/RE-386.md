# RE-386 — The Training character select, and the spawn facing it exposed

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless golden captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: menu, fighter, gameplay, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/fighter_select.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/fighter_select_tests.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `psp-game/src/play.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`, `tests/golden/f1-training-fighter-select.png`

## Question

How does the original pick the Training fighters? The host spawned Mario
against a Mario dummy, with a C-button costume pick on the menu entry.

## Source

`mn/mnplayers/mnplayers1ptraining.c`:

- **Process order.** Each frame, `mnPlayers1PTrainingFuncRun` runs first
  as the scene GObj's `func_run`. Then the process queue runs by priority:
  - the cursor (priority 2);
  - the player's puck, then the CPU's (priority 1, link 20);
  - the puck adjust (link 26) and the costume sync (link 31).
  A ready START pauses the cursor and both pucks
  (`mnPlayers1PTrainingPauseSlotProcesses`).
- **Slots.** `gSCManagerSceneData.player` is 0 outside a 1P game, so the
  player is slot 0 and the CPU slot 1.
- **First visit.** With no saved player fighter, the cursor starts at
  (70, 170) holding its own puck (`mnPlayers1PTrainingResetPlayer`).
- **CPU pick.** With no saved CPU fighter, it is drawn by
  `syUtilsRandTimeUCharRange(12)` until the draw is unlocked. It takes
  costume 0, or costume 1 when the player wears costume 0.
- **Locks.** `mnPlayers1PTrainingCheckFighterLocked` locks Luigi, Captain
  Falcon, Ness and Jigglypuff without their `fighter_mask` bit, which
  `dSCManagerDefaultBackupData` leaves at 0.
- **Cursor.** The stick moves the cursor by a twentieth of its deflection
  outside ±8, inside (0–280, 10–205). The cursor points outside the
  portrait band (y 38–124). In the band, it grabs while holding and
  hovers otherwise.
- **Placing.** A (or a C-button, in that costume) places the held puck on
  the portrait under its centre (`mnPlayers1PTrainingGetPuckFighterKind`).
  The portraits are two rows of six, 45×43 pixels from (25, 36). A locked
  portrait names no fighter. Placing blocks the next grab for 30 ticks.
- **Grabbing.** A on a free puck grabs it, the CPU's before the player's.
  The cursor glides to the pickup point a fifth of the gap per tick.
- **Held puck.** A held puck names the portrait under it each tick, and
  the fighter is remade in its first free costume. A held CPU puck that
  leaves the portraits is placed at once, keeping its fighter.
- **Recall and back.** B on a placed player puck recalls it: ten ticks of
  flight, the cursor's grab on the eleventh, and the recall ends on the
  thirtieth. Otherwise B leaves for the 1P menu from tick 10. So does A on
  the back button.
- **Costumes.** C-buttons re-pick a placed player fighter's costume,
  refusing the CPU's. A fighter only one slot shows, and not yet placed,
  returns to costume 0 (`mnPlayers1PTrainingCostumeSyncProcUpdate`).
- **Placed pucks.** They are pushed back inside their portrait. Two pucks
  on one fighter within 15 pixels push apart.
- **Proceeding.** START after tick 60, with both fighters placed, waits
  30 ticks and loads the stage select. The screen saves the fighters and
  costumes as `training_man_*`/`training_com_*` on every exit. Five idle
  minutes return to the title.

## Implementation

- `ssb_game::fighter_select` ports the logic above: the cursor, the pucks,
  the grab, placing, recall, costumes, ready, the start wait, the idle
  return and the scene data. Thirteen host tests cover it.
- `psp-game` has these screens:
  - The Training entry opens the character select with the saved fighters.
  - Proceeding opens the stage select.
  - The stage select's B returns to the character select.
  - Confirming a stage spawns the player's fighter, and the CPU's as the
    dummy (`play::Dummy::at_spawn` now takes a kind).
  - The random CPU draw reads the clock's low byte, or the frame counter
    under capture.
- The menu's C-button costume pick is gone.
- The select screens' draws are `#[inline(never)]`: with them inline, `run`
  reached 126 KB, 2 KB short of MIPS's branch reach. It is 114 KB now.
- The select draws the portrait grid (locked portraits dimmed, placed
  fighters lit), the pucks and the cursor. It draws no portraits, models,
  names or ready banner.

## Findings

- **Spawn facing.** `sc1PTrainingModeFuncStart` (and the VS start) sets
  each fighter's `lr` from its spawn: left when `pos.x >= 0`, else right.
  `FighterScene::at_spawn` always faces right. On Dream Land the player
  spawns at x = 0, so the original player faces left toward the dummy on
  the left platform. On Peach's Castle the dummy (x > 0) faces right in
  `f1-training-fighter-select`, and should face left. Fixing this changes
  nearly every Training golden and the scripted routes, so it is the next
  batch (TODO).

## Deviations

- The host has no save data, so the four unlockable fighters stay locked.
- The host's menu stands in for the 1P mode menu, so the select's B
  returns to it.
- Every capture scene except `fighterselect` skips the character select
  with preset fighters, and all but `stageselect` also skip the stage
  select. The costume scenes' picks are now preset in
  `capture_training_scene`. They were a C-button tap on the menu entry,
  and their goldens are unchanged.

## Verification

- **Capture scene.** `fighterselect` opens the select at tick 8 and holds
  the stick up-right to carry the puck onto Kirby. It places the puck at
  tick 32, presses START at tick 72 and confirms Peach's Castle at tick
  115.
- **Log.** At tick 160, `PPSSPPHeadless --log` reports both fighters in
  `Wait`.
- **Golden.** `f1-training-fighter-select` shows Kirby and the Mario dummy
  on Peach's Castle, and was identical in both captures.
- **Select screen.** A temporary tick-40 freeze showed the grid with the
  four locked portraits dimmed and Kirby and Mario lit under their pucks.
- **Other goldens.** All 94 existing goldens matched twice.
- **Tests.** Workspace tests pass with an absolute `SSB64_ROM` and one
  test thread.
