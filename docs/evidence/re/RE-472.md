## Question

RE-468 left How to Play matching the N64 through frame 3273 (Luigi) and
4039 (Mario). At 3274 Mario's grab catches Luigi a frame late in the port,
and at the throw's release (3348) the port drops Luigi from the hand, 295
units high. What does the source do, and what diverges after it?

## Method

- N64: the unmodified ROM (SHA-1 `e2929e10…`) under the M64Py flatpak's
  Mupen64Plus with a scratch config and `SDL_VIDEODRIVER=offscreen`, idling
  into How to Play as RE-466 does. New dumps: every fighter joint's `DObj`
  (`translate` at `+0x1C`, `rotate` at `+0x2C`, `scale` at `+0x40`,
  parent at `+0x14`) and its `FTParts` (`+0x84`: cache flags, local and
  world matrices) around the catch and the release, composed on the host
  into world positions; the weapon and item links (`gGCCommonLinks`
  `0x800466F0`, links 4 and 5); a word scan of Mario's `FTStruct` for the
  Fire Flower's counters (`status_vars` at `+0xB18`); and
  `dSCExplainRandomSeed1` (`0x8018E724`) on every frame to 4,448.
- PSP: `explain@4460` under PPSSPPHeadless with `--log`, compared with the
  N64 trace on status, x, y, damage and facing at 0.05 units.

## Findings

| Divergence | Source | Port |
|---|---|---|
| A fighter caught in the hit phase is placed at the hand on the catch frame: the capture plays `CapturePulled`'s first frame and runs its `ProcPhysics` and `ProcMap` at once, reading this frame's hand | `ftCommonCapturePulledProcCapture` | `FighterScene::settle_capture` after the hit phase's first exchange; a catch status samples the hand too (`is_catchstatus`) |
| The hand matrix `ftCommonCapturePulledRotateScale` places the held fighter with is built by `func_ovl0_800C9A38`: the joint's world origin with the scale divided out of its axes, so only the held fighter's `attr->size` scales its child offset (the port applied Mario's 1.12 again: 381.15 against 374.95, and CaptureWait 2.01 off) | `func_ovl0_800C9A38` | `grab::held_attachment` (unit axes) |
| A held fighter's TopN rotation is `func_ovl2_800EDA0C`'s Euler angles from the hand (`syUtilsArcTan2`/`syUtilsArcSin`), which the collision matrices rebuild on `lbCommonSin`; its joints and hurtboxes turn with it | `ftCommonCapturePulledRotateScale`, `ftCommonThrownProcPhysics` | `grab::held_collision_axes`, `item_throw::collision_axes` for the pulled, waiting, Yoshi and thrown statuses |
| The throw's release is a motion event in the catcher's `proc_update`: `ftCommonThrownProcPhysics` first moves the thrown TopN to the hand this frame's play posed, then `ftCommonThrownReleaseFighterLoseGrip` drops TopN to `joints[4]` less 300. ## Result

- How to Play matches the N64 on every frame of the scene, 1 to 4448 for
  both fighters (status, x, y, damage, facing at 0.05 units), against 3273
  and 4039 before. Frame 4449 is the scene's exit.
- `dSCExplainRandomSeed1` matches the N64 on 3,987 of 4,448 frames. The
  differences are effect draws (next divergence, below); no gameplay
  outcome in the scene turned on them.

**Next divergence: effect draws of the generator.** Gameplay matches to
the end, but the generator does not:
- Frames 3250–3253: Mario's shield grab (`GuardOn` → `Catch`). The N64
  draws 12 times at 3250 and 6, 6, 6 after; the port 3, 9, 6, 6, 6 at
  3250–3254. The totals agree from 3254.
- Frame 3992: Mario's `LightThrowF4` item (the Fire Flower) meets Luigi's
  shield (`Guard` → `GuardSetOff`); the N64 draws twice, the port not at
  all. From here on the state differs.
- Frames 4207–4220: after Mario's up smash sends Luigi into
  `DamageFlyTop`, the port draws 4 times at 4207, 4208, 4212, 4216 and
  4220 where the N64 draws 3 at 4207 and none at the others.

## Deviations and remaining differences

- The generator's effect draws (above).
- `anim_frame` of 13 statuses differs from the N64's: a figatree's end sets
  the N64's `anim_frame` to 0 (looping or not: `Wait`, `Fall`,
  `DamageFall`, `DamageFlyN/Hi/Top/Roll`, `Guard`, `GuardSetOff`,
  `CatchWait`, `CaptureWait`, `DeadUpStar`, `RebirthDown`); the port's
  status clock counts on. Positions, statuses and the drawn clip agree in
  How to Play.
- `func_ovl0_800C9A38` divides each parent's row scale out of the child's
  local matrix; the port normalises the composed axes, which agrees while
  the hand's chain is uniformly scaled (every heavy-item joint here).
- An item shot's forwarded accessory reads the previous frame's joints
  (the N64 the current pose); no flame leaves on that call in How to Play.
- The setter first-frame audit (RE-468) is unchanged: How to Play reaches
  none of the listed setters.
