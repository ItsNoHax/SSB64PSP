# RE-473 — How to Play's generator matched to the N64, the effect makers of item hits, Fox's Blaster spawn and the special setters' first frame

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, N64 traces of the unmodified US ROM's code with runtime call hooks, N64 Training traces of a data-only warp copy, host and ROM tests, PPSSPPHeadless trace of all of How to Play and of the opening's Fox scene; physical PSP pending)
Topics: rng, effect, particle, item, weapon, fighter, status, animation, gameplay
Relevant files: `refs/ssb-decomp-re/src/ft/ftmain.c`, `refs/ssb-decomp-re/src/it/{itprocess,itmanager}.c`, `refs/ssb-decomp-re/src/ef/efmanager.c`, `refs/ssb-decomp-re/src/lb/lbparticle.c`, `refs/ssb-decomp-re/src/gm/gmcollision.c`, `refs/ssb-decomp-re/src/ft/ftchar/{ftfox,ftlink,ftsamus,ftyoshi,ftpurin,ftkirby}/*.c`, `crates/ssb-game/src/{rng,particle,wpeffect,combat,weapon,status,link,samus,yoshi,purin,kirby,fighter,setter_tests}.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/item/{hit,mod,tests}.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/tests/particle_runtime.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`, `psp-game/src/{main,demo_screen}.rs`

## Question

RE-472 matched How to Play's gameplay to the N64 on every frame, but
`dSCExplainRandomSeed1` differed on 461 frames: the draw counts disagreed
at frames 3250–3254 (Mario's shield grab), 3992 (the thrown Fire Flower
meets Luigi's shield; the state differs from there) and 4207–4220
(`DamageFlyTop`). Which effect makers draw on the N64, and which does the
port miss or run on another frame? The same batch takes Fox's Blaster spawn
point (RE-472's early-sampled anchor) and the setter first-frame audit
(RE-468).

## Method

- **N64 draw callers.** The unmodified ROM (SHA-1 `e2929e10…`) under the
  M64Py Mupen64Plus with a scratch config, `SDL_VIDEODRIVER=offscreen` and
  `R4300Emulator = 0` (the pure interpreter fetches every instruction from
  RDRAM). At frame 60 the harness writes a `j` to a stub over the first two
  instructions of `syUtilsRandUShort`, `syUtilsRandFloat` and
  `syUtilsRandIntRange` (`0x80018910`, `0x80018948`, `0x80018994`, from
  `build/smashbrothers.us.map`); the stub, at `0x80700100` in the unused
  upper 4 MiB, stores `$ra` with a tag (and `a0`, `a1`) in a ring at
  `0x80701000`, runs the two displaced instructions and jumps back. Once
  How to Play's overlays are loaded the same hook goes on
  `lbParticleMakeGenerator`, `lbParticleMakeChildScriptID` and
  `efManagerMakeEffect`. Each frame's entries are read and symbolised
  against the map (`$ra - 8`). The seed after each frame equals RE-472's
  plain trace, so the hook changes nothing the game draws.
- **Port draw callers.** A `rng_trace` feature (`ssb_game::rng::trace`)
  records each draw's `#[track_caller]` site and each generator's making;
  `psp-game` prints them per frame in the `explain` capture.
- **Fox's Blaster.** The opening's Fox scene (scene 33) on the unmodified
  ROM: the weapon link (`gGCCommonLinks` link 5) and Fox's joints per
  frame; the port's `op-fox` capture prints the spawn.
- **Setters.** A copy of the ROM whose only change is
  `dSCManagerDefaultSceneData` (ROM `0x42CD0`: current and previous scene
  54, `nSCKind1PTrainingMode`; Training fighter at `+0x3B`; the CPU Mario;
  the three stage fields Peach's Castle) with the header CRC recomputed
  (CIC-6103), so the game boots into Training. Scripted pad input (B,
  up+B, down+B on the ground, and after a C-up jump) for Link, Samus, Fox,
  Yoshi, Jigglypuff and Kirby; each frame logs the status, `anim_frame`
  and the weapon and item links. A setter that plays its first frame shows
  `anim_frame` 1 on the frame the status starts; a bare `ftMainSetStatus`
  shows 0.

## Findings

| Frame | N64 draws (callers) | Port | Cause and fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3249–3254 | 3249: `itManagerMakeItemSetupCommon` makes the Fire Flower's spawn swirl (`lbParticleMakeChildScriptID`), whose first update makes generators `0x68`, `0x67`, `0x66`; 3250: their first run, 9 draws, then Mario's dust (3); 3251–3253: 6 each | the swirl was queued with the item's effects and made in the next frame's battle pass, so the generators ran a frame late (3, 9, 6, 6, 6) | `scExplainTryMakeFireFlower`'s item flushes its effects at once (`demo_screen::after_world`) |
| 3992 | `efManagerSetOffMakeEffect` (2) | none | `ftMainUpdateShieldStatItem` ends with the set-off at `gmCollisionGetItemAttackShieldPosition`, sized by shield damage plus damage; the port's item shield stat had no effect (`item::hit::update_shield_stat`, `combat::shield_impact`) |
| 4207–4220 | `ftMainParseMotionEvent`, `efManagerDamageSpawnOrbsRandomMakeEffect` and `...SparksRandomMakeEffect`, each one roll | 4 at 4207 (the orbs' roll made orbs), then orbs every 4 frames | the generator's state, different since 3992; matches once 3992 does |

The audit of the makers the item hit paths reach, against `ftmain.c` and
`itprocess.c`, found the port's item searches made no effects at all:

| Source | Port before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `ftMainUpdateShieldStatItem`: set-off | none | `f.hits.push_set_off` |
| `ftMainUpdateAttackStatItem` (a fighter's attack meets an item's): each side's set-off | none | both, at `gmCollisionGetItemAttackFighterAttackPosition` |
| `itProcessUpdateAttackStatItem` (two items' attacks): each side's set-off | none | both, in the item's search queue |
| `itProcessUpdateDamageStatFighter`: the element's spark (fire, electric, coin, slash with `gmCollisionGetDamageSlashRotation`, else normal light) | none | at `gmCollisionGetFighterAttackItemDamagePosition` |
| `itProcessUpdateDamageStatItem`, `...Weapon`: the spark when the attacker `is_hitlag_victim` | none | at `gmCollisionGet{Item,Weapon}AttackItemDamagePosition` |

The item searches' effects queue with the item (`WeaponEffect::Hit`,
`WeaponEffect::SetOff`) and are made after the fighters' and before the
weapon clashes' (`finish_frame_between`), the item link's place among the
priority-1 search processes.

**Fox's Blaster.** `ftFoxSpecialNProcUpdate` takes joint 17 plus 60 along
the joint's x axis (`gmCollisionGetFighterPartsWorldPosition`) after this
frame's `ftMainPlayAnimEventsAll` and before `ftMainProcPhysicsMap`. The
N64's opening shot (frame 1915, Fox at (437.81, 1744.13), `anim_frame` 25)
stands at (186.67, 2058.01, −25.81) after its first −160 step, so it was
made at (346.67, 2058.01, −25.81). The port's interrupt-time anchor
(`Skeleton::joint_node`, the offset along the root's axis) put it at
(310.58, 1755.78, 42.95). The runtime now samples the joints once the
frame's play has posed them, while the fighter still stands where the last
frame left it, and `status::fox_blaster_spawn` takes `joint_transforms[17]`
plus 60: (346.67, 2058.01, −25.81). The anchor and `weapon_spawn_anchor`
are gone.

The statuses that start at `anim_frame` 0 on the N64 keep their bare
setter: Samus's Charge Shot loop and ends, Fox's Fire Fox travel and ends,
the Reflector's loop and end, Yoshi's Bomb loop and landing, Kirby's Stone
hold and Final Cutter landing from the fall, and Link's Spin Attack end
when the aerial Spin Attack lands (`ftLinkSpecialAirHiProcMap` calls
`ftMainSetStatus` itself). Every timer these statuses read is an
`anim_frame` threshold the motion script names, so the N64's earlier
events (the Boomerang, the Bombs, the takeoffs, the egg, Stone) follow
from the play alone; eleven unit tests that encoded the old timing now
assert the N64's.

## Result

- `dSCExplainRandomSeed1` matches the N64 on all 4,448 frames of How to
  Play (461 differed), and the draw count on every frame; the fighters still
  match on every frame (status, x, y, damage, facing at 0.05 units).
- The opening's Blaster leaves where the N64's does, to the hundredth.
- The setters of 44 statuses across six fighters now play their first
  frame; 42 against their own N64 trace, two (Link's `SpecialAirNReturn`,
  Yoshi's `SpecialAirNRelease`) through a setter shared with a traced
  ground status.

## Tests

- `item::tests::an_item_on_a_shield_makes_its_set_off` (fails without the
  set-off), `item::tests::a_fighters_attack_on_an_item_makes_its_spark`
  (fails without the spark).
- `particle_runtime::the_item_spawn_swirl_draws_nine_times_at_the_next_frame_start`
  (ROM): from the N64's frame-3249 seed, the swirl's making draws nothing
  and the next frame's generator run draws nine times, as the N64 does
  before Mario's dust at 3250 (fails at eight).
- `status::tests::the_blaster_leaves_joint_17_plus_60_along_its_axis`.
- `setter_tests::traced_setters_play_their_first_frame_and_last_the_n64s_frames`:
  17 setters' first `anim_frame` and the frames they hold.

## Verification

- PPSSPPHeadless (software), `explain@4460` with `rng_trace`: the
  generator after every one of the 4,448 frames equals the N64's, with the
  same number of draws each frame; both fighters match the N64 trace on
  every frame. The RE-472 build differs at 3250–3254 and from 3992 on; with
  only the set-off ported, at 3250–3254.
- `op-fox@90`: the Blaster's spawn (346.67, 2058.01, −25.81).
- Host: 2,164 workspace tests pass on one thread with `SSB64_ROM` (Rust
  1.98.0) pointing at the decomp's `baserom.us.z64` (SHA-1 `e2929e10…`;
  see the note below); clippy with warnings denied (also `ssb-game` with
  `rng_trace`) and rustfmt pass.
- Both production PSP applications build on nightly-2026-08-26 (no game
  warnings, five existing viewer warnings); the production game runs the
  opening at 61 FPS under PPSSPP software.
- Goldens: 4 of 198 changed, each traced by reverting fixes and
  rebaselined (captured twice, identical): `f1-training-kirby-hat` and
  `f1-vs-four` (Kirby's setters: restored with Kirby's and Yoshi's
  reverted), `f1-training-yoshi-egg` (Yoshi's), `f1-vs-four-magnifier`
  (frame 2800 of the same CPU battle: still changed with Kirby's, Fox's
  setters and the item effects reverted, restored once the Blaster's old
  anchor came back too). 198 of 198 then match.
- Performance: the joints are sampled once more only on a Blaster's
  frame, the item's swirl opens the particle banks only when How to Play
  makes the Fire Flower, and the item searches emit only on a contact; no
  table scans or `f64` were added.
- Note: a tool error in this batch rewrote the header CRC words
  (`0x10`–`0x17`) of `rom/Super Smash Bros. (USA).z64`; its SHA-1 is now
  `ba145cee…`. Every trace and test here used the unmodified
  `refs/ssb-decomp-re/baserom.us.z64`. The file needs restoring
  (`STATUS.md`).

## Deviations and remaining differences

- The setters without an N64 trace keep the old timing: Kirby's aerial
  Inhale end, his copies, the Final Cutter's landing from the rise, Donkey
  Kong's cargo throw and walk, the Barrel and Tornado captures and the
  item throws. `set_special_n_get` and Yoshi's release serve both
  situations; their aerial halves were not traced.
- The item searches' effects are made after every fighter's sparks; the
  N64 interleaves processes of one priority by link, which agrees while one
  item hits per frame.
- A figatree's end resetting `anim_frame` (RE-472) is unchanged: how the
  port's status clock would carry it through the events and the drawn clip
  needs its own batch.
