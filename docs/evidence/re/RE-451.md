## Presentation boundary

The portable frontend still owns intro gates, announcements, Continue
selection/retry/Game Over clocks, fades and stage-clear scoring/pages.
Only existing display fields become public for the adapter. Continue fades
advance on draw through its existing `draw`, as in the source.

The adapter draws the authored banners, stage markers, fighter/ally names,
bonus pictures, fighter cards, Continue room/spotlight/options/score,
Game Over letters and figures, and stage-clear timer/damage/objective and
bonus tables. Kirby team parts use the source eight part IDs and manager's
final copy part. Yoshi cards use low detail and their six costumes.
Special-model and unported gameplay/ending requests retain their blockers.

Pack v95 adds the sprites in files 11–14, 79–81 and 151, the small digit
cross/colon, the four demo slots (619–622; count 623), and a native camera
payload in reserved `AnimDesc::EFFECT` slot 0xF100. Fighter records grow with
the existing per-slot frame table. Previous packs must be rebuilt.
The generated animation table reproduces byte-for-byte.

## PSP deviations and limits

- The snapshot retains the PSP's native 8888 framebuffer rather than
  quantizing to the N64's 5551 framebuffer. It samples PSP pixel centers into
  300×220 texels and uses the existing GE bilinear wallpaper path. Source
  crop, destination and dimming are preserved; exact N64 pixels are unproven.
- Wallpaper vertices are copied into GU list memory before DMA. The existing
  viewer diagnostic retains its original rectangle; the campaign uses a
  separate active-picture mapping.
- Same-costume Yoshi shade, other fighter shade, 1P tags, scene audio/music,
  missing special fighters and later overlays remain in `PLAN.md`.
- Diagnostic fixtures seed a scene after the real select. Continue and
  stage-clear captures do not prove a real loss/win boundary. Their snapshot
  comes from the preceding intro frame. Bonus pictures do not establish
  playable bonus scenes; later-stage model cases lack PSP captures.
