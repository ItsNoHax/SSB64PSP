# RE-411 — The VS character select's sprites and fighters

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, `reloc_data.us.h`, ROM sprite decode, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless golden captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: menus, match, sprites, visual-regression, pack
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/{players_vs,players_vs_layer,players_vs_layer_tests}.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/{sprite,pack}.rs`, `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`, `tools/romtool/tests/vs_select_sprites.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/meshdraw.rs`, `psp-game/src/{main,players_screen}.rs`

## Question

RE-404 ported `mnPlayersVS`' logic and drew it as plain rectangles. Which
sprites does the select load, where does each piece go, in what colours
and order, which pieces move, blink or appear on their own tics, and how
do the fighters on the panels turn and animate?

## Source

All in `mn/mnplayers/mnplayersvs.c` unless noted. Sprite offsets are from
`include/reloc_data.us.h`.

- **Files.** `dMNPlayersVSFileIDs` loads `MNPlayersCommon` (file 17),
  `MNCommon` (0), `FTEmblemSprites` (20), `MNSelectCommon` (21),
  `MNPlayersGameModes` (18), `MNPlayersPortraits` (19) and
  `MNPlayersSpotlight` (22).
- **Cameras.** Every camera has the viewport (10, 10) to (310, 230), and a
  higher priority draws first: the default camera's black clear (100); the
  wallpaper camera (80, link 26), made by `mnPlayersVSMakeWallpaper`; the
  portraits' fire backgrounds (75, link 36); the flashes (73, link 37); the
  portraits (70, link 27); the cards, "1P" texts, names, emblems and
  "Press Start" (50, link 28); the team buttons (45, link 34); the levels
  (43, link 35); the doors (40, link 29); the HMN/CP/NA buttons (35,
  link 30); the fighters (30); the pucks (25, link 33); the cursors and held
  pucks (20, link 32); and the ready banner (10, link 38).
- **Top bar.**
  - `mnPlayersVSMakeWallpaper` wraps the 64 × 32 CI4 stone tile
    (`StoneBackgroundSprite`, 0x440) over 300 × 220 from (10, 10), with
    `masks` 6 and `maskt` 5. It keeps the ROM attr, so it is opaque.
  - `mnPlayersVSMakeLabels` puts "Free-for-all" or "Team battle" (file 18)
    at (27, 24) in (0xE3, 0xAC, 0x04) or (0x61, 0xAD, 0x49), the back
    button (0x115C8) at (244, 23), and the time or stock selector
    (0x48B0, 0x5270) at (140, 22).
  - `mnPlayersVSMakeTimeSetting` draws an infinite time as the infinity
    sign (0x3EF0) at (194, 24). Other times go through
    `mnPlayersVSMakeGameRuleNumber`, right-aligned on x 208 below 10 and
    212 from 10, at y 23. Each digit steps left by its sprite's width.
    `mnPlayersVSMakeStockSelect` shows the stocks plus one, on 210 or 214.
    The dark digits (0x5388 to 0x5CC8) have envcolor (0x32, 0x1C, 0x0E) and
    a white primitive.
- **Portraits.**
  - `mnPlayersVSMakePortrait` gives an unlocked fighter a fire background
    (0x24D0, link 36) and its portrait (link 27).
  - A locked one gets `mnPlayersVSMakePortraitShadow`: the fire
    background, the fighter's shadow and the question mark (0xF68), all
    on link 27. The shadow uses `mnPlayersVSPortraitProcDisplay`'s
    `(NOISE - TEXEL0) * 0x30 + TEXEL0`. The question mark has envcolor
    (0x5B, 0x41, 0x33) and primitive (0xC4, 0xB9, 0xA9).
  - `mnPlayersVSCheckFighterCrossed` is always false, so no cross is made.
  - Each piece starts at `mnPlayersVSSetPortraitWallpaperPosition`'s x
    (-35 for columns 0 to 2, 310 for 3 to 5). `mnPlayersVSGetNextPortraitX`
    then moves it by `portrait_vel` (1.9, 3.9, 7.8, -7.8, -3.8, -1.8 on the
    top row) until it reaches `portrait_pos_x`. The middle columns arrive
    on tic 20 and the outer ones on tics 32 and 34.
- **Portrait flash.** `mnPlayersVSMakePortraitFlash` puts the white square
  (0x6F0) at the portrait's corner plus (1, 1). Its thread flips the GObj
  hidden on every run and ejects it on the sixteenth. It is made when a puck
  is placed, a CPU is opened, or `mnPlayersVSUpdatePlayerKind` releases a
  held puck. `mnPlayersVSSetCursorGrab` destroys it.
- **Panels** (`mnPlayersVSMakeGate`, at x = player × 69):
  - The card is `RedCardSprite` (0x104B0, CI4, 66 × 91) at (x + 22, 126).
    `mnPlayersVSSetGateLUT` swaps its TLUT for `GateMan1PLUT` to `4PLUT` or
    `GateCom1PLUT` to `4PLUT`. The index is the port, or the team's
    `{0, 1, 3}` in a team battle. The file stores 3P's LUT after 4P's; the
    ROM decode confirms red, blue, yellow and green in the source's order.
    `mnPlayersVSUpdatePlayerKind` sets the LUT only for HMN and CP, so an
    NA slot keeps its last one.
  - `mnPlayersVSMakePlayerKind` draws "CP" (0xFF8) at x + 26, or "1P" to
    "4P" at x + 22 + `pos_x`, at y 131 in black. It is remade on each HMN or
    CP change, which moves it to the end of link 28.
  - The doors (0xCDB0, 0xDFA0) sit at x − 19 + `door_offset` and
    x + 88 − `door_offset`, y 126. Each is scissored by its
    `mnPlayersVSShutter1PProcDisplay` to `...4P` (22 to 88 plus 69 per
    slot, y 126 to 217). `mnPlayersVSShutterProcUpdate` moves
    `door_offset` by 2 a tic: towards 41 for NA and towards 0 otherwise.
    It starts at 41.
  - The HMN/CP/NA button (0x6048, 0x63C8, 0x6748) is at (x + 64, 131).
  - `mnPlayersVSMakeNameAndEmblem` draws the series emblem (file 20) at
    (x + 24, 143) in grey 0x1E for a human and 0x44 otherwise, and the name
    at (x + 22, 201). `mnPlayersVSUpdateNameAndEmblem` hides both for NA,
    or with no fighter and no placed puck. With no fighter it keeps the
    old sprites. `mnPlayersVSUpdateHandicapLevel` hides the name
    (`mnPlayersVSHideFighterName`).
  - In a team battle `mnPlayersVSMakeTeamSelectAll` gives every slot its
    team's label at (x + 34, 131).
  - `mnPlayersVSMakeHandicapLevel` draws "Handicap" at x + 35 or "CP Level"
    at x + 34, y 201, in (0xC2, 0xBD, 0xAD), with the colon at (x + 61,
    202). `mnPlayersVSMakeHandicapLevelValue` puts `MNCommon`'s digit at
    (x + 67, 200). Unless a human's handicap is auto, the arrows
    (0xECE8, 0xEDC8) sit at x + 25 and x + 79, y 201. Their thread flips
    them every 10 runs and drops the left one at 1 and the right one at 9.
    `mnPlayersVSHandicapLevelProcUpdate` destroys all three once the
    slot's fighter is not placed.
- **Pucks and cursors.**
  - A puck is "1P" to "4P" or "CP" (0x9048 to 0xBBC8) at its position.
    `mnPlayersVSPuckProcDisplay` draws `(TEXEL0 - PRIM) * ENV + PRIM` with
    a white primitive and a grey environment of
    `sMNPlayersVSPuckGlowColor`.
  - `mnPlayersVSUpdatePuckGlowColor` adds 9 up to 0xFF and then subtracts 9
    down to 0x80. It steps down in the same call that clamps to 0xFF. The
    colour is not initialised, so a first visit starts at 0.
  - `mnPlayersVSUpdateCursor` draws the hand for the cursor's status
    (0x6F88, 0x76E8, 0x8168). The port's gradient number (0x8268 to 0x8568)
    sits (7, 15), (9, 10) or (9, 15) from it, in each port's
    primitive and environment pair.
  - The pucks' and cursors' draw order is RE-404's `DrawKey`.
- **Ready.** `mnPlayersVSMakeReady` makes two GObjs, and both run
  `mnPlayersVSReadyProcUpdate`. So `sMNPlayersVSReadyBlinkWait` counts
  twice a tic, and each GObj shows while the count is below 30 of 40.
  - The banner wraps the 8 × 17 IA8 strip (0xF530) over 320 × 17 from
    (0, 71), inside the viewport, in (0xF4, 0x56, 0x7F) on black. "Ready
    to fight" (0xF448) is at (50, 76), (0xFF, 0xFF, 0x9D) on
    (0xFF, 0xCA, 0x13).
  - "Press" (0x14D8, US) is at (133, 219) and "Start" (0x1378) at (162,
    219). Both are on link 28.
- **Fighters.**
  - `mnPlayersVSMakeFighter` places each at x = player × 840 − 1250, y −850,
    at `dSCSubsysFighterScales`. It keeps the old model's `rotate.y` when it
    remakes one.
  - The fighter camera (`mnPlayersVSMakeFighterCamera`) looks from
    (0, 0, 5000) at the origin with the default perspective. The lights
    are `scSubsysFighterSetLightParams(45, 45, ...)`.
  - The default `FTDesc` is `nFTPlayerKindDemo`, which starts
    `nFTDemoStatusNull`. That is submotion row 0, each fighter's `Wait`
    figatree (named `EggLay` in the decomp).
  - `mnPlayersVSFighterProcUpdate` turns a fighter that is not placed by
    2° a tic. Once it is placed, the fighter turns 20° a tic until it
    passes 360°, then goes to 0 and plays `mnPlayersVSGetStatusSelected`'s
    status: Win4 for Fox and Samus, Win2 for Yoshi, Jigglypuff and Ness,
    Win3 for Mario and Kirby, and Win1 for the rest. A fighter already
    under 0.1° plays it at once.
  - `mnPlayersVSUpdateFighter` hides the fighter of an NA slot or of a
    slot with nothing under its puck.

## Implementation

- **`ssb_rom::sprite`.**
  - `PLAYERS_COMMON` (59 sprites), `MN_COMMON` (11), `PORTRAITS` (19),
    `EMBLEM_SPRITES` (10) and `SELECT_COMMON` (1) join `FILES`.
  - `decode_gate` decodes the card through `GATE_LUTS[lut]`. `romtool pack`
    stores the card eight times with `SpriteDesc::ROLE_LUT`, whose
    `costume` names the LUT. `Pack::sprite_lut` finds them, and
    `Pack::sprite` skips them.
  - As in RE-410, only rows are added to the sprite table, so `VERSION`
    stays 64. The pack has 335 sprites. It is now 30,405,424 bytes
    (1,277,632 more), SHA-256
    `720bbc897adb249921a50ce9e2382d1ea75ecfcc1881308ed39c198e55e82e67`.
- **`ssb_game::players_vs::layer`** (`players_vs_layer.rs`).
  - `View` holds each slot's `door_offset`, gate LUT, "1P"/"CP" text, name
    and emblem, level, arrows, value, flash, fighter and puck flag. It also
    holds the portraits' x, the glow and the ready counter.
  - The logic calls the `v_*` hooks at the source's call sites:
    `mnPlayersVSMakeGate`, `SetGateLUT`, `MakePlayerKind`,
    `UpdateNameAndEmblem`, `UpdateHandicapLevel`,
    `MakeHandicapLevelValue`, `DestroyHandicapLevel`,
    `MakePortraitFlash`, `DestroyPortraitFlash` and `MakeFighter`, plus
    `UpdateFighter`'s hide.
  - `PlayersVs::tick` then runs the display GObjs' processes in
    `view_tick`: the portraits, shutters, levels, arrows, flashes,
    fighters, glow and both ready GObjs.
  - `PlayersVs::visit` yields `Draw`s back to front in camera order. A
    `Draw` is a sprite, a wrapping sprite, a shadow, a puck with its glow,
    a scissor, or the `Fighters` marker.
  - Sprites are named by `(file, offset)`. The romtool test
    `vs_select_sprites` checks that every one is packed.
- **`psp-runtime` `meshdraw`.**
  - `draw_sprite_tiled` draws a wrapping `SObj` with `Repeat` on each axis
    it overruns.
  - `draw_sprite_glow` draws the puck combiner in two passes. The first is
    the texel times the glow, alpha-blended. The second adds white times
    one minus the glow through the texel's alpha.
- **`psp-game`.**
  - `players_screen` looks each piece up in the pack, clears
    `SP_FASTCOPY` and sets `SP_TRANSPARENT` where the source does, and
    applies each scissor with `Gpu::set_viewport_n64`.
  - The fighters are boxed poses. Each restarts its clip when the layer's
    `serial` changes: `anim::SLOT_WAIT` for `nFTDemoStatusNull`, or
    `SLOT_WIN1` to `SLOT_WIN4`. The poses use RE-409's translate-scale
    rule. They draw under the fighter camera in the slot's costume.
  - The plain slots remain only for a run with no pack.
  - The `vsplayers` capture stays at tick 85, select tic 65. The
    portraits arrived by tic 34. Port 2's CPU, opened on tic 42, has its
    doors open from tic 62, and its flash ended on tic 57.
  - `run` is 20,956 bytes in release (from 20,800).

## Deviations

- **Spotlight.** `mnPlayersVSMakeSpotlight`'s 3D spotlight under a
  fighter whose puck is held is not drawn.
- **Shadow noise.** The locked portraits' shadows draw their texels
  without the `NOISE` term, a 0x30 share.
- **CPU colour and shade.** `ftParamCheckSetFighterColAnimID`'s
  `nGMColAnimFighterComPlayer` on a CPU's model and the team shade are not
  drawn. The costume is.
- **Process order.** The display processes run once after the logic's
  each tic. In the source they share process priority 1 with the pucks, so
  a hook and its process can be one tic apart. A flash or the arrows made
  in a tic have their first run in that tic.
- **Glow on a revisit.** The glow and the ready counter restart at 0 on
  each visit. In the source the statics keep their values.
- **Unset envcolor.** It is read as black, as in RE-410.
- **Training select.** `draw_fighter_select` (RE-386) still draws plain
  slots. `mnplayers1ptraining.c` shares most of these sprites, but its
  layout and cameras differ.

## Verification

- **Host tests** (`players_vs_layer_tests.rs`, 14):
  - the slide-in and arrival tics;
  - the locked portraits' shadow, question mark and fire backgrounds;
  - the shutters opening and closing, with their scissors;
  - the glow's rise, clamp and fall;
  - a CPU's level, arrows, value, flash, hidden name and gate LUT;
  - the arrows at the ends and the value digit;
  - a human with handicap off, on and auto;
  - the fighter's turn, its status after 18 tics, and the hide on NA;
  - the ready blink, 30 of every 40 counts on both GObjs;
  - the rule number's alignment, stocks plus one and the infinity sign;
  - the team labels and gates;
  - the pucks and cursors' order, the CP puck, the glow and the cursor
    number's offset and colours;
  - the camera order.

  `ssb_rom::sprite` checks the new sprites' sizes and formats and the
  gate LUTs' colours against the ROM.
- **Golden.** `f1-vs-players` differs in 521,684 pixels at 2× and was
  rebaselined, identical in both captures. It shows the stone wallpaper,
  "Free-for-all", TIME 3 and BACK along the top, and the eight portraits
  with the four locked ones' question marks. Yoshi stands on port 1's red
  card with his emblem and name. Port 2's grey CPU card shows Donkey Kong
  jumping in his Win1 clip, with "CP LEVEL : 3" between the arrows. The
  two NA slots' doors are shut. The CP puck is on Donkey Kong's portrait
  and the 1P hand is on the CP button. The "Ready to fight" banner and
  "Press Start" show. The other 109 goldens are unchanged, and 110 of 110
  match.
- **Gates.** Workspace tests, pinned 1.98.0 Clippy with warnings denied,
  rustfmt and both PSP release builds pass.

## Conclusion

The VS character select draws `mnPlayersVS`' wallpaper, top bar,
portraits with their slide-in and flash, the four panels with their
doors, labels, names, emblems, levels and arrows, the glowing pucks, the
cursors, the ready banner, and each slot's fighter turning and then
playing its selected clip. The spotlight, the shadow noise and the CPU
colour remain.
