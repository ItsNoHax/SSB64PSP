# RE-519 — The GE drops whole triangles past its guard band; the port clips on the CPU

Status: COMPLETE (PPSSPP, N64 comparison, PSP-2000)
Topics: rendering, clipping, psp-lowering, opening
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-engine/src/guard_clip.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/meshdraw.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/movie.rs`

## Question

Why were the opening room's rug and floor black on the PSP?

## Findings

- The PSP GE drops a whole triangle when a vertex in front of the near plane projects outside its 4096-pixel range. The N64 RSP clips the triangle instead, so large floor triangles that cross the camera plane vanished.
- Fix: homogeneous Sutherland-Hodgman clipping on the CPU against |x|, |y| <= 2w and z >= -w, only for triangles that need it. oot-PSP and sf64-psp also clip on the CPU.
- Code: `guard_clip.rs` (4 tests); `meshdraw.rs` `GUARD_BAND`, `guard_clip_mvp`, `clip_submission` (a cold path on `sceGuGetMemory`); movie scenes set `DrawState::clip_view_proj` (`movie.rs`), fighters and hosts clear it.
- Result: opening-room MAE against the N64 (Rice) at tic 152 fell from 68.93 to 18.63 with this and the shadow fix.
- Cost: paid only when a vertex is outside the band. Frame-time impact on `vs4@2400` and the opening was not measured; it is a candidate for `MS8` profiling.

## GE shade interpolation (batch 193)

- Answer: screen-linear on the PSP-2000 (6.61 ARK, PSPLink v3.2.1), like the N64 RDP; the GE does not divide colour by w.
- Probe: viewer `depth_diag` (temporary), one Gouraud triangle red (-0.9,-0.8,w=1), green (9,-8,w=10), blue (0,8,w=10); clip = (x, y, 0, -z), viewport 480x272. Viewer PRX sha256 f6c94cade345d36a…, `scrshot` sha256 1839574eb4d99155…; shots 4 s apart identical.
- Fit over 9654 interior pixels: mean |err| 0.54 (max 1.0) against the screen-linear prediction, 71.33 (max 88.5) against perspective-correct.
- Consequence: a clipped vertex takes its colour at clip-space t (perspective-correct), the rest is screen-linear, so on-screen shade depends on where the clip edge lies. The N64 clips at the viewport (`FRUSTRATIO_1`); clipping all scene draws there matches it (batch 194, below).

## Viewport clip (batch 194)

- All scene draws (stages, movies, boss wallpaper, ending) clip at `CLIP_RATIO = 1.0` (`FRUSTRATIO_1`); `clip_at_viewport` removed.
- Flat skip (`guard_clip::fate`): a triangle with one colour and normal (the GE lights nothing, fog constant) that crosses only x/y planes and stays inside 2w (`GE_GUARD`, `OUTSIDE_GUARD` outcode bit) draws whole. Texture and depth are perspective-correct either way, shade is one value: only rasteriser rounding moves. Near-plane and past-2w triangles still clip.
- Census (`frame_timer`, temporary), per report: vs4 ~6100-6500 crossing triangles, ~80% flat; Zebes 2900-3900 crossing, 0 flat.
