# RE-404 — The VS character select

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless golden captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: menu, match, visual-regression, psp-platform
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/{players_vs,players_vs_tests,fighter_select,costume}.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`, `psp-game/src/{main,play}.rs`, `tests/golden/f1-vs-players.png`

## Question

The VS mode menu's Start led to the Training character select as a
stand-in (RE-399). How does `mnPlayersVS` pick the VS players, and what
does it hand the battle?

## Source

`mn/mnplayers/mnplayersvs.c`:

- **Process order.** It matches Training (RE-386):
  - `mnPlayersVSFuncRun` runs first;
  - then the cursors (priority 2), in the order their GObjs were made;
  - then the four pucks, the puck adjust and the costume sync (priority
    1, links 20, 26 and 31).
- **Slots and controllers.** There are four slots. Each has a player kind:
  HMN, CP or NA (`nFTPlayerKind`).
  - A slot with a plugged controller has a hand cursor, which starts at
    `(40 + 68 × port, 170)`.
  - `mnPlayersVSUpdateControllerOrders` and `mnPlayersVSUpdateGate`
    follow plugging and unplugging.
  - A saved human on an unplugged port comes back as NA.
- **First visit.** `is_reset_players` in `dSCManagerDefaultBattleState`
  resets the slots (`mnPlayersVSResetPlayer`): each human holds its own
  puck, the teams are red, red, blue and blue, and every level is 3.
- **The kind button.** The HMN/CP/NA button of each panel is at
  `(69 × port + 60…88, 127…145)` for the cursor's point. It cycles an
  unplugged slot between CP and NA. Opening a CPU gives it a random
  unlocked fighter on its portrait.
- **Buttons.** The cursor also reaches:
  - the free-for-all / team toggle (`mnPlayersVSUpdateGameMode`);
  - the team buttons;
  - the CPU level and handicap arrows (1 to 9; a human's handicap only
    with handicap on);
  - the time arrows (`timer_values`: 2, 3, 5, 10, 15, 30, 60, infinite),
    or the stock arrows, which wrap from 0 to 98;
  - the back button.
- **Costumes.** In a free-for-all a repeated fighter takes the next free
  royal costume. In a team battle each fighter wears its team costume
  (`ftParamGetCostumeTeamID`, `dFTParamCostumeIDs[].team`).
- **Back.** B held 40 ticks, or A on the back button, returns to the VS
  mode menu. B on a placed puck recalls it instead.
- **Ready** (`mnPlayersVSCheckReady`). It needs:
  - at least two players with fighters;
  - in a team battle, more than one team;
  - no cursor holding a puck.
- **Proceeding.** START after the first 60 ticks, when ready, waits 30
  ticks and saves the scene data (`mnPlayersVSSetSceneData`). It then
  goes to the stage select, or with the stage select off to the battle on
  a random stage. Five idle minutes return to the title.

## Implementation

- **`ssb_game::players_vs`.**
  - `PlayersVs::new(BattleState, SceneContext, connected)` and
    `tick(&[Pad; 4], time_byte) -> Option<Outcome>`.
  - `BattleState` is `gSCManagerTransferBattleState`, with its `Default`
    taken from `dSCManagerDefaultBattleState`.
  - `Outcome` is `Maps`, `Battle`, `VsMode` or `Title`, each carrying the
    saved state.
  - The Training port shares `puck_fighter_kind` and
    `portrait_edge_velocity`. `costume::costume_team_id` adds the team
    table.
- **`psp-game`.**
  - The VS mode menu's Start and the results' exit open
    `Screen::PlayersVs`, with one controller in port 1.
  - The session keeps the battle state across the VS menus, and the VS
    mode menu reads its rule, team flag, time and stocks from it.
  - On proceeding, the first human and the first other player with a
    fighter become the battle's two fighters. The CPU's level reaches
    `Computer::setup` through `Dummy::at_spawn`.
  - The stage select's B returns to the VS select in VS.
  - `draw_players_vs` draws plain slots: portraits, the top buttons, the
    four panels with their kind buttons, the pucks and the cursors.
- **Capture scene `vsplayers`.** A on Start at tick 20 opens the select
  (first tick 21). The stick carries the held puck to Yoshi, A at 46
  places it, the stick moves the cursor to port 2's NA button, and A at
  62 opens a CPU. A host simulation of the same script confirmed each
  step before capture.

## Deviations

- **Presentation.** The portraits, gates and shutters, the fighter models,
  names, numbers, spotlight, puck glow, flashes, blinking, the portrait
  slide-in and all sounds are not drawn or played.
- **Two fighters.** The battle runs one human against one other player.
  Further players, team battles and handicap are chosen on the select but
  not played: the battle keeps teams 0 and 1 and ignores handicap.
- **Source edge cases.** Where the source reads past an array (shade 4
  after `UpdateGameMode`; the placement priorities of a slot switched to
  CPU; `slots[-1]`), the port treats the value as none. None of these
  cases arises with one controller.

## Verification

- **Tests.** 20 host tests in `players_vs_tests.rs`, among them:
  - the first visit with one controller;
  - the kind button's CP/NA cycle;
  - placing the player's puck, and grabbing and moving the CPU's;
  - the level and handicap arrows;
  - free-for-all and team costumes;
  - the ready rules;
  - START's 60-tick guard and 30-tick wait;
  - B's 40-tick hold, the back button and the recall;
  - the time and stock arrows;
  - unplugging and replugging;
  - the idle timeout.
  Breaking four rules in turn failed nine tests.
- **Golden.** The new `f1-vs-players` golden was identical in both
  captures. It shows Yoshi placed by the player, a CPU on Donkey Kong in
  port 2, and the cursor on its kind button. The other 106 goldens are
  unchanged.
- **Gates.** Workspace tests, Clippy and both PSP builds pass. The release
  `run` is 107,768 bytes.

## Conclusion

VS now picks its players through `mnPlayersVS`'s logic. The select's
presentation, battles with more than two players, team battles and
handicap remain.
