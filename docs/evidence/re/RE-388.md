# RE-388 — Blast-zone KOs and the rebirth halo in Training

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless golden captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: fighter, gameplay, stage, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/dead.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/dead_tests.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/fighter.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/status.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/weapon.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `tests/golden/f1-training-rebirth.png`

## Question

What happens when a fighter leaves the stage? Training had no KO and no
respawn. An older, simplified port sat unused in `status.rs`: it placed
the fighter at the respawn point at once and collapsed the phases.

## Source

- **The check.** `ftMainProcPhysicsMap` calls
  `ftCommonDeadCheckInterruptCommon` every frame, after the position step
  and before `proc_map`. Outside the 1P game's team bounds, it checks
  TopN against `map_bound_bottom`, `_right`, `_left`, then `_top`. A
  top-out draws `syUtilsRandFloat() < 1/6` for `DeadUpFall`, else
  `DeadUpStar`. A ghost and Master Hand are never checked.
- **Death.** `ftCommonDeadResetCommonVars` drops a catch or capture
  (`ftCommonThrownDecideDeadResult`), puts the fighter in the air and
  destroys a held item. It also runs `ftManagerDestroyFighterWeapons`,
  which destroys only Link's Boomerang.
- **Down, left, right.** These wait `FTCOMMON_DEAD_WAIT` (45) ticks,
  invisible and a ghost. The explosion goes off at the death point,
  clamped into the camera bounds on the other axis.
- **Top-outs.** `DeadUpStar` flies toward 60% of `camera_bound_top` and
  away from the camera (`vel_z = −83.33`) for 180 ticks, then waits 45.
  `DeadUpFall` moves the fighter above the camera's eye, then falls toward
  `camera_bound_bottom` for 180 ticks and waits 45.
- **Score.** `ftCommonDeadUpdateScore` takes a stock only under
  `SCBATTLE_GAMERULE_STOCK`. Training's rules are timed, so its fighters
  always come back.
- **Rebirth.** `ftCommonRebirthDownSetStatus` gives the fighter the lowest
  halo number no other fighter in a rebirth status holds. It re-creates
  the fighter with `ftManagerInitFighter(dFTManagerDefaultFighterDesc)`,
  which resets damage, charges, Kirby's copy and the rest. It places the
  fighter at `map_bound_top` above the `nMPMapObjKindRebirth` point plus
  `dFTCommonRebirthOffsetsX[halo]`.
- **Halo timing.** The halo's despawn wait counts down from 390:
  - After 45 ticks the camera takes the fighter back.
  - After 75 ticks the fighter enters `RebirthStand`, which ends with its
    clip.
  - `RebirthWait` ends at 0 in `Fall`, with 120 frames of invincibility.
    Any ground action (`ftCommonGroundCheckInterrupt`) ends it early,
    with the same invincibility.
  - `ftCommonRebirthCommonProcMap` lowers the fighter onto the point along
    a quadratic curve for 90 ticks.
- **Ghosts.** `ftMainSetStatus` clears `is_ghost`, `is_rebirth` and the
  camera mode. A ghost is skipped by `ftMainProcSearchHitAll`, the catch
  search and the hazard search.

## Implementation

- **`ssb_game::dead`.** Ports the check, the four dead statuses, the
  rebirth statuses, the halo waits and curve, the ghost state, the stock
  rule and the fighter reset. The reset keeps the fighter's identity,
  loaded data, costume, input, staling and stage data. It replaces the
  simplified port, whose tests assumed an instant respawn and a
  315-tick `RebirthDown`; 11 new host tests cover the source timings.
- **`Fighter::tick_physics_map`.** Runs the dead and rebirth statuses'
  motion in place of the ground and air ticks, and runs the check on
  every path.
- **Stage data.** `FighterScene::at_spawn` gives each fighter the stage's
  `bounds`, `camera` extents and rebirth point.
- **Hit searches.** Fighter-vs-fighter, weapon, item, hazard and catch
  searches skip a ghost victim.
- **`psp-game`.**
  - It writes the camera eye into both fighters.
  - After each fighter's interrupt tick, it runs a pending rebirth with
    the other fighter's halo.
  - After a death it destroys the fighter's Boomerang
    (`WeaponPool::destroy_boomerang`).
  - `draw_training` skips an invisible fighter's model. This also hides
    a Kirby or Yoshi capture victim, as the source does.

## Deviations

- The check runs after the port's combined ground/air map step instead of
  between the position step and `proc_map`.
- The halo model, explosion, sparkle, screen flash, quake, rumble, sounds,
  score display and the top-out colour fade are not drawn. The explosion
  point is recorded.
- The battle camera still follows only the player, so the ghost and
  dead-up camera modes are recorded but not read.

## Verification

- **Capture scene.** `rebirth` holds the stick left from tick 20 to 100.
  Mario dashes off Dream Land's left end and is KO'd below the stage.
- **Log.** At tick 300, `PPSSPPHeadless --log` reports `RebirthStand`.
- **Golden.** `f1-training-rebirth` shows Mario standing at the rebirth
  point above the top platform, without the halo. It was identical in
  both captures.
- **Viewer golden.** `r2-stage-bonus2-fox` was rebaselined (64 pixels).
  The viewer's Mario used to fall out of frame; he is now KO'd and
  respawns.
- **Full run.** All 96 goldens match twice. Workspace tests pass with an
  absolute `SSB64_ROM` and one test thread.
