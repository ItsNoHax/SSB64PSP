# RE-563 — Battle head-0/head-1 draw phases and ROM-derived head tags

Status: COMPLETE (decomp, ROM tests, host tests, N64 RDRAM and Rice captures, full golden matrix 243 of 243; no hardware run)
Topics: rendering, draw-order, blending, asset-pack, n64-trace, goldens
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/battle_draw.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/task_head.rs`, `crates/ssb-tablegen/src/rom_layout.rs`, `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/meshdraw.rs`

## Question

RE-422 left objects drawing whole at their link. Does the N64 draw items, weapons, fighters and effects per task head, and what did the pack tag as head 1?

## Result

- Port follows the decomp: per pass, head 0 of all links ascending, then head 1 of all links ascending.
- Pack v126 tags head 1 from the ROM, not from a runtime list.
- 15 goldens moved, each with a decomp-order cause.

- `battle_draw::BATTLE` (42 steps) encodes the passes; `draw_training` loops over it.
- A split group runs its head-1 step only if its head-0 step skipped a head-1 primitive (`DrawState::head1_pending`). Non-mesh draws stay in the head-0 step (`mesh_only`).
- Deviations: non-mesh draws on N64 head 1 draw in head 0; stage controller objects on links 9-11 draw after the items; weapons drawn by type; head-1 replay re-poses (CPU only).

## Censuses

- Q1, items with `is_display_xlu` (head-0/head-1 primitives): HEART 0/2, SWORD 1/1, LINK_BOMB 1/1, PK_FIRE 0/3, TARUBOMB 2/1, TARGET 0/1. Others: none. The per-primitive `HEAD1` flag already models the class.
- Q2, link-15 head-0 effects: FoxReflector, PikachuUnk, ThunderJolt, FalconKick, Kirby Cutter Up/Down/Draw; ThunderShock 1/1; particle list 1.
- Weapons with `DOBJLINKS` split by `list_id`; Razor is the only one with head-0 content.

## Pack head mismatch

- Pack tagged whole-head-1 graphs as head 0: Yoshi shield and egg escape (338,0xA860), Falcon Punch (333,0x760), Yoshi entry egg (354,0x530), Pikachu Thunder frames (341), weapons with `wpDisplayDLHead1`.
- Fix in `task_head.rs`, from the ROM:
  - Effects: `proc_display` in {`lbCommonDObjScaleXProcDisplay` 0x800CB4B0, `efManagerYoshiShieldProcDisplay` 0x80101180}.
  - Weapons: `WPDesc.flags` without `WEAPON_FLAG_DOBJLINKS` (`wpmanager.c:259-272`), minus the head-0 display weapons.
  - Tables: `WEAPON_DESC_FLAGS`, `MANAGER_EFFECT_DL_LINKS`, `MANAGER_EFFECT_DISPLAYS` (tablegen); `EFFECT_SCALE_X_GRAPHS` removed.
- Material changes: Falcon Punch and the Yoshi egg take the CLD seed (Z cleared); the boss bullet (344,0x2CB8) takes the weapon seed, `0x80080019` to `0xA00C0259`.
- Pippi swarm (86+0x13598) stays head 0 (`it/itmonster/itspear.c:359-373,408`); the Spear's own swarm stays head 1.

## Not divergences

- D7 fighters: bodies emit on head 0 only (`ft/ftdisplaymain.c:736,746,785-805,879-899`); shadows head 0 (`ft/ftshadow.c`).
- D8 particles: head 0 only (`lb/lbparticle.c:1656-2116`).

## Open findings

- Fireball list entry is `0x1A8`, not the decomp label `0x1D8`: RDRAM `DObj->dl` = 0x802F02C8 = file 297 + 0x1A8; the 6-command prefix sets render mode `0x00553048` (no Z, no FORCE_BL), alpha compare threshold 0 and the combiner. Pack still decodes from 0x1D8 with the weapon seed. Converting from 0x1A8 as a cutout (`0xA0001030`) moved 4 rows at fireball edges; the correct mapping is a blend, not a cutout. Needs an N64 coverage capture (Rice has no coverage).
- Boss bullet translucent seed (344,0x2CB8): no golden, no N64 check.
- Falcon Punch sets `0x00553048` and never restores it; a later link-15 head-1 list setting no mode inherits it on the N64. Per-graph seeding does not model this.
- D-051: intended reorders, rebaselined after review with no hardware proof (RE-422 precedent).
