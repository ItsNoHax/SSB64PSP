# RE-545 — Item, weapon, Mario and Kirby-copy tables from the ROM: monster weapons rehit Damage-type items, US Mario's size

Status: COMPLETE (decomp source and US ROM SHA-1 e2929e10 through `crates/ssb-tablegen`; host tests; no hardware run)
Topics: tablegen, item, weapon, physics, d048
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-tablegen/src/item_attributes.rs`, `crates/ssb-tablegen/src/monster_weapons.rs`, `crates/ssb-tablegen/src/physics.rs`, `crates/ssb-tablegen/src/kirby_copy.rs`, `tools/docs/rom_tables.py`, `crates/ssb-game/src/weapon.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/physics.rs`

## Question

[D-048](../../decisions/D-048.md) moves the remaining relocData attribute
structs to build-time generation: every `ITAttributes` the port uses (45
records in files 251, 225, 240, 253, 260, 264 and 0x127), the monster
weapons' `WPAttributes` (13 records in files 251 and 264), the Smog's
per-play scale (file 86) and Mario's `FTAttributes` physics (file 203 +
0x428), plus Kirby's `FTKirbyCopy` table (file 228 + 0, 27 records of
12 bytes), which the new large-table scan turned up. Before the ROM
replaced the committed transcriptions, each entry had to be compared with
the ROM.

## Evidence

- **Items.** All 45 `ITAttributes` records and all 21 `spin_speed`s the
  port reads equal the committed values field for field (f32 by bits). The
  IDO bitfields are read MSB first, as `ITAttributes` declares them.
- **Smog scale.** Replaying `llITCommonDataDogasSmog` (file 86 + 0x13198,
  joint 1 of the anim-joint table at 0x13190) from the rest scale of the
  `DObjDesc` at 0x13100 for 30 plays gives the committed `SMOG_SCALE`
  exactly.
- **Kirby's copy table.** `copy_id`, `star_damage` and the twelve
  playable rows' `copy_modelpart_id` equal the committed `COPY` and
  `COPY_MODELPART_IDS` ([RE-417](RE-417.md)).
- **Monster weapons rehit items.** Eight of the thirteen `WPAttributes`
  have `can_rehit_item` set in the US ROM: Charmander's flame, Onix's rock,
  Charizard's flame, both swarms, the Hydro Pump, the Smog and the Fire
  Flower's flame. `251_ITCommonData.c` agrees. The committed table set it
  only for the Fire Flower. Every other field of every record matched.
- **The rehit is for Damage-type items only.**
  `itProcessUpdateDamageStatWeapon` (itprocess.c:578) sets
  `is_rehit = (ip->type == nITTypeDamage) && attack_coll->can_rehit_item`.
  The port's `WeaponPool::hit_item` checked only the flag. In the US ROM
  only the crate (`BOX`) and the barrel (`TARU`) are `nITTypeDamage`.
- **US Mario's `size` is 1.12.** `203_MarioMain.c:229-235` gives `1.0f`
  under `REGION_JP` and `1.12f` otherwise, and the US ROM holds 1.12. The
  committed `PhysicsAttributes::MARIO` had the JP 1.0. Its other 23 fields
  matched. A running fighter takes its attributes from the asset pack, so
  live gameplay already used 1.12; the constant only seeds
  `Fighter::new` and `PhysicsAttributes::default()`.

## Outcome

- `ssb-tablegen` generates the item attributes and spin speeds, the
  monster weapons' attributes, `SMOG_SCALE`, `PhysicsAttributes::MARIO`
  and Kirby's copy table. Only offsets, record sizes and the names the
  port gives entries are committed.
- `tools/docs/validate_docs.py` check 10 (`tools/docs/rom_tables.py`)
  fails on a const or static array of 24 or more numeric literals in
  ssb-game, ssb-rom or ssb-engine that `tools/docs/rom_tables_baseline.tsv`
  does not list, and on a listed one that is gone. It does not see
  struct-literal statics, which is how the `ITAttributes` were committed.
- The monster weapons now take the ROM's `can_rehit_item`, and
  `hit_item` adds the decomp's `nITTypeDamage` check. A crate or barrel
  hit by one of those eight weapons is rehit after the 16-frame timer
  instead of being hit once. Before, only the Fire Flower's flame rehit,
  and it rehit any item type.
- Seven unit tests that built fighters with the default physics assumed
  unit size. They now set `size = 1.0` explicitly; the assertions are
  unchanged. A stub build zeroes the generated tables, so the tests that
  read them carry the `ssb64_stub_tables` ignore attribute.

## Batch 107 addendum

- `can_rehit_item` applies to every weapon, not only item-made ones. The
  ROM sets it on Luigi Fireball (Mario's is clear; raw words 0x0160E1BC and
  0x0160E13C), Link Boomerang, Kirby Final Cutter, PK Thunder trail,
  Pikachu Thunder head and trail, Yoshi Star (16 fighter rows pinned by the
  `ssb-rom` weapon_attributes test).
- A group hit gives every member's record the 16-update timer.
- Clank (`nGMHitTypeAttack`) and absorb records carry no timer.
- Laser 3D `can_rehit_fighter` is unobservable (burst life 16).
- Item pickup-timer expiry pushes `SparkleWhiteScale` before the destroy
  dust (itprocess.c:117-122).
- Despawn N64-traced (batch 110, Training build route): item vanish frames, flash hidden flag (1432
  frames) and the white sparkle match; no dust on either side. Golden `f1-training-item-despawn-sparkle`.
