# RE-531 — Speculated float operations on unwritten `Option` payloads and `None`-seeded accumulators trap the PSP

Status: COMPLETE (PSP-2000, 6.61 ARK, PSPLink v3.2.1, pack sha1 f0895674, `stack_poison` and default release builds; PPSSPP never traps)
Topics: hardware, fpu, psp, opening movie
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/map.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/collision.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/weapon.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/map_tests.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/stack_poison.rs`

## Question

The opening's Jungle fight crashed some builds on a PSP-2000 with an FPU invalid-operation trap in `map::move_ground_pushed`, never on PPSSPP or the host. Why, and are there more?

## Findings

1. Mechanism. The PSP FPU traps Invalid, DivByZero and Overflow ([D-050](../../decisions/D-050.md), [RE-516](RE-516.md)); the N64 and the hosts mask them. LLVM speculates float compares, multiplies and divisions above their guard:
   - onto the unwritten payload of a `None` `Option` (stack garbage);
   - onto a `None`-seeded loop accumulator (undef register);
   - by hoisting loop-invariant float math out of a loop (LICM) before the guard that would skip it.
   Garbage that is a signalling NaN (or a zero divisor) traps, so whether a build crashes depends on earlier stack contents. That is why the Jungle fight crashed only some builds.
2. Hardware trap sites (all IUV):
   - `map::move_ground_pushed`, PRX offset 0x2C12C4, `c.olt.s` on `CliffEdge.facing` held as f32 (the opening's Jungle fight);
   - `map::move_air_inner`, 0x1165E8, `mul.s` of `reach.x * facing` from `Option<CliffQuery>` (VS, Dream Land);
   - `collision::line_edge`, 0x2BA244, `c.olt.s` on an `Option<Vec2>` accumulator (VS, Mushroom Kingdom).
3. Fixes, all behaviour-identical on the `Some` paths (the C's `lr` is an integer): `CliffEdge.facing` is a `Facing`; `CliffQuery` is always initialised with an `enabled` flag; `line_edge` seeds from the first point; `edge`, `project_floor_line`, `project_rwall_dist` and `weapon::map_contact` use a `found` flag with an initialised value; `div_nonzero` on the wall and `faces()` divisions.
4. Detector. The `stack_poison` feature (psp-game -> psp-runtime; off by default, never in a release build) fills 16 KB of stack with `0x7FA0_0000` before the fighter, stage, item and weapon passes. Needs a PSP; PPSSPP never traps. With the fix reverted it traps at site 1 in 5 s on `op-jungle@330`. The host test `map_queries_compute_nothing_on_unwritten_stack` (`--release`, x86_64 Linux) is a weaker, codegen-fragile backstop.
5. Sweep coverage. The `stack_poison` build ran clean on: the opening, 1P (game, race hazards, boss, metal, giant, zako, target, platforms), VS on all nine stages, vscpu and vsteam. The default release build ran the whole opening and title for 150 s clean.
6. Residual. `hurtbox.rs` `frame: Option<[[f32; 3]; 4]>` has the same shape and never trapped; `collision::surface_y` has a guarded division LLVM could speculate (use `div_nonzero` if it traps). Re-run the poison build on a PSP after large `map.rs` or `collision.rs` changes.

## Consequences

- D-050 names these patterns and the detector.
- No goldens changed (218 of 218).

## Sweep 2

A second `stack_poison` sweep on the PSP-2000 covered:

- VS with items high on stages 0-8 at CPU level 9, plus levels 1 and 5, with the fighters varied;
- 21 Training scenes, including every item;
- 16 effects-heavy fighter-special scenes;
- Break the Targets and Board the Platforms for all 12 fighters;
- the 1P boss with Mario, Ness and Link.

It found one more trap (trap 4). On Saffron, Ness's PK Thunder blast (status 236) runs `ness::map_blast_contacts` from `map::air_callback`. There, `sy_arc_tan2`'s `x < 0` compare (`c.olt.s`) was hoisted onto the `normal.x` of `Fighter.map_contacts.right_wall`, an unwritten `Option<Contact>` payload copied from the stack into the fighter. The trap was FPU IUV at text offset 0x2CFBF8, after about 150 s of `vs4@9000;stage=6;items=5;level=9;fighters=3,8,11,1`.

`unwrap_or(Contact::NONE)` did not fix it. InstCombine folds `op(select(c, x, K))` into `select(c, op(x), op(K))` and re-speculates on the raw payload. The fix is `ssb_engine::math::defined_or`, which passes the `unwrap_or` result through `core::hint::black_box`. It was applied to:

- the attack-frame matrix (`hurtbox::attacks_collide`, now a flag plus matrix, like the C `is_mtx`);
- `status::animation_ended`, the shadow wall probe, the Zebes acid CPU loop, the boss floor, Pikachu's zip, Thunder endpoints and the title label anchors.

Guarded divisions (`collision::surface_y`, `segment_y`, the shadow lerp, `Vec3::normalized`, the hurtbox basis) use `div_nonzero`. Every `Some`-path value is unchanged; no golden changed (218 of 218).

Diagnostic capture lines `items=N`, `level=N` and `fighters=a,b,c,d` (`profile`/`frame_timer` builds only) drive the sweeps.

Residual, audited and left: sites with a call or store inside the guarded branch, static-table or register payloads, and the MEDIUM list (particle, slope_contour, afterimage and others). The binary scan flags `take_weapon_spawns` (an `add.s` on never-stored slots); it never trapped. The scanner is not a coverage proof.
