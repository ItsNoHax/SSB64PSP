# RE-416 — The weapons' own effects, weapon clashes and the quake's camera shake

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, ROM attribute records, packed-bank runs, host tests broken and restored, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice, RNG-isolation A/B captures, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: weapon, effect, particle, combat, camera, asset-pack, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/{wpeffect,wpeffect_tests,weapon,ness_weapon,pikachu_weapon,effect,combat,camera}.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/{effect,pack}.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/tests/{weapon_attributes,particle_runtime,display_effects}.rs`, `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `tests/golden/f1-training-{yoshi-bomb,captain,kirby,ness-thunder}.png`

## Question

RE-415 made the fighters' effects but left the weapons silent: no dust,
sparkle or shock when a weapon hits, touches a wall or expires, and no
weapon-against-weapon clash (`wpProcessProcSearchHitWeapon` had no port,
RE-407). What does each ported weapon make, and when in the frame; how do
two weapons clash; and how does a quake move the camera?

## Source

- **The clash search** (`wp/wpprocess.c`). `wpProcessProcSearchHitWeapon`
  is each weapon's priority-1 process (`wpManagerMakeWeapon`), after the
  fighters' and items' searches. A weapon with `can_setoff`, an attack and
  `GMHITCOLLISION_FLAG_WEAPON` (every weapon's `interact_mask` is
  `GMHITCOLLISION_FLAG_ALL`) walks the weapon link and tests only the
  weapons after itself (`is_check_self`). It skips its owner's weapons,
  with team attack off its team's, one that cannot set off, and a pair
  either side has recorded (`group_id != 7`). The first pair of boxes that
  meets (`gmCollisionCheckWeaponAttacksCollide(other, i, this, j)`) calls
  `wpProcessUpdateAttackStatWeapon(other, .., this, ..)`: at the boxes'
  midpoint, a side whose priority is not above the other's records the
  other (`wpProcessUpdateHitInteractStats`, the whole group for PK Thunder
  and Thunder), takes its staled damage as `hit_attack_damage` and makes
  `efManagerSetOffMakeEffect` at that damage; the searched weapon's side
  comes first. Equal priorities stop both.
- **Priorities.** Every ported weapon's `WPAttributes::priority` is 1; only
  the full Charge Shot (`dWPSamusChargeShotWeaponAttributes[7]`, which
  `wpSamusChargeShotLaunch` writes) has 2. `can_setoff` is clear for the
  Blaster, the Bomb, Thunder's head and trails and PK Thunder's trails.
- **The reaction** (`wpProcessProcHitCollisions`, priority 0): `proc_hit`
  for `hit_normal_damage`, then the shield (`proc_hop` or `proc_shield`),
  then `proc_setoff` once for `hit_attack_damage` (a fighter's attack and a
  clash alike), then the reflector and the absorber. The Boomerang's
  `proc_setoff` is `wpLinkBoomerangProcSetOff` (set in its make function);
  the Egg's explosion and the Bomb's clear theirs; the Star's
  (`wpYoshiStarProcHit`) lives on without `hit_normal_damage`.
- **The weapons' effects** (`src/wp/`, every `efManager*MakeEffect`):
  - Fireball: dust on expiry and on a rebound under `vel_min`, the fire
    grind (script 0x0B) on other rebounds, a white sparkle from its
    `proc_hit` (also shield, set-off, absorb).
  - Blaster: the glow (`lbParticleMakeCommon` 0x62) when made, at a wall,
    on a hop and from its `proc_hit`.
  - Charge Shot: dust at a wall; `efManagerImpactShockMakeEffect` (script
    0x25, `attack_coll.damage`) from its `proc_hit`.
  - Bomb: the multi-explode sparkle (0x22) when its fuse ends and from its
    `proc_hit` and `proc_absorb`.
  - Boomerang: `efManagerDustCollideMakeEffect` on each newly touched
    surface while it flies out.
  - Egg: Yoshi's explosion (script 3 of Yoshi's own bank) and the shell
    (0x54) when its flight ends and from its `proc_hit`; on landing a
    magnitude-2 quake first and a dust cloud last.
  - Star: dust on expiry; a white sparkle from `proc_hit` and `proc_shield`.
  - Final Cutter: dust on expiry and at a wall or ceiling; a sparkle from
    `proc_hit` and `proc_setoff`, none from `proc_shield`.
  - Thunder Jolt: dust at every end; an impact shock from its `proc_hit`.
  - PK Fire: dust on expiry, at a wall and from `proc_absorb` (also its
    `proc_shield`); `proc_hit` makes the pillar.
  - PK Thunder: an impact shock when Ness catches it (tested before its
    lifetime) and from its `proc_hit`; dust at its other ends. Each trail
    shocks from its `proc_hit` and lives on, and draws a frame
    (`syUtilsRandIntRange(3)`) every update.
  - Thunder: its head leaves a fading segment
    (`efManagerPikachuThunderTrailMakeEffect(pos, 10, 3)`) when it meets
    Pikachu, with dust when it expires, and a magnitude-1 quake and a
    sparkle at a floor; each trail draws a frame every update and, under
    `WPPIKACHUTHUNDER_EXPIRE`, becomes a segment `(pos, 6, 0)`. A segment
    holds a struct and draws a frame each process until its lifetime ends.
- **Makers** (`ef/efmanager.c`). The impact shock takes a struct, a speed
  draw then an angle draw (`efManagerDefaultProcUpdate`) and scales by
  damage around 10; the collide dust (script 0x55, list 0) takes a struct
  and draws X, Y, an angle between 45 and 135 degrees and a scale.
- **The quake** (`efManagerQuakeProcUpdate`). Its process runs at priority
  `3 - magnitude`, plays its one `DObj`'s animation
  (`llEFCommonEffects1QuakeMag0AnimJoint` to `...Mag3...`) and, while it
  lasts, calls `gmCameraSetVelAt` with the translation's `(z, y)`, scaled
  by the eye's distance over `EFCOMMON_QUAKE_MAGNITUDE` (6500) past it.
  The camera (link 9, priority 3) adds `vel_at` to `at` last
  (`gmCameraApplyVel`) and clears it.
- **Items.** `itProcessSearchHitWeapon`'s clash branch (`itprocess.c:931`)
  needs an item with `can_setoff`; the ported PK Fire flame and Link's
  Bomb have none, so it stays unreachable.

## Implementation

- **`ssb_game::wpeffect`.** `WeaponEffect` (each maker, the quake, a
  set-off, a Thunder segment and a texture draw), `Emit` (one callback's
  effects), `WeaponFx` (a queue tagged with each weapon's place in the
  link) and `make`. The effect manager gains `impact_shock`,
  `dust_collide`, the `ThunderTrail` display (not drawn) and
  `take_quake`; `HitEffectSink::weapon` makes one, and draws a texture's
  number even without a runtime.
- **Weapons.** Each weapon's update and map callback pushes its effects
  where the source makes them; `Weapon::proc_effects` gives each kind's
  `proc_hit`, `proc_shield`, `proc_setoff` and `proc_absorb` effects to
  every reaction the pool runs (fighters, items, clashes, the Blaster's
  hop). Slots carry their link place (`seq`, renewed when a reflected PK
  Thunder becomes a new weapon).
- **Clashes.** `WeaponPool::search_weapons` (the search, in link order,
  with `ClashAttack` from each slot's staled hitbox), `clash`
  (`wpProcessUpdateAttackStatWeapon`), `record_clash` (group-wide
  records under `WEAPON_RECORD_BASE + slot`), `finish_clashes` (the
  `proc_setoff`s, skipping a weapon a fighter's attack already set off) and
  the two flushes. `WEAPON_PRIORITY` and `SAMUS_CHARGE_SHOT_PRIORITIES`.
- **Frame.** `psp-game` flushes a made weapon's effect after the spawn, the
  main processes' after the items' pass, runs the search before the
  weapon hit searches, `finish_clashes` after the item searches, the
  set-offs through `combat::finish_frame_between` (after the fighters' hit
  effects, before any `ftMainProcParams`) and the reactions' effects after
  every `ftMainProcParams`.
- **Quake.** Pack v67 adds the four quake animations as node-less
  `AnimDesc::EFFECT` slots `QUAKE_ANIM_SLOT + magnitude`. After the effect
  processes `psp-game` replays the last-writing quake's animation to its
  play count (`quake_translate`) into `Camera::quake`; the next camera
  update scales it (`Camera::vel_at`) and adds it to `at` after its own
  update, in both camera modes.

## Evidence

- **Attributes.** `crates/ssb-rom/tests/weapon_attributes.rs` decodes the
  sixteen `ll*WeaponAttributes` records (bitfields from 0x24): priority 1
  for all, `can_setoff`, `can_hop`, `can_reflect`, `can_absorb` and damage
  as the port has them, and two identical attack boxes for the Boomerang
  (both at the origin) and the Thunder trail.
- **The bank.** The impact shock (21 frames), collide dust (16), fire
  grind (24) and Blaster glow (10) run on the packed bank and end without
  an unmodelled feature. The packed quakes replay the archive's lives
  (19, 31, 31).
- **Capacity.** Four fighters' weapons ending every eight frames (dust,
  glow, shock, set-off), two rebounds every sixteen, an Egg and a Bomb
  every 32 and two Thunder segments fading every frame, beside the fighters'
  dust: 68 of 112 particles, 34 structs (the five-free rule refuses
  beyond), 15 of 64 displays. The queues: 16 Eggs landing at once make 64
  of 128; sixteen Fireballs of four owners in one spot make all
  `CLASH_FX_MAX` (192) set-offs; none dropped.
- **What moved the goldens.** A build that restores the random seed
  around every weapon-effect flush and drops the shake matches the old
  goldens in all four changed scenes (and in `f1-training-fireball`,
  `-pikachu`, `f1-vs-cpu` and `f1-vs-four`). With the shake back, the three
  quake scenes equal the full build's captures byte for byte: their change
  is the shake alone, and `f1-training-ness-thunder`'s is the trails'
  texture draws alone.
- **Drawing.** No golden catches a weapon ending. A throwaway build with
  later capture ticks shows the fire grind's sparks at a Fireball's
  bounce (tick 186) and the multi-explode sparkles round Samus's Bomb
  blast (tick 134); it was not kept.

## Deviations

- **When.** The port's hit searches react at once, so the clash search
  runs before them on the weapons as they stand (the source's weapons are
  still whole at priority 1); a clash record may therefore take a record
  slot before a same-frame fighter's. A record naming a freed slot is
  cleared when the slot is reused. Weapons tick before items (an older
  order); their effects are made after the items' pass, as the links run.
- **Not made.** The other `efManagerQuakeMakeEffect` callers (the dead
  setters, `ftCommonWallDamage`, Kirby's capture, the Falcon Dive), Yoshi's
  egg explosion (his particle bank is not packed; no
  draws), the Thunder Jolt's ground effect
  (`efManagerPikachuThunderJoltMakeEffect`: no struct, no draws, Pikachu's
  model) and PK Thunder's trail effects (`efManagerNessPKThunderTrailMakeEffect`,
  `...PKReflectTrail...`: one struct each until the head goes, no draws).
  The Thunder segments hold their structs and draw their numbers but are
  not drawn.
- **Reflect and set-off in one frame.** The port reflects at once and runs
  a clash's `proc_setoff` after; the source sets off first.
- **Quake.** A magnitude-0 quake's shake lands one frame late: the port's
  camera updates before the effect processes, while the source runs a
  priority-3 quake before the camera. Stronger quakes (priorities 2 to 0)
  run after the camera in both.

## Verification

- **Host tests.** Twenty `wpeffect` tests: two Fireballs clash with the
  earlier's set-off first and sparkle; owners and teams spare their
  weapons; the full Charge Shot outranks a Fireball and a lower one does
  not (its shock at 12); a clashed pair of Boomerangs passes through
  afterwards; the Blaster and the Bomb never clash; the Star survives its
  set-off and the Cutter does not; a Fireball's dust on its last frame;
  link order over slot order; an Egg's four landing effects and sixteen at
  once; the Blaster's glows; a Fireball's sparkle where it hits; a Thunder
  trail's frames and segment; the impact shock's and collide dust's draw
  order; a segment's draws and life; a texture drawn without a runtime; the
  clash queue's worst case; the strongest quake writing last; the camera's
  shift after its update; `WeaponPool::reset` as a new pool. ROM tests: the attributes, the makers on the
  packed bank, the weapon melee and the packed quakes.
- **Tests fail when broken.** Swapping the set-off order, dropping the
  owner or team rule, ignoring priority, the records or `can_setoff`,
  destroying the Star, dropping the Fireball's dust, the Blaster's glow,
  the hit sparkle or the trail's frame, unsorted flushing, reordering the
  Egg's effects, a wrong shock speed, swapped dust scatter, a segment
  drawing on texture 3, a runtime-less sink not drawing, a clash queue one
  short, the weaker quake winning, an unscaled quake and a shake applied
  before the camera's update and a reset keeping its queue each fail their
  test (23 mutations).
- **Goldens.** Four rebaselined, identical in both captures:
  - `f1-training-yoshi-bomb` (292,304 pixels at 2×): Yoshi's Bomb landing
    quake lifts the view some 30 pixels at 2×.
  - `f1-training-captain` (305,800) and `f1-training-kirby` (309,084): the
    Falcon Punch's and the Final Cutter landing's quakes shift the view.
  - `f1-training-ness-thunder` (1,320): a flash beside Ness drawn at other
    random numbers.
  - 114 of 114 goldens match, with and without a rebuild.
- **Gates.** Workspace tests with an absolute `SSB64_ROM` and one thread
  (1,496 passed), pinned 1.98.0 Clippy with warnings denied, rustfmt, the
  `no_std` check of `ssb-game` and both PSP release builds pass. Pack v67
  is 30,491,216 bytes, SHA-256
  `954ce58095a7eb524c79cb9ad11919a35ed7883861a23e6bd0d1a4e73082ab37`.
  The release `run` is 18,480 bytes (was 19,004) with a 17,904-byte frame;
  the largest frame is `enter_training` at 159,432 bytes (159,408 before;
  STATUS's "about 128 KB" was stale). The session boxes the weapon pool
  (11.7 KB with its queues), whose construction had doubled `run`'s frame,
  and Training resets it in place (`WeaponPool::reset`).

## Conclusion

Every ported weapon makes the effects its callbacks make, where and in the
order the source makes them, weapons clash by the source's search,
priority and records and react through their own `proc_setoff`, and a
quake shakes the camera from its own animation.
