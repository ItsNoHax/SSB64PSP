# RE-305 — Build-time N64 3-point texture compensation

Status: COMPLETE (software and PPSSPP; physical-PSP capture unavailable this batch)
Topics: texture, asset-pipeline, pack, psp-ge, visual-regression
Relevant files: crates/ssb-rom/src/filter_compensation.rs, crates/ssb-rom/src/n64_filter.rs, tools/romtool/src/main.rs

**Prerequisite.** RE-304 measured the real GE convention: filtered coordinates
receive +0.5 texel, weights truncate to four fractional bits, and channel
arithmetic truncates. This batch uses that verified `sample_bilinear()` and
RE-219's exact `sample_3point()`; it does not add a runtime pass or tessellate
geometry. Runtime remains ordinary `GU_LINEAR`.

**Algorithm.** Pack conversion samples each authored triangle at a fixed 8x8
barycentric lattice in its real S10.5 UV space. Clamp/repeat and the already
pre-expanded mirror image are included. Texgen, whose final UV depends on the
live matrix/normal, conservatively uses dense full-tile coverage. A projected,
48-iteration RGBA least-squares solve minimizes the requested squared error;
alpha texels are held exactly at their source values. Every rounded candidate
is then remeasured through the integer reference samplers. The admission gate
requires at least 1% of baseline samples at >=8/255, baseline max >=16, >=5%
SSE reduction, fewer >=8 samples, and no max-error increase beyond 8/255.
Observed selected variants had zero max-error increase.

**Formats and animation.** CI4/CI8 candidates are first quantized to the
existing immutable palette. Palette entries and meaning are never rewritten.
An indexed animated texture is left unchanged because duplicate frame-0 palette
colours can have different meanings in later palettes; the pack retained the
same 114 animated textures. Direct promotion to RGBA8888 requires >=25% SSE
improvement after indexed quantization fails and <=64 KiB level-0 growth.
Texture identity includes the source/palette/addressing fields, algorithm
version, real-coverage hash, and animated relationship. `TextureIDCurrent`
sprite frames continue to be converted independently and never mutate shared
runtime data.

**Archive result (US ROM).** The final pack emits 406 compensated variants:
274 remain CI4 and 132 use RGBA8888. Across those variants, the unweighted
per-variant mean max-channel error falls from 1.947/255 to 0.876/255; samples
>=8/255 fall from 7.06% to 2.04%, and >=32/255 from 0.483% to 0.198%.
Level-0 promotion cost is 494,368 bytes. The full pack grows from 29,389,216
to 30,172,496 bytes (+783,280, 2.67%, including mip levels/descriptors), with
1,997 textures. Dream Land `103:0xE20` falls 3.630 -> 0.691 mean and 25 -> 1
max while staying CI4. Its visible `103:0x5F0` variants fall from 4.309–7.995
mean to 2.062–5.195; alpha holds their residual max. Kirby face
`328:0x1CF60` variants also improve and either remain CI4 or promote only when
the measured gate justifies it.

**Remaining deviation.** A single texture cannot make a four-tap bilinear
surface equal the RDP's piecewise triangular surface at every coordinate.
Holding alpha texels and animated palette indices exact intentionally leaves
some error. Texgen uses conservative rather than scene-pose coverage. Physical
PSP spot checks were unavailable; PPSSPP and deterministic host references
are not claimed as physical-device proof.
