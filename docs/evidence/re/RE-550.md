## Question

When are the PK Thunder trail effect (link 15, reflected 18) and PK Wave made, and how are they drawn?

## Evidence

- **N64 trace** (warp ROM, input `45:1:b+y=80`): wave made at Hold af1 (t68); trails t70/72/74/76; trail effect t78 at the head position of t68, then 2 frames behind trail 3.
- **Cadence.** `gcRunAll` queues a weapon made in the weapon pass behind the running ones and walks into it: a new trail runs its first update on its creation frame. Trails are 2 frames apart (was 3).
- **RNG.** Each new trail draws `rand_int_range(3)` on its creation frame; this shifts `syUtilsRand` for later particles. Proven by A/B: with the trail RNG neutralised the update moves 0 sparkle px (jibaku 975 native px all from it), 36 native px of trail pose remain in `f1-cpufx-up`.
- **Trail effect.** Pose = ring[cursor-10], rotate.z = atan2(step) - 90 deg; reflected: head rotate.z - 90 deg, translate head.pos - vel*10. XLU seed `G_RM_AA_XLU_SURF`, no Z_CMP (`EFFECT_XLU_GRAPHS`, pack v122).
- **PK Wave** (335 + 0x9A10, joint 5): kind 80 (`func_ovl0_800C99CC`): joint-5 world translation, root RotRpyR y = lr*90 deg; plays through hitlag; ends with the blast.
- **Goldens.** `f1-training-ness-thunder` ~410 native px (wave arc, sparkles), `f1-cpufx-up` ~181, `f1-training-ness-jibaku` ~975.

## Deviations

- Trail effect destroy: the head refreshes `pk_alive` each pass; unrefreshed is ejected (N64 ejects directly); same frames.
- Wave draw uses `Fighter::joint_world` (does not mark the joint query).
- Trail colour: prim/env come from `dWPDisplayPKThunder{Prim,Env}Colors` by trail_id (tablegen layout, ROM values); the pack seeds SetPrim/SetEnv into the trail graph so the combiner resolves to TEXTURE_BLEND. Colours match the N64 crop; plumbing only, not a renderer-path change.
- **Trail frame.** `TextureRand(3)` frame drawn: three meshes keyed (335, sprite) for sprites 0x85C8, 0x81C0, 0x7DB8, each with the PRIM/ENV splice; `PKThunderTrail.texture` indexes `pk_trail_frames`. Bisect (`texture*0` matched the old build exactly): `f1-training-ness-thunder` moves 769 native px, `f1-cpufx-up` 381. N64 trail (batch8 t90) mixes branchy and zigzag frames per segment like the output. Not a D-051 renderer-path change.
