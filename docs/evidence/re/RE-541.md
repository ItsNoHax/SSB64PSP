# RE-541 — Kirby's Stone bank scripts and the ground Thunder Jolt burst and timing

Status: COMPLETE (decomp source, N64 warp-ROM cpufx-s frame trace and screenshots, PSP stdout traces, PPSSPP goldens; pack v119 unchanged; no hardware run)
Topics: effects, particles, weapon, fighter, n64-trace, goldens
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/{effect,fteffect,wpeffect,pikachu,pikachu_weapon}.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`

## Question

Two effects RE-415 left unmade:

- the Kirby particle bank's scripts behind effect kinds 0x4C and 0x4D;
- the burst `efManagerPikachuThunderJoltMakeEffect` draws for a ground Thunder Jolt.

When the burst was compared against the N64, the PSP jolt also turned out to be one frame late and 46 units off. Why?

## Source

- **Kirby bank.** `func_ovl2_801031E0` (kind 0x4C, Stone start) and `func_ovl2_80103280` (kind 0x4D, Stone cancel) each start Kirby bank script 2 or 5 with no generator-link mask. They make a `Ready` transform, process it once, then set `translate` to the position. Neither keeps a struct. The bank's scripts draw `syUtilsRandFloat`, which is the shared game RNG.
- **The burst.** `efManagerPikachuThunderJoltMakeEffect(pos, rotate)` makes `dEFManagerThunderJoltEffectDesc`: file 342, DObj desc 0x2258, link 15. There is no `EFFECT_FLAG_USERDATA` and no struct. The root takes `translate = pos` and `rotate.z`, and joint 1 plays the desc's animation. `efManagerHaveStructProcUpdate` ejects it when the animation ends.
  - `wpPikachuThunderJoltGroundProcUpdate` makes it on each push-frame restart.
  - `wpPikachuThunderJoltGroundMakeWeapon` makes it on the conversion from air to ground.
- **The spawn.** `ftPikachuSpecialNProcAccessory` (`ftpikachuspecialn.c:18`; no ProcUpdate exists) places the jolt at joint 11 (`FTPIKACHU_THUNDERJOLT_SPAWN_JOINT`). It runs after `ftMainProcPhysicsMap` and the parts transform, so the joint has the current frame's pose and the post-move TopN (RE-555). The match on this trace held because Pikachu did not move in physics that frame.
- **Same-frame ground process.** `gcRunAll` walks the weapon link in order. The ground weapon that an air jolt makes in its map process is linked after it, so `gcRunAll` processes it in the same frame.

## PSP before

- The jolt spawned at joint 11's frame-20 position (-1307.900, 1031.465) rather than frame 21. It had life 99 at (-1279.616, 1003.181), 46 units behind the N64.
- It converted to the ground weapon one frame late, at x -1230.435. The landing projected `hit.position` and did not use the full-step x.
- The new ground weapon waited a frame before its first process.

## Implementation

- `Effects::kirby_bank_ready` implements kinds 0x4C and 0x4D. `fteffect` dispatches `KIRBY_BANK_2` and `KIRBY_BANK_5` to it.
- `DisplayKind::ThunderJolt` is a link-15 display with no struct, `life::THUNDER_JOLT = 9`, and is processed by `efManagerHaveStructProcUpdate`. `Effects::thunder_jolt` makes it, and the weapon queues it as `WeaponEffect::ThunderJolt` at the push frame and at the ground make. The PSP draws asset slot 8 (342, 0x2258): the root uses the jolt's translate and `rotate.z`, and joint 1 is composed from the desc animation.
- `spawn_pose` (`THUNDER_JOLT_POSE`, Accessory phase) re-places the queued jolt at joint 11 after the map step; the PSP host samples the joints for it (RE-555).
- The floor landing projects the full-step position (`project(s, wanted)`). It falls back to the hit point.
- After the conversion, `tick_map` runs the new ground weapon in the same frame.

## Open

- **Burst edges (measured, limitation).** File 342 DL 0x2170 sets G_AC_THRESHOLD with blend colour alpha 0, so the PSP gate stays the RE-424 `alpha >= 32` (`pack::alpha_gate`); the N64 passes the same pixels. Texel alpha is 1-bit (RGBA16 TLUT), so every soft edge comes from the filter. Against N64 cpufx-s3 el162-164 (320x240 vs the 960x544 golden, normalised by 2.3125 x 2.267): spike width 2.22 vs 2.20-2.24 native px, partially blended share 0.78 vs 0.66-0.75, boundary 776 vs 576-580 native px, area 263 vs 185-194 (23-36% larger; ~11% framing, ~20% unattributed), fragments 6 vs 7-12. No GE state reproduces eighth-quantised coverage or the VI mix. Regression: `f1-cpufx-jolt`, `-jolt-end`. Open: the ~20% area (`PLAN.md`).
- **Pikachu during SpecialN.** From el 162 the PSP draws Pikachu translucent or greyed, even with the burst hidden, and draws electric arcs at the top left. The N64 shows neither. The suspect is the SpecialN colanim. This was there before this batch.
