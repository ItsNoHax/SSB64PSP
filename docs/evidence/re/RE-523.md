# RE-523 — Animation joint flags hide fighter models; focus-2 zoom camera falls back out of bounds

Status: COMPLETE for Yoshi and Fox/DK/Kirby; Captain Falcon's car phase has no N64 capture
Topics: fighter entry, camera, yoshi, animation flags
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-rom/src/skeleton.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/meshdraw.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/battle.rs`

## Question

Why was Yoshi visible inside his egg, and why did the focus-id-2 entry camera (Arwing, Kirby's star, Captain's car) frame differently from the N64?

## Findings

- Yoshi's AppearR/L figatree `SetFlags` writes flags = 2 (`DOBJ_FLAG_HIDDEN`) on anim joint 1 (the model root) for ticks 0-60. `ftDisplayMainDrawDefault` skips a hidden node with its subtree, and skips only the display list when `NOTEXTURE` (bit 0) is set. The port ignored joint flags. `Skeleton::joint_flags`/`any_flags`/`hidden` expose them and `draw_fighter_posed_hiding` honours them; low-detail nodes map onto the high-detail ones the skeleton drives.
- `gmCameraPlayerZoomFuncCamera` runs the default battle camera while the zoomed fighter's TopN is outside the camera bounds or |z| > 1000 (`gmCameraCheckPausePlayerOutBounds`). Otherwise `gmCameraUpdatePlayerZoom` runs with FOV 28, target distance from the closeup zoom, pan 0.1 and `cam_offset_y` added. The port always zoomed. `entry_zoom` is now an `Option` and `scene.rs` applies the fallback test.
- `is_magnify_display` is FALSE until Go, so magnifiers and player arrows stay hidden through the entry (`Battle::is_magnify_display`).

## Verification

- Yoshi matches the N64 at frames 246, 258 and 264.
- Focus 2: MAD against the N64 at the matching frame fell from 26-40 to 26.1/10.2/26.9/11.0 at ticks 260/300/340/370 (the residue is timer and emblems); Arwing ticks 160-240 give 6.0-11.6, best of a +-4 frame window.
