# RE-373 — Samus in Training: Charge Shot and Bomb meshes and transforms

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, US ROM bytes, pack readback, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: fighter, weapon, texture, asset-pipeline, visual-regression, rendering
Relevant files: `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/pack.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/weapon.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/samus.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/gu.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`, `tests/golden/f1-training-samus*.png`

## Question

What do Samus's Charge Shot and Bomb draw, and with which transforms?

## Source

- `218_SamusSpecial1.c`: the Charge Shot's `WPAttributes.data` is file
  321 + 0x270, with no `p_mobjsubs`. `dWPSamusChargeShotWeaponDesc` has
  flags 0, so `wpManagerMakeWeapon` adds one DObj on that display list.
  Its transform kinds are `Tra` and 0x2E (46).
- `217_SamusMain.c` + 0x0C (`llSamusMainBombWeaponAttributes`): the Bomb's
  `data` is file 320 + 0xE0D8 and `p_mobjsubs` is 320 + 0xE008. That table
  holds one `MObjSub` (0xE018) whose `palettes` array (0xE00C) names 0xDF60
  and 0xDF38. Its transform kinds are `Tra` and 0x46 (70).
- `wpSamusBombProcUpdate` toggles `mobj->palette_id` between 0 and 1 on the
  blink timer. It spins `rotate.z` by 20° per update in the air and 10° on
  the ground, times `lr`. `wpSamusBombExplodeInitVars` sets the DObj's
  `dl = NULL`, so the explosion draws nothing.
- `wpSamusChargeShotProcUpdate` sets the charging shot's X/Y scale to
  `gfx_size / 30` of the current level and subtracts 18° × `lr` from
  `rotate.z` every update, charging or released.
- `gcPrepDObjMatrix` case 46 (`objdisplay.c`) keeps the translated position
  and replaces rotation with the camera's axes, spun in the screen plane by
  `rotate.z` and scaled by X/Y (Z takes X's scale).
- Kinds 66 and up call `sGCMatrixFuncList[kind - 66]`. Battle scenes install
  `dLBCommonFuncMatrixList` (`lbcommon.c`), whose entries are
  `{proc_diff, proc_same}` pairs. Kind 70 is pair 4,
  `func_ovl0_800CA194`: it rewrites the MVP's rotation rows as a rotation
  about world Z by `rotate.z` and keeps the translation row. The Bomb
  therefore takes no other rotation and no scale.

## Findings

- The decomp's symbol split labels file 321's 0x230..0x2A0 as seven
  vertices. The ROM bytes from 0x270 are display-list words (`E7`, `E3`,
  `E2`, `FC`, `F9`, `E8`, texture loads, `01` loading the four vertices at
  0x230, `06`) through `DF` at 0x348. The Charge Shot is one 30×30 textured
  quad.
- Kind 71, the Fireball's second transform (RE-372), is pair 5,
  `func_ovl0_800CA5C8`, which rotates by `rotate.x` and `rotate.y`. It is
  not ported here.

## Implementation

- `romtool` packs the Charge Shot list as (321, 0x270). It converts the Bomb
  list once per palette: (320, 0xE0D8) for `palettes[0]` and (320, 0xDF38),
  the palette's own offset, for `palettes[1]`. Pack v52 is 27,087,568 bytes.
- `SamusBomb::rotate_z` and `SamusState::charge_spin` hold the spins. They
  are for presentation only.
- `Gpu::model_transform_billboard` builds the kind-46 matrix from the
  Training camera's `look_at` basis. `psp-game` draws the charging shot at
  `charge_shot_position` while `is_charging`, and each released shot at its
  own scale. Bombs draw under `T · Rz(rotate_z) · MODEL_SCALE` with the
  mesh for their `blink_palette`.
- The capture scenes `samus`, `samusshot` and `samusbomb` spawn Samus.

## Deviation

The port fires a new weapon on release. The source launches the charging
weapon in place, so its spin continues. The released shot therefore
restarts `rotate.z` at 0.

`f1-training-samus` (level-2 charging shot on the arm cannon),
`f1-training-samus-shot` (level-1 shot in flight) and
`f1-training-samus-bomb` (the Bomb on the floor after Samus walks off it).
Each new golden was identical in both captures. Workspace tests pass with an
absolute `SSB64_ROM` and one test thread. Pinned 1.98.0 Clippy with warnings
denied and workspace rustfmt pass.
