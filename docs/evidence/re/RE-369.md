## Question

RE-367 packed the shield pose and moved the collision sphere to the posed
`YRotN`, but no PSP scene raised a shield. Does the regular Training tick
reach that pose and collision transform with live input?

## Scene and observation

The `shield` scene confirms Intro and Training at ticks 4 and 8, settles
Mario on Dream Land, holds Z from tick 40, and holds a (40, 40) stick from
tick 50. It freezes at tick 60. The script uses the same controller input
and fighter tick as play, with no forced status or pose. The stick is below
the guard roll and jump thresholds.

PPSSPPHeadless `--log` at the capture tick reports
`player_status=Common(Guard)`, `raised=true`, `joint_present=true`, sector 1,
frame 0, and blend range 0.707. Mario stands at (0, 0, 0); the transformed
shield center is (75, 255, 0), and its scaled X axis is (0, 0, -8.56).
The center comes from the posed `YRotN`, rather than the feet fallback.
The screenshot shows Mario's guard pose. It does not draw the shield bubble (drawn from RE-384).

`f1-training-shield` is byte-identical across two captures and matches the
new golden. The existing `f1-training-jab` control also matches twice.
This proves the current PSP software render and collision-transform wiring
at one live Training frame; it does not compare the pose to original N64
pixels or demonstrate physical-PSP output.
