# RE-526 — The jungle opening's white Samus silhouette is a hardware fog-blender result that the Rice reference ignores

Status: COMPLETE (explained; no port change)
Topics: opening movie, jungle scene, reference-tool caveat, fog blender, N64 captures
Relevant files: `refs/ssb-decomp-re` (display list walk only)

## Question

At the jungle scene's tic 21 the PSP draws Samus mostly white while the N64 capture shows her coloured. Which one is wrong?

## Findings

- N64 RDRAM: Samus is in DamageFlyTop (0x36) at status tic 1 with hitlag 7; her colanim is DamageCommon with color1 `ffffffe6`.
- The display list at 0x8018f7e8 sets `SetFogColor ffffffe6` and render mode `c4112078` (`G_RM_FOG_PRIM_A`) before her matrices, so real hardware blends her about 90% white.
- The Rice video plugin used for the captures ignores that blender, so the capture shows her coloured. The PSP pose also matches FlyTop.

The port is correct. Treat the frame as a known reference artefact; do not tune the renderer to it. More generally, a capture from Rice cannot show fog-blender render modes.
