## Question and sources

Close the moving-group and cliff-pose gaps from RE-348/RE-352 without inventing
per-frame changes to ordinary fighter diamonds.

Sources: `src/mp/{mpcollision,mpprocess,mpcommon}.c`,
`src/ft/ftmanager.c`, `ft/ftmain.c`, `ft/ftphysics.c`,
`ft/ftcommon/ftcommoncliff{catchwait,climb,attack,escape}.c`,

`sys/{objanim,objdisplay,objtypes}.{c,h}` and fighter relocData motion tables
under `refs/ssb-decomp-re/`. Checked against all 12 playable fighters in the

## Findings

- Ordinary fighters alias `p_map_coll` to their current `map_coll`.
  `mpCommonCopyCollDataStats` temporarily substitutes another body's shape
  and a supplied previous position. This is not an animation-driven resize.
  `move_air_from_shape` preserves separate previous/current probes; normal
  movement passes the same shape for both. The previous deferral's wording
  was wrong.
- `mpProcessUpdateMain` computes 250-unit substeps from the full displacement,
  including group carry. Own motion is divided across steps; group speed is
  applied once, in the first step. Diff queries shift the previous probe by
  group speed; current diamond-edge checks use Same queries. Integer local
  vertices, original connectivity and float translations remain separate.
- Group states are None/On/Show/Off/Hidden (0–4); states below Off exist.
  Translation applies when the DObj has animation or status is not None.
  On/Off suppress the group's animation callback. Animation flags crossing
  zero/nonzero set Show/Hidden. Renderer flags differ: bit 0 hides only the
  mesh, bit 1 hides the subtree; siblings continue drawing.
- Ground fighters, weapons and items receive line speed. Item carry precedes
  bounds and still runs in hitlag. Thunder Jolt's crawler remains Air in the
  source, so it projects against moving geometry without ground carry.
- Cliff status ordinals 84–99 provide 16 clips. Catch, Quick/Slow dispatch,
  phase one and phase two end on authored animation clocks; Wait loops.
  Hanging TopN is `corner + (TransN.z * facing, TransN.y) * size`.
  Phase two snaps to the live corner plus five units inward, then runs the
  source ground/air TransN physics and map callbacks.
- `cliff_status_ga` begins at attribute offset `0x2B8`. The source declares
  five words but indexes six kinds; slow escape reads the next word at
  `0x2CC`, zero for all 12 fighters. Mario, Samus, Luigi and Ness have an
  Air quick attack (bit 1); every other playable choice is Ground. Pack v40
  stores these six choices as a mask in the former FighterDesc tail padding
  at +220; its 224-byte stride is unchanged.
- Every playable cliff file has enough entries for its leading TransN and
  active model parts. Kirby's cliff escape has additional trailing entries.
  The packer previously required exactly model-count + 1 before honouring
  a runtime-joint descriptor. It now accepts additional trailing entries;
  the motion descriptor determines whether to shift the model bindings.
- Cliff drop/damage supplies the outside-corner position as `pos_prev` to
  the default collision sweep, retaining the live hanging pose as target.
  It does not teleport TopN to the supplied position. Runtime hit resolution
  consumes this callback before drawing; stick/timeout drops consume it
  during the fighter map tick.

## Implementation boundary

Shared PSP StageMap publishes DObj translations, speeds and visibility to
fighters, weapons and items and draws those same poses. Stage-specific
controllers, hazards and the match stage loader remain separate work. Moving
floor shadows and cliff visual/audio effects remain. No unsupported renderer
heuristics or physical-PSP claims are introduced.
