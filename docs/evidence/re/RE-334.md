# RE-334 — Luigi motion scripts, shared Mario specials and Fireball row

Status: COMPLETE (decomp source, host tests, both PSP builds, PPSSPP smoke; Luigi not reachable in `psp-game`, physical PSP not captured)
Topics: fighter, gameplay, weapon, grab, animation
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/luigi_attack.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/status.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/weapon.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/grab.rs`, `tools/gen-anim-table.py`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`

**Question.** What does Luigi share with Mario, and where do his US motion
scripts, weapon row and grab data differ?

**Source.** `ftluigistatus.h` (`dFTLuigiSpecialStatusDescs`) uses the same
status IDs (220–228) and the same `ftMario*` callbacks as Mario.
`ftMarioSpecialNProcAccessory` selects `fireball_item_id = 1` for Luigi, which
indexes `dWPMarioFireballWeaponAttributes` in `wpmariofireball.c`.
`222_LuigiSpecial1.c` gives the Fireball `WPAttributes`. `220_LuigiMainMotion.c`
gives every attack, catch and throw window. `dFTLuigiMotionDescs` in
`ftdata.c` names Mario's figatrees for all but the dash attack, down tilt and
forward smashes. `dLuigiMain_thrown_status` equals Mario's table, and
`joint_itemheavy_id` is 28 for both.

**Findings.**

- The port reuses `MarioStatus` for Luigi, as the source does. Only the
  motion scripts, the Fireball row and the throw descriptors differ.
- Super Jump Punch: `SuperJumpPunchGround` has no `End` and falls through
  into `0x17FC`. The 25-damage sweet spot lives for frame 2 only. The
  one-damage boxes replace it without a clear, so a target hit by the sweet
  spot is not hit again. Flags 1 and 2 fire at frame 7, not Mario's 9.
- Luigi Cyclone never clears its opening boxes. The frame-43 finishers
  replace them, so one hit record covers the whole move. The grounded
  script replaces only slots 0 and 1, and its centre box stays live until
  frame 45. The flag timing (frame 43) equals Mario's.
- `DashAttack` creates its frame-44 finisher after `Wait(6)` and then runs
  `WaitAsync(4)`, which has already passed. The finisher is cleared in the
  same frame and never reaches a collision check.
- Luigi has only the three forward-tilt motions, and no `LandingAirN` or
  `LandingAirLw` motion. The down aerial's `SetFlag1(20)` therefore selects
  `LandingAirNull`. Mario's motion table has the same null entry, but the
  existing Mario port still enters `LandingAirLw`.
- Fireball row 1 (US): lifetime 80, gravity 0, launch angle 0, speed 36,
  damage 6. The rebound factor, terminal velocity and minimum speed equal
  Mario's.
- Luigi's forward throw uses status 55 (`DamageFlyRoll`) and his back throw
  status 52 (`DamageFlyN`), the reverse of Mario's pair. The release frames
  equal Mario's.

**Deviations.**

- The Super Jump Punch intangible frame (`SetHitStatusAll(3)`) and the
  up-smash head intangibility wait on the hit-status system.
- Both Fireball rows use one angle on the ground and in the air, so the
  spawn request carries no situation.

**Verification.** Host tests cover every Luigi move's joint table, the dash
finisher, the Cyclone hit record, the Super Jump Punch sweet spot and launch
frame, the Fireball spawn frame and row values, the jab finisher, the
down-air landing, and the throw descriptors. The generated animation table
adds 21 Luigi attack slots, fills the Mario special slots for Luigi, and
packs his grab and thrown clips.
