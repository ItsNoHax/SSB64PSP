# RE-420 — The KO halo's rays, quake and star-KO far plane; the results' emblem and confetti

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, `reloc_data.us.h`, ROM display lists, packed-asset ROM tests, host tests, N64 warp-boot references, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice and broken by disabling each draw, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: effects, rendering, camera, asset-pack, menus, visual-regression, psp-platform
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-rom/src/{mesh,effect,skeleton,pack}.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/tests/{halo_rays,emblems,particle_runtime}.rs`, `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/{dead,ko,camera,particle,effect,results_emblem,results_layer}.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/{meshdraw,particles,scene}.rs`, `psp-game/src/{main,results_screen}.rs`, `tests/golden/*.png`

## Question

RE-410 and RE-412 left five gaps: the rebirth halo's rays were hidden, a
KO made no quake, a star KO vanished past the port's 10,000-unit far
plane, and the VS results drew neither the winner's 3D series emblem nor
the confetti. What does the original do for each?

## Source

- **Halo rays** (file 85, `EFCommonEffects3`). The halo's first DObj
  (`RebirthHaloDObjDesc`, 0x2AC0) has a second display list on DL link 1
  at 0x2A88, which calls 0x2890: `G_SETCOMBINE 0x00FFFFFF 0xFFFDF2F9`,
  cycle 1 colour `(0 − 0) · 0 + PRIMITIVE` and alpha `(0 − 0) · 0 +
  TEXEL0`, over an I8 texture, under a blending render mode. So the rays
  are white at the texel's intensity. The converter's
  `combiner_flat_color` checks only the colour cycle, so it packed a flat
  colour and dropped the texture.
- **Same class elsewhere.** Across the archive, 13 meshes have a flat
  colour, a texture, a blending mode and an alpha formula that reads
  `TEXEL0`: the halo's 0x2A88 and 0x2810, the catch swirl
  (`CatchSwirlMObjSub`, file 84 0x2500–0x2698, `fcff97ff ff2dfeff`,
  PRIMITIVE colour and TEXEL0 · PRIMITIVE alpha), the item-get swirl
  (file 85 0x2EF0–0x30A0), Kirby's vulcan-jab effect (file 348 0xA78,
  graph 0xB20), graph 86 + 0x1158 (no decomp symbol) and the opening
  movie's room logo (file 52 0x1BFC8).
- **Quake.** `ftCommonDeadInitStatusVars` (called by the Down, Left and
  Right setters) and `DeadUpFall`'s landing call
  `efManagerQuakeMakeEffect(2)` directly, not through
  `ftParamMakeEffect`. `DeadUpStar` makes no quake.
- **Far plane.** `dGMCameraPerspDefault` (`gm/gmcamera.c`) is near 256,
  far 39,936. A star KO flies about 15,000 units away.
- **Emblem** (`mn/mnvsmode/mnvsresults.c`). `mnVSResultsMakeEmblem` builds
  the winner's `FTEmblemModels` tree (file 35; `dobjdescs`, `mobjsubs`
  and `matanim_joints` by fighter kind, Luigi sharing Mario's and
  Jigglypuff Pikachu's; offsets from `reloc_data.us.h` 0x0–0x5A84) on DL
  link 33. `gcAddMatAnimJointAll(gobj, joints, color)` gives every MObj
  `anim_wait = AOBJ_ANIM_CHANGED` and `anim_frame = color`
  (`sys/objanim.c`), and the single `gcPlayAnimAll` fast-forwards each
  script to that frame. No animation process is added, so the colour
  holds. `color` is the winner's port, or `{0, 1, 3}[team]` in a team
  battle. Each script steps `Light1Color` and `Light2Color` one frame per
  colour: red, blue, yellow, green, then the CPU's grey. The DObj sits at
  (0, 100, −11000), scale 25 in x and y. `mnVSResultsEmblemProcUpdate`,
  from tic 40, shrinks it by 0.15 per tic to 10 and raises it by 11 per
  tic to y 1000. `mnVSResultsMakeEmblemCamera` is priority 60 (between
  the two wallpaper fades' 70 and 55), link 33, eye (0, 0, 1800) on the
  origin, viewport (10, 10)–(310, 230), `dGCPerspDefault` (30°, 100 to
  12,800).
- **Confetti.** `mnVSResultsFuncRun` calls `mnVSResultsMakeConfetti` on
  `sMNVSResultsMakeResultsTic` unless no contest:
  `efManagerConfettiMakeEffect` at (0, 1000, −1000) with
  `is_genlink_mask` FALSE (the bank ORed with
  `LBPARTICLE_MASK_GENLINK(3)`, list 4), then at (0, 1000, −400) with
  TRUE (list 0), each starting `efcommon` script 0x70. The fighters'
  camera draws DL links 18, 15, 10 and 9. Link 10 is list 4's
  `efDisplayZPerspAAXLUProcDisplay` (`G_RM_AA_ZB_XLU_SURF`,
  depth-tested), link 15 list 1 and link 18 lists 0 and 2.

## Implementation

- **Pack v72** (34,455,072 bytes, SHA-256
  `527e07c595bc22503683d60997f1342f482ee51a63b3ffddbf59a442aafd3d6b`,
  31,936 more than v71; rebuilt twice, identical). No layout change.
  - `mesh::State::material_now` keeps the texture of a flat colour that
    comes with a texture, a blending mode and an alpha formula that reads
    the texel. It is packed as a `TEXTURE_BLEND` whose base and target are
    both the flat colour, which the GE draws as `Cv = PRIM`, `Av = Af ·
    At`.
  - `effect::EMBLEMS` and `EMBLEM_BY_FIGHTER` name file 35's ten trees.
    `romtool` binds each tree's `MatAnimJoint` to the materials its
    `MObjSub` resolved, so their prims carry `LIGHT1_ANIM | LIGHT2_ANIM`.
  - Against v71 exactly 23 meshes change: those 13 and the ten emblem
    meshes, whose prims split by material.
- **KO.** `DeadState::quake` records the request, and
  `KoEffects::observe_with` makes it through `Effects::quake(2)`. The
  port's `REBIRTH_HALO_RAYS_NODE` hide is removed.
- **Projection.** `camera::DEFAULT_NEAR`/`DEFAULT_FAR` are
  `dGMCameraPerspDefault`'s. Training's and the particles' battle
  projections use far 39,936 with the near plane `camera::GE_NEAR` (1; see
  Deviations). The particles' depth cull keeps 256/39,936.
  `particles::draw_lists` draws any list set, and list 4 depth-tested at
  its GE depth for the planes of the projection in use
  (`Camera::ge_planes`).
- **Results.** `ssb_game::results_emblem` holds the emblem's state,
  update and camera, `CONFETTI` and `confetti_due`. `results_layer`
  yields `Draw::Emblem` between the two fades. `results_screen` makes the
  emblem with `EffectMaterialAnimator::start_at(color)` and one tick, and
  owns the results' own particle pools and effects, which run on link 0
  after the confetti is made. The particles are drawn under the
  fighters' camera in link order (4, 1, 0 and 2).
- **Scenes.** `starko` puts Rebirth's Mario in `DeadUpStar` on the floor
  at tick 60 and captures 150 ticks later. `vsresultsemblem` is
  `vsresults` at results tic 100 (tick 742).

## Evidence

- **ROM tests.** `tests/halo_rays.rs` decodes the rays' combiner from the
  ROM and checks that the packed prim keeps its texture as a white
  `TEXTURE_BLEND`, translucent. `tests/emblems.rs` checks that every
  emblem prim carries both light animations and that frames 0–3 give
  every emblem red, blue, yellow and green over a (0x26, 0x26, 0x26)
  ambient, and frame 4 grey over black. `particle_runtime.rs` runs both
  confetti scripts for 1,230 frames: lists 4 and 0 only, eight
  generators that never end, inside the pools.
- **N64 references.** Two warp-boot builds of the decomp, patched and
  reverted (SHA-1 back to `e2929e10…`), captured with
  `SDL_VIDEODRIVER=offscreen` and keyed to a tic counter read from RDRAM:
  - Build A: Training on Dream Land, Mario against a Mario CPU; catch
    swirl made at tic 180, a KO below the stage at 260, a star KO at 700.
  - Build B: a one-stock VS battle on Dream Land, Luigi against a
    level-3 Kirby CPU; Luigi dropped below the blast line at battle tic
    450; results captured by `sMNVSResultsTotalTimeTics`.
- **Per-part N64 comparison.**
  - *Halo rays: match.* N64 tic 400 shows a white glow fading upward over
    Mario's legs above the platform; the port's `rebirth` shows the same.
  - *Other changed meshes.* The catch swirl on the N64 (tic 183) is soft
    orange streaks, not the flat pinwheel the viewer golden showed; the
    new `r1-catch-swirl-flat-color` is soft streaks: match. The item-get
    swirl, vulcan jab, graph 86 + 0x1158 and the room logo are packed but
    drawn by no port scene, so none was captured.
  - *KO quake: match in kind.* The N64 frames 266–270 after the KO shift

    RE-416's magnitude-2 quake. The two scenes frame the KO differently
    (the port's Mario dashes off the left end), so the offsets are not
    compared pixel for pixel.
  - *Star KO: match.* N64 tics 820–850 show Mario as a few pixels over
    the treetop under the 1P arrow, and a sparkle at 880. The port's
    `starko` (+150 ticks) shows him in the same place; with far 10,000 he

  - *Emblem: match.* N64 results tics 40–100 show a uniform blue Kirby
    star (Kirby is port 2) shrinking and rising; at tic 100 it spans 0.24
    to 0.73 of the width in both. At tic 457 it sits dim behind the
    results text in both.
  - *Confetti: match.* Both start at tic 121 and at tic 457 show similar
    density and colours falling in front of and behind the fighters.
  - *Not matched.* At tic 457 Kirby's Win pose differs (mouth open on
    the N64). It is the same in the old golden, so it is not this

## Deviations

- **Near plane.** With `dGMCameraPerspDefault`'s near 256, the first
  verify changed 52 goldens. Most were a few edge pixels, but in `shield`,
  `nessmagnet` and `vsshield` the fighter drew over its bubble. An N64
  capture with the port scene's input (Z, stick (40, 40)) shows the
  bubble over the whole body.
  - The shield list (file 163 + 0x248) is a flat 60 × 60 quad on DL link
    15. Link 15 is `G_RM_AA_ZB_XLU_SURF`, and fighters are
    `G_RM_AA_ZB_OPA_SURF`. Matrix function 79 places the quad at
    `YRotN`'s world translation (`parts->mtx_translate`). Kind 44 then
    rewrites the MVP's rows 0–2 after `gSPMvpRecalc` and keeps row 3. So
    on paper the quad is depth-tested at the joint.
  - The port's joints span z −79.5 to 44.1 around a centre at z 0. With
    exact depth, the fighter's near parts win, as they do in the port.
  - With near 1, the GE's 16-bit depth steps about 280 units at battle
    distance, so the quad ties the fighter and covers it, as the N64
    capture shows.
  - Why the N64 (Mupen64Plus, Rice) covers the fighter is not
    established; Rice's handling of this path is one candidate. The port
    keeps near 1 (`camera::GE_NEAR`) and takes only the far plane. The
    particles' own depth cull keeps 256.
  - Resolved by RE-421. The bubble is not depth-tested on the N64.
    `efDisplayCLDProcDisplay`, the priority-3 display object on links 15
    and 18, leaves `G_RM_CLD_SURF` with `G_ZBUFFER` cleared, and the
    bubble's list, which sets neither, draws under it. The near plane is
    now 256 and `GE_NEAR` is removed.
- **Emblem depth.** At z −11,000 the emblem's centre is exactly on the
  results camera's far plane (12,800). Both the N64 and the port draw
  it.
- **Results particles.** The results screen keeps its own particle pools
  and effects: `efParticleInitAll` and `efManagerInitEffects` for that
  scene.

## Conclusion

The rebirth halo draws its rays, a KO shakes the camera, and a star KO
stays visible to the end of its flight. The VS results draw the winner's
series emblem in the winner's colour, shrinking and rising, and the
confetti falling through the fighters' camera. All five match N64
warp-boot captures. The battle projection takes the original's far plane
but not its near plane, because the near plane changes how the shield
bubble covers the fighter.
