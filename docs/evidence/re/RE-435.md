# RE-435 — The Poké Ball's thirteen Pokémon and their weapons

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, ROM attributes and animation replays, N64 Mupen64Plus references of two Training scenes, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: items, combat, weapons, asset-pack, rendering, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/item/{mmonster,mmonster_tests,mod,map,monsters,normal}.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/{monster_weapon,weapon,map,wpeffect,effect}.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/{mmonster,pack}.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/tests/mmonster.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`, `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/gu.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `tests/golden/`

## Makers

`itMainMakeMonster` now makes `ITKind` 32 to 44 (`itmonster/`): Onix,
Snorlax, Goldeen, Meowth, Charizard, Beedrill, Blastoise, Chansey, Starmie,
Hitmonlee, Koffing, Clefairy and Mew (`item::mmonster`). Each maker undoes
the ball's map projection (all but Snorlax's and Mew's put the root back at
the ball), lifts the item by its map box's bottom (all but Snorlax), and
rises at 16 for 22 frames under its descriptor's procs. Onix, Goldeen and
Beedrill clear their owner (`itMainClearOwnerStats`), but
`itMainMakeMonster` copies the ball's owner, team, player and handicap back
after the maker, so their bodies keep `is_damage_all` and hit their thrower
while their weapons, which copy the item's owner, do not. Blastoise's maker
takes the ball's owner and team itself and faces its nearest opponent
before the lift. The `ITAttributes` are file 251 + 0x72C ... 0xC74 and
0x838 (`reloc_data.us.h`); a ROM test decodes all thirteen. Every Pokémon
is a light `Fighter` item with no hurtbox; Goldeen, Chansey, Koffing and
Mew start with the attack off.

The descriptor's `proc_update` ends the rise and sets the first status,
then counts `multi` down once more. That trailing decrement is visible:
Onix's fly status lasts 30 frames, Koffing makes 31 clouds (32 less one),
and after Onix's fly status `multi` wraps to 0xFFFF, so its first model
turn waits a frame longer.

## Opponent searches

Blastoise, Starmie and Hitmonlee walk the fighter link for the nearest
fighter (XY, squared) that is not the owner and not on the item's team
(US); a tie goes to the later fighter. With no such fighter the source reads
an uninitialised `victim_gobj`. The port then leaves the facing and the
target as they were (a deviation that no Training or VS battle reaches).
`OwnerView` gains the fighter's team.

## Clefairy

`itPippiCommonSelectMonster` draws `rand(12)` and runs entry `index` of
`dITPippiStatusProcList` on Clefairy, naming the kind `index + 32`. Every
`ip->kind == nITKindX` test in those statuses then fails: no voice, no
status animation, no display-list swap, the "other" spawn offsets, and
Snorlax's fall at 5.2 times rather than 4. Entry 11 is Mew's flight (kind
44's), run as Clefairy with the attack off.

## The floor test that does not stop

`itMapTestAllCheckCollEnd` (Goldeen, Charizard's fall, Chansey's appear and
fall) and `wpMapTestAllCheckCollEnd` (Onix's rocks) call
`mpProcessRunFloorCollisionAdjNewNULL`, which notes the floor line the
bottom crossed and does not move the object. Goldeen therefore bounces from
below the floor line, and a rock turns at a tenth of its speed on a new line
and then falls through it. `crate::map::floor_crossing` is that test; the
next `itMapTestLRWallCheckFloor` puts a Charizard or Chansey on the noted
line.

## Animation values gameplay reads

Beedrill leaves its appear status when its GObj animation frame reaches 51
(`ITSPEAR_SWARM_CALL_WAIT`); the script writes frame 51 on its 51st play and
ends on the next. The Hydro Pump's attack offset is its child's X times its
facing, and the Smog's attack radius is its child's X scale times 50
(`WPAttributes::size`, not halved). Both scripts are fixed, so the port
keeps them as `hydro_offset_x` (60 + 144.375 per play) and `SMOG_SCALE`;
a ROM test replays all three against file 86. The appear script every maker
adds (86 + 0x13624) only scales, and Charizard's attack script only turns
its root, so neither moves an item.

## Weapons

`MonsterShot` covers all ten item weapons (`monster_weapon::ShotKind`):
Saffron's two and the rock, coin, Charizard's flame, both swarms, Hydro
Pump, Swift and Smog, with their file-251 `WPAttributes` (a ROM test decodes
every field the port reads). Each callback follows the source: a coin and a
Swift die on any hit, shield, set-off or absorption with their effects; the
Hydro Pump and Charizard's flame survive them; the rocks, swarms and Smog
have none. Reflection takes the reflector as owner and player, runs the
kind's `proc_reflector`, then the damage bonus. The Smog's `can_shield` is
clear, so it reaches a shielding fighter's hurtbox. Clefairy's swarm has
`can_rehit_fighter`; its records clear after 16 updates
(`WEAPON_REHIT_TIME_DEFAULT`). Hits credit the weapon's player, and the
rocks and swarms make the victim's hit effect (`is_hitlag_victim`). The
item weapons' attacks sweep from their previous attack position, which is
not `position - velocity` for the standing Hydro Pump.

Onix waits until every rock it made has left the stage
(`itIwarkWeaponRockProcDead` counts them); each rock that meets a new floor
also counts toward its rumble. The weapon pool queues both events and
`ItemPool::observe_weapons` delivers them before the next item pass, with
the free weapon structs: a weapon maker fails without one, before it draws
anything, so a failed rock retries the next frame after drawing its
position and speed but not its facing.

## Chansey's eggs

`itMLuckyMakeEggProcUpdate` lays three eggs 30 frames apart through
`itManagerMakeItemSetupCommon` (the normal Egg maker, swirl and appear
spin), retries a failed make on the next frame, and counts on without
making one when Eggs are off. A hit while laying adds 4 frames to the wait.

## Drawing

All thirteen trees and the weapons' are already packed (file 86's scene
graphs). New in the pack: each maker's appear script on each tree and each
status script (`AnimDesc::ITEM` slots 3-15 and 16-28), the Hydro Pump's and
Smog's `anim_joints` (`WEAPON` slots 5 and 6), and the Hydro Pump's, Smog's
and Beedrill swarm's `p_matanim_joints` (86 + 0xFB70, 0x131E0, 0xE560;
0xE560 is the swarm's material table, at `WPAttributes + 0xC`). The weapon
trees take the weapon render seed (`wpDisplayDrawNormal`'s state).

`psp-game` draws each Pokémon under its makers' matrix kinds: most roots
are `TraRotRpyR` then 0x48 (camera-relative rotation, scaled by the `DObj`;
`Gpu::model_transform_camera_rotated_scaled`), Snorlax and Mew plain world
trees, Koffing's root kind 40 over a `TraRotRpyRSca` child, Chansey's child
kind 44 and Beedrill's 0x48. Goldeen's child has no matrix and draws under
its root. Onix, Blastoise and Hitmonlee swap in their attack lists. The
weapons replay their scripts from their play counts.

## N64 reference

A temporary decomp build booted Training on Dream Land with two Marios and,
at the end of `sc1PTrainingModeFuncStart`'s fighter loop, made a Poké Ball
109 above Mario + (-900, -400 and 600 in X) that Mario owns, made a Pokémon
from it with `itManagerMakeItemKind(..., ITEM_FLAG_COLLPROJECT |
ITEM_FLAG_PARENT_ITEM)`, copied the ball's stats as `itMainMakeMonster` does
and destroyed the ball: Meowth, Chansey and Blastoise, then Charizard,
Starmie and Koffing. The patch was reverted and the decomp rebuilt. The
`trainingpokemona`/`trainingpokemonb` scenes make the same Pokémon the same
way. The original decomp rebuild is restored to SHA-1
`e2929e10fccc0aa84e5776227e798abc07cedabf`. References remain local under
`~/ppsspp-test/re435data/`; PSP views under `~/ppsspp-headless-test/re435*`.

The views show Meowth's coins, Chansey's eggs, Blastoise's leftward Hydro
Pump, Charizard's flames, Starmie's Swift and Koffing's Smog. The models
and attack types agree qualitatively. They are not an aligned simulation
comparison: PSP scene A at tick 120 shows 30% against 36% in the N64
frame-119 reference; scene B shows 24% against 12% at N64 frame 120, with
another fighter trajectory. The earlier session observed different boot
phases; that does not establish the cause of these later differences.
Damage, hit ordering and trajectories still need an event-aligned trace.

## First weapon process

The completion gate caught a missing maker-time attack position. The new
`MonsterShot` initially left its attack history empty; its first process
therefore tested only the new centre. `wpManagerMakeWeapon` instead calls
`wpProcessUpdateHitPositions` before returning, changing New to Transfer;
the next main process changes Transfer to Interpolate and sweeps from the
maker's point. `MonsterShot::make` now records that point. A regression test
covers both Saffron weapons and the stationary Hydro Pump's first offset.
The Hydro Pump test's previous expectation of a collapsed first sweep was
corrected to this source behavior.

This also restores the existing Charmander golden exactly. Before the
fix, that control differed by 34,728 pixels, principally flame and hit
particles; after it, no existing targeted golden needs a rebaseline.
