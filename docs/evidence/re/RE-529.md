# RE-529 — Opening-movie models are lit by their display lists' lights, not the pack's fixed bake

Status: COMPLETE (PSP-2000, 6.61 ARK, PSPLink v3.2.1, `frame_timer` build of `opening@2900`, before/after A/B; PPSSPP goldens)
Topics: opening movie, lighting, sector scene, Yoster scene, hardware, performance
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-rom/src/anim_color.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/meshdraw.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/movie.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/pack.rs` (`shade_normal`)

## Question

The sector hull and the Yoster nest drew dark and grey against the N64 (sector MAE 32.5 at tic 132). Why?

## Findings

- A lit vertex with no runtime light scope draws the pack's bake (`shade_normal`): one fixed light direction (20,45) degrees, 0.35 ambient, light colours ignored (the RE-065 deviation).
- The original movie lists set their own lights. From an RDRAM dump, the sector ships (file 73, list 0x6000) write `G_MW_LIGHTCOL` before every part: LIGHT_1 ffffff (one part b3b3b3), LIGHT_2 (ambient) 808080. `mvOpeningSectorFuncLights` loads one light with direction (50,70,49), `ftDisplayLightsDrawReflect(45,45)`. The RSP shades 0.5 + n.l.
- Fix: `light_scope` takes `static_lights`; a LIT primitive that writes both LIGHT1_COLOR and LIGHT2_COLOR takes the existing CPU RSP lighting path (RE-514) with its literal registers. `set_scene_light` sets the flag for the movie's Persp camera; `begin_frame` clears it, so battle draws are unchanged.

## Hardware cost

PSP-2000, `frame_timer` build, `opening@2900` runs the whole opening from tic 0; per-report (60 frames) fps and busy time, before against after the change. The heavy spans (reports 4-10, 20-21) are the same scenes either way:

| Reports | FPS before | FPS after | busy before (us) | busy after (us) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4-10 | 29.9-36.7 | 30.5-38.5 | 17.8-21.7k | 17.2-20.3k |
| 20-21 | 33.5, 29.7 | 33.5, 29.8 | 24.8k, 28.7k | 23.7k, 28.4k |
| 22 | 46.7 | 46.3 | 12.6k | 12.7k |

No measurable cost: differences are within run-to-run noise. The scenes were not mapped to report numbers, so Yoster, Sector and Clash are inside these spans, not isolated. The earlier figures (RE-476, RE-499) of 45-57 FPS were taken at `opening@3000` onward and are consistent.

## Remaining

Battle stages still use the bake. Evaluating display-list lights there changes every battle golden and costs CPU; tracked in `PLAN.md`.

Caveat (RE-530): "no FPS change" holds, but its baseline and the heavy-spans note predate RE-514/RE-519 and were already the slow state (30-38 FPS); current spans and numbers are in RE-530.
