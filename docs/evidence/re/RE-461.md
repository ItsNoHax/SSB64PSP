## Question

How do the six menus that show and edit the backup behave, what do they
draw and in what order, and how can the PSP hold their sprites with about
6 MB free in the worst scene?

## Per-scene sprite packs

`romtool pack` writes `ssb64-menus.pak` beside the unchanged pack: an
index (`SMNU`, pack version, six offsets) and one ordinary pack per scene,
64-byte aligned. Exact-in-5551 images (the screen adjust guide) are 5551,
the rest 8888. The collage, fonts and portraits stay in the resident pack.

| Scene | Bytes |
|---|---:|
| Option | 498,304 |
| Screen Adjust | 295,184 |
| Backup Clear | 386,416 |
| Data | 415,632 |
| VS Record | 160,896 |
| Characters | 2,709,952 |

`assets::load_menu_pack` reads the index and only that scene's bytes when
the scene starts; the host frees the previous scene's buffer first and
drops it on leaving the menus, as `lbRelocLoadFilesListed` loads each
scene's files. The resident pack stays v103, byte for byte
(`96b70bba…690c`).

## PSP deviations

- Screen Adjust keeps, saves and restores the offsets but the PSP's LCD
  shows the whole frame, so the picture does not move.
- Option keeps and saves stereo or mono; the port has no mono mix.
- Menu sound effects and music are not played (scene audio is deferred).
- Characters' fighter plays each status's clip from the pack; the
  status's motion-script events (effects, colour animations including
  DamageE1's, model and texture parts, sounds) are not run. Win and Lose
  run their demo scripts' part events as in the selects.
- Sound Test is not ported: choosing it comes back to Data with its tab
  chosen. The title and attract demo are the port's Intro screen.
- The Japanese subtitles under the Option, Backup Clear and Data tabs are
  US-build empty GObjs and are not drawn.

## Verification

- Host tests: change waits, each menu's timing, navigation, writes and
  transitions, the backup-clear blink and double confirm, Sound Test
  gating, VS Record sorting, page turns and digit layout (100.0 and tenths),
  Characters' tables, locked pages and track runs; a cross-check walks every
  menu state and requires each drawn sprite to be in its scene's pack or the
  resident one and every scene-pack sprite to be drawn; a ROM test decodes
  every menu-pack sprite and LUT variant.
- N64 references: warp ROMs booting into Option and Data (decomp patched,
  reverted, ROM SHA-1 `e2929e10…` confirmed) captured Option, Screen
  Adjust, Backup Clear's list and confirm, Data, Characters and VS Record's
  three pages. PSP captures of `option`, `screenadjust`, `backupclear`,
  `datamenu` and `vsrecord` overlay them with mean channel differences of
  1–5 (edges only). `characters` matches the page layout; its random
  motion differs by design.
