`nFTKindNNess`) and Giant Donkey Kong (`nFTKindGDonkey`) need for the
campaign's stages 6, 10 and 12 to run as the original does?

TopN's scale was missing for every fighter before this record: the port
already scaled TransN's root motion and the held offset by `size`, and
divided `MakeAttackCollScaled` offsets by it, but neither drew the model
nor placed the joints at that scale. Kirby's width over Mario's on Kongo
Jungle's countdown is 1.43 on the N64 (f0696) and 1.48 on the PSP after the
fix; without it the ratio would be 1.82. Metal Mario's height over the
upper truss's width is 0.31 on the N64 and 0.33 on the PSP.

## Pack

v102 resolves every motion slot the variants' tables name (195 to 230
each, 6,246 clips in all; clips share their base's scripts), packs their
model and texture parts, low-detail entries and emblem and stock sprites.
`romtool` unions the part events of fighters sharing a graph (Giant Donkey
Kong wears Donkey Kong's, Polygon Luigi Polygon Mario's). A joint without
a `modelparts_desc` keeps its own list for any part (`ftParamSetModelPartID`).

## Intro camera and lights (batch 40)

- Intro cameras are kind 7 (fighters: view in the RSP modelview, no LookAt,
  so the ucode's zero LookAt gives constant texgen) and kind 15 (stage
  camera, priority 40, draws the VS opponents; `syMatrixModLookAtReflect`,
  LookAt = world right/up).
- With the view in the modelview the RSP transforms lights and LookAt by
  model * view: lighting acts in eye space. Proven by N64 ParaLLEl LookAt
  pokes into F3DEX2 DMEM (`0x8003f6b0` + `0x1f8` / `0x210`) and a kind 15 to 7
  ROM copy.
- Port: `RspCamera {Projection, ModelView, ModelViewReflect}` per draw;
  `DrawState::modelview_camera` folds the camera basis into the light rows
  and texgen columns (`meshdraw.rs` `rsp_rows`; `math.rs` `camera_basis`,
  `modelview_rows`). Fighter lit prims write both `G_MW_LIGHTCOL`
  registers themselves, so the scene ambient was not the cause.
- N64 compare (ParaLLEl frame 400 vs PSP @200): Polygons region mean N64
  (143.6, 80.6, 130.2), PSP (144.3, 80.2, 130.4). Metal Mario reflection
  pattern now matches; non-sky fighter mean N64 (105.5, 92.0, 91.8), PSP
  (98.5, 82.8, 81.9), previously (103.6, 94.1, 95.7).
- D-051 classification: lighting state (light rows, texgen columns), not
  chunking or batching. No intro golden exists and all 245 prior rows match
  by pixel hash after the change.
