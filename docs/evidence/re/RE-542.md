# RE-542 — Link-15 creation order across display and fighter effects; the ground Thunder Jolt's node 4 texture script

Status: COMPLETE (decomp source, raw ROM words, N64 warp-ROM cpufx-s frame traces and screenshots, PPSSPP goldens; pack v120; no hardware run)
Topics: effects, draw-order, pack, weapon, n64-trace, goldens
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/{effect_order,effect}.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/pack.rs`

## Question

RE-540 sorted the fighter-owned link-15 effects (shield, Yoshi shield, egg escape, magnet, sing, spin, punch, kick, reflector) by creation order. Three things were left:

1. The link-15 display effects (`Effects` pool: damage-fly orbs, sparks, dust, the Thunder Jolt burst, Thunder Shock, Vulcan Jab and the rest) and Samus's grapple glows still drew in a separate pass after the sorted fighter effects. Can one order cover both?
2. `resolve_one_mat_anim` declined the texture script of the ground Thunder Jolt's node 4 (file 342, MObjSub table at 0x1B68). Why, and does the N64 ever show the missing frame?
3. Effects made on the same frame by two fighters take port order on the PSP. Does the original's process order differ?

## Source

- **Link 15 is one list.** `gcDLLinkGObjTail` appends every priority-2 link-15 GObj, fighter-made or not, to one list; `gcDrawAll` draws it in that order. A display effect and a fighter effect drawn on link 15 are therefore ordered only by when their GObjs were made.
- **Within a frame.** `gcRunAll` runs `sGCProcessQueue` by priority, high first, and in GObj link order inside a priority:
  - fighters: `ftMainProcUpdateInterrupt` 5, `ftMainProcPhysicsMapDefault` 4, capture 3, search catch 2, search hit 1, params 0;
  - weapons, items and effects: main 3, hit search 1, hit collisions 0.

  Every fighter-owned link-15 effect is made from the fighter's priority-5 or priority-4 process (status changes, `proc_accessory`, motion events). Most display effects are made by priority-3 and lower processes (weapon updates, hit collisions, `efManager*ProcUpdate`). A fighter effect made on a frame so precedes that frame's display makes, and follows every earlier frame's.
- **Ground jolt node 4.** The node's MObjSub (342 + 0x1B68) has a texture script whose `sprites` array is `[0xC18, NULL, 0x408]`. The raw ROM words confirm the NULL in slot 1. `read_sprites` declined any table holding a NULL, so the whole script was dropped.
- **The NULL is never selected.** `wpPikachuThunderJoltGroundMakeWeapon` calls `gcSetAllAnimSpeed(0.5)`, which covers DObjs and MObjs, and `gcAddAnimAll` keeps the speed. Every push-frame restart (`wpPikachuThunderJoltGroundProcUpdate`) restarts the animation at anim frame 7.5. The material script's time therefore never passes 7.5, and the texture-index key that would select slot 1 is never reached. The N64 shows only frames 0 and 2.

## N64 evidence

- **One tail-appended list** (`~/ppsspp-test/cpufx/cpufx-{d,s,s3,u}.tsv`, warp-ROM copies of baserom SHA-1 e2929e10). The `DL 15` rows list the link-15 GObjs in draw order; the priority-2 entries are the effects.
  - cpufx-d el 41-61: Fox's reflector (`efManagerFoxReflectorProcUpdate`, made at el 31, a fighter effect) comes first; the display effects made after it (`NoEject` at el 41, `gcPlayAnimAll` at el 44) are appended behind it, and stay in that order when an earlier one is ejected (el 61).
  - cpufx-s el 57-63 and cpufx-s3 el 58-64: each new Thunder trail display is appended at the tail, behind the effect made at el 57; ejected entries leave the others' order unchanged (el 74-78).
  - cpufx-u el 31-89: the two `HaveStruct` effects, then `gcPlayAnimAll`, then the PK Thunder trail, each appended in make order.
- **Ground jolt anim time** (cpufx-s3 el 162-168): the ground jolt's anim frame in the `WP` rows goes 0.000, 0.500, ... and the weapon restarts it at 7.5.
- **Same-frame makes by two fighters.** No trace has two fighters making link-15 effects on one frame from different processes. The cpufx ROMs (`mkrom.py`) mirror controller 0 to every port, so all fighters act in the same process on the same frame. A capture would need a warp ROM with distinct per-port inputs.
- **Pikachu during SpecialN** (the PLAN item from RE-541). The N64 frames cpufx-s3 el 159-168 and cpufx-s el 166-174 show Pikachu opaque yellow; at cpufx-s el 170 the Thunder Jolt burst has gone. The PSP `f1-cpufx-jolt` capture (cpufx-s@561, el 162) shows Pikachu as a faint grey silhouette (the tail is see-through against the sky), the burst's blue spikes still large, and white arcs at the top left.

## Implementation

- **Merge (item 1).** `ssb_game::effect_order` keys every link-15 effect by the effects' `GObj` counter (`Effects.seq`):
  - a display effect by its make's `seq` (`display_key`);
  - a fighter effect by the counter's value at the end of the previous frame plus a sub-stamp (`CreationOrder::begin`, `CreationOrder::stamp`), so it sorts after every earlier frame's display and before its own frame's.

  `collect` merges both into one stably sorted list, clamped at `MAX_ENTRIES`. `Kind::GrappleGlow` joins the fighter kinds. A counter that goes back (a new match) restarts the base at 0. In `psp-game`, `draw_link15_effects` replaces the old `BattlePart::Effects15` tail, `draw_display_effects(..15)` and `draw_grapple_glows`: it draws the spin weapon graphic first (unchanged), then the merged list, then the link-15 particles as before.
- **Node 4 (item 2).** `resolve_one_mat_anim` falls back to `sparse_opening_sprites` for file 342 (`GROUND_THUNDER_JOLT_FILE`), as it already did for the opening files. The NULL slot gets the nearest sprite; it is unreachable on the N64. The pack moves to v120 with no layout change. Test: `ground_jolt_node_4_texture_script_attaches` (sprite offsets `[0xC18, 0xC18, 0x408]`).
- **Process order (item 3).** Not modelled. Same-frame fighter effects still take port order, then `Kind` order. Deferred in PLAN until an N64 trace with distinct per-port inputs exists.

## PSP deviation

None forced by the PSP. The fighter-effect key is reconstructed by the renderer from the counter, because fighter effects are `FxSlot`s rather than pool GObjs; the order it gives matches the original's for every case above except same-frame makes from two fighters' different processes.

## Verification

- Host tests: `cargo test -p ssb-game -- --test-threads=1` (1405 pass; `effect_order` has nine tests, among them `display_effects_merge_by_make_order`, `a_new_match_restarts_the_base`, `a_full_frame_is_clamped_to_the_output` and `order_is_stable_for_equal_keys`); `cargo test --release -p ssb-rom -p romtool` (all pass, with `ground_jolt_node_4_texture_script_attaches`).
- Both PSP builds succeed.
- Full matrix: 223 of 224 match, with no rebaseline. The one miss is `r2-mario-entry-pipe`, which is nondeterministic (RE-506). No golden captures two link-15 effects that overlap and change order under the merge, and none captures a frame where the node 4 script's texture differs from the one drawn before.

## Open

- **Process order** for same-frame link-15 makes by two fighters (item 3), pending a warp ROM with distinct per-port inputs.
- **Pikachu during SpecialN.** Not this batch's code path: the fighter draw (link 9) and its colanim fog, the colanim's `ShockSmall` makes (link 18) and the burst's timing. `FighterPikachuSpecialN` (set by `ftPikachuSpecialNProcAccessory` with `FTSTATUS_PRESERVE_COLANIM`) loops forever: three times (`SetColor1` 0,0,255,50; wait 1; `SetColor1` 255,255,255,90; wait 1; `ClearColorAll`; wait 1), then `Effect` kind 10 (`nEFKindShockSmall`) on joint -1 (a rotating effect joint), then `Goto` itself. `set_constant_fog` changes only RGB, so the translucency has another cause; the burst is large at el 162 because that is its first frame, not a timing difference (corrected by RE-543: the capture is el 162, not el 170).
