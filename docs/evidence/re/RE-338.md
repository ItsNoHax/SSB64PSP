# RE-338 — Captain Falcon motions and Dive capture placement

Status: COMPLETE (decomp source, host tests, both PSP builds, PPSSPP Falcon-fighter smoke; Captain Falcon not reachable in `psp-game`, physical PSP not captured)
Topics: fighter, gameplay, grab, capture, animation
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/captain.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/captain_attack.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/grab.rs`, `tools/gen-anim-table.py`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`

**Question.** How do Captain Falcon's US motions and Falcon Dive capture
callbacks place the two fighters, and which velocity does Falcon Kick scale
after contact?

**Source.** `235_CaptainMainMotion.c` supplies normal, special, catch and throw
motion events; `236_CaptainMain.c` names heavy-item joint 29.
`ftcaptainspecialn.c`, `ftcaptainspecialhi.c`, `ftcaptainspeciallw.c` and
`ftcommoncapturecaptain.c` supply the callbacks and per-victim offsets.
`ftcommonattack1.c` and `ftcommonattack100.c` supply the jab and rapid-loop
transitions. `dFTCaptainMotionDescs` maps statuses to figatrees.

**Findings.**

- Twenty common normal slots cover two jabs, dash attack, five forward tilts,
  three forward smashes, the other grounded attacks and five aerials. The
  third jab can enter a five-pulse rapid loop.
- Aerial Falcon Punch launches at motion frame 40 at up to 30 degrees from
  horizontal, damps velocity by 0.92 during its middle phase, then returns
  to drift and fast-fall physics. Falcon Kick's first six hit or shield
  callbacks halve `vel_scale`; its physics callback multiplies `vel_air`,
  including when the fighter is grounded. It does not scale `vel_ground`.
- Falcon Dive searches with two boxes at frame 13, removes the second at
  frame 14 and both at frame 45. An airborne victim is drawn toward Captain's
  heavy-item joint by at most 180 units per tick. A grounded victim stays
  still while Captain moves toward it by the same limit. The catcher then
  enters the throw phase and releases the victim with the move's descriptor.
- The generated table adds 35 Captain attack/special slots and fills his
  common grab/throw slots. Slot 255 is a looping figatree; the five motion
  pulses supply the gameplay loop clock.

**Deviations.** Fighter map collision is floor-only, so Falcon Kick wall
rebound and Falcon Dive cliff catch cannot fire. Capture searches use the
current root-sphere hurtbox instead of original per-joint hurtboxes. Effects,
sounds and rumble are not emitted by the portable move callbacks. Captain
Falcon is not selectable in `psp-game`.

coasting, both Dive capture-placement branches, and animation/throw slot

lengths agree with the decompilation. Both PSP builds pass. Pack v37 rebuilt
from the ROM at 23,037,360 bytes; SHA-256
`2629e02d8bddedf58def1a8b4159a0993b0bc550aba108074d677743504cfe2e`.
The Falcon-fighter PPSSPP golden matches. That neutral-pose capture does not
