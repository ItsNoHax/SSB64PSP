# RE-390 — The CPU player's Training behaviours drive the dummy

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: cpu-ai, gameplay, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/computer/{mod,behave,behave_tests,tests,scripts}.rs`, `tools/gen-computer-scripts.py`, `crates/ssb-game/src/map.rs`, `psp-game/src/play.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `tests/golden/f1-training-cpu-{walk,jump}.png`

## Question

The Training dummy stood still with neutral input. What does the
original's CPU do in Training, and how does it drive the fighter?

## Source

- **Behaviour.** `sc1PTrainingModeInitVars` makes the dummy a CPU at
  level 3. `sc1PTrainingModeUpdateDummyBehavior` sets its
  `trait = nFTComputerTraitNone` and a behaviour from the CPU menu:
  Stand, Walk, Evade, Jump or the full AI (`dSC1PTrainingModeDummyBehaviors`).
  The menu starts on Stand.
- **Frame.** `ftComputerProcessAll` counts `behavior_change_wait` down.
  While no command is running, it picks an objective through the
  behaviour's `proc_com` and follows it, then runs
  `ftComputerUpdateInputs`. That interprets the command byte script into
  `fp->input.cp`, which the fighter reads as its controller.
- **Objective status.** Every Training behaviour starts with
  `ftComputerGetObjectiveStatus`. It can answer on its own:
  - the ledge options from `CliffWait`;
  - the get-up options from `DownWait`;
  - leaving `Ottotto` and `SquatWait`;
  - a throw from `CatchWait`;
  - wiggling out of captures, eggs and sleep;
  - Kirby's inhale and Ness's PK Thunder aim.
  Otherwise it sends the fighter to recover when nothing is below it
  (`func_ovl2_800F8FFC`) or the Zebes acid is near. At level 3 or more it
  can tech the landing of a damage fall (`CounterAttack`).
- **The behaviours.** Stand stands, or walks home from `RebirthWait`.
  Walk paces to random points within ±2,500 of home, clamped to its
  floor. Evade keeps 2,500 units from the nearest threat, flipping at the
  floor's end. Jump alternates a target 1,100 above home with home every
  30–60 ticks.
- **Walking.** `ftComputerFollowObjectiveWalk` goes around ceilings and
  walls between the fighter and its target and dodges live item attacks
  and the Twister. It jumps toward higher targets, fast-falls, drops
  through platforms, and while recovering uses the double jump and, with
  a level-scaled chance, up special. Recovery aims come from a reachable
  floor edge (`ftComputerCheckSetTargetEdge*`) or from the lowest outer
  edges found at setup.

## Implementation

- **Input scripts.** `tools/gen-computer-scripts.py` expands the decomp's
  `FTCOMPUTER_EVENT_*` macros into the 49 input scripts, in the pointer
  table's order (which swaps 24 and 25), and the `nFTComputerInput*`
  names.
- **`ssb_game::computer`.** Ports the interpreter, the command setters
  and `ftComputerSetupAll`'s predictions and cliff scan.
- **`computer::behave`.** Ports the objective status, the five Training
  procs and the Stand, Walk, Evade, Recover and CounterAttack objectives.
  A `World` reports what the CPU reads: the map, the line extent
  (`gMPCollisionBounds.current`), the stage bounds, the stage and the
  opponents. Fourteen host tests cover it.
- **Map queries.** `map::line_edge` and `map::lines_of` add the edge and
  per-kind line queries the AI needs.
- **`psp-game`.** `play::Dummy` owns a `Computer` at level 3 with the
  Stand behaviour, set up on spawn. Its tick feeds the CPU's controller
  to the fighter, neutral while the VS countdown locks control. The
  `cpuwalk` and `cpujump` capture scenes pick Walk and Jump, standing in
  for the unported Training CPU menu.

## Deviations

- **Full AI.** `ftComputerProcDefault` (attacks, items, allies) is not
  ported. The VS CPU and the menu's full-AI option use Stand for now.
- **World view.** Items, the Twister and the Zebes acid level are not
  reported to the CPU.
- **Taunt.** The branch keyed on `attack_count` and `attack_knockback`
  is left out: the port does not count landed attacks.
- **Stand-ins.** The map body's top stands in for `damage_coll_size.y`.
  `coll_data.floor_dist` comes from the nearest floor projection.
- **Line crossings.** The `...LineCollisionSame` checks are plain segment
  crossings against lines of the same kind.

## Verification

- **Stand.** All 98 existing goldens still match twice, as the Stand CPU
  keeps the stick neutral where the old dummy did.
- **Walk and Jump.** At tick 200 in `cpuwalk`, `PPSSPPHeadless --log`
  reports the dummy in `WalkMiddle`; at tick 120 in `cpujump`, in `JumpF`.
  Their goldens were identical in both captures. The battle camera
  follows only the player, so the dummy is at the frame's edge.
- **Tests.** Workspace tests pass with an absolute `SSB64_ROM` and one
  test thread.
