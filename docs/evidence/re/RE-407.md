# RE-407 — VS team battles

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: match, combat, items, weapons, cpu, hud, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/{team,team_tests,combat,grab,link,weapon,ness_weapon,battle,battle_tests,dead,capture_kirby,players_vs}.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/item/{mod,hit,pk_fire,tests}.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/computer/{behave,behave_tests}.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`

## Question

The VS select saved team battles (RE-404), but every battle ran as a
free-for-all. Which searches does the team rule gate, what team does a
fighter, weapon or item carry, and what does the HUD colour by team?

## Source

- **The rule.** Every hit and catch search opens with
  `is_team_battle == TRUE && is_team_attack == FALSE && a->team ==
  b->team` (or its negation): `ftMainSearchHitFighter` (both the hurt
  and the clank branch), `ftMainSearchHitWeapon`, `ftMainSearchHitItem`,
  `ftMainSearchFighterCatch`, `itProcessSearchHitFighter`,
  `itProcessSearchHitItem`, `itProcessSearchHitWeapon` and
  `wpProcessSearchHitWeapon`. The item searches also let an
  `is_damage_all` item through. `dSCManagerDefaultBattleState` has
  `is_team_attack` FALSE; only VS Options changes it.
- **Teams.** `scVSBattleStartBattle` passes `players[i].player` as
  `FTDesc::team`: the port in a free-for-all, the team in a team
  battle (`mnPlayersVSSetSceneData`). Training passes `players[i].team`,
  0 and 1. `ftManagerInitFighter` (the rebirth) does not touch `team`.
- **Owners.** `wpManagerMakeWeapon` copies the parent fighter's,
  weapon's or item's team; `wpProcessProcHitCollisions` and
  `itProcessProcHitCollisions` give a reflected weapon or item the
  reflector's; `itMainSetFighterHold` the holder's;
  `itNessPKFireMakeItem` the spark's; `itMainClearOwnerStats` the
  default team 4.
- **Throws.** `ftParamSetThrowParams` sets `throw_gobj` and
  `throw_team` from the thrower. `ftMainSearchHitFighter` and
  `itProcessSearchHitFighter` count a thrown fighter's attacks for
  `throw_team`; the weapon and item clank branches test `throw_team`
  against the weapon's or item's team.
- **CPU.** The fighter walks of `ftComputerCheckFindTarget`,
  `ftComputerCheckEvadeDistance`, `ftComputerWaitGetTarget`,
  `func_ovl3_80135B78` and `ftComputerCheckSetEvadeTarget` skip
  `this->team == other->team` with no rule test. The item and weapon
  walks (`ftComputerCheckTargetItemOrTwister`, `func_ovl3_80135B78`,
  `ftComputerCheckFindItem`) test the rule.
- **HUD.** `ifCommonPlayerDamageInitInterface` colours each emblem with
  the stage's `emblem_colors[players[i].color]`, which the select sets to
  `dIFCommonPlayerTeamColorIDs[team]` in a team battle. The shield
  (`efManagerShieldProcDisplay`) is coloured by `fp->player`, the port,
  in any battle.
- **Sudden death.** `scVSBattleSetScoreCheckSuddenDeath` sums each
  team's `score - falls` and sends every member of a tied team on; the
  copied battle state keeps `is_team_battle`.

## Implementation

- **`ssb_game::team`.** `TeamRules { is_team_battle, is_team_attack }`
  with `spares(a, b)`, and `TEAM_DEFAULT`. The battle hands it to the
  searches: an argument of `combat::search_all`,
  `grab::nearest_catch`/`search_catch` and
  `link::apply_spin_attack_hits`, and a `team_rules` field of the
  weapon and item pools. There is no global.
- **Teams.** `Fighter::team` (the port by default, kept by
  `dead::reinit`); `WeaponSpawn::team` and a per-slot team in the weapon
  pool, inherited by trails and taken from the reflector; `Item::team`
  and `damage_team`; `PKFireSpawn::team`.
- **Throws.** The port's only throw pointer is Kirby's star
  (`combat::throw_port`). `CaptureKirbyState::thrower_team` holds
  `throw_team`, and `combat::hit_team` is `throw_gobj ? throw_team :
  team`.
- **Battle.** `Battle::with_teams` and `team_rules`; the team case of
  `sudden_death`.
- **CPU.** `computer::behave::is_opponent` filters `psp-game`'s
  opponent list.
- **`psp-game`.** `VsRules::team_rules` from the battle state; every
  fighter takes its `Entrant::team`; the pools take the rule; the
  emblems take `players[i].color`.
- **Golden.** `vsteam`: Mario and Kirby (red) against Fox and Donkey
  Kong (blue) in team costumes on Dream Land. From tick 935 Kirby's
  attack overlaps Mario for 14 ticks and passes through; the capture is
  at tick 940.

## Not ported

- `wpProcessSearchHitWeapon` (weapon against weapon) has no port yet.
- The CPU's item and weapon reports are empty in `psp-game`, so their
  filters have nothing to filter; `World`'s fields say which rule a host
  must apply.
- `ftCommonThrownProcStatus` also sets the throw pointer after a common
  throw, but the port skips `SetDamageThrown`, so a thrown fighter has no
  attacks and every read of that pointer is inert.
- VS Options (Team Attack on) and the player tags and magnifier, which
  also read `players[i].color`.

## Verification

- Host tests: team attack off skips a teammate's attack, weapon, item,
  item hurt and catch, and lands with it on and in a free-for-all; a
  star counts for its thrower's team; a reflected weapon takes the
  reflector's team; the CPU's target search skips a teammate; a team
  tie sends both teams to sudden death; a rebirth keeps the team. Each
  fails with its rule broken.
- `tools/golden.sh verify`: the 108 existing goldens match; the new
  `f1-vs-team` matches a second capture.
