# RE-354 — Weapon lifecycle and shield-hop callbacks

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp, host tests, both PSP builds and PPSSPP Training goldens; no physical PSP)
Topics: gameplay, weapons, combat, shield, camera, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/{weapon,camera}.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `tests/golden/f1-training-fireball.png`

## Question and sources

Close three weapon gaps from RE-333, RE-335 and RE-351: map-bound removal,
the Boomerang's off-camera removal, and `proc_hop` for every `can_hop`
weapon other than the Boomerang.

Sources: `src/wp/{wpprocess,wpmain,wpmanager}.c`, each weapon's
`WPDesc` and callbacks under `src/wp/wp*/`, `src/ft/ftmain.c`
(`ftMainUpdateShieldStatWeapon`), `src/ft/ftparam.c`
(`func_ovl2_800EB924`), `src/gm/gmcamera.c`, `src/sys/{vector,matrix,objman}.c`
and `guLookAtF` (`refs/sf64-psp/src/libultra/gu/lookat.c`).

## Findings

- `wpProcessProcWeaponMain` runs `proc_update`, adds `vel_air` (and ground
  line speed), then deletes a weapon strictly outside
  `map_bound_{bottom,right,left,top}` or with `|z| > 20000`, before
  `proc_map`. `proc_dead` may veto: only a held Egg (`is_throw` clear) does.
  Charge Shot clears an owner link that is always NULL; Boomerang, Pikachu
  Thunder and PK Thunder heads notify their owners.
- The Rust Mario/Luigi Fireball never moved in open air: its tick set the
  position only on a map contact. It had done so since the first Fireball
  port. The source moves every weapon by `vel_air`.
- `wpLinkBoomerangCheckOffCamera` runs only when the lifetime has not
  expired. It counts down `homing_delay` (130), then on every ninth frame
  projects `translate` through `gGMCameraMatrix`. The Boomerang is removed
  when the result is more than 40 pixels outside the viewport. The projection is
  `vscale / 4 * clip / w` with `|w|` clamped to 0.1. Every battle scene sets
  the viewport to `(10, 10, 310, 230)`, so the half extents are 150 and 110
  and `persp.aspect` is 300 / 220. `dGCPerspDefault.scale` is 1.
  `gGMCameraMatrix` is rebuilt when the camera draws, so the check reads the
  previous frame's camera. The lifetimes (160 tilt, 190 smash) outlast
  the delay.
- `wpProcessProcHitCollisions`: with shield damage, an airborne (`ga ==
  Air`) `can_hop` weapon whose `shield_collide_angle` is under 135° runs
  `proc_hop` with the angle less 90° (clamped at 0). Any other weapon runs
  `proc_shield`. `shield_collide_dir` is `{0, 0, ±x}` normalised, so each hop
  turns `vel_air` by twice the angle about ±Z (`syVectorRotateAbout3D`).
- Hop extras: the Blaster resets `scale.x` to 1. The Charge Shot and Bomb use
  `wpMainVelSetLR` (`x >= 0`), and the Star sets `lr` from `x > 0`. Fireball
  and Egg set model pitch, and Blaster, Star and PK Fire set model roll. These
  are presentation only. The air Thunder Jolt only turns.
- `proc_shield`: Fireball, Blaster, Charge Shot, Thunder Jolt, Star and
  PK Fire (`ProcAbsorb`) are destroyed. The Egg and Bomb explode
  (`ProcHit` returns FALSE). The Cutter flies on, and the Boomerang returns.
  An exploded Bomb has neither callback. A Bomb resting on a floor is
  `Ground` and explodes.
- `ftMainUpdateShieldStatWeapon` records the fighter in the weapon's attack
  record, so a hopped shot passes through that fighter afterwards.

## Implementation and deviations

- `WeaponPool::tick(surfaces, bounds)` deletes out-of-bounds weapons after
  each kind's tick, not between move and map. A map contact places a weapon
  on a stage surface, and surfaces lie inside the blast zone. Both orders
  therefore delete the same weapons, and the owner notifications match.
- `WeaponPool::observe_camera` stores the battle camera. `psp-game` calls it
  before `tick_camera`, which gives the previous frame's view.
  `Camera::project` builds `guLookAtF` (up +Y) and the fast perspective
  from `original_tan`. It ignores the rescale that
  `gmCameraLookAtFuncMatrix` applies when an element passes 32000. That
  rescale changes only the `|w| < 0.1` clamp. The projection uses the N64 battle viewport,
  not the PSP pillarbox.
- `Weapon::on_shield` handles every kind, PK Fire and the Bomb included. The
  attack-record check now reads the shared records for every kind.
- The Fireball now moves by `vel_air` when there is no contact.
