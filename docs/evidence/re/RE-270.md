# RE-270 — Physical confirmation of RE-262/264/269's Fox, Ness, Link and Donkey Kong fixes, and Dream Land's lighting refresh

Status: COMPLETE
Topics: fighter, hardware, lighting, visual-regression, stage
Relevant files: STATUS.md, assets/generated/ssb64.pak, a79b0aa9...5935f, 61b73f96...da44e, tools/compare-screenshot.sh, PLAN.md

**Question.** RE-262 (signed clamp UVs), RE-264 (Fox light-channel/Ness eye
texture/Dream Land Mario+Link lighting) and RE-269 (Donkey Kong padded-tile
decode) all had PPSSPP-software evidence only. `STATUS.md`'s hardware-state
note named these as R2's remaining physical requirement.

**Setup.** PSP Slim, firmware 6.6.1, ARK/Infinity, PSPLink v3.2.1,
`usbhostfs_pc`/`host0:` session. Rebuilt `assets/generated/ssb64.pak` from the
real ROM at current `main` (`a79b0aa9...5935f`, 28,531.3 KiB, matching
RE-269's own rebuilt size) and the plain feature-free EBOOT
(`61b73f96...da44e`).

**Method.** For each of `regression_capture_fox`, `_ness`, `_link`,
`_donkey_kong`, and plain `regression_capture` (Dream Land): built the PRX,
`ldstart`ed it over `host0:`, waited past the tick-240 freeze, checked
`exlist`/`thlist`, took a native 480x272 `scrshot`, killed the module by UID,
and `reset` before the next `ldstart` (project discipline, RE-212). Each
capture was upscaled 2x nearest-neighbour and diffed against its committed
PPSSPP golden with `tools/compare-screenshot.sh` at a generous pixel-count
threshold, then visually inspected.

**Result.** All five loads: zero exceptions (`exlist` empty), `main_thread`
alive. Differing-pixel counts (30,760–59,160 out of 522,240) are the same
edge-antialiasing/dither-noise shape RE-207/208/209/210/212 already documented
and, for two captures, PSPLink's own on-screen overlay text — not a solid
corrupted region. Visual inspection confirms: Fox's face and white gloves
render correctly under the corrected `GU_LIGHT0` state (RE-264); Ness shows
two smooth oval eyes, no inward spike (RE-263/264); Link's face renders
correctly under the signed-UV fix (RE-262) with correct shield/tunic colour;
Donkey Kong's wrist/ankle seams show no black rectangles (RE-269); Dream
Land's stage and Mario's lighting match the golden. PSPLink was reset after
every kill; the session ended on the rebuilt plain feature-free EBOOT.

**Conclusion.** RE-262, RE-264, and RE-269 are now confirmed on physical PSP
hardware, not just PPSSPP software. `PLAN.md` R2's Fox/Ness/Link/Donkey Kong
rows in "representative fighters render" and the RE-264/269 physical-
confirmation notes in `STATUS.md` are closed. PSP-1000 coverage,
long-duration/exhaustive matrix coverage, and the depth-mask-diagnostic
physical replay remain open and are deliberately out of scope for this
session (current work targets the asset viewer, not the full hardware
matrix).

**Confidence: high; zero exceptions, matching golden shape, and per-fighter
visual inspection all agree across five independent loads.**
