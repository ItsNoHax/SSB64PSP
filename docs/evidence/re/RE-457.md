campaign's thirteenth stage to run as the original does, from the intro
camera through the defeat's fade?

## Deviations

- `proc_update` and `proc_interrupt` run from `Fighter::tick_interrupt`
  (they read the map), in the source's order.
- `ftPhysicsSetAirVelTransN` reads the runtime's TransN sample delta; the
  Appear status places TopN at TransN's sampled translation.
- The defeat zoom centres on `Fighter::damage_joint_id`, recorded as
  `ftMainProcessHitCollisionStatsMain` does (fighter, weapon, item hit:
  the struck joint; ground: 0). `onepbossdefeat` seeds 300% without a hit,
  so its zoom centres on joint 0; a real hit (N64: joint 20) zooms on it.
- The boss stock snap runs on the first interface update after the defeat
  init; the magnifiers are hidden until "Go" and through the defeat
  (`Battle::is_magnify_display`).
- `is_use_fogcolor` scales the shade of the lit prims by the grey level
  (`DrawState.fog_env`), as file 344's 25 lit prims read ENV; the one unlit
  prim does not. While `colanim.color2` is active
  (`ftdisplaymain.c:1180`) ENV is color2 RGBA (`DrawState.fog_env:
  Option<[u8;4]>`, `shade::env_color`, `rsp_light::combined_times_env`).
  The only SetColor2 script (`dGMColScriptsFighterBossYubideppou3`) is
  opaque white: effect <= 1 LSB; alpha not routed; no N64 capture. D-051:
  lighting state.
- The comets' tint: the pack seeds PRIM/ENV commands into graph 0x8960
  (file 114) so the combiner folds to TEXTURE_BLEND; the runtime sets the
  per-comet colours. Wallpaper matanims are bound in the pack
  (`mobj_script`); graph 0x11268, shared by wallpapers 2 and 3, carries
  wallpaper 3's script as `extra_scripts` and is restarted per effect.
- Gaps: wallpapers 1 and 2 (damage 90 and 180) and the defeat wallpaper
  have no N64 capture to compare (host test only); no hit-landing defeat
  fixture or boss golden row.
