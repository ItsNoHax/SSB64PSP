## Question

campaign's thirteenth stage to run as the original does, from the intro
camera through the defeat's fade?

`stock_damage_all` equals `percent_damage` (`ftmain.c:1463`), so the

## Deviations

- `proc_update` and `proc_interrupt` run from `Fighter::tick_interrupt`
  (they read the map), in the source's order.
- `ftPhysicsSetAirVelTransN` reads the runtime's TransN sample delta; the
  Appear status places TopN at TransN's sampled translation.
- The defeat zoom centres on joint 20 (the first hurtbox), not
  `damage_joint_id`, which the port does not record.
- `is_use_fogcolor`'s darkening of the background attacks is not drawn.
- The effects' material animations do not play, and the comets' ENV tint
  is not reproduced by the baked combiner: their lines draw white
  (N64 reference: coloured).
- No audio; the boss stock snap effect is not ported. The magnifiers are
  hidden during the boss wait only (the source hides them until "Go" in
  every battle).
