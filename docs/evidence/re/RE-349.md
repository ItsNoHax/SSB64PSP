# RE-349 — Combat audit: hurtboxes, hit application, damage velocity, hitlag

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, merged host/ROM tests, PSP builds and PPSSPP smoke; authored hurtbox reach and physical PSP remain)
Topics: combat, gameplay, hit resolution, physics
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/attack.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/hurtbox.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/physics.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/fighter.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/status.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/weapon.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/grab.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`

**Question.** Where does the ported hit path disagree with `ftmain.c`
(`ftMainSearchHitFighter`, `ftMainUpdateDamageStatFighter`,
`ftMainProcessHitCollisionStatsMain`, `ftMainProcParams`,
`ftMainProcPhysicsMap`) and `ftcommondamage.c`?

**Findings and fixes.**

| Area | Port before | Source | Now |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hurtbox | One 75-unit sphere at the fighter's feet, always a middle hit | Up to 11 joint boxes (`FTAttributes::damage_coll_descs`); the hit position goes into joint space and is tested against `size + radius / scale` either side of `offset` (`gmCollisionTestRectangle`); the first box hit gives the `placement` column | `crate::hurtbox` with Mario-through-Captain US tables; Hi/N/Lw column from `placement`. Catches test only `is_grabbable` boxes. The egg uses `dFTCommonYoshiEggDamageCollDescs` on TopN. Root sphere when no table/joint is available; Kirby/Pikachu/Jigglypuff/Ness tables remain deferred |
| Knockback | `recent_damage = 0` | `recent_damage = damage_queue`, which already holds this hit (`ftMainCheckGetUpdateDamage` runs first) | Captured damage passed as `recent_damage` for fighter and weapon hits |
| Crouch | Full knockback | `Squat`/`SquatWait`: knockback × 2/3 before resistance | Ported |
| Percent | `saturating_add` to 65535 | `ftParamUpdateDamage` caps at 999 | `Fighter::add_damage` everywhere |
| Facing | Kept | `lr = damage_lr` | Turns to `damage_lr` |
| Grounded hit | Always grounded Hi/N/Lw status, velocity added as-is | Knockback with any component along the floor normal launches into the air with the ground-table status (`jumps_used = 1`); a tumble launches, bouncing at 0.8 when more than 100° into the floor; otherwise `vel_damage_ground` slides along the floor | Ported; `Status::keeps_situation` lets `Damage{Hi,N,Lw}1-3`/`DamageAir1-3` exist on either side and land or fall without changing status (`mpCommonUpdateFighterKinetics`) |
| Damage velocity | Constant for hitstun, then snapped to zero | Independent of hitstun; decays 1.7/frame along its angle in the air, by `material × traction × 0.25` on the ground (`ftMainProcPhysicsMap`) | `physics::update_damage_velocity` each non-hitlag frame |
| Hitstun physics | Drift and fast-fall | `ftCommonDamageCommonProcPhysics`: gravity and air friction only until hitstun ends | Ported for the damage family |
| `DamageAir1-3` exit | `DamageFall` | Common damage procs: `mpCommonSetFighterWaitOrFall` → `Fall` | Ported; only the `DamageFly*` family ends in `DamageFall` |
| Shield | Health could reach zero in `GuardSetOff`, breaking only after the stun | `ftMainProcParams` breaks the shield the frame health reaches zero | `ShieldBreakFly` at once |
| Weapon direction | By position | `damage_lr` from travel when `|vel.x| >= 5`; `shield_lr` always from travel | `HitDirection::from_weapon` |
| Hitlag | Never set | `ftParamGetHitLag` (US `damage / 3 + 5`, crouch × 2/3) on the defender (`damage_lag`/`shield_damage`) and attacker (`attack_damage`/`attack_shield_push`) | Set on both; the runtime skeleton clock pauses while frozen |

**Not changed.** Other elements, rebound and authored shield damage (`sd`)
remain gaps; sleep and ground/air gates exist in the later fighter ports.
Smash DI is not ported: frozen fighters skip map collision in
the port, so the nudge could cross a floor. `DamageFlyRoll` (RNG), the
`Down` bounce chain, multi-hit logs per frame, `knockback_resist_passive`
remain gaps. Kirby Stone's damage resistance is supplied by RE-343.

**Verification.** Host tests for each row (hurtbox transform, placement,
grabbable boxes, knockback with the hit's damage, crouch, 999 cap, launch,
slide and bounce branches, decay in air and on ground, shield break,
weapon direction, hitlag frames, DamageAir exit). `ssb-game` also builds
`no_std`. Hurtbox scale and joint indices assume `joint_transforms`
carries the source world matrices, as the hitbox path already does
(RE-330); this needs a ROM run, PPSSPP and hardware to confirm reach
against the original.

**Merge integration.** Remote commit `3889d7d` originally used RE-343;
that ID already names Kirby's moveset on the other branch. Its combat record
is preserved here as RE-349. Both histories are retained by a merge commit.
The reconciled hit path keeps sleep, Stone, Rest/star intangibility, Ness
invincibility and later fighter callbacks while adding joint hurtboxes,
captured damage, direction and hitlag. Weapon direction reaches the new
Thunder Jolt, Cutter and PK paths too; stationary trails use zero X speed.
The Ness bystander-throw test now includes the hitlag pause while still
requiring release at animation frame 27 and exact 16/10 victim/bystander
damage. Kirby's zero-knockback throw branch uses the shared 999% cap.

**Merged verification.** 1,120 workspace tests with the local US `SSB64_ROM`;
Rust 1.98 workspace/all-target Clippy with warnings denied, rustfmt and
no-default-features build. Both PSP release builds and `psp-game`
`strict_render` pass. PPSSPPHeadless `f1-training-fireball` matches the
existing golden (zero differing pixels). Pack v38 is unchanged. This checks
integration, not authored hurtbox reach against the running original.
No physical-PSP validation or full golden matrix is claimed.
