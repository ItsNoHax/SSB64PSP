## Question

## Batch 39: 1P stages 6 and 8, facing and same-frame interrupts

- 1P stage 6 and 8 CPU replays bit-exact t391-1500 (`castle_replays.rs`, 4 1P replays pass).
- Fixes (each test fails on revert):
  - team-damage RNG draws (`behave.rs`);
  - Link trait hold-back (`attack.rs`);
  - special-N reversal negates `vel_ground` (`ftParamSetStickLR`);
  - DK SpecialNLoop cancel runs Wait's interrupt the same frame (`ftDonkeySpecialNLoopProcUpdate` -> `mpCommonSetFighterWaitOrFall`), also in Kirby copy;
  - dead chain order (`ftCommonDeadInitStatusVars` -> `ftCommonDeadUpdateScore`): break-anim rand per glyph, StockSnap, -1/+1 score effects, explode effect, all in the fighter's process (RE-413, RE-464).
- Deviation: KO particles and fall-interface effects pass through `game_static!` globals (`ko::PENDING`, `hud` FALL_*), because a 56 B `FallInterface` in `Effects` and a 64 B particle field in `Fighter` raised the deepest stack; the globals keep it at 392440 B.
- Audit, same-frame interrupt: `ftmain.c:1499-1506` reads `proc_interrupt` after `proc_update`, so a status set from `proc_update` (animation end or timer) runs its interrupt that frame. Fixed: Fox SpecialN (`ftFoxSpecialNProcUpdate`), Samus SpecialLw ground and charge full (`ftSamusSpecialNLoopProcUpdate`), Captain Attack13 and SpecialLw (`ftCommonAttack13ProcUpdate`, `ftCaptainSpecialLwDecideSetEndStatus`), Rebound, FuraFura, FuraSleep (`ftCommonReboundProcUpdate`, `ftCommonFuraSleepProcUpdate`), RebirthWait -> Fall (`ftcommonrebirth.c:173-184`), cliff phase-2 end (`ftCommonCliffCommon2ProcUpdate`). `proc_map` (Ottotto) and `proc_interrupt` sites unchanged. Kirby-copy loops gained the escape check (`ftkirbycopysamusspecialn.c:166-175`, `ftKirbyCopyDonkeySpecialNLoopProcInterrupt`).
- Audit, facing: the port's `vel_ground.x` is world, the original's is facing-relative. `lr` written alone -> `status::set_lr` reverses `vel_ground.x`; `lr` and `vel_ground` negated together (Turn, TurnRun, LiftTurn, `ftcommonthrow.c:20-22`, `ftdonkeythrowfturn.c:24-31`) -> flip `facing` alone. Applied: SetStickLR (AttackS4, turn fsmash, Mario SpecialHi), Fox SpecialLwTurn, item-throw turn (`ftCommonItemThrowUpdateModelYaw`), hammer turn (`ftCommonHammerTurnUpdateModelYaw`), Kirby SpecialNTurn end (`ftKirbySpecialNWaitUpdateLR`), Fox reflect hit (`ftFoxSpecialLwHitSetStatus`), Ness absorb (`ftNessSpecialLwProcAbsorb`); Ness blast `goto setdown` precedes the `lr` write (`ftnessspecialhi.c:750-773`), so grounded down-bounce keeps both. Throw turns flip facing alone; DK ThrowFTurn now consumes `motion_script.flags[1]`. Airborne writes (landing recomputes, `mpcommon.c:884`) and overwritten velocities checked, no change.
- Red silhouette (CPU Mario at the vsshield Fireball set-off, capture 574/575) is N64 behaviour: Mario(SpecialN) runs colanim 39 (`dGMColScriptsFighterMarioSpecialN`, `SetColor1 FF0000FF`), drawn with `G_RM_FOG_PRIM_A`, which blends to fog red at alpha 255; Mario's parts carry no NOFOG flag.
