# RE-389 — The VS battle's game status, countdown, timer, KO credit and sudden death

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: match, gameplay, fighter, visual-regression, psp-platform
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/battle.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/battle_tests.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/combat.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/status.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/dead.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`, `tests/golden/f1-vs-countdown.png`, `tests/golden/f1-vs-sudden-death.png`

## Question

What runs a VS battle from the countdown to the results, and who gets
credit for a KO?

## Source

- **Frame gate.** `scVSBattleFuncUpdate` calls
  `ifCommonBattleUpdateInterfaceAll` every frame. By
  `gSCManagerBattleState->game_status`:
  - Wait and Go run `gcRunAll`.
  - End pauses every GObj but the interface
    (`ifCommonBattleInterfaceProcUpdate`) and falls through to
    `ifCommonBattleBossDefeatUpdateInterface`. That counts the restore
    wait down, then `ifCommonBattleInterfaceProcSet` sets Set with three
    more ticks before the next scene loads.
- **Countdown.** `ifCommonEntryAllThread` first runs on the scene's first
  frame and sleeps 90 ticks. It then makes `ifCommonCountdownThread`,
  which says "3", "2" and "1" at its timer's 120, 180 and 240. At 300 it
  says "Go": `ifCommonAnnounceGoSetStatus` unlocks every fighter
  (`ftParamUnlockPlayerControl`) and sets Go. That is frame 391.
- **Timer.** `ifCommonTimerFuncRun` counts scheduler tics only while Go.
  The first Go frame resets the count, so time starts on the second.
  With `SCBATTLE_GAMERULE_TIME` and a limit other than 100 (infinite),
  `time_remain` runs down from `I_MIN_TO_TICS(time_limit)`. At 0,
  `ifCommonAnnounceTimeUpInitInterface` sets End with a 90-tick restore
  wait.
- **KO credit.** `ftCommonDeadUpdateScore` credits `damage_player` with
  the KO (`score`, `total_kos_players`), or counts a self-destruct. A
  stock battle takes a stock, and `ifCommonBattleUpdateScoreStocks` gives
  a team with none left the next place from the last. When one team is
  left, `ifCommonAnnounceEndMessage` sets End with 90 ticks.
- **`damage_player`.** `ftMainProcessHitCollisionStatsMain` sets it from
  the winning hit:
  - the attacker's player for a fighter;
  - the owner for a weapon or item, unless the owner is the victim, which
    sets `GMCOMMON_PLAYERS_MAX`;
  - unchanged for acid, `MAX` for the other stage attacks.

  `ftMainSetStatus` clears it on a grounded status without
  `FTSTATUS_PRESERVE_DAMAGEPLAYER`. The damage, wall-damage and
  shield-break setters and the capture exits keep it.
- **Start facing.** `scVSBattleGetStartPlayerLR` faces each fighter toward
  the nearest other player's spawn.
- **Sudden death.** `scVSBattleStartScene` then runs
  `scVSBattleSetScoreCheckSuddenDeath`. A time battle whose top
  `score - falls` is tied starts a stock battle for the tied players, with
  no stocks and every fighter at 300% (`scVSBattleStartSuddenDeath`,
  `is_skip_entry`). `ifCommonSuddenDeathThread` says "Go" after 90 ticks.
- **Defaults.** `dSCManagerDefaultBattleState` is a time battle, 3
  minutes, `stocks = 2`.

## Implementation

- **`ssb_game::battle`.** Holds the game status, the frame gate
  (`begin_frame` → Run/Frozen/Done), the countdown clock, the timer,
  placement, KO credit (`on_fall`), the winner, the start facing and the
  sudden-death battle. Nine host tests cover it.
- **`Fighter::damage_player`.** Set from a new `HitLogEntry::attacker`,
  with `Preserve::DAMAGE_PLAYER` on the seven setters the source marks.
  `dead::DeadState::scored` reports each `ftCommonDeadUpdateScore`.
- **`psp-game`.**
  - The second menu entry is VS. It uses the character select and the
    stage select (`maps_vsmode_gkind`).
  - The battle runs on the Training world. The CPU pick is the second
    fighter, facing by the VS rule.
  - The player's input is neutral until "Go", and stage controllers see
    `started` only after it.
  - A frozen frame skips the world.
  - A tie goes to sudden death on the same stage; otherwise a results
    screen lights the winner's slot, and A returns to the menu.
- **`run`.** The per-frame block moved out of `run`
  (`training_frame`, `start_sudden_death`). `run` is 118 KB.

## Deviations

- CPU AI is not ported, so the second fighter never moves.
- The character select is the Training one, standing in for
  `mnPlayersVS`. The rules are the defaults; there is no VS mode menu.
- VS fighters skip the entry and Appear statuses (`is_skip_entry`). Those
  play from 32-bit `AnimJoint` clips the pack does not carry yet.
- There is no pause menu (START does nothing in VS), countdown or timer
  sprite, announcer or `mnVSResults` screen.
- The timer counts one tic per frame. The source counts scheduler tics,
  which match at full frame rate.

## Verification

- **`vs`.** At tick 300, `PPSSPPHeadless --log` reports status `Wait`
  with 10,800 ticks left. The `f1-vs-countdown` golden shows Mario and the
  Mario dummy during the countdown.
- **`vstimeup`.** This one-minute battle runs out at tick 3,999 with no
  KOs, a tie. Its sudden death starts, and at tick 4,200 the log reports
  status `Go` with the dummy at 300%. `f1-vs-sudden-death` shows it.
- **Goldens.** Both new goldens were identical in both captures, and all
  98 goldens match twice.
- **Tests.** Workspace tests pass with an absolute `SSB64_ROM` and one
  test thread.
