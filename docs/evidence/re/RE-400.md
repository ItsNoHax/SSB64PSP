# RE-400 — The VS results' rankings and exit

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless golden captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: menu, match, visual-regression, psp-platform
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/{results,results_tests}.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`, `tests/golden/f1-vs-no-contest.png`

## Question

The results screen lit `Battle::winner()` and left on any A or START
(RE-389). How does `mnVSResults` rank the players, and when does it let
them go?

## Source

- **Kind and exit wait** (`mnVSResultsInitVars`). The rule and
  `is_team_battle` pick Time or Stock, Royal or Team. A pause-menu reset
  (`is_reset`) is No Contest. START may leave after 410 ticks in a time
  battle, 370 in a stock battle and 200 with no contest
  (`mnVSResultsCheckExit`). The next scene is `mnPlayersVS`.
- **Points** (`mnVSResultsInitRankings`). KOs are `score` and TKOs are
  `falls`, each capped at 999; points are KOs less TKOs.
- **Places.**
  - A stock battle takes the battle's places.
  - A time battle orders players by points (`mnVSResultsOrderResults`),
    teams by their summed points. In sudden death, equal points are
    broken by the battle's place. Equal points, or in sudden death equal
    places, share a place.
  - No contest puts everyone in place 0.
- **Winner** (`mnVSResultsGetWinPlayer`).
  - Free-for-all: the first port in place 0.
  - Teams: the first-place player with the most points, then the most
    KOs, marking shared winners. The source's shared-winner loop compares
    KOs without checking presence, and the port keeps that.

## Implementation

- **`ssb_game::results::Results`.** Ports the kind, the capped counts, the
  points, the places, the winner and the exit wait. Four host tests cover
  it.
- **`psp-game`.**
  - The results screen is built from `Results` (`make_results`). Its
    plain slots stand taller for a better place and light the winner and
    any shared winner.
  - START after the wait goes to the character select.
  - A pause-menu reset now reaches no-contest results, as the source does.
    RE-398 had sent it to the menu.
  - The new `vsnocontest` capture pauses at 500, resets at 520 and
    captures at 560.

## Deviations

- **Presentation.** The results' fighter models, poses, text, numbers,
  confetti, wallpaper, win BGM and announcer are not drawn or played.
- **Other results work.** Backup saving, unlock messages and auto-handicap
  are not ported.
