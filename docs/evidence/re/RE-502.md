# RE-502 — Gameplay square root is `sqrt.s` everywhere; PSP, host and N64 agree bit for bit

Status: COMPLETE (PSP-2000, 6.61 ARK, PSPLink v3.2.1, PRX b0582e87…; PPSSPP; host)
Topics: hardware, determinism, performance
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-engine/src/math.rs`, `crates/ssb-engine/src/math/sqrt_golden.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/particle.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/psp_texture.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/sqrt_selftest.rs`

## Question

Does the port's square root equal the original's, on the PSP and on the host?

## Findings

- Original: libultra `sqrtf` is a leaf `sqrt.s` (`src/libultra/gu/sqrtf.s`,
  `smashbrothers.us.yaml:180`), correctly rounded single precision; every
  game caller goes through it.
- Port before: device `ssb_engine::math::sqrt` and copies in `ssb-rom`
  `particle.rs` and `psp_texture.rs` were a 4-step Newton-Raphson, wrong by
  1 ULP on 25.0% of normal inputs (62,320 of 249,001) and on 1,506 of 4,096
  denormals. The host used `std` sqrt (unguarded), so PSP differed from host.
- Now: one `sqrt_exact` (`sqrt.s` on the PSP, `std` on the host, `libm` on
  other no_std targets) and one guarded `sqrt` (v <= 0 gives +0 on every
  target, RE-469). `quart_sum` stays unguarded like the decomp.
- Hardware self-test over 257,227 inputs (`ssb_engine::math::sqrt_golden`):
  0 wrong; output hash 0x86fb3ce827c2930e on PSP, host and PPSSPP. Extends
  RE-497 to denormals (not flushed), +-0, +-inf, NaN (both encodings) and
  negatives. Sticky flags afterwards: invalid only. FCR31 under PSPLink is
  0x00000e00. Re-enabling FPU exceptions with `ctc1` while the invalid cause
  bit is latched traps at the `ctc1` (PSPLink "Bus error (instr)", Cause
  0x10000018); clear the cause with an exact FPU op first.
- Cost: 0.53 us (Newton-Raphson) against 0.097 us (`sqrt.s` plus guard) per
  call on the PSP-2000. Golden routes make 30-140 calls per tick: 27-60
  us/frame, under 0.4% and below link-order noise. No FPS claim.
- Goldens: a probe over all 201 scenes saw 400,608 of 1,601,911 calls differ
  in 171 scenes, yet only `r2-metal-mario-fighter` changed (2 native pixels,
  in the texgen acos polynomial path, rebaselined; not compared with an N64
  capture). Gameplay scenes are pixel-identical.

## Limits

`sin_cos`, `atan2`, `tan` and `acos` still differ between PSP, host and N64
(host uses `std`, the N64 libultra); whole-route PSP == host equality is not
established by sqrt alone.
