# RE-538 — Effects fidelity: weapon camera interests, Spin weapon graphic, FxSlot materials, egg scale, Kirby costume and particles

Status: COMPLETE (PPSSPP goldens and N64 pixel captures of the cpufx scenes; pack v119; no hardware run)
Topics: effects, camera, kirby, yoshi, link, n64-trace, goldens
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/camera.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/weapon.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/effect.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/kirby.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/costume.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/skeleton.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`

## Question

After RE-536 the cpufx scenes still differed from N64 pixel captures. Which differences are missing behavior, and which are not gaps?

## Findings

1. Weapon camera interests (`gmCameraUpdateInterests` weapon loop) were unported. `camera::calculate_interest_scaled` now takes up to 8 weapons: each is clamped to the bounds, then to the fighters' extents +-1000, and widens the box by 1000. The loop runs only when fighters exist. `weapon::camera_follow_positions` supplies Boomerang, Egg (also the held egg) and the PK Thunder head unless reflected. Whole-frame MAE vs N64, cpufx-u el70 61.3 to 38.4, el80 62.7 to 26.7; f1-cpufx-up el66 60.1 to 23.5. Residual: the PSP zooms out later at el56-70.
2. Link Spin Attack weapon graphic: the `wpLinkSpinAttack` DObj tree (file 324 + 0x11908, anim 0x11990, matanim 0x11A40) is drawn at weapon link 14, before link-15 effects. Pack v119 adds weapon anim slot 7. Transform and alpha timing match the N64 MObj/DObj trace; the band stays less saturated orange than N64 at el48-52 (crop MAE el46 29.4 to 23.0, el56 37.1 to 31.5).
3. FxSlot material phase: MObjs take the creation play together with the DObj, as `gcPlayAnimAll` does, for every slot with both an anim and an object (magnet, sing, spin swirl, kick, Thunder Jolt, PK Thunder). Visible effects: PK Thunder head shows the purple ring; Magnet improves; Sing is unchanged. The orange egg explosion in f1-training-yoshi-explosion (hash aebfb6f9) is not from this lockstep: it matches the N64 (anim 68: N64 mean (235,185,66), PSP (234,182,67)). Its cause is frame 0 of Yoshi particle bank script 3 (`efManagerYoshiEggExplodeMakeEffect`) now landing on the N64 timing after the egg and camera fixes; the old golden exploded a few frames early.
4. Yoshi egg scale comes from the figatree joint 3 YRotN scale (`ANIM_FLAG_YROTN`, `skeleton::trailing_runtime_pose`, `egg_joint_scale`): N64 S(1,1.5,0.8) at el38, S(1,1.27,0.67) at el42. Drawn X by scale.x, Y by scale.y; also fixes the throw spawn and the camera interest.
5. Kirby port-1 costume: `costume::vs_free_costumes` ports `mnPlayersVSGetFreeCostumeRoyal` (a repeated fighter takes the next free costume).
6. Kirby particle bank 3 (`particles_unk0`) and Inhale wind specks (`efManagerKirbyInhaleWindMakeEffect`, script 0xC, offset (lr*800, 230)): the effect follows the fighter anchor, is guarded by owner and users_num, and the struct is kept on failure as in C. flag0 is set by the Inhale loop script. Specks appear at el62-80 on the facing side; Kirby crop MAE about 18.5.
7. Not gaps (no code change):
   - Magnet tint: an RDRAM display-list and FTStruct walk (el49-70) shows Ness colanim 63 with fog alternating 00ffff8c/ffffff8c; the PSP gives [0,255,255,140], identical. Fox 00ffff50 (colanim 41) matches. The visible difference is Rice omitting the fog blender.
   - Falcon Punch phase: both versions create the flame at Captain anim frame 42 and remove it at 55; the offset is a global ~2 el phase of that scene.
   - cpufx-k framing: best-fit scale vs N64 is 1.00-1.01 at el30-90; the 4% gap does not reproduce.
8. `r2-mario-entry-pipe` fails at base 8c32d3e as well (RE-506: its hash is not a function of the code alone); not rebaselined.

## Conclusion

Eight PPSSPP goldens rebaselined: f1-cpufx-up, -special, -kirby; f1-training-link, -link-spin, -yoshi, -ness-thunder, -yoshi-explosion. Open items are in PLAN.md.
