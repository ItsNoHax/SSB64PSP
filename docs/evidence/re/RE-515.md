# RE-515 — Training stock icons, and the 2D sprite edge fringe

Status: COMPLETE (icons); fringe measured, fix open in PLAN.md
Topics: training, hud, rendering, sprites
Relevant files: `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/hud.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/training.rs`

## Question

Does the N64 draw stock icons next to the damage displays in Training, and how do the PSP's match?

## Findings

- Training starts from the default battle state: TIME rule, 2 stocks, `is_single_stockicon` TRUE (`dSCManagerDefaultBattleState`). N64 RDRAM read every frame from el 0 gives `is_single_stockicon` 1 1 and `stock_count` 2 2 for both players. One icon per player is drawn, after the damage displays and before the stat displays.
- Pixel check on N64 warp ROMs (Training, Pikachu and Purin): every opaque texel of the icons equals the ROM-decoded 8x10 CI4 sprite at (27,185) and (97,185), offset (0,0). The PSP HUD (icons and damage displays) sits within 0.25 N64 px of the N64.
- RE-513's "no stat display" N64 shots were the VS scene (`gSCManagerBattleState` was the VS transfer state), not Training; RE-513 carries the correction.
- Sprite edge: the PSP draws every 2D sprite at the non-integer scale 1.236 with Linear filtering, and most transparent texels are black, so edges get a dark fringe (not all: RE-517 measured the ROM bytes and fixed the fringe by a bake-time bleed). The GE rect model here (87 of 117 pixels within 2 levels) is superseded by RE-517's calibrated one (180 of 180). The same applies to VS icons, damage digits and emblems. Fixed in RE-517.

## Consequences

- 105 `f1-training-*` goldens rebaselined; each diff lies within the two icon regions.
- Open: alpha-bleed or unfiltered sprites, measured against `ssb_rom::n64_filter` (D-043); see PLAN.md.
