# RE-463 — The golden matrix's drift since RE-444: three intended changes

Status: COMPLETE (capture bisection across pack versions, a revert-only control at HEAD, decomp and `reloc_data.us.h` checks; 116 goldens rebaselined)
Topics: visual-regression, fighter, rendering, gameplay, pack
Relevant files: `tests/golden/*.png`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/capture_yoshi.rs`, `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`, `refs/ssb-decomp-re/src/ft/ftchar/ftkirby/ftkirbyspecialn.c`, `refs/ssb-decomp-re/src/ft/ftparam.c`, `refs/ssb-decomp-re/src/sc/sc1pmode/sc1pbonusstage.c`

## Question

At 64d4ef6, 116 of 195 goldens differed (115 `psp-game` scenes, 184 to
381,196 pixels, and the viewer's `r2-bonus-platform-small`). RE-462 had
found them already differing at RE-461. The goldens were last rebaselined
at d390eb4 (RE-444). Which commits moved them, and is each move intended?

## Method

Each tested commit got its own pack, built in a scratch worktree with that
commit's `romtool` (pack versions v91 to v103 span the range), then
`tools/golden.sh verify` on a sample of scenes.

| Commit | Pack | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 2c3d0a7 (RE-457) | v101 | every sampled `psp-game` scene matches; `r2-bonus-platform-small` differs (1,556) |
| cda829f (RE-458) | v102 | all eight sampled scenes differ by exactly HEAD's counts |
| cda829f, TopN scale reverted | v102 | 125 of 126 `f1-*` scenes match; `f1-training-yoshi-egg` differs (1,188) |
| 64d4ef6, TopN scale reverted | v103 | 193 of 195 match: only `f1-training-yoshi-egg` (1,188) and `r2-bonus-platform-small` (1,556) |
| 75abbe2 / 3e52c33 (RE-454) | v97 / v98 | `r2-bonus-platform-small` matches / differs (1,556) |
| 2bb881e / 80bbcee (RE-449) | v93 | `f1-training-yoshi-egg` matches / differs (1,188) |

The scale revert sets `attributes.size` to 1 at the three sites RE-458
added (`FighterScene` joint axes and node anchors, the battle draw's model
scale). 2bb881e's pack only builds with 05eaa59's sprite fix applied
(`BadFormat(4, 4)` otherwise); without it the staged pack was HEAD's and the
capture was a blank Training screen, which was discarded.

## Findings

| Cause | Scenes | Check |
|---|---|---|
| RE-458 (cda829f) draws every fighter and places its joints at TopN's `attr->size` (`ftManagerMakeFighter`) | 114 Training and VS scenes: fighters' size and pose, and the gameplay that follows from scaled joints (grabs, Pokémon, team and four-player battles) | RE-458's N64 references: Kirby's width over Mario's 1.43 on the N64, 1.48 after the fix, 1.82 without it |
| RE-449 (80bbcee) credits Egg Lay's 5% to the Training combo | `f1-training-yoshi-egg`: DAMAGE 000% → 005%, COMBO 00 → 01 (plus the scale) | `ftCommonYoshiEggSetStatus` calls `ftKirbySpecialNApplyCaptureDamage(..., 5)`, whose `ftParamUpdatePlayerBattleStats` adds `combo_damage_foe` and `combo_count_foe` |
| RE-454 (3e52c33) binds file 136's platform MObj and material tables | `r2-bonus-platform-small`: the edge strips' colours | `llBonus2CommonPlatformSmall{DObjDesc 0x3da8, MObjSub 0x3720, MatAnimJoint 0x3f00}` in `reloc_data.us.h`; `sc1PBonusStageInitPlatforms` attaches exactly those |

No difference is unexplained: with the scale reverted at HEAD, the only two
scenes left are the two other causes. Every difference is an intended,
source-backed change, so the 116 goldens were rebaselined (twice-captured,
deterministic). The full matrix then matches 195 of 195.
