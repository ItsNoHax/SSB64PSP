## Question

RE-466 left How to Play matching the N64 to frame 735: Mario's third jab
hit Luigi a frame early, its collisions and Luigi's joints a fraction of a
unit from the N64's. RE-467 left Donkey Kong slapping one loop too many
and Samus off course in the opening's jungle, and neither Samus's grapple
beam nor Yoshi's tongue drawn. What does the fighter code still do
differently?

## Method

- N64: the unmodified ROM (SHA-1 `e2929e10…`) under `tools/n64-headless`
  with a scratch config, idling into How to Play as in RE-466 (status, x,
  y, damage, facing, hitlag each frame; attack collisions at `fp+0x294`,
  stride `0xC4`; joint world positions through `joints` at `fp+0x8E8` and
  `FTParts` at DObj `+0x84`), and booting into the opening (scene byte
  `0x800A4AD0`, tic `0x8004501C`). How to Play's update runs on
  `dSCExplainRandomSeed1` (`0x8018E724`, `syUtilsSetRandomSeedPtr`); its
  value was logged each frame to 2,000.
- PSP: `explain@4460` and `op-<scene>@N` under PPSSPPHeadless with
  `--log`; the explain line now prints the shared generator's seed.
  Compared at 0.05 units, with status, damage and facing.

## Result

- How to Play: the PSP matches the N64 (status, x, y, damage, facing,
  0.05 units) through frame 3273 for Luigi and 4039 for Mario, against
  735 and 1475 before; the generator matches to the trace's end (2,000).
  Mario's later differences follow Luigi's.
- The opening: Kongo Jungle matches the N64 frame for frame (638 samples,
  Donkey Kong's slaps and Samus's run, rolls and shield), and so do the
  Mario, Donkey Kong, Link, Samus, Yoshi, Kirby and Fox scenes. Pikachu's
  positions and statuses match; his Fall's `anim_frame` does not wrap
  (below).
- Samus's beam strands draw through the grab (PPSSPP capture against the
  N64's); Yoshi's tongue part is made and ejected with his catches (host
  and ROM tests; the opening's camera faces it head-on).

**Next divergence (frame 3274).** Mario's grab catches Luigi. The N64's
`ftCommonCapturePulledProcCapture` runs `ProcPhysics` and `ProcMap` on the
catch frame, placing Luigi at Mario's hand (x 374.95); the port places him
from the next frame's physics (104.79, then 372.99 against 369.51), and
CaptureWait holds him 2.01 units off (374.67 against 372.66) to 3334. The
throw then matches until its release at 3348, where
`ftCommonThrownReleaseFighterLoseGrip` moves a thrown fighter's TopN to
its joint 4 less 300 before the knockback; the port keeps the hand
position, so Luigi leaves 295 units high (368.71 against 73.64). A trial
port of the drop with the held fighter's joints under the catcher's joint
rotation landed 30–75 units off and was not kept: the N64's `FTParts`
matrices for the thrown fighter were stale in the dump, so the joint the
drop reads is not yet measured.

## Host replay

`explain_movement.rs` replays both scripts on the ROM's collision with
posed skeletons from the pack, the hit search and `ftMainProcParams`, to
frame 1964 (Luigi's KO is next; the replay has no blast zone or rebirth).
It sets the generator to the N64's state before the 1899 knockback draw
(the replay runs no effects). Breaking the `is_fastfall` reset fails it at
frame 1899 (962.69 against 1105.49).

## Verification

- Host: 2,139 workspace tests pass on one thread with `SSB64_ROM` (Rust
  1.98.0); clippy with warnings denied and rustfmt pass. New ROM tests
  (`fighter_fidelity.rs`) pin the sine table, Donkey Kong's loop periods
  and the hidden parts; status tests pin the fast-fall reset, the forward
  smash's facing, Turn's TopN yaw, the smash voice's draw, the landing lag
  and the Super Jump's launch frames; the rebirth keeps its jostle box.
- Both production PSP applications build on nightly-2026-08-26 (no game
  warnings, five existing viewer warnings). PPSSPP (software) boots into
  the opening at 61 FPS. Free memory in the Jungle scene was not
  re-measured; the fighter state grew by a few bytes.
- Goldens: 105 of 195 changed, captured twice and rebaselined; 195 of 195
  then match. Bisected: 75 return with the old camera table (the
  `lbCommonSin` words it had wrong: 355, 440, 453, 663, 677, 685, 795);
  three more with full-size hurtboxes (Pokémon, Venusaur, the eaten
  utility) and 16 with exact-sine collision matrices (held items, Pokémon,
  the slides); `samus-shot` is the Charge Shot setter's first frame.
  Against the predecessor tree, ten move with this session's changes:
  `fireball` (Mario's setter play), `cpu-walk` and `menu` (TopN's yaw
  through the CPU's turn), `link-bomb-turn`/`-loose` (the forward smash
  facing the stick), `cpu-item` (the fast-fall reset) and the four CPU
  battles, which the smash voice's draw, the ground step in `vel_air` and
  TopN's yaw each move without any one restoring them.

## Deviations and remaining differences

- `anim_frame` of statuses whose figatree loops (Fall, DamageFall,
  DamageFlyRoll) counts on rather than wrapping; positions and the drawn
  clip are unaffected.
- The setter audit (a decomp setter calling `ftMainPlayAnimEventsAll`
  where the port's does not) still lists Link's, Samus's, Yoshi's,
  Jigglypuff's and Kirby's specials, Fox's Fire Fox and Reflector starts,
  Donkey Kong's cargo throw and walk, the Barrel and Tornado captures and
  the item throws; none is reached by How to Play or the opening, so each
  waits for a trace (`PLAN.md`).
- A captured fighter is placed a frame late, and a thrown one is released
  from the hand (above).
