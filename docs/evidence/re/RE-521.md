# RE-521 — Primitive-alpha shadows and untextured prim-alpha surfaces

Status: COMPLETE (N64 display-list walks, Rice captures, PPSSPP)
Topics: rendering, alpha, shadows, bonus, kirby
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-rom/src/mesh.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/meshdraw.rs`

## Question

Why were the room box shadows opaque black, and why did Bonus stages and the Final Cutter wave lack translucent surfaces the N64 draws?

## Findings

- These prims are unlit and untextured with combiner `FCFFFFFF FFFE773B` (`SHADE` colour, `PRIM` alpha) under an XLU blender; the alpha is the list's own SETPRIMCOLOR (room shadow 0x4C, Bonus layer-0 rectangles 0x19 and 0x0c, Kirby wedge 0xCC).
- Fix: the gate `flat_prim_alpha_untextured` (`mesh.rs`) keeps the prim alpha, and `material_colors` applies the live prim track only when the mesh has `PRIM_ANIM` (`meshdraw.rs`), so a list SETPRIMCOLOR after the MObj wins (RE-322 semantics).
- Bonus layer-0 (files 124-148, Bonus 2 `137+0x800`): blender `CLR_IN*A_IN + CLR_MEM*(1-A)`, no Z; vertex colour light blue. Port pixel (45,55,70) equals background (32,40,56) blended with `a1bfcc` at 0x19/255 exactly. The rectangles were previously missing from every Bonus stage.
- Kirby wedge (`328+0x1D238`, prim alpha 0xCC, head 1 `G_RM_AA_XLU_SURF`): candidate pixel (208,227,213) equals 0.8 x 255 + 0.2 x (21,115,45).
- Room box shadow darkens by 0.702 = 1 - 0x4C/255 in every channel.
- Open: the N64 Bonus backdrop quads (`124+0x480..0x780`, texture `120+0x5a8`) show a teal/blue pattern where the port draws a flat (32,40,56).
