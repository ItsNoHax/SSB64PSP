# RE-507 — Vortex particles, the Hyrule Twister and the effect matrices match the ROM

Status: COMPLETE (host, PPSSPP and N64 RDRAM trace; not hardware proof for frame time)
Topics: determinism, particles, reverse-engineering
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/particle.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/effect.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/wpeffect.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/stage/hyrule.rs`, `crates/ssb-engine/src/math.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/particles.rs`, `tools/r4300/matrix_hashes.py`, `tools/r4300/vortex_hashes.py`

## Question

Do the vortex particle motion, the Hyrule Twister and the 0x45/RotRpyR/Sca/0x44 effect matrices equal the original's?

## Findings

- Vortex update order (lbparticle.c:1347): sin/cos of the axis angles via
  `syGetSinCosUShort` (s1.15/32768); `vel.z += vortex.f`;
  `r = |unk38| + vel.z*tan(|unk3C|)` (division by cos follows the /0 rule);
  `r *= vel.y`; `vel.x += gn.gravity`; `r /= 32768`;
  `pos = R(axis) * (cos*r, sin*r, h) + gn.pos`, f32 operation order as in
  `vortex_step`. Kind-2 generators draw the RNG in the source order;
  lingering vortex generators and `lbParticleEjectStructID` are ported;
  script 11 is FireGrind. Sca and 0x44 matrices are FTOFIX32.
- tools/r4300 oracle now covers lbCommonMatrixRotSca (0x800C82AC,
  0x7aa61fcfd076712c), RotRpyR, PerspFastF, PerspF and the vortex math.
  ovl0 is mapped (ROM 0x043220 to vram 0x800C7840).
- N64 RDRAM trace (warp-boot decomp ROMs, Mupen headless, per-frame dump of
  the RNG seed, generator queue, particle banks and Hyrule ground vars):
  TwisterMake script 3 makes 3 generators (kind 2 tex 0, kind 2 tex 2, kind
  0 glow); first particles at summon+4/+5/+20; Summon to Move at +80;
  Move/Turn alternate; Stop, Subside, eject follow. Host probes of the Rust
  particle system (961 frames, 15331 samples) and of the real Hyrule
  controller on the real map (1700 frames) are bit-exact in every generator
  and particle field and in RNG, status and Twister position. A one-frame
  shift makes every frame differ (the probe can fail). PSP in-game RNG
  equals the N64 seed over 1691 ticks. Capture tick = N64 stage frame + 9
  (the capture's first 8 ticks are pre-stage scene setup).
- The earlier impressions of a late, taller or faster Twister were refuted:
  the harness frame base and eye comparisons across different matchups and
  cameras caused them. gmcamera.c:1007's temporary persp scale does not
  reach the particle projection and a uniform scale cancels in the w divide.
- Goldens: f1-training-twister added and three scenes rebaselined; the
  grhyrule bank was already in the pack, so no pack rebuild.
