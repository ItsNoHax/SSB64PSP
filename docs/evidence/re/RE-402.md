# RE-402 — The VS battle entry: Entry, Appear and the entry focus

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, pack test, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: fighter, animation, match, camera, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/{appear,appear_tests,status,stale,physics,fighter}.rs`, the fighters' `crates/ssb-game/src/*.rs` updates, `crates/ssb-rom/src/{skeleton,objanim}.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/tests/appear_clips.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`, `psp-game/src/{main,play}.rs`, `psp-asset-viewer/src/play.rs`

## Question

VS fighters stood at their spawns from the first frame (RE-389). How does
the original bring them in, now that the clips are packed (RE-401)?

## Source

- **Before the entry.** `ftManagerMakeFighter` puts every VS fighter in
  `Entry` (`ftCommonEntrySetStatus`): invisible, shadow hidden, ghost.
  Sudden death sets `is_skip_entry`, so its fighters stand at once.
- **The entry focus** (`ifCommonEntryFocusThread`). It is made on frame 91
  with `syUtilsRandIntRange(3)` (RE-394), and calls
  `ftCommonAppearSetStatus` on each fighter in link order, spaced by
  `dIFCommonEntryFocusSleepTics` (22, 15 or 60 ticks):
  - with fewer than three fighters it first sleeps one interval;
  - `id` 1 first sleeps 90 more;
  - `id` 2 zooms the camera on each fighter 30 ticks after its entry
    (`gmCameraSetStatusPlayerZoom`, FOV 28) and returns the camera to
    default after the last.
- **`ftCommonAppearSetStatus`.**
  - It records the spawn as `entry_pos`, with the facing and the floor
    line.
  - It sets `lr = 0`, so the model faces the camera. Captain Falcon's
    leftward entry sets `is_rotate`: yaw 180° and mirrored placement.
  - It puts the fighter in the air, in its own status from
    `dFTCommonEntryAppearStatusIDs`, and starts the entry effect.
- **The statuses.** Appear physics puts TopN at `entry_pos` plus the clip's
  TransN translation and runs no map step.
  - Motion flag 2 shows the shadow, and flag 1 makes the Poké Ball's rays.
  - At the clip's end, `ftCommonAppearProcUpdate` restores the facing, the
    spawn position and the floor, and sets `Wait`.
  - Captain Falcon's entry chains Start into End (`ftCaptainAppear*`).
    Ness's chains Start, Wait and End (`ftNessAppear*`).
- **Ordinals** (`ft<name>.h`):
  - Mario 221 and 222;
  - Fox 223 and 224;
  - Donkey Kong, Samus, Yoshi and Pikachu 220 and 221;
  - Link 224 and 225;
  - Kirby 250 and 251;
  - Jigglypuff 228 and 229;
  - Captain Falcon 224 to 227;
  - Ness 221 to 225.

## Implementation

- **`ssb_rom::skeleton`.** A 32-bit mode plays an entry slot's joints
  through `objanim::StageJoint`. `frame()` reports the stream's
  `anim_frame`, and the lead joint's pose stays readable as joint 0. A
  pack test plays Mario's `AppearR` to its end on tick 121, with the lead
  joint rising more than 100 units.
- **`ssb_game::appear`.**
  - `Entry` holds the spawn, the facing, the floor and `is_rotate`.
  - It has `entry_set_status`, `appear_set_status`, the update with the
    phases, and `tick_status` (the physics, which skips the map).
  - `model_yaw` gives the draw's turn, and `slot` maps each status to its
    clip.
  - `EntryFocus` gives each fighter's entry tick and `id` 2's zoom
    windows.
  - The lengths come from RE-401: 120 frames, Captain Falcon 90 and 30,
    Ness 40, 50 and 30.
- **Status tables.** The status enums gain the Appear ordinals; the
  per-fighter dispatchers leave them to `appear`, and `stale` gives them
  no attack. `RootMotion` carries TransN's translation.
- **`psp-runtime`.** The fighter scene reports the translation, draws with
  `fighter_turn`, and runs the player zoom camera while `entry_zoom` is
  set.
- **`psp-game`.** A VS battle puts both fighters in `Entry`, and sudden
  death stands them at once. `entry_frame` enters each fighter on its
  focus tick and sets the next frame's zoom.

## Deviations

- **Entry effects.** The pipe, Arwing, barrel, capsule, beam, egg, star,
  Poké Ball and car are not ported.
- **Other entry state.** The entry camera mode and the player tag are not
  ported.
- **Master Hand and the Poly fighters** have no entry.
- **One-frame offsets.** The entry and zoom run in `psp-game`'s frame
  order, which may differ from the original's by one frame.

## Verification

- **Tests.** Five host tests cover:
  - hidden to entering to `Wait` at the spawn, with TransN placement;
  - Captain Falcon's turned two-phase entry;
  - Ness's three phases;
  - every fighter's clip mapping;
  - the focus schedule: 22 and 44; 105 and 120; 60 and 120 with zooms
    at 90–150 and 150–210.
- **Log.** A per-tick log of `vs` (removed afterwards) showed `id` 1:
  - the player enters at 196, the CPU at 211;
  - each rises about 420 units through TransN and returns to its spawn;
  - both stand in `Wait` before "Go".
- **Goldens.** Six VS goldens were rebaselined, each captured twice:
  - `f1-vs-countdown` shows Mario mid-entry;
  - `f1-vs-cpu` shows the CPU still fighting;
  - the sudden-death and time-up goldens differ only in the idle pose
    phase.
  106 of 106 goldens match.
- **Gates.** Workspace tests, Clippy and both PSP builds pass. The release
  `run` is 104,616 bytes.
