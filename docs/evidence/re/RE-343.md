# RE-343 — Kirby motions, Stone, Final Cutter and Inhale

Status: COMPLETE (decomp source, host tests, both PSP builds, PPSSPP smoke; copy abilities not ported, Kirby not reachable in `psp-game`, physical PSP not captured)
Topics: fighter, gameplay, grab, capture, weapon, combat, animation
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/kirby.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/kirby_attack.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/capture_kirby.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/grab.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/weapon.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/attack.rs`, `tools/gen-anim-table.py`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`

**Question.** How do Kirby's US motion scripts and callbacks drive his
normals, multi-jump, suplex, Final Cutter, Stone and Inhale, and how does the
swallowed fighter move while Kirby holds it?

**Source.** `228_KirbyMainMotion.c` (US) supplies every motion event, the
throw descriptors and the `FTKirbyCopy[27]` table. `229_KirbyMain.c` gives
heavy-item joint 30, the thrown-status table (all `ThrownCommon`) and the
Final Cutter wave's `WPAttributes` at offset 0x8. `ftkirbyspecial{n,hi,lw}.c`,
`ftkirbythrowf.c`, `ftcommoncapturekirby.c`, `wpkirbycutter.c`,
`ftcommonjumpaerial.c`, `ftcommonattack1.c`, `ftcommonattack100.c`,
`ftcommonthrow.c` and `ftmain.c` supply the callbacks. `dFTKirbyMotionDescs`
and the `nFTKirbyMotion` enum pair statuses with figatrees.

**Findings.**

- Sixteen common normal slots (three forward tilts, one forward smash) plus
  `LandingAirF` and `LandingAirB`, and 31 of Kirby's own slots, are added to
  the animation table; the aerial Inhale statuses reuse the ground figatrees.
  `LandingAirF` and `LandingAirNull` (`LandingAirX_0x18F4`) both spawn
  landing hitboxes; neutral, up and down air land in `LandingAirNull` at
  50/40/22 percent.
- Several aerial scripts re-arm collisions: forward air makes seven two-frame
  pulses with `RefreshAttackCollID`, down air ten. Up air's
  `MakeAttackColl(12)` is overwritten on the same frame, because its
  following `WaitAsync(8)` has already passed at frame 10. The 10-bit angle
  fields 934 and 939 are -90 and -85 degrees.
- The rapid jab ends its loop script with `PauseScript`. On the figatree wrap
  the script resumes into the adjacent `0x120C` block (one pulse and flag 1
  at frame 2, then `Goto(JabLoop)`), so every cycle after the first has six
  pulses. Kirby needs four rapid inputs.
- Aerial jumps 2–5 use `dFTKirbyJumpAerialFVelocities` (60, 52, 47, 40); a
  held stick or held C button (not a tap) jumps again once the jump's motion
  sets flag 1 at frame 28. Drift is scaled by 0.8.
- The suplex (`ThrowF`, US descriptor 55/13/70/70/0/100) leaves the ground,
  falls with no physics callback, and releases in `ThrowFLanding` at frame 8
  toward the back; `SetAirJumpAdd` at frame 9 makes Kirby airborne again.
- Grounded Final Cutter stays on the floor until `SetAirJumpMax` at frame 23
  sets `ga = Air` without a status change. The aerial status computes its
  TransN velocity with TopN scaled to 0.8. The landing makes the wave at
  frame 3, 200 ahead of TopN. The wave (size 250, 361/50/0/70, 6 damage,
  20 frames, speed 100) follows the floor line, flies straight past an edge,
  dies on walls and ceilings, and survives its hits.
- Stone's `is_damage_resist` is cleared by every `ftMainSetStatus`, so the
  setters and map callbacks restore it. US health is 38 with an 18-frame
  minimum hold. Only `SpecialLwUnk`, `SpecialAirLwHold` and
  `SpecialAirLwFall` carry the 20-damage hitbox. Absorbed damage deals no
  damage or knockback; once health runs out, only the overflow passes.
- Inhale searches with two TopN boxes during the loop. On a catch, the
  victim's offset from Kirby's capture point (160 ahead, 100 up) shrinks by
  at most 28 across and 36 up per frame; within 32 units Kirby eats. The
  victim becomes invisible and intangible and has 500 breakout inputs, each
  mash worth six. A spits a star (10 damage, velocity 120 forward); B or a
  stick below -40 copies (6 damage, star 100 at 75 degrees backward) and
  records the victim's copy. A spat star is intangible for 30 frames,
  decelerates by 4 per frame, carries the `DamageBumpHit` box with
  `FTKirbyCopy::star_damage`, never hits its thrower, then pops out with
  velocity (22, 70) and becomes tangible four frames before it falls.

**Deviations.** The ten copy-ability statuses are not ported: a Kirby with a
copy still inhales, and copy loss on damage needs an RNG. Fighter map
collision is floor-only, so ledge catches, the drop-through wiggle and star
reflections off walls and ceilings are not ported. Kirby's decay of the
Inhale offset runs in Kirby's catch update rather than the victim's physics
callback; both read and write the same vector once per frame. The wave finds
the owner's floor line from the map surfaces under its spawn point. Effects,
sounds, color animations, model-part swaps and the victim's shrink scale are
presentation and are not emitted. Kirby is not selectable in `psp-game`.

**Verification.** Host tests cover the jump velocity table and flag gate, the
rapid loop's post-wrap pulse, Stone absorption and duration, Inhale release
lag, the pull-in and eat, copy recording and star direction, Final Cutter's
air switch and wave, Kirby's landing lags, star travel, pop-out and copy
loss, and an end-to-end inhale, swallow and spit through the grab link.
