## Correction: locked fighters run no CPU

`ftMainProcInterrupt` runs `ftComputerProcessAll` only inside
`if (!fp->is_control_disable)`. The port ran the CPU during the VS
countdown (`ftManagerMakeFighter` locks every fighter until "Go") and
only discarded its controller. Its RNG draws moved
`ifCommonEntryAllThread`'s `syUtilsRandIntRange(3)` entry focus and every
later draw. The CPU no longer runs while locked, so all twelve VS
goldens change. `vscpu` moved from tick 690 to 900 (the CPU's hit lands,
7%) and `vsshield` from 685 to 885 (the set-off's grey row, in tick 884's
update, from a capture log).
