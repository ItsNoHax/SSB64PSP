## Source and scope

The source is `itcommon/it{sword,bat,harisen,starrod,lgun,fflower,hammer}.c`,
`ftcommonitemswing.c`, `ftcommonitemshoot.c`, `fthammer.c` and the six
`ftcommonhammer*.c` modules. Their original five item statuses share the
portable equipment lifecycle; the maker's descriptor callback is kept as
Init. All 20 normal item makers now allocate their own item kind.

ROM-backed tests decode the seven file-251 item records (0x190, 0x1D8,
0x220, 0x48C, 0x268, 0x2E4, 0x374), the Ray Gun, Star Rod and Fire Flower
weapon records (0x2B0, 0x4D4, 0x32C), the alternate Star Rod record
(0x508), and the five Fire Flower angles (0x360).

## Source quirks preserved

| Evidence | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `itBatFallProcMap`, `itHarisenFallProcMap`, `itStarRodFallProcMap` | Init/Fall discard the random-destruction return from `itMapCheckDestroyDropped`; thrown/dropped callbacks propagate it. |
| `itStarRodWeaponStarMakeWeapon` | Smash changes the global descriptor pointer before allocation. Later tilts keep smash damage 12, while their maker still selects tilt speed 80 and lifetime 30. The alternate ROM record also changes hit sound 38 to 37. The pool keeps this descriptor choice. |
| `itStarRodWeaponStarProcUpdate` | Lifetime is tested before decrementing: 30 live updates, then destruction and sparkle on update 31. Fire Flower decrements first and expires on update 30. |
| `ftCommonHammerLandingProcUpdate` | The source increments its landing frame and tests `<= 4`, so its first update returns to HammerWait. |
| `ftcommonstatus.h` and `ftCommonHammerFallSetStatus` | HammerFall's table says Ground, but its setter explicitly changes kinetics to Air. The portable status takes the setter's situation. |
| `ftdata.c`, `FTAnimDesc`, `ftPhysicsApplyGroundFrictionOrTransN` | Swing dash always applies TransN motion. Yoshi, Kirby and Jigglypuff's smash motions also set bit 0x40000000, read as `is_use_transn_joint` despite the macro name `FTANIM_FLAG_XROTN_JOINT`; other smashes use friction. |

## Fighter callbacks

The four swing kinds use the original 16-status table and rates (Bat
smash 0.75; Fan neutral/tilt/smash 2.0). Fan's proc_hit scales its item to
1.5 for two updates. Star Rod consumes its script flag and ammo even if
weapon allocation fails. Forward smash throws an empty shooting item.

Ray Gun and Fire Flower fire in proc_accessory, after physics/map,
forwarded effects and refreshed animated hand transforms. Host callers
without a skeleton run the complete physics pass; the runtime samples
joints between the map pass and accessory. Ground shooters stop at an
edge; moving-floor loss and landing switch to the corresponding shooting
status with the animation clock and Flower pause intact. Air shooters
keep an existing fast fall but accept no new fast-fall input.

Flower's first attempt costs two ammo, later ones one. Its eight-update
interval traverses angle indices 0,1,2,3,4,3,2,1. Animation pauses on flag
1; release resumes it after at least five attempts and a 20-update release
lag. Empty attempts still count. The motion attack id renews after each
eight-attempt cycle.

Hammer pickup starts 720 ticks. LightGet skips countdown; other states,
including hitlag, count down after the fighter colour update. At 120 it
records the item's warning request; at zero it destroys the item and
returns the fighter to Wait/Fall. Six Hammer statuses preserve their
motion clock, attacks and model/texture/colour state when switching
between Hammer motions. Turn takes 12 steps and flips facing halfway;
jump squat, short hop, platform pass, landing and DamageFall escape
follow their source callbacks.

## Weapons and drawing

The item-weapon pool also carries the three fighter-owned equipment
shots, their stale damage and owner/team credit. Ray Gun has two attack
centres: its head and offset -5 scaled/rotated with the root. Scale grows
by 10 to 160/3; hop/reflection reset it to 1. The maker's initial tail
precedes its facing rotation, as in wpManagerMakeWeapon. All boxes test
shields before hurtboxes. The tail also tests item hurtboxes.

Star Rod rolls by -0.2 times facing and makes its source spark on odd
lifetime values. Hop/reflection keep their source facing writes. Flower
renews its 30-tick lifetime and two particles on reflection, survives
normal hits, and clears item rehit records after 16 weapon updates.

The seven existing file-86 object trees draw from descriptor 1 as their
root, loose or under the fighter's light-item joint, with their root/child
yaw and Fan scale. Star Rod ammo uses the existing shared star list.
Pack v86 adds Ray Gun's standalone list at 86 + 0x40A8, keyed by its
weapon record (251, 0x2B0), under WEAPON_EXTERNAL. Flower uses the existing
item-bank particles. There is no pack layout change or committed ROM data.
