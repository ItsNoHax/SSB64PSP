## Source behavior

The five `itground` modules are `itglucky.c`, `itmarumine.c`,
`ithitokage.c`, `itfushigibana.c` and `itporygon.c`. Their order is Chansey,
Electrode, Charmander, Venusaur and Porygon. `grYamabukiMakeMonster` now
creates real shared-pool items; their completion closes the gate and draws
its next wait, while a knocked-out Chansey or Charmander clears its handle.

The two firing makers share `dGRYamabukiMonsterAttackKind`, initially 4.
They draw a pattern only after successful allocation, and advance an equal
or bit-overlapping result once modulo 4. INSTANT (2) fires regardless of
animation frame; WAIT (bit 1) fires only from root frame 40 through 120.
Charmander's wait decrement is an `else` branch, giving a nine-frame
interval. Venusaur decrements on its spawn frame, giving sixteen frames.
Both select texture IDs directly rather than through a material animation.

Porygon and Venusaur replace all attack-event fields at item timers 0 and
8. `ITMonsterEvent` sizes are radii, unlike `ITAttributes.size`, which the
item manager halves. Treating both tables alike shrinks these attacks.
Electrode hides its model when its animation ends, clears its attack
records and explodes for six updates. Event sizes are 700, 350 and 300;
the authored timer-6 event is unreachable because destruction occurs first.
Its explosion preserves the attribute's weight/scale/base knockback.

Charmander's flame survives hurt and shield contacts, records the victim,
expires after twenty updates and disappears on map contact. Its absorb
flag is set but its `proc_absorb` is NULL: absorption does not delete it.
Razor Leaf accelerates by 5 times facing each update and expires after
twenty-four updates; it has no map callback and deletes on hit. Reflection
renews only the flame's lifetime. The razor turns its old facing around;
assigning the reflector's facing is a different operation.

The application now runs the item main link before the weapon main link,
matching source GObj links 4 and 5. A newly made monster shot therefore
receives its first weapon update in the same frame.
Gate-close events from item main run before the weapon link, preserving
the next gate wait's RNG position; damage-phase events run after collision.
The flame's two `itcommon` particle scripts (2 and 0) run their make-time
process after their position and velocity are assigned. Pack bank 4 maps to runtime
bank 2, alongside common bank 0 and Yoshi bank 1.

## Separate archive file and root clock

The gate's `map_nodes` points into archive file 160; the Pokémon models,
joint clips and direct sprite tables live in file 159. A per-asset source
file override preserves the gate binding while loading the five item
trees. The promoted item root is node 1, as in RE-429. Its clock supplies
the firing and egg windows, and its local X/Y receives the map offset.

The ROM-backed `saffron_item_clocks_and_texture_frames_match_the_rom`
compares every packed root position and frame bit-for-bit with scripts
0x3F8, 0x828, 0x1A28, 0x23D8 and 0xF38 through animation completion.
It also checks the six runtime objects (gate plus five items) and both
packed sprite frames for Charmander and Venusaur. Their alternate meshes
use the same OPA item seed as their normal graphs. The Razor Leaf graph
at 0x2A50 uses the weapon wrapper's translucent seed.

Pack v81 adds five joint clips (40 stage/item clips total) and four direct
texture meshes. The binary pack layout is unchanged.

## Remaining dependency

Chansey's egg callback follows the source's normal-item-switch gate,
80–85 frame window, one-egg count, random velocity and allocation retry.
The current runtime has no normal-item switches or Egg/container item
port, so the gate is disabled: Chansey consumes the attempt without a
random draw or an Egg spawn. The host test covers both disabled and
enabled/retry paths through `ItemAnims`. Connecting enabled switches and
the real Egg maker remains a separate item batch.

## Capture scope

Five diagnostic Training scenes select one Pokémon directly, open the
gate and run its normal item callbacks to tick 90. Charmander and Venusaur
use WAIT so their active frame and shot are visible. The camera targets
the monster origin minus 600 X, with eye 5,000 Z above it. These scenes
pin current PSP rendering; they do not prove random gate selection,
Chansey eggs, Electrode's explosion frame, damaged flight or N64 equality.
