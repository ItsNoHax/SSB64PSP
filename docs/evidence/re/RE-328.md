# RE-328 — Camera task-head-1 XLU reset outside stage layers

Status: COMPLETE (decomp source, ROM graph census, pack diff, 68 PPSSPP goldens)
Topics: rendering, material, alpha, blending, depth, pack, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-rom/src/mesh.rs`, `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`

**Question.** RE-323 left the camera's head-1 reset out of graphs other than
stage layer 1. Could an earlier graph under the same camera replace that
render mode before an item, effect or fighter graph draws?

**Source and census.** `sys/objdisplay.c:2561-2614`'s `func_80016338`
sets `G_RM_AA_ZB_XLU_SURF` for buffer 1. The camera display driver calls
it before running head 1 (`objdisplay.c:3278-3301`). The existing graph
planner identifies 103 ROM graphs with a head-1 `DObjDLLink`. An archive
census of their own render-mode writes finds only three graphs that change
head 1's mode. Each writes `0x0C1849D8` and restores `0x005049D8` (the XLU
mode) before returning. The ROM-backed
`camera_head1_graphs_restore_xlu_mode` test preserves this invariant. Thus
the following graph under that camera sees the XLU mode too. This is the
missing external seed; it is independent of a stage layer's own reset.

**Change.** `convert_sequence` now seeds its head-1 `State` with the full
camera XLU render mode. A graph's own `G_SETOTHERMODE_L` still overrides it;
head 0 still uses its existing external material seed. The reduced
`camera_head1_reset_reaches_graphs_without_a_stage_layer_seed` test checks
blending, primitive alpha, depth test and disabled depth write.

**Measured impact.** Relative to pack v36 (`01029162…`), 247 of 8,343
primitive flag records change. The mesh and primitive counts remain 3,338
and 8,343. Six texture variants are added by filter compensation; the pack
grows from 22,247,328 to 22,361,792 bytes (SHA-256 `a5eacfa2…`). No ROM
asset is committed.

The full 68-scene PPSSPP software matrix was captured twice. Five scenes
changed, with zero differences between their two new captures, and were
rebaselined: `r1-mvopeningroom` (85,248 pixels at 2x), `r1-stage-sector`
(580), `r1-catch-swirl-flat-color` (30,892), `r2-bonus-platform-small`
(1,628), and `r2-stage-mushroom-kingdom` (2,012). The previously solid
Sector Z glow now blends, the opening room's back wall and curtains become
visible through the foreground surfaces, and Catch Swirl blends into the
background. The other 63 scenes match their prior goldens.

**Limit.** This census covers the packed graph display lists. Runtime
effects that issue render-mode commands outside those graphs would require
their own ordered state model. No such command was found in these 103
graphs. Physical-PSP visual recapture remains deferred with the broader
hardware golden matrix.
