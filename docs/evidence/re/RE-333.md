# RE-333 — Held-fighter damage, stale moves, handicaps, bystander throws and held scale

Status: COMPLETE (decomp source, host tests, both PSP builds; no ROM, PPSSPP or physical PSP this batch)
Topics: fighter, gameplay, grab, capture, throw, combat, collision
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/stale.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/attack.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/grab.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/fighter.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/physics.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/status.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `psp-game/src/play.rs`

**Question.** How does the source damage a held fighter, stale repeated
moves, apply handicaps, let throws hit bystanders and scale a held
fighter's attachment?

**Source.**

| Behaviour | Source |
|---|---|
| Held damage | `ftParamGetCapturedDamage` halves damage (`d * 0.5 + 0.999`). `ftCommonDamageUpdateMain`'s `capture_gobj` branch keeps the hold while `damage_queue < FTCOMMON_DAMAGE_CATCH_RELEASE_THRESHOLD` (6, `ftCommonDamageCheckCaptureKeepHold`); otherwise `ftCommonThrownDecideFighterLoseGrip` drops both links, the held fighter takes its damage status and the catcher takes `ftCommonThrownSetStatusNoDamageRelease` (`{ -1, 0, 361, 0, 0, 20, 0 }`, attack handicap 9, `lr = fp->lr`). |
| Stale queue | `ftParamGetStale` walks four of five `stale_info` slots back from `stale_id` with `dFTParamStaleTable = { 0.75, 0.82, 0.89, 0.96 }`; the newest slot matching the current motion is skipped and extends the walk. `ftParamUpdateStaleQueue` skips duplicates and self-hits. `ftMainSetStatus` sets a new motion ID when the status's `mflags.attack_id` is none or differs; `Attack11`, `AttackLw3`, each `Attack100` loop and each Fox blaster repeat always set one. |
| Handicap | `ftParamGetCommonKnockback` multiplies by `damage_ratio * 0.01`, `dFTCommonDataHandicapTable[atk - 1][0]`, then `[def - 1][1]`. Training copies `dSCManagerDefaultBattleState`: ratio 100, handicap 9 (US `{ 1.09, 0.9174312 }`). |
| Bystander throws | `ftMainSearchFighterAttack` skips `this_fp->capture_gobj`. `dMarioMainMotion_ThrowB` makes one box on `jid` 10 for frames 18–46; `dFoxMainMotion_ThrowB` two on `jid` 20 for 11–19. |
| Held scale | `ftCommonCapturePulledRotateScale` multiplies the negated first-child translation by TopN's scale, which `ftManagerMakeFighter` sets to `attr->size` (Donkey Kong 1.25, Giant Donkey Kong 2.0). |
| Condensed boxes | Mario `DSmash` (4 boxes, `jid` 25/25/20/20), Mario `AttackAirN` (25/20/5 twice) and Fox `DSmash` (25/20) had been merged into fewer boxes or one joint. |

**Implementation.** `ssb_game::stale` owns motion IDs, the per-fighter
queue and the handicap table. A fighter hit stales the box's damage and
records the move; throws stale when the catcher queues the release and
record when the damage lands (`GrabEvent` carries a `StaledThrow`). Held
fighters take halved damage; a breaking hit sends
`GrabEvent::CaptureHitRelease` to the catcher. `apply_hit_from` skips the
defender's own catcher, and `MARIO_THROW_B`/`FOX_THROW_B` hit bystanders.
`PhysicsAttributes::size` comes from the pack's `FighterDesc::size` and
scales the held child offset. `apply_hit_from` now takes the attacker
mutably; `apply_hitbox_at` returns a `HitOutcome`.

**Deviations.**

* Motion counts are per fighter, not global. A queue only holds its own
  fighter's counts and compares them for equality, so staling matches; only
  the 16-bit wrap point differs.
* Damage is staled at contact, not at `MakeAttackColl`. They differ only if
  another of the attacker's motions lands while the box is live.
* Hits resolve one at a time. The branches where the catcher and the held
  fighter are hit in the same frame, and catcher hitlag, need the source's
  deferred damage queue.
* Weapons are not staled, and fighter hits still pass `recent_damage = 0`
  where the source passes the frame's `damage_queue` (`TODO.md`).

**Verification.** New host tests: stale table walk, self-skip, rounding,
motion-ID rule, handicap neutrality; light and strong hits on a held
fighter; Mario's and Fox's back throws hitting a bystander while the held
fighter is skipped; a staled second throw (16 → 12); Donkey Kong's 1.25
held offset; restored joint patterns. Existing tests keep their exact
values under handicap 9. `cargo test --workspace`: 933 pass. Both PSP
release builds pass. Not run: the ROM, PPSSPP goldens, physical PSP.
The handicap factors round like the source's: over 2,500 sampled base
knockbacks, 343 differ from the old neutral value by one ulp (17.0 is
unchanged). Golden scenes whose hits land may therefore move slightly and
need a PPSSPP re-run before the next golden baseline.
