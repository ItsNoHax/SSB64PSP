# RE-333 — Samus motion scripts, specials and weapons

Status: COMPLETE (decomp source, host tests, both PSP builds, PPSSPP smoke; Samus not reachable in `psp-game`, physical PSP not captured)
Topics: fighter, gameplay, weapon, grab, animation
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/samus.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/samus_attack.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/weapon.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/grab.rs`, `tools/gen-anim-table.py`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`

**Question.** What do Samus's US motion scripts, special statuses and
weapons do, and where must the port depart from the source structure?

**Source.** `216_SamusMainMotion.c` gives every attack, catch and throw
window. `ftsamusspecialn.c`, `ftsamusspecialhi.c` and `ftsamusspeciallw.c`
give the status callbacks; `ftsamus.h` and `wpvars.h` their constants.
`wpsamuschargeshot.c` holds the eight Charge Shot rows. The Charge Shot's
knockback comes from `218_SamusSpecial1.c`. The Bomb's `WPAttributes` are the
words at offset 0x0C of `217_SamusMain.c` (`llSamusMainBombWeaponAttributes`):
size 160, angle 361, knockback 65/0/10, 9 damage, map diamond 75/0/-75/75,
reflection disabled. `ftMainSetStatus` fast-forwards a script that starts
past frame 0 without running its flag or `SetAirJumpMax` events.

**Findings.**

- `AttackAirU` creates its 4-damage finisher pair and clears it in the same
  frame, with no wait between. The pair never reaches a collision check. The
  port keeps only the five 2-damage pulses.
- `SetAirJumpMax` sets the air kinetics without a status change. Grounded
  Screw Attack is airborne from frame 4 while it keeps status 227. Grounded
  Bomb becomes airborne at frame 3, and its update moves it to the aerial
  status with a Y velocity of 40. After a landing, the grounded scripts do
  not hop again because the fast-forward skips the event.
- The grounded Bomb's flag-3 walk drift is unreachable: the status leaves
  the ground at frame 3, and the flag is set at frame 10.
- The Catch script sets flag 1 = 17 and flag 2 = 9 at frame 20. As a result,
  `CatchPull` starts at frame 9 and then 9/17 of a frame earlier for each
  later beam frame. The beam's two boxes ride joint 36 from frame 20 to 39.
- Samus's thrown table is `ThrownCommon` for every catcher kind.

**Deviations.**

- A charging Charge Shot has no attack collision in the source. The port
  stores it as fighter state and requests a launched weapon on release. A
  full 16-slot pool therefore refuses the shot at release, not at the start
  of the charge.
- A grounded Bomb slides along its floor line and becomes airborne past the
  line's end. It does not test walls while grounded.
- The Charge Shot loop's shield-roll escape and Screw Attack's intangible
  frames (`SetHitStatusAll(2)`) wait on the unported escape and hit-status
  systems. Screw Attack's ledge auto-catch waits on ledge detection.

**Verification.** Host tests cover charge timing and stored full charge,
charge loss on hit, aerial recoil and its reset on landing, the Screw Attack
takeoff and finisher, the Bomb hop and spawn frame, the Charge Shot level row
and wall contact, Bomb bounce, settle, fuse, explosion and single hit, the
Grapple Beam window and pull frame, the throw release frame, and the
up-smash and up-air pulse records. The generated animation table adds 31
Samus slots after the grab slots and packs Samus's grab and thrown clips.
