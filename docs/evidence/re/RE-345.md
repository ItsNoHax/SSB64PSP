# RE-345 — Pikachu Thunder Jolt wall normals and authored edge topology

Status: COMPLETE (decomp and ROM geometry, host tests, both PSP builds, one PPSSPP smoke; fighter wall/ceiling solver and physical PSP not validated)

**Question.** Which side does an original wall normal face, and can a native
Thunder Jolt crawler reconstruct its next surface from coordinates alone?

**Sources.** `mpCollisionGetLRCommonLWall`, `mpCollisionGetLRCommonRWall`,
`mpCollisionGetLRAngle` and `func_ovl2_800FB31C` in `mpcollision.c`;
`wppikachuthunderjolt.c`, `wppikachuthunder.c`, `wpmap.c`;
`ftpikachuspecial{n,hi,lw}.c`, `ftpikachustatus.h`, `ftdata.c`,
`ftkirbycopypikachuspecialn.c`, and US motion/weapon attributes in
`242_PikachuMainMotion.c`, `243_PikachuMain.c`, `244_PikachuMain.c`.
Independent geometry check: US ROM stage file 255 (Dream Land), collision
file 104, geometry header at `0x1F34`.

**Findings.**

- Source LWall calls the angle routine with `lr = -1`, RWall with `+1`.
  Vertical wall normals therefore face negative and positive X respectively.
  The port's wall-normal signs were reversed. Dream Land confirms it:
  RWall 5 is at positive X, LWall 6 at negative X. Corrected the shared weapon
  normals and the synthetic test fixtures which had mislabeled wall sides.
- The neighbor builder joins original vertex IDs, not equal coordinates.
  It scans all original line IDs, with the last matching line winning.
  Dream Land floor 3 has IDs `[5,6]`, ceiling 4 `[0,1]`, RWall 5
  `[6,7,8,9,0]`, LWall 6 `[1,2,3,4,5]`. Normalized floor/ceiling neighbors
  are `(6,5)`; both walls have `(4,3)`. Pack v38 uses the former two-byte
  collision-vertex padding for the original ID; the vertex stride stays
  eight bytes. Runtime surfaces carry line, segment and vertex identity.
- A crawler projects onto the current polyline, attempts an authored edge
  transition when it leaves that line, then checks incoming crossings.
  It chooses the nearest crossing before rejecting the excluded neighbor.
  Ceiling contacts destroy the crawler; aerial ceiling contacts only correct
  position. Edge turns over 100 degrees destroy it. Ground lifetime carries
  over from the aerial jolt; floor speed is 55, damage changes from 10 to 7.
- Thunder's head has no attack collision. It emits a stationary trail at its
  previous position before moving down 450 per tick. Trails share victim
  records with the head, including future trails. The head lasts 40 ticks;
  trails expire when their ten-tick counter falls below six. The trail's
  authored offsets ±240 become ±120 under its source scale of 0.5.
  Self-collision removes only the head. Damage during Loop/Hit marks the head
  Destroy and suppresses its eventual owner notification without removing it.
- Preserve the original status table's unusual attack IDs: Quick Attack uses
  `SpecialLw`, Thunder `SpecialHi`. Pikachu re-chains into Attack11, without
  Attack12. Quick Attack starts after 20 ticks, zips for five, reads a second
  direction at End frame 9, requires an angle over 42 degrees and scales the
  second zip by 0.9. Its first aerial map callback ignores passable floors;
  later callbacks do not. Kirby's copied jolt uses joint 0 with a facing-scaled
  200 X and 200 Y offset and its own ground/air figatrees.
