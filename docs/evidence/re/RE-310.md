# RE-310 — Critical authored-UV coverage and independent filter validation

Status: COMPLETE (host and PPSSPP smoke; physical PSP not captured)
Topics: texture, asset-pipeline, pack, psp-ge

Relevant files: `tools/romtool/src/filter_coverage.rs`, `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/filter_compensation.rs`

**Problem.** The N=8 barycentric lattice from RE-305 samples broad triangle
coverage but can miss the worst piecewise 3-point boundary (`sfrac+tfrac=32`)
and GE 1/16 fractional transitions. A 2x2 saddle and a narrow real-UV-shaped
triangle have a maximum 128/255 filter discrepancy at S10.5 `(17,15)`; the
old lattice's maximum is only 112/255.

**Coverage.** For each authored UV triangle, the packer keeps every original
N=8 barycentric point and exact UV vertices. It visits touched texel cells,
probes the diagonal at sums 31/32/33 (including the middle representable
point where a narrow triangle intersects each line), half texels, GE
fractional boundaries, and cell edges that include clamp edges, repeat seams,
and prebaked mirror seams. The new cell-local probes are integer S10.5
coordinates inside the triangle. A touched sliver with no representable integer point cannot
contribute an S10.5 sample. A separate denser holdout probes other diagonal
positions, both axes' GE boundaries, half texels, and seams. The two final
sets are sorted, deduplicated, and disjoint. Per-cell caps are 8 training and
48 holdout points; whole-primitive targets are 4,096 and 16,384, with the
N=8 lattice always retained and other probes spread deterministically when
the budget is exceeded. Texgen retains a full-tile training grid and uses a
shifted full-tile holdout because its live UVs depend on pose. A degenerate
UV footprint with no distinct S10.5 holdout point cannot admit compensation.

**Admission.** Continuous solve, palette-index optimization, and GE-exact
integer refinement still train on the optimization set. The selected final
texture is measured against the source on independent holdout points before
packing. A static candidate is rejected if holdout squared error, `>=8`
count, or `>=32` count increases, or if maximum error increases by more than
8/255. Translucent visible SSE and cutout classification have their own
holdout gates. Animated shared-index candidates must pass the holdout in
every reachable palette state. The policy-scoped metric excludes opaque
alpha, which the runtime material does not read. Algorithm/cache version is
7. Runtime rendering and `GU_LINEAR` are unchanged.

**US archive comparison.** The old N=8 build logged 427 static compensation
variants (318 CI4, 109 direct) and seven accepted animated indexed variants,
and packed 1,998 textures in 30,058,816 bytes. On that *sparse* coverage,
the 109 direct variants' final `>=32` count summed to 17 and their per-variant
maxima summed to 1,643 (RE-309). Those old numbers cannot be directly compared
to the new, much denser holdout because both sample count and accepted variant
population changed.

The new build evaluates 1,119 static candidates and accepts 63 (53 direct,
10 CI4); none of the 74 animated indexed candidates passes the combined
training and holdout gates.
Rejected variants retain their source texture. The emitted pack has 1,821
textures and is 29,328,784 bytes (730,032 bytes smaller than the old pack).
On the *same new holdout coordinates* for the 63 accepted static variants:

| Metric | Source | Final | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Exact squared error | 28,265,247 | 22,582,451 | -20.1% |
| Samples with error >=32/255 | 1,599 | 805 | -794 |
| Sum of per-variant maximum errors | 2,111 | 1,842 | -269 |

Across all 1,119 evaluated static variants, including source fallbacks,
holdout `>=32` samples fall 64,503 to 63,709 and summed maxima fall 44,046
to 43,777. The large rejection count is a measured consequence of sparse
training improvements that do not generalize to the independent critical
coordinates; admitting them would introduce real holdout regressions.

**Verification.** Synthetic tests cover the saddle miss, clamp/repeat and
prebaked mirror seams, 2x2/4x4 textures, a large stretched triangle, caps,
determinism, and a candidate that improves training but regresses holdout.
ROM-gated workspace tests and `ssb-rom` no-std check pass; both release PSP
applications build. The rebuilt pack loads back cleanly, and a software
PPSSPPHeadless Dream Land capture displays the stage. PPSSPP is not physical
PSP proof. Two independent full builds and a final build with the degenerate
UV guard emit the same SHA-256 pack
`5179c3b379bf09552556621613051eeab668e6c33f9b46973c928037c94b7cba`.
