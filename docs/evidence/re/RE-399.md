# RE-399 — The VS mode menu's rule, time and stock

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless golden captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: menu, match, visual-regression, psp-platform
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/{vs_mode,vs_mode_tests}.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`, `tests/golden/f1-vs-mode-menu.png`

## Question

Every VS battle used fixed rules: a three-minute time battle (RE-389). How
does `mnVSMode` choose the rule, the time and the stocks?

## Source

- **Buttons** (`mnVSModeMain`). The menu has four buttons: VS Start,
  Rule, Time/Stock and VS Options.
  - Up and down (D-pad, C buttons or the stick past 20) move the cursor,
    wrapping at both ends.
  - A or START on VS Start or VS Options leaves, after
    `mnVSModeSaveSettings`. B goes back to the mode select.
  - The first ten ticks ignore input. Five idle minutes return to the
    title.
- **Settings.**
  - **Rule.** On the Rule button, left and right (D-pad, L/R or C
    buttons, or the stick) step Time, Stock, Time Team and Stock Team,
    without wrapping.
  - **Time.** On Time/Stock in a time rule, the value steps 1 to 99 and
    infinite (100), wrapping both ways.
  - **Stock.** In a stock rule, the stored stock count steps 0 to 98
    (1 to 99 on screen), wrapping.
- **Repeat.** Held input repeats through `sMNVSModeChangeWait`, from
  `mndef.h`'s `mnCommon*` macros:
  - 12 ticks for a button;
  - `(160 ∓ stick) / 7` for the cursor and the rule;
  - `/ 14`, doubled on a change of direction, for the value;
  - 8 more on some edges.
  A released stick and buttons clear the wait.
- **What it writes.** `mnVSModeSaveSettings` writes `game_rules`,
  `is_team_battle`, `time_limit` and `stocks`. Those drive the battle
  (RE-389) and the stock display (RE-396).

## Implementation

- **`ssb_game::vs_mode::VsMode`.** Ports `mnVSModeFuncStartVars` and
  `mnVSModeMain`, including the repeat waits. It returns
  Start/Options/Back/Title. Five host tests cover it.
- **`psp-game`.**
  - The main menu's VS entry opens a `VsMode` screen seeded from the last
    settings.
  - VS Start saves the rules, then goes to the character select and the
    stage select, and the battle starts with those rules.
  - B returns to the menu. VS Options is not ported and does nothing.
  - The screen draws plain slots: the buttons with the cursor lit, the
    four rules with the chosen one lit, and the time or stock as a bar.
    The menu's sprites are not converted.
  - Capture scenes on the direct route still skip the menu. The new
    `vsmode` capture goes through it: Rule to Stock, then one more stock,
    captured at tick 60.
- **Branch range.** `vs_mode_frame` and `new_fighter_select` keep the
  menu's code out of `run`.

## Deviations

- **Teams.** Team rules are chosen but play as free-for-all; team battles
  are not ported.
- **VS Options.** Not ported.
- **Idle check.** The idle return reads "no input" as no buttons and a
  centred stick. `scSubsysControllerCheckNoInputAll` is not ported
  exactly.
- **Presentation.** The menu's sprites, sounds, arrows and blink are not
  drawn.

## Verification

- **Golden.** The new `f1-vs-mode-menu` shows the cursor on Time/Stock,
  Stock lit and a four-stock bar. It was identical in both captures. 105
  of 105 goldens match.
- **Gates.** Workspace tests, Clippy and both PSP builds pass. `run` is
  121,264 bytes in release and 123,808 in `golden_capture`. The limit is
  131,072, so new screens must stay out of line.
