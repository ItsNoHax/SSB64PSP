# RE-508 — Display-matrix kinds of the remaining consumers, Whispy's leaves and dust

Status: COMPLETE (host, PPSSPP and N64 RDRAM trace; not hardware proof for frame time)
Topics: determinism, particles, rendering, reverse-engineering
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/effect.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/wpeffect.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/stage/pupupu.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/skeleton.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/particle.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/movie.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/boss.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/particles.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`

## Question

Do the remaining float `from_trs` display consumers use the original's matrix kinds, and do Whispy's leaves and dust match the ROM?

## Findings

- Matrix kinds were read from the C at each site. Fighter root in the movie
  path (ftmanager.c:755-775) is TraRotRpyRSca with the real fighter scale; the
  boss wallpaper is TraRotRpyRSca; the Yoshi egg and the rebirth halo (child
  scale inside its Sca chain) use the display chain; Sword/Fan are
  TraRotRpyRSca, Bat/StarRod/RayGun/FireFlower/Hammer and the containers
  (capsule, crate, barrel, Bonus 3 barrel bomb) are TraRotRpyR per the itdesc
  transform types; Kirby jolt ground, cutter and Falcon kick placement are
  TraRotRpyR. Scale-only unit helpers stay Rust-internal.
- Whispy (grpupupu.c:290-305, 474-518) makes leaves and dust particles
  unconditionally in every Dream Land battle. The particle scripts draw from
  the global RNG (lbparticle.c), so Whispy's particles consume the same
  `sSYUtilsRandomSeed` the CPUs use. Leaves creation itself draws nothing
  (blink-coincidence poke), so the inline blink roll order is exact.
- N64 RDRAM trace (warp-boot Training Dream Land, Mupen headless): 2400
  natural frames (two winds) and 1500 lr-0 frames: generators, particles and
  RNG seed bit-identical every frame; a one-frame shift makes all differ.
  Stage tick n = capture tick n; wind Open n=961, Blow 1015, Stop 1269, Wait
  1289 in Training. N64 bank id 1 and PSP runtime id 7 differ by design.
  The four non-Whispy effects in the lr-0 trace (generator ids 12-15, the
  global generator counter, not script ids) are DustLight puffs (script 0x55)
  at frames 1212/1279/1285/1321, made when the Training dummy is pushed off
  its platform by Whispy's left wind. Rust makes the same four; an earlier
  "Rust makes none" came from the old dumper. The host test
  `whispys_left_wind_lands_the_dummy_in_a_dust_puff_as_on_the_n64` covers the
  frame-1212 puff.
- Consequence: every Dream Land golden past the first wind (about tick 1400
  in VS, 961 in Training) moves with any RNG-consumer change.
  `f1-vs-time-up` was rebaselined. `f1-vs-four-magnifier` drew no magnifier at
  `vs4@2800` in the new timeline (magnifiers only around render frames
  151-370 and 1314-1373), so it moved to `vs4@1330` (Mario's uncapped scale
  1.794) to keep magnifier coverage.
- The magnifier cap experiment: the raw scale at the old `vs4@2800` was 2.397
  (below the 3.0 cap), measured before Whispy's draws changed the timeline;
  that explains why RE-505 left the old hash unchanged: the magnifier distance
  did not move at that frame.
- Goldens: f1-training-whispy-wind added (`trainingpupupu@1110`);
  f1-opening-run, f1-training-heavy-throw, -heavy-smash, -pokemon-b
  (matrix kinds), f1-vs-time-up and f1-vs-four-magnifier rebaselined.
  The grpupupu particle bank is in `ssb_rom::particle::BANKS` and loaded by
  name: no pack rebuild.
