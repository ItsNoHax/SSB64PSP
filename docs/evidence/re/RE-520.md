# RE-520 — Shade-only combiners lower to white-RGB textures with the texel alpha kept

Status: COMPLETE (N64 display-list walk, PPSSPP)
Topics: rendering, combiner, materials, opening, bonus
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-rom/src/mesh.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/pack.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/skeleton.rs`, `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`

## Question

Why did the room spotlight cone and logo glow, and the Bonus 2 glow bar, draw dark and as thin grey outlines?

## Findings

- RDRAM walks (opening room `52+0x22E18`, `52+0x1C358`; Bonus 2 `137+0x31f0`) show colour combiners that read no texel in the RGB formula: `SHADE` or `SHADE*PRIM`, with `TEXEL0` only in alpha (`FCFFFFFF FFFE7339`).
- The GE's Modulate always multiplies the texel colour, so the I-texel darkened the colour (edge pixels read I x alpha, about 207,207,169).
- Fix: `TextureRef::rgb_unused` is set when the colour is SHADE times constants with no texel and no additive constant (`combiner_rgb_is_shade_only`). romtool whitens that texture's palette and texels and keeps alpha, giving the GE `Cv = Cf*1`, `Av = Af*At`, and adds the flag to `TexKey`.
- `flags::ALPHA_NO_PRIM` (pack.rs): when the alpha formula is `TEXEL0`, the MObj prim alpha track must not replace vertex alpha.
- 14 prims got `rgb_unused`, 6 prims `ALPHA_NO_PRIM`. Room MAE against Rice at tic 542 fell from 31.47 to 6.66.
- Deviations: a whitened texture whose MObj animates the palette or swaps images uses unwhitened runtime data (none in the room); `SHADE + constant` shapes still modulate the texel.
