# RE-405 — VS battles with up to four fighters

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: match, fighter, grab, hud, psp-platform, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/{grab,fighter,map,map_tests}.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`, `psp-game/src/{main,play}.rs`, `tests/golden/f1-vs-four.png`

## Question

The VS select (RE-404) picks up to four players, but the battle ran one
human against one CPU: a `play_state` and one `dummy_state`, written out
as a pair. What does the original do per fighter, and what does the port
need for three CPUs?

## Source

- **Spawns.** `scVSBattleStartBattle` and `scVSBattleStartSuddenDeath`
  loop over players 0–3 and place each present one with
  `mpCollisionGetPlayerMapObjPosition(player)`: the spawn is the slot.
- **Facing.** `scVSBattleGetStartPlayerLR` faces the nearest other
  present player's spawn, skipping players on the same `player` (team).
- **Order.** Every per-fighter process runs in
  `gGCCommonLinks[nGCCommonLinkIDFighter]` order, which is port order.
- **Catches.** `ftMainProcSearchCatch` runs `ftMainSearchFighterCatch`
  over every other fighter. `ftMainUpdateCatchStatFighter` keeps the
  nearest in x (strict `<`, so the first wins a tie), and `proc_catch`
  runs for that fighter alone.
- **Cliffs.** `mpCommonRunFighterSpecialCollisions` skips a ledge that
  any other fighter holds facing the same way.
- **Entry.** `ifCommonEntryFocusThread` counts `pl_count + cp_count`.

## Implementation

- **`ssb_game::grab`.**
  - `GrabState::partner` keeps the last linked port. Several releases
    clear `catch`/`capture` before queueing their event, so the link
    alone cannot name the recipient. `partner(f)` gives it.
  - `nearest_catch(catcher, others)` is the nearest-in-x search.
    `search_catch` catches the chosen fighter.
- **`ssb_game::fighter`/`map`.** `occupied_cliffs` holds up to three
  other fighters' ledges.
- **`psp-runtime`.** `tick_camera` takes a list of extra interests, and
  `FighterScene::camera_interest` gives one.
- **`psp-game`.**
  - The dummies are `[Option<Box<Dummy>>; 3]`, where index `i` is port
    `i + 1`. Training uses the first.
  - `training_step` runs three out-of-line passes (interrupt, physics,
    hits) over the fighters in port order. Each fighter's grab events go
    to its partner.
  - The HUD, emblems, stock icons, shadows, models, entry effects, item
    owners and shields cover four ports.
  - A `Roster` of entrants replaces the man/com pair. `vs_roster` builds
    it from the full battle state:
    - the first human runs on port 0 with the pad;
    - every other player with a fighter becomes a CPU at its own spawn,
      with its own level.
  - `Battle::new` gets every present player. The entry focus counts all
    fighters, and sudden death restarts the tied ones.
- **Stack.** `rust-psp`'s `module!` gives the main thread 256 KB of
  stack (PPSSPP logs `sceKernelCreateThread(main_thread, …, 262144, …)`).
  - Before this batch, `run`'s frame was about 975 KB, because the
    session (105 KB) and `EffectVisuals` (383 KB) lived on it. PPSSPP
    does not catch the overflow.
  - The session is now boxed. `EffectVisuals::new_boxed` builds the
    players on the heap one field at a time, since `Box::default` built
    all 393 KB on the stack first.
  - `run`'s frame is now about 4 KB. The largest frame is
    `enter_training` at about 128 KB.
- **Capture scene `vs4`.** Mario against three level-3 CPUs (Fox, Donkey
  Kong and Kirby) on Dream Land, captured at tick 870.

## Deviations

- **Camera.** It frames only the player. The source frames every
  non-ghost fighter. Turning that on changes most VS goldens and is left
  for its own batch.
- **Teams and handicap.** Every battle is a free-for-all. Combat has no
  team-attack rule.
- **Pause** is the port-0 player's only.
- **Sudden death without the player.** When the player is not among the
  tied, sudden death is skipped and the results follow.
- **The human's port.** A human saved in a slot other than 0 swaps ports
  with player 0 but keeps its own spawn.
- **Player-only effects.** Magnet, Sing, the Spin Attack swirl, Falcon
  Punch and Kick, the reflector, the charging Charge Shot and the held egg
  still draw for the player only.
- **CPU view.** CPUs target every other fighter. Their world still has
  no items or hazards.

## Verification

- **Tests.** Two new `grab` tests cover the nearest catch and a release
  finding its partner after the link drops. Breaking the comparison failed
  the nearest-catch test.
- **Goldens.**
  - After the pair-to-loop refactor, all 107 goldens matched unchanged
    before any VS change.
  - The new `f1-vs-four` golden was identical in both captures. It shows
    Mario tumbling above Fox, Donkey Kong and Kirby, with four damage
    displays (43%, 13%, 6% and 15%), a red emblem and three grey, and
    four stock icons. The CPUs have hit each other as well as Mario.
  - The purple star in the sky is not an entry effect: switching the
    entry-effect draw off left the frame identical.
  - The older 107 goldens are unchanged.
- **Gates.** Workspace tests, Clippy and both PSP builds pass. The release
  `run` is 20,508 bytes.

## Conclusion

VS runs up to four fighters in the original's port order, with
nearest-fighter catches and per-port HUD. The main thread's stack now
fits. The multi-fighter camera, teams, handicap and the player-only
effects remain.
