# RE-527 — A two-cycle shade × texel-lerp combiner is lowered by baking the lerp into the texture; two-cycle pass-through alpha is accepted

Status: COMPLETE on host and PPSSPP; physical PSP not re-run
Topics: combiner lowering, two-cycle, Yoster clouds, texture bake, pack v117
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-rom/src/mesh.rs`, `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/pack.rs`

## Question

Yoster's clouds (`llGRYosterMapCloudDisplayList`) drew with dark fringes and no blue tint. Why, and how can the PSP draw them?

## Findings

- The list is two-cycle. Cycle 1 lerps the texel colour between two constants, `(ENV-PRIM)*TEX0 + PRIM`; cycle 2 multiplies that by SHADE. Alpha is the cycle-1 texel alpha passed through, under `G_RM_AA_ZB_XLU_SURF2`.
- `Combined::mul` refused shade × lerp, so the colour fell back to shade × texel (dark fringes, no tint), and `combiner_alpha_blend` declined every two-cycle combiner, so the soft alpha was lost.
- Lowering: `combiner_rgb_shade_texel_lerp` requires an s term and an s·t term and no constant or texel-only term. `romtool` bakes `rgb_lerp_texel` into the texture (RDP fixed point `((a-b)*c + (d<<8) + 0x80) >> 8`, palette and direct paths; `TexKey` carries `rgb_lerp`). The PSP then draws shade × baked texel.
- Alpha: a two-cycle combiner is accepted when cycle 2's alpha is `(A-B)*0 + COMBINED`, i.e. `((lo>>18)&7, lo&7) == (7, 0)`. The same rule already applied to `combiner_alpha_is_texel_times_prim`.
- Result: MAE against the N64 frame at tics 20/59/101/140 fell from 19.05/16.89/16.60/16.32 to 14.51/10.77/9.87/9.83.

## Deviation

The lerp is baked at pack time. A material whose MObj swaps the image or palette at runtime would draw the un-lerped data. The Yoster clouds have no material animation. `romtool pack` audits every lerped prim (`count_rgb_lerp_swaps`) and fails on a mat-anim palette swap; image swaps convert through the same lerp. US ROM: 11 lerped prims, 0 image swaps, 0 palette swaps; no prim draws un-lerped.
