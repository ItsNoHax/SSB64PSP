# RE-510 — Fighter slope contour: leg IK, FULL TopN tilt, motion_id -2 animation hold

Status: COMPLETE, bit-exact against N64 RDRAM traces
Topics: fighters, animation, collision, reverse-engineering
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/slope_contour.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/fighter.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/collision.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/reaction.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/kirby.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/skeleton.rs`, `crates/ssb-tablegen/src/motion.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`

## Question

What does `mpCommonUpdateFighterSlopeContour` do to a grounded fighter, and where does it sit in the frame?

## Findings

- `func_ovl2_800EBD08` (ftparam.c:2523) rewrites the thigh/knee rotations
  every grounded frame from the floor slope under each foot, and the FULL
  flag also tilts TopN `rotate.x`. Ported in `slope_contour.rs` (matrix
  helpers bit-exact, leg twist 800EBB3C/EBA6C, point query 800EBC0C,
  `world_to_local` 800EE018). The foot offset test is 800DDF74, which reads
  FTAttributes words 0x31C/0x320 (`unk_0x31C/0x320`, generated by
  `ssb-tablegen`, D-048).
- Frame order: proc_map, then proc_slope, then TransformAll, then
  effects/accessory. SET_SLOPE_CONTOUR zeroes TopN `rotate.x` unless FULL
  stays on. Pending-slope sites (throw/catch) run the step immediately.
- The Training floor is not flat: y 1399.25 at x=-210, 1398.12 at x=516, so
  the |ty| >= 1e-4 test passes and the IK runs every grounded frame there.
- motion_id -2 hold: statuses with no figatree (CatchWait, CaptureWait, and
  Guard/Down waits) get no PlayAnim; ftParamUpdateAnimKeys' else branch
  (ftparam.c:431-476) re-applies each held AObj every frame with anim_wait set
  to AOBJ_ANIM_END, restoring it afterwards, so the IK input is a fixed point.
  The first tick of a status does not hold (PlayAnim already advanced the old
  clip); holding from frame one left 9 frames different (max 0.0033). Poke
  experiment: rotations written into the thigh are overwritten next frame.
- Three bugs found by the Jungle FULL-tilt trace and fixed:
  1. `normal_of` was 1 ulp off; now the mpCollisionGetFCAngle formula
     (mpcollision.c:613-644): `1/sqrtf(py^2+1)`, `(py*s, s)`, then
     `syVectorNorm3D`. No golden moved from this alone.
  2. DownWaitD/DownWaitU preserve slope contour
     (FTCOMMON_DOWNBOUNCE_STATUS_FLAGS, ftcommondownwaitbounce.c:9/51); the N64
     Fox kept `rotate.x` 0.34326 in DownWaitU.
  3. Kirby SpecialN/AirN Catch and Eat pass FTSTATUS_PRESERVE_SLOPECONTOUR.
- N64 traces (patched decomp): Mario 399/399, Samus 419/419 and 344/344 (thigh
  and knee joints exact), Jungle jm1 899/899 (TopN rotate.x, position and
  Mario legs exact, 1513 fits) and Fox jm2 779/779 (20254/20254 joints).
- Goldens: 19 rows changed, all deterministic and all leg IK; f1-training-venusaur
  also FULL tilt.
- Hold golden: `f1-training-catch-slope` (`catchslope@110`), where Mario grabs
  on Jungle's left slope, CatchWait from 97, contour 3
  (`ftcommoncatch2.c:73` PRESERVE_SLOPECONTOUR). Guard cannot exercise the hold:
  `ftCommonGuardOnSetStatus` passes FTSTATUS_PRESERVE_NONE, so its contour is 0.
  Mutations (2x px): no hold 2036; held branch skipped 2036; hold from
  the status's first tick 251160. Host test `catch_wait_holds_the_slope_legs`
  fails on the no-hold mutation.
