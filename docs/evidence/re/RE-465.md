## Question

How do How to Play (`scExplain`) and the auto demo (`scAutoDemo`) run, and
how do they join the title's attract loop that RE-462 left skipping them?

## Data and pack

- Pack v105 (46,795,968 bytes) adds How to Play's wallpaper (file 88's
  sprite, `GRKind` 11). The stage itself was already packed.
- `ssb64-menus.pak` gains `MenuScene::Explain` (727,144 bytes): 26 captions
  and markers, the stick's, spark's and overlay's textures as sprites keyed
  by texture offset, and three blobs: the phase table with each caption
  pointer resolved to its graphics-file offset, the input scripts, and the
  graphics file up to `0x5C48` for the material animations. The auto demo's
  names were already resident (`campaign::NAMES`).
- ROM tests: the table's captions are all packed, the phases total 4450
  frames, both scripts parse, the taps fire on frames 15, 39, 63 (tap up)
  and 15, 40, 65 (tap forward), the spark runs 8 frames.

## N64 comparison

Unmodified ROM idling from boot (`tools/run-n64-headless.sh`, scratch
config): How to Play starts on frame 4337 of an unscreenshotted run. The
PSP's `explain@N` captures land on the same phase, caption, stick state and
camera framing as the N64 at explain frames 285, 486, 687, 888, 1089 and
1290. An RDRAM trace of both fighters (battle state `0x8018E7F0`, players at
`+0x20`, stride `0x74`, damage at `+0x38`) against the PSP's `re465` log
lines matches every damage event through explain frame 783 (2, 4, 8, 10, 12) and
positions exactly while the scripts walk, then shows three divergences in
shared fighter code, not in the scene:

1. Appear: the N64 rises from frame 0 (y 27.6 ahead), peaks at 414 not 420
   and lands on the floor (y -6) at frame 105; the port lands at the entry
   position (y 0) and settles later.
2. Dash end: the N64 walks on the frame Luigi's dash ends (frame 157); the
   port spends one frame in Wait, leaving Luigi 15.6 units behind.
3. Turn: walking left, the stick tapped right at frame 254 gives the N64 a
   five-frame Turn before Dash (frame 259); the port dashes out of Turn on
   its second frame, 4 frames early, because `ftCommonTurnProcInterrupt`'s
   pivot gate (`is_allow_turn_direction`, `lr_dash`) is not ported. Mario
   ends up 191 units right of the N64's; the second jab combo's kick then
   misses (N64 +4 at frame 796), and at frame 1441 Luigi is knocked out,
   which the N64 never does.

The auto demo, captured from frame 8700 of the same idle run: How to Play
ends near frame 8787, Characters' demo shows the two demo fighters (Samus,
then Kirby), the auto demo starts on Dream Land with players 1 and 2 under
30% and 3 and 4 between 40% and 99%, closes in on Samus with her name, then
on Kirby, pulls back, and the N64 logo follows near frame 10951. The PSP's
`autodemo` capture matches the name's place and size, the close-up's
framing and the interface.

The divergences above are out of this batch's scope and are the next gameplay batch
(STATUS.md). How to Play's scripts make a deterministic oracle for them:
`explain@N` with `--log` prints each fighter's status, damage and position.

## Deviations

- The PSP has one pad: any A, B or START tap ends a demo.
- The fade (`lbFadeMakeActor`, display link 10) is drawn at the start of
  link 10 over the battle viewport; the stick, spark and overlay quads are
  drawn as 2D rectangles at their ortho positions (1 unit = 1 pixel).
- The N64 logo and the opening movie (`mnStartup`, 20 `mvOpening*` scenes
  with their own fighters, cameras, key scripts and stages) are not ported:
  the auto demo goes back to the title, whose next demo is How to Play.
- The auto demo's start points are drawn before the world is made, so the
  random draws of its setup are not interleaved with the stage's as on the
  N64; its battle is random on both and was compared for presentation only.
