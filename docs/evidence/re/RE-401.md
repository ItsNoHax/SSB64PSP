# RE-401 — The battle-entry clips in the pack

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, ROM decode, pack test, PPSSPPHeadless goldens unchanged, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: animation, pack, fighter, asset-pipeline
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-rom/src/{anim,anim_table,objanim,pack}.rs`, `tools/gen-anim-table.py`, `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/tests/appear_clips.rs`

## Question

The fighters' Appear entry was parked because its clips are not figatrees
(RE-389, TODO). How are they stored, and what does packing them take?

## Source

- **Where they are.** Each fighter's Appear motions resolve through its
  own motion enum (`nFT<Name>MotionAppearR`…) into `dFT<Name>MotionDescs`:
  - Mario and Luigi: files 633 and 634;
  - Fox: 777 and 778;
  - Donkey Kong: 932 and 933;
  - Samus: 1090 and 1091;
  - Link: 1246 and 1247;
  - Yoshi: 1953 and 1954;
  - Kirby: 1412 and 1413;
  - Pikachu: 2081 and 2082;
  - Jigglypuff: 1505 and 1506;
  - Captain Falcon: 1648 to 1651 (Start and End);
  - Ness: 1799 to 1803 (Start, Wait and End).
- **What they are.** They are `FTANIM_FLAG_ANIMJOINT` clips: 32-bit
  `AObjEvent32` streams, not figatrees.
- **Correction to the parked finding.** The TODO said the table led with
  three runtime joints (TransN, XRotN, YRotN). The ROM says one:
  - Every table is one entry longer than the fighter's model joints (Link's
    two, the last unused).
  - Under a one-joint lead, Mario's clip leaves model joints 3 and 9
    unanimated, as all his figatree clips do.
  - A three-joint lead would leave joint 1 unanimated, which every
    figatree clip animates.
- **Lengths.**
  - Each joint of a clip ends on the same tick: 121 for the one-part
    entries, 91 and 31 for Captain Falcon's Start and End, and 41, 51 and
    31 for Ness's Start, Wait and End.
  - The end is where `anim_frame` reaches the stream's end, which
    `ftCommonAppearProcUpdate` waits for.

## Implementation

- **Slots.** `tools/gen-anim-table.py` adds the seven entry slots
  (`anim::SLOT_APPEAR_R` to `SLOT_APPEAR_WAIT`, 607 to 613), and
  `anim::is_anim_joint_slot` marks them.
- **Length helper.** `objanim::script_end_tick` ticks a 32-bit stream to
  its `End`.
- **romtool.** It packs these slots with the lead joint unbound and the
  model joints after it, at the common end tick less one. It rejects a
  clip whose joints end unevenly.
- **Pack v63.** It is 28,254,080 bytes, SHA-256
  `8bfe1acc108ec0305fd7ab8b96c06077c2961d6d244bcb11d0bdfafef1130197`.
- **Per-slot lengths.** `anim::decode_fighter` reads these slots' lengths
  the same way. A first pack build that ran the figatree decoder on them
  dropped 24 fighters' descriptors and changed 79 goldens.

## Deviations

- **Poly fighters.** Poly Kirby and Poly Pikachu's entry clips do not
  match their skeletons and are not packed. The Poly fighters make no
  entry (`nFTCommonStatusEntryNull`).
- **Not played yet.** Nothing plays the clips. The next batch adds a
  32-bit mode to the fighter skeleton, the entry statuses and the entry
  focus timing.

## Verification

- **Pack test.** `tests/appear_clips.rs` checks, for every playable
  fighter:
  - the lead joint is unbound;
  - joint 1 is the first model joint;
  - Mario's two gaps;
  - the lengths (120, Captain Falcon 90 and 30, Ness 40, 50 and 30).
- **Goldens.** All 106 goldens are unchanged.
- **Gates.** Workspace tests, Clippy and both PSP builds pass. The
  missing-slot census counts 62 entry clips: 29 for the twelve and 33 for
  Metal Mario, the Poly fighters and Giant Donkey Kong.
