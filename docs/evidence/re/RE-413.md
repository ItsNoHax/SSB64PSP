# RE-413 — The in-game particle runtime and the first hit effects

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, packed `efcommon` bank run in ROM tests, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice, RNG-isolation A/B captures, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: effect, particle, fighter, combat, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/{particle,particle_tests,effect,effect_tests,combat,hurtbox,ko,ko_tests,hazard}.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/item/hit.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/motion/mod.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/tests/particle_runtime.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/{particles,meshdraw}.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `tests/golden/f1-{training-jab,training-rebirthblast,vs-cpu,vs-four,vs-team,vs-results}.png`

## Question

The particle banks are packed (RE-172–189), but no match runs them, so a
hit draws no spark and a KO has no streaks or star sparkle (RE-412). How
does a battle run `LBParticle`, which hit effects does the hit pipeline
make, and in what order against the game's one random sequence?

## Source

- **Pools.** `efParticleInitAll` (`ef/efparticle.c`) allocates 112
  particles, 24 generators and 80 transforms (`lbParticleAllocStructs`,
  `lbParticleAllocGenerators`, `lbParticleAllocTransforms`). The particle
  and generator `GObj`s sit on link 0 with `func_run`s
  (`lbParticleStructFuncRun`, `lbParticleGeneratorFuncRun`), which
  `gcRunAll` runs before the process queue (`sys/objman.c`), so particles
  update at the start of every frame. A particle's `bank_id >> 3` picks one
  of 16 lists (`LBPARTICLE_MASK_GENLINK(n)` is list `n + 1`).
- **Banks.** `efDisplayInitAll` loads `efcommon` into
  `gEFManagerParticleBankID`. `grCommonSetupInitAll` runs first, so on Dream
  Land, Hyrule and Yoshi's Island the stage's bank is bank 0 and `efcommon`
  bank 1; the id only has to be consistent.
- **Draw.** `efDisplayInitAll` draws lists 0 and 2 at display link 18 and
  list 1 at 15 with `G_RM_CLD_SURF` (blend, no depth), list 3 at 25 with
  `G_RM_XLU_SURF` and list 4 at 10 with `G_RM_AA_ZB_XLU_SURF`.
  `lbParticleDrawTextures` projects each particle through its transform's
  matrix (`syMatrixTraRotRpyRScaF`) and the camera, culls outside `[-1, 1]`
  in x and y or `[0, 1]` in depth, and draws a texture rectangle of half
  size `size / w` times the composed matrix's column lengths, flipped when
  the transform is scaled negative, doubled and mirrored for
  `MASKS`/`MASKT`. The combine is `G_CC_MODULATEIA_PRIM`, or
  `(PRIM - ENV) * TEXEL0 + ENV` with `ENVCOLOR`; the alpha gate is
  threshold 8 (ENV alpha with `ALPHABLEND`, dither with `DITHER`).
- **Effect structs.** `efManagerInitEffects` allocates 38 `EFStruct`s; a
  maker that does not force needs five free (`efManagerGetNextStructAlloc`).
  Each hit maker (`efManagerDamageNormalLightMakeEffect`, `...Heavy...`,
  `...Fire...`, `...Electric...`, `...Coin...`, `efManagerSetOffMakeEffect`)
  starts its script under a new transform whose `proc_dead` frees the
  struct, runs the first update (`LBParticleProcessStruct`), then places
  the transform, scales it by damage and, for most, adds
  `efManagerDefaultProcUpdate` (priority 3) with a random velocity. The
  heavy spark's `proc_dead` makes a light one where it was; the coin's
  makes `efManagerDustExpandSmallMakeEffect` 200 units above.
- **Choice.** `ftMainProcessHitCollisionStatsMain` (`ft/ftmain.c`) makes one
  effect per logged hit at `gmCollisionGetCommonImpactPosition` of the
  attack point and the damage box's centre: fire, electric or coin by
  element, a fighter's slash (`efManagerDamageSlashMakeEffect`, a display
  effect), and otherwise a light spark under 180 knockback or a heavy one,
  plus, for a fighter's attack with `fgm_level > 0`, the orbs and the sparks
  (metal dust for a metal fighter) — all display effects. A weapon or item
  hit makes one only with `is_hitlag_victim`: only Link's Boomerang and two
  Pokémon weapons set it for weapons, and items take `is_give_hitlag`.
- **Set-offs.** `ftMainUpdateDamageStatFighter` makes a set-off for a hit on
  a non-normal box or a soaked one, `ftMainUpdateAttackStatFighter` one per
  stopped side of a clank, and `ftMainUpdateShieldStatFighter` one at the
  shield joint's point.
- **KO.** `efManagerDeadExplodeMakeEffect` starts
  `dEFManagerDeadExplodeGenID[(type % 2) * 4 + player]` on list 2 under a
  `Ready` transform turned like the blast, with no struct.
  `efManagerSparkleWhiteDeadMakeEffect` starts 0x5C on list 2, scaled 5.
- **Game set.** `ifCommonBattleInterfaceProcUpdate` pauses every process
  but resumes the particle `GObj`s with every list skipped but 2 and 3, so
  the blast's streaks play on through the "Game Set" freeze.

## Implementation

- **`ssb_game::particle`.** The three pools with the source's free lists
  and 16 lists, `lbParticleMakeStruct` and the makers,
  `lbParticleUpdateStruct` (every opcode, waits, size and colour blends,
  lifetime, gravity and friction), the eject paths with `proc_dead`, the
  generators (kinds 0, 1, 3 and 4), `syUtilsArcTan`'s rational
  approximation, the skip bits, and `lbParticleDrawTextures`' matrix and
  projection (`prepare_draw`, `project`). Per-particle state is some 100
  bytes; the pools are about 30 KB, built in place on the heap
  (`Particles::write_new`).
- **`ssb_game::effect`.** The 38-struct pool and the makers listed above,
  the three `proc_dead`s, `efManagerDefaultProcUpdate`, and
  `EffectRuntime`, which runs a frame's start (particles, generators, then
  the struct moves).
- **Hit effects.** `HurtHit` carries the box's world centre; each log entry
  carries a `LogEffect` (impact point, attacker's player, `fgm_level`, the
  slash angle). `process_hit_collision` queues the chosen `HitEffect`s on
  the victim after its search's set-offs, and `finish_frame_with` hands each
  fighter's queue to a `HitEffectSink` before any `ftMainProcParams`, which
  draws random numbers too. `AttackColl::fgm_level` is read from
  `MakeAttackColl` and `SetAttackCollSoundLevel`.
- **`psp-runtime::particles`.** `PackBanks` (runtime bank 0 is pack bank 0)
  and `draw`, which projects with the battle projection `draw_training`
  sets and draws lists 4, 1, 0, 2, 3 through
  `meshdraw::draw_particle_rect`: 2D sprites, blended, no depth test,
  `GreaterOrEqual` 8, mirrored halves for the mask flags.
- **`psp-game`.** The `Hud` owns the particles and structs, reset with each
  battle. `training_frame` runs the particles before `training_step`,
  passes the runtime to the hit pass, makes the KO particles in
  `KoEffects::observe_with`, and runs lists 2 and 3 in the game-set freeze.
  `draw_particles` draws after the KO effects, under the screen flash.

## Evidence

- **The bank at work.** `crates/ssb-rom/tests/particle_runtime.rs` runs
  every maker a match reaches on the packed bank (119 scripts) to its end:
  the light spark lasts 18 frames, the heavy with the light it becomes 23,
  fire 19, electric 16, the coin with its dust 24, set-off 15, the blast's
  streaks 80 (17 particles, 5 generators, all kind 0) and the sparkle 25.
  None sets `VORTEX` or `ATTACH` or makes a vortex generator. Every drawn particle is `ENVCOLOR`
  (with ENV alpha 0) or plain; none uses `NOISE`, `DITHER` or
  `ALPHABLEND`. The set-off and the blast use `MASKS` and `MASKT`.
- **Capacity.** Four heavy hits and four set-offs every eight frames for
  two seconds, beside two blasts and a sparkle, peak at 83 of 112
  particles and 23 of 80 transforms, and no maker is refused.
- **What moved the goldens.** A build that restores the random seed around
  every particle run and maker leaves `f1-vs-four` and `f1-vs-team`
  identical to their old goldens; with the draw also off, `f1-training-jab`
  matches. `f1-vs-results` still changed until the game-set freeze's
  particle run was isolated too. Every change is the sparks themselves or
  the source's random draws.

## Deviations

- **Not drawn.** The display hit effects: the slash, the orbs, the sparks
  and the metal dust (their random draws are missing too). Weapon set-offs
  (clanks and shield hits) and Link's Boomerang's hit spark. The fighters'
  motion-event effects (dust, `efManagerDustLightMakeEffect` and others) and
  the weapons' own (`efManagerImpactShockMakeEffect`,
  `efManagerDustExpandSmallMakeEffect` on a wall). The stages' particle
  banks.
- **Order.** The KO particles are made after the fighters' processes, not
  inside the dead status setters.
- **Pool.** Display effects (the blast, the halo, the entry effects) take no
  `EFStruct`, so the five-free rule counts only particle effects.
- **Draw.** The combine's alpha is `PRIM.a * TEXEL0.a`, exact while ENV
  alpha is 0 (every script a match reaches); `NOISE` is drawn as the plain
  combine, `DITHER` without a gate; list 4 draws without its depth test (no
  common maker uses it). The projection is the PSP battle projection
  (38°, the pillarbox's aspect); the depth cull uses the original camera's
  planes (256, 39,936).
- **Not modelled.** The vortex generator and `LBPARTICLE_FLAG_VORTEX`, the
  attach `DObj`s and the distance opcodes (unused by any script a match
  reaches), a generator's `dobj`.
- **Colour animations.** `ftParamSetColAnimID` on hit (the electric
  skeleton, the hit-status flashes) is left for the next batch.

## Verification

- **Host tests.** Ten particle tests (lists, the 112 cap, lifetime,
  `DEAD`, size and colour blends, gravity and friction, one `proc_dead` per
  transform, a generator's rate and life, `arc_tan2`, projection and cull),
  fourteen effect tests (placement, damage scales, the five-free rule,
  struct release, heavy to light, coin to dust, the blast's script and
  turn, the element and knockback choice, orbs and sparks, set-offs for an
  invincible box and a clank, the display effects making nothing) and two
  KO tests. Two ROM tests run the packed bank.
- **Tests fail when broken.** Raising the 180 knockback line, dropping a
  clank set-off, allowing four free structs, dropping the coin's 200 units
  and dropping `lifetime + 1` each fail a test. The draft's `syUtilsArcTan`
  transcription (a misplaced term) failed `arc_tan2`, and was fixed.
- **Goldens.** Six rebaselined, each identical in both captures:
  - `f1-training-jab`: a red and orange light spark in a ring at Mario's
    fist on Wario's back.
  - `f1-training-rebirthblast`: yellow stars rise along the blast column,
    under the flash.
  - `f1-vs-cpu`: a small blue-white spark between Mario and Wario.
  - `f1-vs-four`, `f1-vs-team`: the battles diverge from the source's
    random draws (proved above).
  - `f1-vs-results`: Kirby's win pose shades by one or two levels, from the
    freeze's random draws.
  - The other 105 are unchanged; 111 of 111 match twice.
- **Gates.** Workspace tests with an absolute `SSB64_ROM` and one thread
  (1,442 passed), pinned 1.98.0 Clippy with warnings denied, rustfmt, and
  both PSP release builds pass.
- **Size.** The release `run` is 25,920 bytes (was 20,064). No pack change.

## Conclusion

A match runs the source's particle runtime on the common bank. Every
fighter's hits make their fire, electric, coin, light and heavy sparks and
their set-offs, and a KO draws its streaks and the star sparkle. The
display hit effects, the motion-event dust and the damage colour
animations remain.
