# RE-421 — The shield bubble draws with no depth test; the battle near plane is 256

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, ROM display list, RDRAM display-list trace and joint dump from a warp-boot build, N64 captures under Rice and GLideN64, packed-asset ROM tests, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice and broken by restoring the old seed, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: effects, rendering, camera, asset-pack, shield, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-rom/src/{mesh,pack}.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/tests/effect_cld_seed.rs`, `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/camera.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/particles.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `tests/golden/*.png`

## Question

RE-420 kept the battle projection's near plane at 1. With
`dGMCameraPerspDefault`'s 256, the fighter drew crisp over its shield
bubble in `shield`, `nessmagnet` and `vsshield`, but an N64 capture shows
the bubble tinting the whole body. With near 1, the GE's 16-bit depth is
too coarse to separate the bubble from the fighter, so the two tied and
the bubble won. How does the N64 really draw the bubble over its fighter?

## Source

- **Link and order.** `dEFManagerShieldEffectDesc` is on DL link 15. The
  battle camera (`gmCameraDefaultProcDisplay`) draws passes in order: links
  12–6 (fighters, stage layer 1), then 15, 14 and 13, then 18–16. Within a
  link, display objects draw by priority, highest first.
  `efManagerMakeEffect` adds every effect at priority 2.
- **Link 15's state.** `efDisplayMakeCLD` puts `efDisplayCLDProcDisplay`
  on links 15 and 18 at priority 3, and `efDisplayMakeXLU` puts
  `efDisplayXLUProcDisplay` there at priority 0. The first sets head 1 to
  `G_RM_CLD_SURF`, `G_AC_THRESHOLD` against blend alpha 8, and clears
  `G_ZBUFFER`. The second restores `G_RM_AA_ZB_XLU_SURF` and sets
  `G_ZBUFFER`. So every priority-2 effect on those links draws its head-1
  lists after the first. The trail effects
  (`efManagerPikachuThunderTrailProcDisplay`, Ness's) set
  `G_RM_AA_XLU_SURF` and put the CLD mode back afterwards, as does
  `efManagerYoshiShieldProcDisplay`.
- **The bubble's list** (file 163 + 0x248, raw bytes): `D9DDFFFF 00000000`
  (clears lighting and smooth shading), `E2001E01 00000001`
  (`G_AC_THRESHOLD`), `G_SETCOMBINE fc309661 552eff7f`, PRIM
  `FFFFFFC0`, blend alpha 8, a 32 × 32 I texture, `G_VTX` of four vertices
  at 0x208 — (30, 30, 0), (30, −30, 0), (−30, −30, 0), (−30, 30, 0) — two
  triangles, then it restores lighting and `G_AC_NONE`. It sets no render
  mode and no `G_ZBUFFER`.
- **Matrix and position.** The root is matrix function 79
  (`func_ovl0_800C994C`): `YRotN`'s whole world matrix
  (`parts->mtx_translate`), with `gGCScaleX` its x-axis length. The drawn
  node is kind 44: after `gSPMvpRecalc` it rewrites the MVP's rows 0–2 with
  the projection's diagonal times the accumulated scale and keeps row 3.
  The quad is therefore camera-facing at `YRotN`, and since its vertices
  have z 0, its depth is the joint's. It has no offset toward the camera.

## Evidence

- **RDRAM display-list trace.** A warp-boot build of the decomp (Training
  on Dream Land, Mario against a Mario CPU; Z held from frame 100 and the
  stick at (40, 40) from 108 to 118, injected in `ftmain.c` from the
  D-pad) stored nothing extra. The whole 8 MB RDRAM was dumped at frame
  140, and the frame's display list was walked from task buffer 0 with
  the RSP's geometry-mode and othermode state tracked. The bubble's
  `G_VTX` runs with geometry mode `0x000404` (`G_ZBUFFER` clear) and render
  mode `0x00504341` (`G_RM_CLD_SURF` with `G_AC_THRESHOLD`: no `Z_CMP`, no
  `Z_UPD`). The commands before it are `efDisplayCLDProcDisplay`'s
  (`E200001C 00504340`, `E2001E01 1`, `F9 …08`, `D9FFFFFE 0`), then the
  shield's own PRIM, ENV, matrix, `D5000001` and 12 `G_MW_MATRIX` words.
  Without `G_ZBUFFER` the RSP sends triangles without depth, and without
  `Z_CMP` the RDP compares none. This is the command stream the hardware
  runs, independent of the emulator's renderer.
- **Joint depths.** The same build copied `YRotN`'s matrix and every
  joint's world position to 0x80700000. At frame 140 `YRotN` is at
  (−176.4, 1617.7, 0.0), scale 9.48; the fighter's joints span z −49.7 to
  +51.6. Under the camera matrix, the near joints (z +43 to +52, clip
  z/w 0.829) are in front of the quad (0.8315). So a depth test would draw
  the near half of the fighter crisp, as the port did with near 256.
- **Two renderers.** Rice and GLideN64 both show the bubble over the whole
  body. The CLD state explains why, and a hardware RDP would do the same.
- **N64 captures versus the port.**
  - *Shield: match.* N64 frames 112–118, with the stick held, show Mario
    turned away inside a red bubble that tints him from his hat to his
    shoes. The rebaselined `f1-training-shield` shows the same pose and
    tint. The camera framing differs, because the two scenes are not the
    same tick.
  - *PSI Magnet: match.* N64 frame 125 shows the green field over Ness and
    over the floor in front of him. `f1-training-ness-magnet` now draws its
    lower arcs over the floor, which the depth test used to hide.
  - *Sing: match.* N64 frame 125 shows the rings and notes over
    Jigglypuff and the ground. `f1-training-purin` now draws the lower
    rings and the purple note over the ground.
  - *Fighter overlaps: match in kind.* A Training jab route on the N64
    overlaps the two Marios. Exact depth decides which parts are in front.
    The two scenes' poses differ, so they are not compared pixel for
    pixel.
- **Other depth-sensitive draws at near 256.** The 45 other changed
  goldens differ by 120 to 2,252 pixels (at 2×). All the differences are
  on fighters (arm, hand and body occlusion; two overlapping fighters) or
  on stage edges under a fighter: the bridge in `fighter-select` and the
  tilted platform's front edge in `vs-yoshi`. The halo, its rays, the KO
  blast, the star KO, the entry pipe and the particles do not change. The
  entry effects are on link 10, which keeps the camera's Z-buffered mode,
  and the particles' list 4 already used `Camera::ge_planes`, now
  256/39,936.

## Implementation

- **`Head1Seed`** (`mesh::InitialMaterial::head1`): `CameraXlu`, which is
  the camera's `G_RM_AA_ZB_XLU_SURF` and stays the default; `Inherit`,
  which `WEAPON_EXTERNAL` uses and which replaces the old
  `initial != WEAPON_EXTERNAL` test; and `EffectCld`, which sets
  `G_RM_CLD_SURF` (0x00504340) and the alpha-compare threshold and clears
  `G_ZBUFFER` on the head-1 stream. `SHIELD_EXTERNAL` uses `EffectCld`.
- **`romtool`'s `EFFECT_CLD_GRAPHS`**: the 14 other effects in the `EFDesc`
  table that draw their `DObjDLLink` lists on links 15 and 18
  (DamageSlash, DamageFlyMDust, CatchSwirl, ReflectBreak, DeadExplode,
  ItemGetSwirl, Thunder Shock, Vulcan Jab, the Final Cutter trail, the
  grapple beam, Sing, PSI Magnet, the PK Thunder wave and the spin
  attack). Effects that draw on head 0 (`gcDrawDObjTreeForGObj`,
  `lbCommonDObjScaleXProcDisplay`) are not affected by the seed.
- **Pack v73** (34,455,072 bytes, SHA-256
  `f07a105b814d8a5e42577bbc389ee0b0006cf673c80be7ae25aa026823c793de`,
  rebuilt twice with identical results; layout unchanged). Compared with
  v72, exactly 31 prims change. They are the lists among these graphs that
  set no render mode, and they lose `DEPTH_TEST`, `ZMODE_XLU` and
  `Z_BUFFER`. With the shield seed alone, only the bubble's prim changes
  (0x492D1 to 0x12C1). Yoshi's egg shield (338 + 0xA860) sets its own
  non-Z render mode, so it is unchanged.
- **Projection.** `camera::GE_NEAR` is removed. Training's battle
  projection and the particles' `ge_planes` use `DEFAULT_NEAR` (256) and
  `DEFAULT_FAR` (39,936).

## Verification

- **Tests.** `mesh::tests::an_effect_head1_list_draws_under_the_cld_seed_with_no_depth_test`
  checks that a head-1 list with no render mode tests depth under
  `CameraXlu` and does not under `EffectCld`, with the threshold set.
  `tests/effect_cld_seed.rs` checks the packed bubble, Sing (351 + 0x1F38)
  and PSI Magnet (352 + 0x8E0) prims: no depth test, no depth write, no
  `Z_BUFFER`, translucent, and the bubble with its threshold. v72's bubble
  prim (0x492D1) has `DEPTH_TEST`, so this test fails on v72.
- **Attribution.** With the new pack but near 1, only `purin` (1,904
  pixels) and `ness-magnet` (568) change. With the old seed at near 1,
  the bubble already won the depth ties, as RE-420 found.
- **Negative test.** A pack built with `EffectCld` mapped back to the
  camera's Z-buffered mode, at near 256, changes `f1-training-shield`
  (1,652 pixels), `f1-vs-shield-damage` (2,420), `f1-training-purin`
  (2,464) and `f1-training-ness-magnet` (2,188) from the new goldens. The

## Deviations

- This deviation predates this batch and does not come from it.
  `draw_stage_animated` draws all four stage layers before the fighters.
  On the N64, layer 3 (link 17, `grDisplayLayer3*ProcDisplay`,
  `G_RM_AA_OPA_SURF` with `G_ZBUFFER` cleared) draws after the fighters
  and the link-15 effects, and over them. Dream Land has a layer 3
  (file 108 + 0xD188, nine nodes). Layer 3 writes no depth, so the old
  depth-tested bubble drew over it in the port too, and the order is
  unchanged. No compared capture shows an effect or a fighter crossing
  it. This is in TODO.

## Conclusion

The N64 does not depth-test the shield bubble. `efDisplayCLDProcDisplay`,
the priority-3 display object on links 15 and 18, leaves `G_RM_CLD_SURF`
with `G_ZBUFFER` cleared, and the bubble's list, which sets neither, draws
under it. So does every other link-15/18 `DObjDLLink` effect whose list
sets no mode. The converter now seeds those head-1 lists with that state,
and the battle projection uses the original's planes, 256 to 39,936. The
bubble, the PSI Magnet field and Sing's rings cover their fighter and the
ground in front, as in the N64 captures, and fighter parts are ordered by
exact depth.
