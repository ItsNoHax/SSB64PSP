# RE-414 — The fighters' colour animations and the electric skeleton

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, ROM relocation reads, host tests broken and restored, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: fighter, combat, animation, effect, pack, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/{colanim,colanim_scripts,colanim_tests,attack,combat,hurtbox,reaction,status,fighter,pikachu,kirby,kirby_copy,samus,grab,hazard,capture_yoshi,ko,ko_tests}.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/motion/{mod,scripts}.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/{fighter,pack,scene_deps}.rs`, `tools/{gen-colanim-scripts,gen-motion-scripts}.py`, `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/meshdraw.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `psp-asset-viewer/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`, `tests/golden/r2-{mario,samus,kirby}-skeleton.png`

## Question

RE-412 ported `GMColAnim` for the two KO scripts only. Which colour
animations does a match start (`ftParamCheckSetFighterColAnimID`,
`ftParamResetStatUpdateColAnim`, the motion scripts' `SetColAnim`), with
which scripts, priorities and lifetimes, and how does the electric damage
animation draw the fighter's skeleton?

## Source

- **The interpreter.** `ftMainUpdateColAnim` (`ft/ftmain.c`) also runs
  loops and subroutines on one stack of five words (`p_subroutine`,
  `loop_count` and `p_goto` share it: a loop takes two, a call one),
  `SetColor2`, `ClearLight`, `PlayFGM`, `SetSkeletonID` and the two
  `Effect` events (`ftParamMakeEffect`). `ClearColorAll` clears both
  colours and `skeleton_id`, not the light. `ftMainRunUpdateColAnim` gives
  an ended animation to `ftParamResetStatUpdateColAnim`.
- **The table.** `dGMColScriptsDescs` (`gm/gmcolscripts.c`) has 86 entries
  (US). It is indexed by id, and the ids are its order. The
  `GMColAnimKind` names for ids 41 to 49 do not match the scripts there:
  id 41 is `...FoxSpecialLw` (the enum calls it `DonkeySpecialNLoop`), 44
  `...LinkSpecialHi`, 48 and 49 Donkey Kong's. The `relocData` motion files
  use the enum names, which give the ROM's numbers, so the fighters' motion
  scripts set the right scripts. Two scripts run off their end into the
  next array (`FoxSpecialHiStart`, `PikachuSpecialHiStart`).
- **Standing states.** `ftParamResetStatUpdateColAnim` resets, then starts
  in turn: the hit-status flicker for `ftParamGetBestHitStatusPart`
  (invincible or intangible), the charge flash for a full Giant Punch or
  Charge Shot (Kirby's copies too), PSI Magnet's hold while `is_absorb`,
  healing, the Star, `NoDamage` while `invincible_tics` or
  `intangible_tics` run, and the Hammer; each wins only on priority.
- **Call sites.**
  - `ftMainSetStatus`: `SetHitStatusPartAll`/`SetHitStatusAll(Normal)`
    (which start `HitStatusNormal`, priority 30), then an unlocked
    animation ends through `ftParamResetStatUpdateColAnim` unless the call
    carries `FTSTATUS_PRESERVE_COLANIM`.
  - `ftParamSetHitStatusColAnim`, from both hit-status setters and so from
    the motion scripts' hit-status events: a roll or dodge flickers white.
  - `ftParamSetTimedHitStatusInvincible`/`...Intangible` start `NoDamage`
    (priority 11): the 120 frames after a respawn replace the rebirth glow
    (priority 10). `ftMainProcUpdateInterrupt` ends it when the last timer
    runs out (`colanim_id == 0xA`).
  - `ftCommonDamageInitDamageVars`: for a hit that did damage,
    `ftCommonDamageCheckElementSetColAnim` (fire and ice by damage level,
    electric through `dFTParamSkeletonColAnimIDs`, anything else
    `DamageCommon`), then `ftCommonDamageCheckMakeScreenFlash` over 160
    knockback. A hit that leaves the status, a held or holding fighter's
    hit, and Inhale's capture damage flash through
    `ftCommonDamageSetDamageColAnim`, which runs a frame at once.
  - `ShieldBreakFly`, `FuraFura`, `FuraSleep`, `FallSpecial`, the fast fall
    (`ftPhysicsCheckSetFastFall`, a frame at once), full Giant Punch and
    Charge Shot charges (and Kirby's copies), Thunder Jolt (and Kirby's),
    Quick Attack's start (with `SetHitStatusAll(Intangible)`), Stone's
    health flashes (`ftKirbySpecialLwUpdateColAnim`, US 22 and 10), losing a
    copy, and the motion scripts' `SetColAnim` (8-bit id, 18-bit length) and
    `ResetColAnim`, which `ftMainUpdateMotionEventsForward` skips.
  - 130 `ftMainSetStatus` calls keep the animation: every map switch of a
    special that has one, and a move's later statuses (Fox's Reflector and
    Fire Fox, Giant Punch, Charge Shot, Stone, PSI Magnet and PK Thunder,
    the shield-break chain, Ness's entry).
- **Electric skeletons.** `dFTParamSkeletonColAnimIDs` gives 0x14 to most
  fighters, 0x1C to Samus, 0x18 to Kirby and Jigglypuff and 0x10 to the
  others. The decompilation's names for 24 and 28 are swapped: 24
  ("Samus") flashes skeleton 2, which only Kirby's and Jigglypuff's
  `FTAttributes::skeleton` has, and 28 ("Balloon") is Samus's. The shared
  script darkens the fighter (0x141414, alpha 0xFF) for two frames, shows
  skeleton 1 for two, clears for one, loops, then flashes blue and white.
  `ftDisplayMainDrawAll` draws `ftDisplayMainDrawSkeleton` while
  `skeleton_id` is set and `attr->skeleton[id]` exists: every joint from
  `nFTPartsJointCommonStart` draws its `FTSkeleton` entry (a list, or a
  `dls` pair whose first half runs before the joint's matrix) under its
  own materials, or nothing. `FTAttributes::skeleton` (0x344) is three
  words: a gate joint, then the two sets. The ROM gives 12 fighters set 1,
  and Kirby and Jigglypuff set 2; the polygons and Metal Mario have none.

## Implementation

- **`ssb_game::colanim`.** The whole interpreter; `ColAnimId` is the
  table index, with constants named after the table's scripts. The 93
  scripts, the table and the 130 preserve calls are generated by
  `tools/gen-colanim-scripts.py`. `reset_stat_update`, `run_update`,
  `run_update_interrupt`, the element and screen-flash choices, and
  `preserved` (Luigi runs Mario's specials, Giant DK Donkey Kong's).
- **Call sites.** All of the above for the statuses the port reaches. The
  screen flash reaches `KoEffects` through `Fighter::screen_flash`.
  Pikachu's Quick Attack start is now intangible, and his special map
  switches keep the hit status, as the source's flags say.
- **Motion scripts.** `tools/gen-motion-scripts.py` read every
  `GMColAnimKind` and `efKind` name as 0. It now reads both enums; only
  `SetColAnim` and `Effect` words changed.
- **Pack v66.** `ssb_rom::fighter::skeletons` follows
  `FTAttributes::skeleton` through the relocations. `romtool` plans each
  set over the fighter's high-detail graph and converts it with the
  graph's materials and vertex threading, and stores each part as a
  costume override at `SKELETON_COSTUME_BASE + set` (0x101, 0x102): 182
  meshes for 12 fighters, no pre-matrix list with triangles dropped.
  `object_costume_count` ignores them.
- **Drawing.** `draw_fighter_model` draws the skeleton set when the
  animation shows one and the fighter has it; a node without a part draws
  nothing (`meshdraw`). The viewer's `skeleton NAME` scene draws set 1.

## GE approximation

The colour reaches the fighter as RE-412's constant fog: `G_RM_FOG_PRIM_A`
blends every pixel towards `color1` by its alpha, and the GE fog factor is
held at `1 - alpha` across the model. The light turns by
`lr * light_angle_x, light_angle_y`. Not reproduced: `color2` (only Master
Hand's `Yubideppou3` sets it), `FTPARTS_FLAG_NOFOG` parts (they fog too,
including Kirby's skeleton-2 part with flag 0x40) and the per-vertex fog on
the GE.

## Deviations

- **Effect events.** `Effect` and `EffectItemHold` are read and skipped:
  the flames, sparks, dust and sparkles of the fire, electric, charge,
  fast-fall, Up+B and Star scripts are not made. Their makers draw from the
  shared random sequence (`efManagerFlameLRMakeEffect` four times,
  `...ShockSmallMakeEffect` five), so after such an animation the port's
  sequence runs ahead of the original's by those draws.
- **Not reached.** Healing, the Star, the Hammer, the Star Rod and the Bat
  (no such items), Master Hand and the 1P and menu scripts. Kirby's and
  Yoshi's captures and Yoshi's throw hold set intangibility implicitly in
  the port, so they start no flicker; the fighter is hidden there.
- **Other.** `PlayFGM` (no audio). The draw gate on
  `joints[skeleton[0]]->dl` always passes (no model parts). Other
  fighters' map switches still drop `FTSTATUS_PRESERVE_HITSTATUS`.

## Verification

- **Host tests.** Sixteen colour-animation tests: the table and
  priorities, every script to its end, the fire loops and subroutines, the
  skeleton sequence (Mario, Kirby's skeleton 2, Samus's base), element and
  screen-flash choice, `DamageCommon` into the standing states, a roll's
  flicker and its end, the respawn's 120-frame flicker, Mario's Fireball
  flash from his motion script, the preserve table, the charge flash and
  the fast fall. A ROM test reads the skeletons of the 12 fighters against
  `relocData/2xx_*Main.c`.
- **Tests fail when broken.** A `LoopEnd` that never counts down, a
  `NoDamage` expiry that checks the wrong id, Kirby given 0x1C,
  `ftMainSetStatus` writing `hitstatus` without its animation, a skipped
  `SetColAnim`, the `damage != 0` guard dropped, the charge case removed,
  the preserve table ignoring `from`, and a fast fall that does not run its
  first frame each fail their test.
- **Goldens.** Eleven rebaselined, identical in both captures:
  `f1-training-fireball` (Mario full red on the Fireball's first frame),
  `-luigi` and `-samus-shot` (lit from the side by the Fireball and Charge
  Shot scripts), `-link-spin`, `-captain`, `-pikachu`, `-pikachu-air`,
  `-ness-thunder`, `-ness-magnet` (their specials' flashes), `f1-vs-cpu`
  (Mario white with `DamageCommon`'s first flash) and `f1-vs-team` (Mario
  dark red with the shield-break flicker). The new `r2-mario-skeleton`,
  `r2-samus-skeleton` and `r2-kirby-skeleton` show set 1: Mario's white
  skull, ribs and bones; Samus's skinned green frame; Kirby's faceless
  body. 114 of 114 goldens match twice. No game golden shows an electric
  hit.
- **Pack.** v66, 30,491,056 bytes, SHA-256
  `d5e4ba7460a28330a3d9e8818e3e146945234c18f429d2c215989c121d4bfd89`.
- **Gates.** Workspace tests with an absolute `SSB64_ROM` and one thread,
  pinned 1.98.0 Clippy with warnings denied, rustfmt, and both PSP release
  builds pass. The release `run` is 26,440 bytes.

## Conclusion

Every colour animation the port's statuses reach runs from the source's
scripts and table, and the electric skeleton draws for every fighter that
has one. The animations' effect events, and so their random draws, remain.
