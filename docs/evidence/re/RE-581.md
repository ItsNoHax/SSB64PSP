# RE-581 — Master Hand TopN yaw on turns; 1P intro depth clear (batch 85)

Status: VERIFIED (host tests, PPSSPP goldens, N64 capture comparison)
Topics: boss, facing, intro, depth, camera
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/boss.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/spgame/intro.rs`, `psp-game/src/campaign_screen.rs`

## Findings

1. Master Hand facing: the original `ftBossCommonInvertLR` (ftbosscommon.c:10-17), `ftBossMoveSetStatus` (ftbossmove.c:68-70) and the Wait human turn (ftbosswait.c:84-88) flip `lr` and rewrite TopN `rotate.y = lr * 90 deg` in the same call. The port flipped `facing` only; `topn_lr` refreshed at the next status set, so the model and joint-derived hitboxes kept the old yaw through the whole motion. Fix: `invert_lr` writes `topn_lr`.
2. `F_CST_DTOR32(90)` and `F_CLC_DTOR32(90)` give the same bits (0x3FC90FDB); no separate yaw needed. The `lr == 0` guard stays (`0 * 90 deg = 0`).
3. Evidence: host probe showed `topn_yaw` stale for whole Hippataku/Yubideppou1/Move motions; 3 new boss tests fail without the fix.
4. Intro depth: every intro fighter camera (player, allies) sets `COBJ_FLAG_ZBUFFER` (sc1pintro.c:1572); on the Mario stage the second stage camera (Luigi) does too (sc1pintro.c:1343-1345). objdisplay.c:2659-2667 clears Z over the viewport (10,10,310,230) before the camera draws. Each clearing camera owns one model, so one clear per model is exact.
5. The port drew all intro models into one depth buffer: Mario's glove depth-won over Luigi during the slide-in, and Luigi's glove was cut by Mario's overalls when settled. Now `camera_clears_depth` drives `gpu.clear_depth()` per model at its priority.
6. Golden `f1-onep-intro-mario` (`onepmario@123`): at T 123, 233 native px differ from before; settled T 126/129, 174 native px; T 108-120 identical. The settled frame now matches the N64. Existing 13 onep/boss/intro rows bit-identical.
7. `onepgiant` (Donkey stage, clearing cameras at priorities 70/60/50) also changes; it has no golden and no N64 capture.
8. Master Hand return after GootsubusuEnd matches the N64 per tick (1034 ticks, 0 status mismatches, |d| <= 0.05). Boss warp ROM frame map: PSP tick = N64 frame + 106. GootsubusuEnd TransN moves the hand 400-700 units in 3 ticks, so a 6-tick pairing error looks like a position bug.
9. `onepgiant`: ParaLLEl-RDP confirms the per-camera clears: Kirby (priority 50) over Mario (60) over Yoshi (70), landmarks within 1 N64 px of the PSP. Rice HLE skips the inset Z fills (`func_8001663C`), so Rice shows wrong overlap; use ParaLLEl for intro depth questions. Frame map: Rice t = PSP - 106; ParaLLEl = Rice + 104. Golden `f1-onep-intro-giant`.
10. Open: on ParaLLEl Mario's open-mouth line vanishes from ~frame 124; PSP and Rice keep it (PLAN).
