# RE-396 — Stock icons above the damage display

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: interface, match, fighter, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/{hud,hud_tests}.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`

## Question

RE-393 packed each fighter's stock icon per costume. When and where does a
VS battle draw it?

## Source

- **Which display.** `scVSBattleStartBattle` calls
  `ifCommonPlayerStockInitInterface` for every VS battle. `mnPlayersVS`
  sets `is_single_stockicon` in a time battle, so each player gets:
  - `ifCommonPlayerStockSingleMakeInterface` in a time battle: one icon,
    drawn while `stock_count != -1`. Its `stock_count` is the menu's stock
    setting, so it always shows.
  - `ifCommonPlayerStockMultiMakeInterface` in a stock battle: one icon
    per stock left (`stock_count + 1`), up to six.
  Sudden death clears `is_single_stockicon` and has 0 stocks, so it shows
  one icon.
- **Placement.** Icons sit at `player_pos_x - 24`, ten apart in the multi
  display, and 20 above the damage display's centre. They draw in the
  costume's palette (`stock_luts[fp->costume]`), with `attr`
  `SP_TEXSHUF | SP_TRANSPARENT`.
  - The single icon's x is truncated after halving the width. The multi
    icons keep the half texel.
  - Past six stocks the multi display shows an icon, a cross and digits
    (file 36). The VS menu cannot reach that.
- **Draw order.** The stock display is made after the damage display and
  before the timer.

## Implementation

- **`ssb_game::hud::stock_icons`.** Yields each icon's origin for either
  display.
- **`psp-game`.** `draw_stocks` draws each fighter's
  `ROLE_STOCK`/costume sprite between the damage display and the timer.
  It does this in VS only; Training makes no stock display.

## Deviations

- **More than six stocks.** The digits display (file 36) is not ported.
- **Stock steal.** Team battles' stock steal
  (`ifCommonPlayerStockStealMakeInterface`) is not ported.

## Verification

- **Tests.** A host test covers the single icon, four multi icons, sudden
  death's one, and none out of stocks.
- **Goldens.** The four VS goldens gained two 8×10 faces each: red Mario
  for the player and the yellow costume for the CPU. That is 636 pixels
  per golden. They were rebaselined, each captured twice. 102 of 102
  goldens match.
- **Gates.** Workspace tests, Clippy and both PSP builds pass. The release
  `run` is 119,512 bytes.
