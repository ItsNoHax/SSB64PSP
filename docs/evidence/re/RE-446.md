## Questions

ROM, and how do the stage-made effects (`grYosterCloudVaporMakeEffect`,
`efManagerSparkleWhiteScaleMakeEffect`) reach the port's runtime?

## Source behaviour ported

- `grBonus3MakeGround`: GBumpers without the Castle knockback (the maker
  tests `gkind`), each with its script; a barrel every 180 ticks from the
  181st (a full pool skips one); the finish check reads the scene player
  grounded on Detect. The barrel point count must be one ("Too many
  barrels!" spins forever; the port panics).
- TaruBomb: fall (4/90), landing always rolls (the bounce and smash
  branches need an upward landing), roll gains `-(atan2(n) - 90°) * 1.4`,
  a wall, any contact or 10% smashes it into the barrel pieces, the 1.4
  explosion and a quake; explosion lasts 6 frames.
- Pipes: the down-tap check runs from the squats, landings, teeters,
  down-stand and both guard interrupts after their pass checks;
  `DokanStart` slides 25/frame and turns non-Mario/Luigi fighters to the
  camera; `DokanWait` is a 30-frame `gcGetInterpValueCubic` trip, hidden,
  effect-skipping and pause-ignoring; one draw in four (`<= 0.25`) exits
  at the wall pipe, projected onto the right wall plus the body width,
  into `DokanWalk` (facing right, then Fall); else `DokanEnd` grounds the
  fighter on the projected floor and turns it back after frame 30.

## Port deviations

| Deviation | Why |
|---|---|
| The pipe setters that project the map run right after `status::update` (`dokan::run_pending`), not inside `proc_update` | `status::update` has no map; nothing runs between them |
| The entry's `grInishiePakkunSetWaitFighter` is a flag the stage consumes at the start of its process | Fighters never hold the stage; the plants' process (priority 3) still runs after |
| Stage effects queue in the controller and `Stage::flush_effects` makes them right after `Stage::tick` | Same frame slot and order as the source's direct calls |
| Yoshi's Island's bank has the fixed runtime id 5 | `efParticleGetLoadBankID` order only names the bank; a fixed id needs no stage at draw time |
| `is_jostle_ignore` is recorded but unread | Fighter jostling is not ported |
| Race to the Finish's ground, barrel model and pieces are not in the pack; `Bonus3Bumper` trees and TaruBomb draws resolve to nothing | The 1P Game is not ported, so no scene enters it |
