# RE-344 — Kirby's copy abilities for the ported fighters

Status: COMPLETE (decomp source, host tests, both PSP builds, PPSSPP goldens; Pikachu, Jigglypuff and Ness copies not ported, Kirby not reachable in `psp-game`, physical PSP not captured)
Topics: fighter, gameplay, weapon, grab, capture, combat, animation
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/kirby_copy.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/kirby.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/kirby_attack.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/grab.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/weapon.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/attack.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/status.rs`, `tools/gen-anim-table.py`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/anim.rs`

**Question.** How do Kirby's copied neutral specials for Mario, Luigi, Fox,
Samus, Donkey Kong, Link, Captain Falcon and Yoshi differ from the fighters'
own, and which figatree and motion script drives each status?

**Source.** `ftkirbycopy{mario,fox,samus,donkey,link,captain,yoshi}specialn.c`,
`ftkirbystatus.h` (`dFTKirbySpecialStatusDescs`, statuses 231–249 and
287–302), `dFTKirbyMotionDescs` in `ftdata.c`, `ftcommonspecialn.c` and
`ftcommonspecialair.c` (`dFTKirbySpecial{,Air}NStatusList`),
`ftKirbySpecialNInitPassiveVars` in `ftkirbyspecialn.c`,
`wpLinkBoomerangCheckOwnerCatch`, and the scripts in
`228_KirbyMainMotion.c` (US).

**Findings.**

- The decompilation's motion-script and figatree labels for the copies are
  shifted or loose. The pairing record is `dFTKirbyMotionDescs`: Mario's
  copy runs `LuigiFireballGround`/`LuigiFireball`, Luigi's the
  `LuigiFireballGround_0x1DC8`/`LaserGround` labels, and Fox's
  `LaserGround_0x1E18`/`LaserAir`. The aerial Egg Lay figatree is
  `EggThrowAir` and its release `EggThrowEndAir`. Each End/Full Giant Punch
  pair shares one figatree. 25 figatrees cover the 33 statuses; their
  lengths agree with the ROM (676 lengths verified).
- Status selection is by `passive_vars.kirby.copy_id`. Mario and Luigi share
  the Fireball setter, which picks the Luigi statuses and Luigi's Fireball
  row from `copy_id`.
- Fireball: flag 0 at frame 16 on joint 17. Blaster: flag 0 at 25 (air 15)
  on joint 17, 70 along X; B after flag 1 (29, air 15) fires again as a new
  motion. The ground Blaster falls off edges; the air one lands into
  Wait or Landing.
- Charge Shot mirrors Samus's, with the shot at joint 0 plus (0, 200, 210).
  Kirby's recoil counter `copysamus_charge_recoil` is reset only when a new
  copy is taken, not on landing as Samus's is. `proc_damage` spends the
  charge until the End status fires the shot.
- Giant Punch charges one level per 12-frame loop wrap (double speed at
  level 10). Kirby's End boxes (joint 15, 14 damage, sizes 280/340) gain 2
  per charge level at creation; the Full boxes deal 36, with a third on
  joint 5. The ground release sets `vel_ground.x = level × 8`. `proc_damage`
  spends the charge only during Start and Loop.
- Boomerang: flag 0 at frame 26 on joint 0. `copylink_boomerang_gobj` is the
  same "boomerang out" state the weapon pool keeps for Link; the pool's
  owner catch calls `ftKirbyCopyLinkSpecialNGetSetStatus` for Kirby.
- Falcon Punch: boxes on joint 15 from frame 42 to 47 (24 damage size 250
  at 400 ahead, 26 damage size 200), base knockback 30 grounded and 40
  aerial. The aerial boost at frame 40 uses the Falcon stick angle; from 40
  to 55 both velocities multiply by 0.92 as a single, then the drift allows
  fast fall.
- Egg Lay: catch box on joint 30 (Yoshi: 31), size 300, 100 up, frames
  18–23; the swallowed fighter's capture, egg and breakout are Yoshi's
  `CaptureYoshi` and `YoshiEgg`. Throw descriptor 20/5 equals Yoshi's.

**Deviations.** Pikachu, Jigglypuff and Ness copies are not ported; a Kirby
with one still inhales. Giant Punch's `SetHitStatusAll(3)` window needs
hit-status intangibility, and Charge Shot's and Giant Punch's escape
interrupts need `ftCommonEscape`; neither is ported (same as the fighters'
own ports). Ledge catches are not ported. Copy loss still needs an RNG.
Effects, colour animations, model-part hats and sounds are presentation and
not emitted.

**Verification.** 13 new host tests: status selection and inhale fallback,
Fireball frame and Luigi row, Blaster refire, Charge Shot levels and recoil
(including recoil surviving a landing), Giant Punch charge, bonus, full
cancel and damage reset, Boomerang empty and catch, aerial Falcon Punch
boost and damping, Egg Lay window and release, ground/air switches, and an
end-to-end copy Egg Lay through the grab link that lays Mario in an egg.
Workspace tests: 1022 passed; clippy (1.98) clean. `romtool anims --verify`:
676 lengths agree. Pack v37 rebuilt from the ROM: 23,228,080 bytes,
SHA-256 `b7dbbb1fb62f511d5e69eaeceb4b5e2e6d08c0addcc94373a6ba37d8d8ecb556`.
Both PSP builds pass, `psp-game` also with `strict_render`.
PPSSPPHeadless: 72 of 72 goldens match. No golden reaches a copy ability.
