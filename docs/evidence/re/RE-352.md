## Decoded constants

`ITAttributes` bitfields are checked against typed relocData and US ROM
words. Bob-omb (`251_ITCommonData.c:624`, file 251, offset `0x424`)
anchors the layout: knockback base 30, type 3 (Throw), **initial damage 1**;
attribute word `+0x34 = 0x5A464010`. Damage 30 belongs to its explosion
attack events (`251_ITCommonData.c:650–653`). This corrects the handover's
attribution of damage 30 to its initial attributes.

| Data | ROM location | Values |
|---|---|---|
| Link Bomb attributes | File 225, `0x40` | Damage 2; angle 80°; knockback scale/weight/base 20/0/60; velocity scale 60%; type Throw; hit status None; damage box 100³; map top 113, bottom −113, width 113; attack size 220; hop/reflect/shield enabled; clank disabled |
| Bomb explosion events | File 225, `0x88` | `(timer, angle, damage, size)`: `(0, 361, 5, 300)`, `(2, 361, 5, 230)`, `(4, 361, 5, 150)`, `(6, 361, 5, 0)`; Fire element throughout |

## Implementation

- Shared 16-slot pool: last-freed-first allocation, separate creation-order
  list, source main/search/collision processes, rehit records, lifetime,
  pickup clock, bounds and item map callbacks.
- Fighter links: held metadata, published pickup candidates and ordered
  requests. Hold follows the light-item joint; release sweeps the map.
  Damage drops use the source hitlag/stack gate and
  `damage_queue > rand_int_range(60)`; death destroys the held item.
- LightGet: same floor line, strict bounds, closest horizontal distance.
  Light throws: flag0 releases, flag3 turns halfway; flags1/2 overlay
  damage/velocity/signed angle. Angle 361 selects the common descriptor.
  Guard dash/run slide and five-frame guard-roll throw windows are ported.
  Dash throws use TransN, other ground throws friction, air throws drift;
  landing selects Wait or Landing at the source descent threshold.
- Link: pull creates a held Bomb; down-B with a Bomb selects F4/AirF4,
  while another held item suppresses the move. All seven Bomb statuses,
  fuse, bloat, recoil, damage threshold and explosion events are ported.
  The fuse runs in the hand; explosions clear ownership and hit Link.
  Damage 7 explodes, 6 recoils. F4 throws at speed 66 after the Bomb's 60%
  scale: damage `(2 + 66 × 0.1)` truncates to 8 before the explosion.
- PK Fire sparks queue independent flame items. Incoming damage costs
  `3 × damage_highest` lifetime. Fighter/item/weapon attacks search item
  damage boxes; item attacks search clank, reflector, shield/hop and fighter
  hurtboxes through the hit log. Reflect damage grows `×1.8 + 0.99`, capped
  at 100; shield hop requires an angle below 135°.
- Weapon item contacts share four victim records with fighter contacts;
  a fifth distinct victim replaces record zero. Weapon item callbacks run
  after all item hurt searches, so early Fireball deletion or Bomb/Egg
  explosion cannot change later contacts within that pass.
- PSP frame: publish/tick/requests per fighter → weapons → item owner
  observation/tick/sync with `stage.bounds` → catches → fighter searches →
  item attacks → weapon hits → item hurt → finish fighter hits → drops →
  item resolve → flame spawns → landed records. PSP animation sampling
  supplies dash-throw TransN.

## Scope and deviations

- Team checks are skipped. Colour animation, effects, sound, spin and the
  pickup arrow are not ported. Throw-turn joint yaw is presentation; facing
  flips at the source halfway frame. Item models/effects are not drawn.
- Heavy/swing/shoot/consume items remain outside this batch.
- Weapon–item set-off is unreachable: no ported item can clank.
- Pikachu's Thunder and both thunder-trail kinds never hit items.
- Release sweeps use the shared sub-stepped `move_air` solver.
- `itProcessSearchHit*` runs after all fighters' searches instead of being
  interleaved per fighter. Existing weapon–fighter callback timing remains
  as recorded in RE-351; weapon–item callbacks wait for the item search pass.
- Ignoring the Bomb's random landing-despawn return is **source behavior**:
  its map callback discards `itMapCheckDestroyDropped`'s return and returns
  FALSE. The despawn RNG still runs.
- PSP LTO expanded the application loop to about 42,500 assembly
  instructions, with two branches spanning about 33,680 instructions;
  assembly failed with `out of range PC16 fixup`. Keeping item request,
  main tick and weapon-spawn processes out of line (`#[inline(never)]`)
  fixes the pinned PSP build without changing gameplay or toolchain flags.
