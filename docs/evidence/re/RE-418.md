# RE-418 — Drawing the damage shield, Yoshi's egg shield and the Fireball's spin

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, ROM display list, packed-asset ROM tests, host tests broken and restored, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice and broken by disabling each draw, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: shield, weapon, effect, asset-pack, rendering, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-rom/src/{mesh,pack,skeleton}.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/tests/egg_shield.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/{combat,status,weapon}.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/{gu,meshdraw}.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `tests/golden/f1-training-{yoshi-shield,fireball,luigi}.png`, `tests/golden/f1-vs-shield-damage.png`

## Question

RE-384 drew the shield bubble in the player's colour but not its grey
damage colour or Yoshi's egg shield, and RE-372 left the Fireball without
the spin `wpMarioFireballProcUpdate` gives it, behind an undecoded matrix
kind (0x47). What do the three draw?

## Source

- **The damage row** (`ef/efmanager.c`, `ft/ftcommon/ftcommonguard2.c`).
  `ftCommonGuardSetOffSetStatus` sets the shield effect's
  `is_damage_shield`; `efManagerShieldProcDisplay` then draws row 4 of
  `dEFManagerShieldColors` (PRIM white, ENV 0xC0 grey, both alpha 0xC0);
  `efManagerShieldProcUpdate` clears it. The set-off runs in
  `ftMainProcParams` (process priority 0, `ftmanager.c`) and the effect's
  update at priority 3 (`efManagerMakeEffect`); `gcRunAll` runs priorities
  5 down to 0. So the flag is raised after the effect's update, drawn that
  frame and cleared at the next one: grey for exactly one frame.
- **The egg shield.** Every guard path that raises `is_shield` for Yoshi
  (the end of `GuardOn`, `ftCommonGuardSetStatusFromEscape`) calls
  `efManagerYoshiShieldMakeEffect` and `ftParamHideModelPartAll`, which
  clears the list of every joint that has one (`modelpart_id_curr` is 0 for
  a joint with a list, `ftmanager.c`). `ftCommonGuardUpdateShieldVars`'s
  release, and any status change without `FTSTATUS_PRESERVE_MODELPART`,
  restore the parts as `is_shield` drops (`ftMainSetStatus` clears it). So
  Yoshi is hidden, and the egg drawn, exactly while he is Yoshi and
  `is_shield` holds; `GuardOn`'s start shows neither.
- `dEFManagerYoshiShieldEffectDesc` draws file 338's direct list at 0xA860
  (`llYoshiModelShieldDObjDesc`, the Egg Throw's egg) with transform kinds
  0x50 (battle function `func_ovl0_800C99CC`: a translation to `YRotN`'s
  world position, no scale) and 0x2C (kind 44, a camera-facing quad scaled
  by the DObj's X and Y). The make sets X and Y scale to 1.5.
  `efManagerYoshiShieldProcDisplay` sets ENV to (0xAE, 0xD6, 0xD6) times
  `1 - shield_health / 55` (clamped at 0), alpha 0.
- The list's `G_SETCOMBINE` (0x0040FE81, 0x55FFF3F9) is
  `(SHADE - ENV) * TEXEL0 + 0` in both cycles, alpha `TEXEL0`; it clears
  `G_LIGHTING`, and its four vertices are white. The egg is its texture at
  full health and loses ENV as the shield wears: it darkens, green and
  blue faster than red, towards (81, 41, 41) for a white texel at 0
  health.
- **The Fireball's spin** (`wp/wpmario/wpmariofireball.c`,
  `lb/lbcommon.c`). The update adds `rotate_speed` (20° Mario, 25° Luigi)
  to `rotate.x`; `wpMainVelSetModelPitch` sets `rotate.y` to ±90°. The
  `WPDesc`'s transforms are `Tra` then 0x47. Battle kinds index
  `dLBCommonFuncMatrixList` in `{proc_diff, proc_same}` pairs from 66, so
  0x47 is `func_ovl0_800CA5C8` (the same indexing gives RE-384's 0x4F).
  It writes rows `R · gGCMatrixPerspF` over the MVP's rotation, with
  row-vector `R = Rx(rotate.x) · Ry(rotate.y)`: a rotation relative to the
  camera, not the world. At ±90° yaw the spin axis is the view axis, so the
  Fireball rolls in the screen plane (clockwise moving right).

## Implementation

- **Pack v69** (30,710,256 bytes, unchanged size): `MeshMaterial::shade_minus_env`
  and `flags::SHADE_MINUS_ENV` (bit 28) mark a textured prim whose two
  cycles are `(SHADE - ENV) * TEXEL0`. Only two file-338 meshes (keyed
  0xA860 and 0xA858) carry it; the vertices keep the shade.
  `EffectColors::vertex_color` now takes the prim's flags and, for this
  shape, subtracts a live ENV per channel, clamped at 0. That equals the
  RDP's clamp of the product, since `TEXEL0` is non-negative and the
  vertices share one shade.
- **Game state.** `GuardState::is_damage_shield`, set by
  `set_guard_set_off` and cleared at the start of `proc_params_with` (the
  effect update's slot); `combat::shield_color_row`,
  `is_yoshi_egg_shield`, `yoshi_shield_env` and `YOSHI_SHIELD_SCALE`.
  `FireballAttributes::rotate_speed` and `MarioFireball::rotate_x`,
  advanced each update that does not expire.
- **Drawing.** The bubble takes `shield_color_row`'s row. The egg draws
  after the bubble: a kind-44 billboard at `YRotN`'s world position,
  `MODEL_SCALE × 1.5`, with ENV overridden to `yoshi_shield_env`; Yoshi's
  model is skipped while `is_yoshi_egg_shield`. `Gpu::model_transform_camera_rotated`
  builds kind 0x47's axes from the camera's right, up and back, and the
  Fireball draws with `[rotate_x, yaw]`.
- **Scenes.** `yoshishield` (Yoshi holds Z from tick 40, captured at 600:
  health 21, ENV (107, 132, 132)) and `vsshield` (a VS battle, Z held from
  "Go" at 398; captured at 685, after tick 684's update, where the CPU's
  Mario Tornado sets the shield off).

## Evidence

- **ROM tests** (`egg_shield.rs`): the list's one combine is the shape
  above in both cycles; only file 338's meshes carry the flag; the egg's
  packed shade is white.
- **Logs.** A per-tick capture log of `vsshield` shows `GuardSetOff` with
  the damage row on tick 684 alone (685 is back to row 0), health 37. The
  `yoshishield` capture line shows `Guard`, the egg up, health 21 and ENV
  (107, 132, 132).
- **Images.** The `vsshield` bubble is white-grey around Mario beside the
  CPU's tornado (red at tick 684's capture, the frame before). The
  `yoshishield` egg, checked number by number: the ROM's four vertices
  (file 338 + 0xA820) have colour (255, 255, 255); at health 21,
  `blend` = 1 − 21/55 = 0.618 and ENV = (107, 132, 132) (`(u8)` truncation);
  `(SHADE − ENV) · TEXEL0` for a white texel is (148, 123, 123). A build
  with ENV forced to 0 draws the egg's two main texels as (231, 231, 231)
  and (57, 214, 57); the golden has (134, 111, 111) on 1,948 pixels and
  (33, 103, 27) on 988, which are 231 × (148, 123, 123) / 255 and
  (57 × 148, 214 × 123, 57 × 123) / 255 to the unit. So the egg reads as a
  warm dark grey with darker green spots at this health, not red; it would
  approach (81, 41, 41) only near 0 health. Luigi's Fireball turns visibly
  between the old and new goldens; Mario's, a few updates old, turns
  slightly.

## Deviations

- The egg-break particles `ftCommonGuardUpdateShieldVars` makes when
  Yoshi's shield drops (`efManagerEggBreakMakeEffect`) are not made (TODO).
- The grey row's frame follows the port's hit resolution: the flag clears
  in `proc_params_with`, which runs every frame for every fighter, as
  `ftMainProcParams` does.

## Verification

- **Host tests.** A set-off selects row 4 until the next `proc_params`;
  Yoshi's egg (and hidden model) comes at the end of `GuardOn` and goes
  with the release, and its ENV follows health; both Fireballs spin by
  their `rotate_speed` per update. Removing the clear or the spin fails
  its test.
- **Goldens.** Two added (`f1-training-yoshi-shield`,
  `f1-vs-shield-damage`), identical twice. Skipping the egg changes
  `yoshi-shield` by 5,800 pixels; dropping its ENV by 5,800; drawing
  Yoshi's model under it by 1,264; the player's row instead of the damage
  row changes `vs-shield-damage` by 4,248. Two rebaselined for the spin,
  identical twice: `f1-training-fireball` (2,176 pixels at 2×) and
  `f1-training-luigi` (1,712); the old goldens are the unspun draw. All
  119 goldens match twice with pack v69.
- **Gates.** Workspace tests with an absolute `SSB64_ROM` and one thread
  (1,508 passed), pinned 1.98.0 Clippy with warnings denied, rustfmt, the
  `no_std` builds and both PSP release builds pass. Pack v69 is 30,710,256
  bytes, SHA-256
  `a768f845423d7ba18fa5583bf72286bb3ab47cfcf8f1801f410d320ae1ed6f12`.
  The release `run` is 20,740 bytes (unchanged) with its 18,176-byte
  frame; the largest frame is still `enter_training` at 159,456 bytes.

## Conclusion

A set-off's frame draws the bubble in the grey damage row, Yoshi guards
hidden inside his egg, which darkens as the shield wears through its
`(SHADE - ENV) * TEXEL0` combiner, and the Fireballs roll in the screen
plane under battle matrix function 0x47.
