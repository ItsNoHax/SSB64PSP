## Question

RE-465 found How to Play drifting from the N64 at frame 254: Turn dashed
early, a dash's end spent a frame in Wait, and the entry rose late, peaked
6 units high and landed at y 0. What does the shared fighter code have to
do for How to Play's deterministic scripts to move Mario and Luigi as the
N64 does?

## Method

- N64: the unmodified ROM (SHA-1 `e2929e10…`) idles into How to Play
  (frame 0 = 4337 frames after boot) under `tools/n64-headless` with a
  scratch config. Each frame reads both fighters through the battle state
  `0x8018E7F0` (players at `+0x20`, stride `0x74`, gobj at `+0x58`;
  `FTStruct` at gobj `+0x84`: `status_id` `+0x24`, `lr` `+0x44`,
  `percent_damage` `+0x2C`, `hitlag_tics` `+0x40`, `vel_ground` `+0x60`;
  gobj `anim_frame` `+0x78`; DObj translate `+0x1C`). Words are
  byte-reversed per the trace traps note. The attack collisions
  (`attack_colls` at `fp+0x294`, stride `0xC4`) and the joints' world
  positions (`joints` at `fp+0x8E8`, `FTParts` at DObj `+0x84`,
  `mtx_translate` row 3) were dumped around the jabs.
- PSP: `explain@4460` under PPSSPPHeadless with `--log`; the `re465` line
  prints status id, anim frame, position and velocities. PSP tick =
  N64 frame + 2.

## Result

The N64 and PSP traces now agree exactly (0.05 tolerance, status and
damage): Luigi through frame 735 and Mario through 1475 (bar two frames of
jostle at 742–743 from Luigi's hit), against frame 254 before.

**Next divergence (frame 736).** Mario's `Attack13` kick hits Luigi one
frame early. Its collisions are within 0.2–1.1 units of the N64's
(`(335.43, 246.27)` against `(335.22, 246.07)` and `(325.48, 180.40)`
against `(325.11, 180.27)`), and Luigi's `DamageN1` joints within
0.3–0.7 units (joint 9 `(586.81, 211.01)` against `(587.53, 210.92)`):
the hit is margin-limited, decided by sub-unit figatree pose differences.
Luigi then runs about 8 units off, and at 1475 Mario's up tilt hits Luigi,
which the N64's misses; the scripts diverge from there.

## Deviations

- The appear's skeleton and every clip now start on the status's current
  frame. A status set outside the tick (VS entry, results' demo) is posed
  one frame on at its first tick, as the N64's How to Play is; VS entry
  ordering was not separately traced.
- Air TransN helpers (`apply_air_vel_transn_all`) still do not scale by
  `attr->size`, and the cliff statuses' ground step uses the facing, not
  TopN's yaw; How to Play does not reach them.

## Verification

- Host: `crates/ssb-rom/tests/explain_movement.rs` replays both scripts
  on How to Play's collision from the ROM, with the ROM's attributes and
  the generated motion table, and checks 69 frames × 2 fighters (status,
  x, y) from 119 to 700 against N64 numbers; breaking Turn's pivot gate
  fails it at frame 259. `the_device_trig_matches_libm` bounds the device
  trig. 2,096 workspace tests pass on one thread with `SSB64_ROM`; clippy
  (1.98.0, warnings denied) and rustfmt pass.
- Both production PSP builds; PPSSPP (software) idles from the title into
  How to Play at 60 FPS.
- Goldens: 175 of 195 changed. With the trig change reverted 18 return
  (the viewer's fighter, water and rotated-camera scenes, the selects,
  `f1-vs-players`, `f1-training-interface-wait`); the other 157 are
  fighter scenes moved by the floor at creation (no opening fall, Wait's
  tag clock), the clip and TransN timing, and the status chains (camera
  framing follows the fighters, so most training scenes shift whole).
  Masks reviewed: changes sit on fighters, their effects and the camera;
  a first run hid the CPU's "CP" tag until `mpCommonSetFighterWaitOrFall`
  was added. All 175 rebaselined (captured twice); 195 of 195 match.
