# RE-406 — The battle camera frames every fighter

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: camera, match, fighter, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/{camera,dead,dead_tests,appear,appear_tests}.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `psp-asset-viewer/src/main.rs`

## Question

The battle camera framed only the player (RE-405). How does
`gmCameraUpdateInterests` choose what to frame for each fighter?

## Source

- **The interests** (`gm/gmcamera.c`, `gmCameraUpdateInterests`). It
  walks the fighter link and switches on `camera_mode`:
  - **Ghost:** skipped.
  - **Entry or Explain:** framed at `entry_pos`, with the entry's `lr`.
  - **DeadUp:** framed at `status_vars.common.dead.pos`, moved by
    `gmCameraSetDeadUpStarPosition` in place of the bounds clamp:
    `x = camera_bound_top × x / map_bound_top` and
    `y = camera_bound_top`.
  - **Default:** framed at the fighter's position, clamped by
    `gmCameraSetBoundsPosition`.
  - Every mode adds `cam_offset_y`.
- **The modes.**
  - `ftCommonAppearInitStatusVars` sets Entry.
  - `ftMainSetStatus` resets every mode to Default except Entry.
  - `ifCommonAnnounceGoSetStatus` sets every fighter back to Default at
    "Go".
  - `ftCommonDeadUpStarSetStatus` and `...UpFallSetStatus` set DeadUp
    and record `dead.pos`.
  - `ftCommonSleepSetStatus` (out of stocks) sets Ghost and `is_ghost`,
    as a rebirth's first ticks do.
- **Timing.** The camera GObj's process runs at priority 3
  (`gmCameraMakeDefaultCamera`), after every fighter's priority-4
  physics.

## Implementation

- **`ssb_game::dead`.**
  - `CameraMode::Entry` is added, and `on_set_status` keeps it.
  - `DeadState::up_pos` records where a top-out starts.
  - The sleep after the last stock sets Ghost and `is_ghost`.
- **`ssb_game::appear`.** `appear_set_status` sets Entry. `on_go` ends
  it.
- **`ssb_game::camera`.** `Interest::dead_up` marks a target that
  already has the star position, and `calculate_interest` then skips the
  clamp.
- **`psp-runtime`.**
  - `FighterScene::camera_interest(stage)` returns the interest for the
    fighter's mode, or `None` for a ghost.
  - `tick_camera` frames the fighter's own interest and the others'.
- **`psp-game`.**
  - `tick_battle_camera` runs after the physics pass. It frames the
    player and every other fighter in link order through the player's
    camera.
  - The pause camera's ease-back and its out-of-bounds path use it too.
  - The first Go frame calls `on_go` on every fighter.

## Deviations

- **Weapon interests.** `gmCameraUpdateInterests`'s second half widens
  the box for some weapons. It is not ported.
- **1P game.** The 1P-game enemy branch (`gmCameraSetTeamBoundsPosition`)
  is not ported.
- **One-frame offset.** "Go" ends the entry mode at the start of the Go
  frame's world step. The original does it from the announcement's
  thread, which may be one frame apart.

## Verification

- **Tests.**
  - The entry mode survives into `Wait` and ends at `on_go`.
  - A dead-up interest skips the clamp.
  - The last stock's sleep is a camera ghost.
- **Goldens.** 39 goldens were rebaselined, each captured twice. They are
  every Training and VS battle whose two or four fighters now widen the
  box:
  - `f1-vs-countdown` shows the CPU entering on the left platform;
  - `f1-vs-four` frames all four fighters;
  - Training scenes pull out to keep the dummy in view.
  The scenes whose dummy already sat inside the player's box are
  unchanged. 108 of 108 goldens match.
- **Gates.** Workspace tests, Clippy and both PSP builds pass.

## Conclusion

The battle camera frames every fighter by its camera mode, as the
original does. Weapon interests and the 1P-game branch remain.
