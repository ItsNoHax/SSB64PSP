## Question

What camera and transition behavior do the three 1P bonus courses use when
the player pauses and enters the course?

## Evidence

- `ifCommonBattleGoUpdateInterface` reads `MPGroundData.zoom_start` and
  `zoom_end`, calls `gmCameraSetStatusMapZoom`, and sets pause kind Bonus.
  The map camera sets `target_dist` to the distance between those points,
  eases `at` toward `zoom_start` using `func_ovl2_8010C4D0`, eases `eye`
  toward `zoom_end` by 10%, and eases FOV toward 38 degrees.
- `MPGroundData` stores the two `Vec3h` points at offsets 0x9A and 0xA0.
  The bonus pause menu draws its first ten decals plus the L and Retry decals;
  L reloads the active bonus scene.
- `sc1PBonusStageFuncStart` makes a black `lbFade` actor with length 12.
  `lbFadeProcUpdate` increments alpha for twelve ticks and keeps the actor for
  two more ticks before ejecting it. Its zero alpha color selects a black
  overlay that fades from opaque to transparent.

## Implementation

`GroundData` now reads both points and pack v100 stores them after the team
bounds in each `StageDesc`. The PSP pause state starts the source map zoom,
draws the bonus retry decals and reloads the current course on L. The bonus
controller tracks the fade only while the battle world runs, and the PSP
draws its black alpha overlay over the course.
