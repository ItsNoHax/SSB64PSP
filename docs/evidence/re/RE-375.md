# RE-375 — Yoshi in Training: Egg Throw egg and Yoshi Bomb stars

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, ROM display lists, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: fighter, weapon, asset-pipeline, visual-regression, rendering
Relevant files: `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/pack.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/weapon.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/yoshi.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`, `tests/golden/f1-training-yoshi*.png`

## Question

What do Yoshi's Egg Throw egg and Yoshi Bomb stars draw, under which
transforms and render state, and how do they spin?

## Source

- `llYoshiMainEggThrowWeaponAttributes` (file 247 + 0x0C) has `data` =
  file 338 + 0xA860, a direct display list, and no `MObjSub`, joint or
  material animation. Its map collision (150/0/-150/150) and size 200
  match the host constants. The same list is the YoshiShield manager
  effect's graphic.
- `llYoshiMainStarWeaponAttributes` (file 247 + 0x40) has `data` = file 86
  + 0x5458 (`dITCommonObject_StarRod_Weapon_data`), also direct, with no
  tables. The same list is the KirbyStar manager effect's graphic.
- Neither descriptor sets `WEAPON_FLAG_DOBJLINKS` or `WEAPON_FLAG_DOBJDESC`.
  `wpManagerMakeWeapon` therefore displays both with `wpDisplayDLHead1`,
  under `wpDisplayMain`'s `wpDisplayDrawNormal`: `G_ZBUFFER` cleared and
  `G_RM_AA_XLU_SURF`.
- The egg's list (ROM bytes decoded at 0xA860) sets its own combiner, alpha
  compare and render mode (no `Z_CMP`/`Z_UPD`). The discovered mesh keyed
  (338, 0xA860) already converts it that way. `wpYoshiEggThrowProcDisplay`
  sets the environment colour to 0 first; the packed primitive's
  environment colour is 0.
- The star's list (0x5458) sets its combiner, primitive and environment
  colours and alpha compare, but no render mode. It inherits
  `wpDisplayDrawNormal`'s state, as Mario's Fireball does (RE-300).
- `dWPYoshiEggThrowWeaponDesc` has main transform `TraRotRpyRSca` and
  secondary kind 0x2E (46). `gcPrepDObjMatrix` kind 46 (`objdisplay.c`)
  overwrites the matrix's rotation and scale cells with the perspective
  diagonal times a spin by `rotate.z` and `scale.x`/`scale.y`. Only the
  main transform's translation survives. This is the Charge Shot's
  billboard (RE-373).
- The egg is made at Egg Throw motion flag 1 (frame 4).
  `ftYoshiSpecialHiUpdateEggVectors`, from `proc_physics`, sets its
  translate to the `FTYOSHI_EGGTHROW_JOINT` (joint 3) world position and
  its scale to that joint's scale. Joint 3's rest scale is 1
  (`dYoshiModel_JointTree[3]`).
- `wpYoshiEggThrowProcUpdate`: on the first update after `is_throw`, it
  runs `wpYoshiEggThrowInitVars`, which sets the velocity and
  `angle = DTOR(throw_force * -2.1 - 1.5)` and zeroes `translate.z`. On
  each later frame that does not expire, it adds `angle` to `rotate.z`
  before gravity. The explosion (`wpYoshiEgg{Hit,Expire}InitVars`) sets
  `DObjGetStruct(weapon_gobj)->dl = NULL`, so nothing draws.
- `dWPYoshiStarWeaponDesc` is `TraRotRpyRSca` with no secondary transform.
  `wpYoshiStarProcUpdate`, after the lifetime check, sets `scale.x` and
  `scale.y` to `wpYoshiStarGetScale` and adds `0.24 * lr` to `rotate.z`.
  `wpYoshiStarProcHop` and `wpYoshiStarProcReflector` set `rotate.z` to
  `atan2(vel.y, vel.x)`.

## Implementation

- Pack v54 adds a copy of file 86's 0x5458 list converted under
  `InitialMaterial::WEAPON_EXTERNAL`. Discovery already keys the
  default-seed copy (86, 0x5458), so the weapon copy is keyed by the
  attributes record that names it, (247, 0x40). Pack v54 is 27,096,032
  bytes.
- `YoshiEgg` gains `rotate_z` and `spin_step`, and `YoshiStar` gains
  `rotate_z`, for presentation only. `yoshi::held_egg_position` returns the
  joint-3 world position while the egg is held.
- `psp-game` draws the held and thrown egg with
  `Gpu::model_transform_billboard` (scale 1), skipping an exploded egg. It
  draws each star under `T · Rz(rotate_z) · S(scale, scale, 1)`.
- The capture scenes `yoshi` (the egg 16 frames after the throw) and
  `yoshibomb` (the two stars 6 frames after they appear) spawn Yoshi. Both
  capture lines log `anim_frame`, `egg_held` and the egg and star counts.

## Deviations

- Superseded in part by RE-378: `psp-game` fed no TransN to the Yoshi
  Bomb, so the `yoshibomb` golden showed no hop and a landing near tick
  51. With TransN, Yoshi lands near tick 59 and the capture moved to
  tick 68.

- The held egg draws at joint 3's pose at draw time. The source samples
  it in `proc_physics`, before the map step moves the fighter; the two
  differ only while that step moves Yoshi.
- The held egg uses scale 1, joint 3's rest scale. The Egg Throw
  figatree's scale track for joint 3 was not checked.
- The egg's shatter, egg-break, dust and white-sparkle effects are not
  drawn.

## Verification

`tools/golden.sh verify --twice` matched all 82 scenes, including the new
`f1-training-yoshi` and `f1-training-yoshi-bomb`. Each new golden was
identical in both captures. The capture logs show the egg thrown
(`egg_held=false eggs=1`, `SpecialHi` frame 39) and two stars in
`SpecialLwLanding` frame 9. A host test covers the egg and star spin
steps. Workspace tests pass with an absolute `SSB64_ROM` and one test
thread.
