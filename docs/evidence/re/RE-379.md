# RE-379 — Pikachu in Training: Thunder Jolt, weapon draw order and the cutout alpha gate

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, ROM display lists, PPSSPP source, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: fighter, weapon, asset-pipeline, rendering, visual-regression
Relevant files: `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/{pack,mesh,skeleton}.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/pikachu_weapon.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`, `tests/golden/*`

## Question

How do the aerial and ground Thunder Jolts draw and animate? The batch
also found four pipeline and renderer faults.

## Source: Thunder Jolt

- `dWPPikachuThunderJoltAirWeaponDesc` (flags 0x00, `Tra` then kind 0x2E)
  and `llPikachuSpecial1ThunderJoltAirWeaponAttributes` (file 244 + 0x00)
  name file 342's direct list at 0x270 and the one-script table at 0x360.
  The script spins `RotZ` and pulses `ScaXYZ`, then loops. Kind 46 (RE-373)
  draws a camera-facing quad spun by `rotate.z` and sized by `scale.x/y`.
- `dWPPikachuThunderJoltGroundWeaponDesc` has flags 0x03 (`DOBJDESC |
  DOBJLINKS`). Its attributes (file 244 + 0x34) name the tree at 0x1888,
  the MObjSub table at 0x1018, the transform table at 0x1A20 and the
  material table at 0x1AE0. Node 1 sweeps `TraX` from -390 to 600 over 9
  frames. Nodes 2–7 blink through `SetFlags`, and each material script
  cycles the texture ID. Every node script loops at frame 9.
- `wpPikachuThunderJoltGroundMakeWeapon` sets the anim speed to 0.5
  (`gcSetAllAnimSpeed`). It sets root `rotate.y` to 180° or 0° from the
  surface kind and the new `lr`. `wpPikachuThunderJoltGroundProcReflector`
  sets it again. `GetStatus` sets root `rotate.z` to
  `atan2(-angle.x, angle.y)` of the line.
- `wpPikachuThunderJoltGroundProcUpdate` restarts the animation
  (`gcAddAnimAll` then `gcPlayAnimAll`) when `GObj::anim_frame` equals
  `WPPIKACHUJOLT_ANIM_PUSH_FRAME` (7.5). Every line transition in
  `GetStatus` and `CheckDestroy` restarts it too. The first play after
  `gcAddAnimAll` only parses (`AOBJ_ANIM_CHANGED`), so the push lands 16
  plays after the weapon is made and every 15 after that. At 0.5 speed,
  node 1's sweep cancels the jolt's 55-unit step, so the sparks hold still
  and then jump ahead.

## Findings

1. **Wrong MObjSub pairing.** RE-125 paired the ThunderJolt effect graph
   (342, 0x2258) with table 0x101C, a demand-search match.
   `dEFManagerPikachuThunderJoltEffectDesc` names
   `llPikachuSpecial3ThunderJoltMObjSub` (0x20A0, two slots). Table 0x1018
   (2 NULL + 6) belongs to the ground Thunder Jolt, whose `WPAttributes`
   names it. Both pairings are now explicit. RE-253's texture-key census lost its
   one format/size conflict with this fix: file 342's 0xC18 had been
   decoded as IA8 and as CI4. Restoring the old pairing brings the conflict
   back, and the census baseline is now 0.
2. **Head-1 seed.** `mesh::convert_sequence` seeded DL-link-1 lists with
   the camera's `G_RM_AA_ZB_XLU_SURF` even under the weapon seed. A weapon
   draws through `wpDisplayMain`, and its `wpDisplayDrawNormal` resets
   head 1 (`G_ZBUFFER` cleared, `G_RM_AA_XLU_SURF`) before its lists run.
   The Final Cutter wave (RE-378) had depth test on because of this.
3. **Draw order.** `gmCameraProcDisplay`-style passes draw DL links 6–12
   (fighters 9, items 11), flush, then links 13–15 (weapons 14,
   effects 15). `psp-game` drew weapons and effects before the fighters,
   so a fighter could cover weapons and effects that clear `G_ZBUFFER`.
   They now draw after both fighters.
4. **Cutout gate under PPSSPP.** The `CVG_X_ALPHA | ALPHA_CVG_SEL` cutout
   was issued as `sceGuAlphaFunc(Greater, 0)`. PPSSPP's software renderer
   rewrites `GREATER 0` to `NOTEQUAL 0` (`OptimizeRefByteCompare`), and its
   rectangle path treats that as a no-op test (`AlphaTestIsNeedless`). It
   then draws alpha-0 texels. The aerial jolt's 64×64 CI4 quad drew its
   alpha-0 palette entry as an opaque blue square. Measured on the object
   alone in `psp-asset-viewer`: `Greater(0)` kept the blue, while
   `Greater(1)`, `Greater(128)` and `GreaterOrEqual(1)` removed it. The
   gate is now issued as `GreaterOrEqual(1)`, the same test on 8-bit alpha.
   It changes no pack byte, because texture compensation evaluates the
   same predicate.

## Implementation

- Pack v56 (27,184,192 bytes):
  - The aerial list (342, 0x270) becomes a one-node object
    (`DIRECT_WEAPON_GRAPHS`).
  - The ground tree (342, 0x1888) gets its MObjSub and material scripts
    (`WEAPON_MAT_ANIM_JOINTS`).
  - Both are packed under the weapon seed, with weapon animation slots
    2 (0x360..0x3F8) and 3 (0x1A20..0x1AE0), both windowed.
- `ThunderJolt` gains `anim_epoch`, `anim_ticks`, `model_rotate_y` and
  `rotate_z()`, for presentation only. It restarts its animation on
  landing, at the push frame and on line transitions.
- `StageAnimator::tick_speed` and `EffectMaterialAnimator::tick_speed`
  play at 0.5.
- `psp-game` draws the aerial jolt as a kind-46 billboard from the animated
  pose. It draws the ground jolt tree under `R(0, model_rotate_y,
  rotate_z)` and hides the nodes whose flags say so.
- Capture scenes: `pikachu` (the ground jolt 8 plays into its first push
  cycle) and `pikachuair` (an aerial jolt 11 frames into its flight).

## Deviations and gaps

- Ground node 4's material script (0x1AE0 table slot 4, texture IDs 0/2)
  is not attached: `resolve_one_mat_anim` declines it. That spark keeps
  its first texture.
- The ThunderJolt effect (`efManagerPikachuThunderJoltMakeEffect`) and the
  jolt's dust and shock effects are not drawn.
- The Falcon Kick result has not been compared with an N64 capture.

## Verification

Host tests cover the ground jolt's creation (epoch 1, 0 plays, 180° yaw)
and its push restarts at play 16 and every 15 after. Capture logs show
the ground jolt at `(epoch 2, 1 play)` 16 frames after landing.
`tools/golden.sh verify --twice` matched all 87 scenes. Workspace tests
pass with an absolute `SSB64_ROM` and one test thread.
