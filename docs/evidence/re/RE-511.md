# RE-511 — Fighter TopN display matrix, query marks, trace-verified gameplay fixes

Status: COMPLETE, bit-exact against five N64 RDRAM traces
Topics: fighters, rendering, animation, items, reverse-engineering
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/combat.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/item/mod.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/item_throw.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/motion/mod.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/kirby.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/pikachu.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/slope_contour.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`

## Question

How does the original draw the fighter TopN (display matrix kind 0x4B), which gameplay sites mark a joint as queried, and what gameplay differences did the N64 traces expose?

## Findings

- TopN (kind 0x4B, `lbCommonFighterPartsFuncMatrix`) is F2L of the collision
  local when the joint was queried, `TraRotRpyRSca` when unflagged and
  `TraRotScaInv` when flagged. With `gLBCommonScale` = 1 the inverse word is
  256, so the flagged form equals the unflagged one word for word for every
  angle; no flagged-distinct frame exists (33 tilted flagged unqueried Yoshi
  frames confirm it).
- Query-mark sites: `itMainSetFighterHold/Release` mark the hand synchronously
  from the fighter proc (a p5 request's TopN mark is then cleared by
  ftmain.c:1847); the held-item display (kind 0x52, 800C9A38) marks the hand
  chain after the fighter draws, gated by `itDisplayCheckItemVisible`, and
  survives a hitlag frame because PlayAnim is skipped; the sword trail
  (`ftMainProcParams`, not in hitlag) marks Link joint 11 or the item-light
  parent chain; Link Bomb release, Pikachu AttackS4 spark, boss-defeat zoom,
  catch swirl.
- `ftParamGetJointID(-2)` on MakeAttackColl is the item-light joint: item-swing
  hitboxes (sword, bat, fan, star rod) ride the hand, not TopN. Before this,
  they sat on TopN (real gameplay bug).
- Pikachu `AttackS4ProcUpdate` draws `syUtilsRandIntRange(2)` per spark flag;
  skipping it desynced the RNG.
- Kirby: Loop interrupt runs on the Start->Loop frame, inhale releases after
  LAG-1 loop updates; thrown release takes joint 4 through its collision
  locals and zeroes z, `vel_air.z`, sets `jumps_used = 1`;
  `physics::integrated_position` rounds like the original.
- Beam Sword map collision is top 422, bottom -422, width 72.
- N64 traces (patched decomp, host replay of status, queried set and 12 TopN
  words per frame): t1 Mario/Fox 619, t2 Kirby/Captain 619, t3 Link/Mario 619,
  t4 Yoshi/Mario sword 669, t5 Yoshi/Mario slope 769; all exact.
- Goldens: 123 of 134 psp-game rows changed (all 69 asset-viewer rows
  unchanged): 92 TopN display, 27 display plus new marks, 1 item-swing
  hitbox (training-hammer), 2 Kirby inhale (training-kirby-hat,
  vs-four-magnifier), 1 capture/throw (vs-four). With every change switched
  off 130 of 134 rows return to the HEAD hash.
- vs-four attribution (batch 188, per-site ablation at 0df2bfb4; hashes
  c7708d6d before, a38cb161 after): two causes, both needed:
  - release `f.pos.z = 0.0` after the throw (`grab.rs` `release_with`):
    Fox throws Kirby (catch tick 766, release 788), Kirby at z 0 not 60;
    3,700 px in Kirby's box. `vel_air.z` and `jumps_used` lines: no pixels.
  - TopN 0x4B display formula on Mario and Kirby (~400 px and the rest);
    Fox and DK no pixels. So vs-four is also a TopN-display row.
  - No pixels at tick 870: catch swirl mark, pulled, thrown mark,
    `thrown_part4` (1-2 ulp), hand-matrix and `settle_catch` changes,
    Captain mark (no Captain in vs4).
