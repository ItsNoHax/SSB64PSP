# RE-312 — Final 3-point residual census

Status: COMPLETE (census plus post-RE-313 deployment; host and PPSSPP verified, no physical PSP capture)
Topics: texture, asset-pipeline, pack, psp-ge

Relevant files: `tools/romtool/src/residuals.rs`, `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`,
`tools/romtool/src/residual_fixes.rs`, `tools/residual-ab-capture.sh`,
`crates/ssb-rom/src/filter_compensation.rs`,
`docs/rendering/three-point-residuals.md`

**Goal.** After RE-305 to RE-311, find every remaining materially bad
3-point residual. Classify why each one remains. Record enough data to choose
a manual fix per texture. This batch adds no heuristic and changes no packed
byte.

**Method.** `romtool residuals <rom>` rebuilds the pack with a report-only
recorder. The recorder clones what `convert_texture` packs and what each
primitive binds, including the primitive's real filter coverage. The command
refuses to report if the rebuilt pack differs from
`assets/generated/ssb64.pak` (SHA-256 `622d20e4...0428d615`). Each variant is
measured on its independent holdout coverage only: the RE-310 cell-local
holdout, the RE-311 disjoint-pose holdout plus the conservative box, or the
phase-4 full-tile lattice for the fallback. The report measures these
alternatives:

* uncompensated and current `GU_LINEAR`
* `GU_NEAREST`
* the pipeline's own continuous solve without its gates. This needed a
  pack-neutral split of `solve_samples` into `solve_samples_ungated` and the
  gate.
* a dense-training variant of that solve
* the pipeline's index optimizers
* holdout-fitted oracles: unconstrained RGBA8888, alpha-held, and a
  flip-minimizing cutout silhouette search
* per-use-site oracles
* a +/-2/32 uniform UV phase shift

A phase-uniform set (16 samples per cell, all 32 sub-texel residues) checks
every phase-sensitive conclusion. The holdout's RE-310 boundary probes
over-sample 1/32 phases. The first UV-shift result was +22% on the holdout,
but it shrank to about 1% on the uniform set. Only 9 variants hold up on
both sets. The packed level 0 is decoded from the pack bytes and compared
with the recorded texels. The padded power-of-two image is also sampled
with GE addressing. Two runs give byte-identical Markdown, JSON and images.

**US archive result.**

* 1,585 mesh-texture variants and 4,073 use sites were measured.
* 110 variants are clean (max < 8/255).
* 204 variants are minor.
* 1,271 variants are materially bad. The threshold is the pipeline's own
  admission rule: >= 1% of samples >= 8/255 with a maximum >= 16, or >= 0.1%
  of samples >= 32/255.
* 1,090 of the 1,271 are `BILINEAR_SURFACE_LIMIT`. For these, the best
  holdout-fitted oracle keeps at least half the colour error and is still
  material.
* Disposition: 12 variants have a deployable measured fix, 185 are
  hand-edit candidates (bounded only by an oracle), 1,062 are accepted, and
  19 are suspected bugs.
* 271 of 303 cutout variants already flip the alpha test when uncompensated.
  The cutout rule therefore freezes their alpha. A flip-minimizing alpha
  search removes 45% of the flips (104,366 to 57,493).

**Suspected bugs.**

1. `convert_texture` compensates only paletted sources. RGBA16, RGBA32, IA
   and the RE-283 direct-colour bypass reach the non-paletted branch
   uncompensated, and the branch's `Psm5551` path is unreachable. This
   covers 225 variants, 165 of them materially bad. RE-305 describes the
   compensation as general.
2. Nine variants pack texels that differ from the decoded N64 reference.
   * I4/I8 textures drawn with a TLUT loaded: the decoder ignores the TLUT,
     and the packer quantizes the intensity image to TLUT colours. `mesh.rs`
     does not track `G_MDSFT_TEXTLUT`.
   * CI textures whose loaded TLUT is shorter than the index range: these
     indices decode to (0,0,0,0) but pack to the nearest loaded entry.

The actual N64 behaviour for both cases needs the decomp/ROM before any
change is made.

No padded-addressing disagreement exists, and no accepted compensation
regresses the holdout.

**Not measured.** LBParticle frames and the fighter shadow texture use a
separate converter with no primitive coverage. Framebuffer textures carry no
texels.

**Follow-up (RE-313).** Both suspected bugs are fixed. The I4/I8 cases draw
with `G_TT_NONE` and only inherited a stale TLUT across task display lists;
the short-TLUT indices read stale TMEM, measured on the original game. Every
format now enters the compensator. The regenerated report lists 0 suspected
bugs and 0 level-0 mismatches.

`PrimDesc` pack v31 stores signed S10.5 phase on each draw (zero by default),
and `TextureMapping` includes both components in its cache key. The draw path
adds the normalized phase after the tile-origin subtraction, material UV
affine and +0.5 GE linear correction; regular texgen gets it in the texture
matrix, and linear texgen uses the authored-coordinate path after generating
its own origin-adjusted UV. The texture-matrix path neutralizes a prior GE
TexOffset register before it runs. Both frame reset and cache invalidation
force the next draw to reinstall its own mapping. Clamp/repeat and prebaked
mirror continue to use the packed texture dimensions. There is no global
phase adjustment.

Dense training uses RE-312's four phase-varied points in each cell touched by
training or validation, excluding all validation points. It is selected only
for the exact report-listed use sites. The existing converter applies its
usual format, promotion and alpha gates, GE-exact integer refinement, and
independent validation. A selected successful conversion gets a distinct
immutable texture-cache identity. The four accepted cases promote to
RGBA8888; natural formats remain in force for the rejected cases.

## Final deployable pass and manual-fix triage (2026-09-24)

The regenerated report's three remaining section 5a rows were re-tested at
their exact use sites through the unchanged packer gates: v6 `52:0x2EE8`
(no solve), v109 `71:0x4830` (stays RGBA5551, holdout -5.0%/-6.4% per site,
-5.7% overall) and v111 `71:0x6840` (stays RGBA5551, -4.1% against the
shipped texels). None promotes to RGBA8888 and none reaches the 25% bar, so
nothing was deployed. The trial pack differed; the reverted pack rebuilds
byte-identically to SHA-256 `97b3f4d8...5707f216`. These rows are recorded in
`residual_fixes::DENSE_GATE_REJECTED`, so the report no longer counts their
report-only dense fit as deployable. Section 5a is now empty. The three
variants are `accept`.

The 189 `hand-edit-candidate` rows were triaged in the new report section
11. Section 5b's oracle is fitted on the sparse holdout it is scored on,
which has about 1-2 samples per touched texel cell. Scored on the
phase-uniform set, which it never saw, that oracle is *worse* than the
shipped texture for most rows, often by several hundred percent. The report
therefore adds a cross-phase bound. It fits on the phase-uniform set U1
(16 samples per cell, all 32 residues) and scores on U2, the same cells
shifted by (4, 4)/32 texel. This is the out-of-sample estimate for a
hand-authored same-resolution texture. Summed over all 189 rows, the
holdout oracle claims -65.6%. The cross-phase fit gives -2.3%. Only 62 rows
keep at least 25% and 13 keep at least 40%.

Cutout silhouettes do not transfer. The flip-minimizing alpha search cuts
alpha-test flips 30-60% on U1, where it is fitted. On U2 it usually adds
flips, with or without an RGB refit. The 3-point and GE-bilinear alpha
contours differ in shape, so an alpha edit moves disagreements to other
phases. No `ALPHA_EDIT` qualifies. Animated-palette rows (Meta Crystal,
Zebes acid) have their error spread evenly over all palette states. No
index-only or per-state direct variant keeps a cross-phase gain there. The
five texgen variants keep 86-88% of their error even under the
holdout-fitted oracle (8-10% gain on U1), so no texture-only change helps.

The triage result is 1 `MANUAL_FIX_RECOMMENDED` (v766 `120:0x418`,
Board-the-Platforms Mario, RGBA8888 override, -47.4% cross-phase, +1,728 B
level 0), 12 `VISUAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and 176 `ACCEPT`. Most review rows are
large CI4 backdrops, where RGBA8888 costs 0.2-1.8 MB of level 0. No manual
edits were implemented. Runtime and pack are unchanged.
