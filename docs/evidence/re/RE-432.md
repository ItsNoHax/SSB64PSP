## Gameplay port

`itbox.c` and `ittaru.c` join Capsule and Egg in `item::container`. Crate
(file 251 + 0x5CC) and Barrel (+ 0x634) are heavy: ROM tests check their
attributes, the shared four-step explosion table (20/350, 15/250, 10/150,
1/0, deleted at multi 8) and their model keys (86 + 0x6778, 86 + 0x71A8).
Both take damage in Wait and after release; Crate opens at 15 percent and
Barrel at 10. Thrown/dropped hits open them. Crate's opening rerolls
utilities without the terminal explosion weight
(`itBoxCommonCheckSpawnItems`). Barrel's thrown/dropped map callback opens
on a floor hit at `vel.y >= 90` and then always destroys, rolls below 30
(lifetime 360, counting only while still, blinking under 60) and rebounds
in between; a rolling barrel opens on a wall and returns to Dropped off a
ledge. `itBoxContainerSmashMakeEffect` is a display effect of seven pieces
(42 RNG draws, 90 ticks, gravity 1.3) drawn from the direct display list
86 + 0x68F0, packed by key (pack v83). The default switches enable all
four containers.

Fighters pick heavy items with each fighter's heavy reach. `HeavyGet`
ends in `LiftWait`, or Donkey Kong's `ThrowFWait`; `LiftTurn` flips facing
on step 4 of 8 while turning the model yaw. A or B selects
`HeavyThrow{F,B,F4,B4}` (70 at 60°, 90 at 20°); Donkey Kong's cargo
statuses check the heavy throw first and use his own throw statuses,
which land and lose ground without dropping. Damage and floor loss drop
the item from the common lift statuses.

## Hidden parts and the figatree walk

The held crate first drew at the fighter's feet. Mario's item-heavy joint
28 is not a mask-enabled model part: `setup_parts` is `0xFFFFFF00`, so
joints 4–27 exist. Joint 28 is `FTAttributes.hiddenparts[3]`
(`{28, 4, 1, 0}`): `ftMainSetStatus` inserts it as joint 4's last child
when the motion's `anim_desc` sets bit `0x10000000`
(`ftMainAddHiddenPartID`), before `lbCommonAddFighterPartsFigatree` binds
scripts in a pre-order walk from `TopN`'s child. Every fighter's fourth
hidden part is its `joint_itemheavy_id`. The packer bound scripts to
mask-enabled parts only, so the trailing script (RE-331's "unbound
trailing model part") was dropped and joint 28 stayed at rest.

`tools/gen-anim-table.py` now emits `HIDDEN_PARTS`, each motion's
`anim_desc` bits 31..5. `ssb_rom::fighter::figatree_order` rebuilds the
tree (mask-enabled descriptors under the latest node one level up, then
each enabled part by kind: 0 last child, 1 first, 2 second, 3
interposed) and walks it; `romtool pack` binds by that order for motions
with hidden model parts (158 clips: Catch/Throw, heavy items, Donkey
Kong's cargo, Yoshi's Egg Lay, Kirby's Inhale and Egg Lay, Ness's
yo-yo smashes). Motions without them keep the existing binding; a ROM
test proves the walk reproduces it for all 12 fighters and that every
hidden-part motion's table covers its walk. 70 more joint scripts bind.

## The item root

`itManagerMakeItem` calls `lbCommonEjectTreeDObj` on descriptor 0's DObj
after setup, so an item's root is descriptor 1's DObj and the position
replaces its translation. The Crate descriptors (0,0,-225) and (0,0,225)
therefore do not offset the loose model. `itMainSetFighterHold` inserts a
kind-82 attach DObj (`func_ovl0_800C9F70`: the fighter joint's matrix,
axes normalized, its own transform unused) above the root, and
`gcSetDObjTransformsForGObj` gives the attach descriptor 0 and the root
descriptor 1. Held, the crate sits 225 along the joint's Z. The
`->child` that `itBoxThrownSetStatus`/`itTaruDroppedSetStatus` rotate is
that root, since they run before the release ejects the attach DObj.
`psp-game` draws every container this way; Capsule's loose model moves 90
units down as a result.

## N64 reference

A temporary decomp build booted Training on Dream Land with Mario and a
crate made at Mario + (75, 500) (`sc1PTrainingModeFuncStart`), with the
held-item display's container halt (`"Error : wrong item!"`) disabled; the
patch was reverted and the decomp rebuilt to SHA-1 `e2929e10…`. Route: A
at frame 330, B at 380. The loose crate covers Mario. The lift, the flat
`LiftWait` pose with the crate on his back, the throw arc and the
explosion match the PSP `trainingheavy` captures (A at 60, B at 110)
frame for frame within camera framing. Before the root fix, the loose
crate drew one crate-width aside and the held one one crate-width out.

## Consequences

- `f1-training-grab` changes 259,600 pixels (2x): the held fighter hangs
  from the animated item-heavy joint, in front of the catcher instead of
  inside him. Not compared with the N64.
- Yoshi's tongue tip (joint 31, his catch box) now extends 340–785 units
  ahead in the catch window. `yoshiegg` caught only because the tip sat
  at rest inside Yoshi; it now backs off, turns and tongues at tick 330,
  and captures the laid egg on the top platform at 460 (318,388 pixels).
- `f1-training-capsule` changes 1,280 pixels from the item-root rule.
