# RE-398 — The VS pause menu and its camera

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, ROM decode, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless golden captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: interface, match, camera, pack, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/{pause,battle,battle_tests,camera}.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/sprite.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`, `tests/golden/f1-vs-pause.png`

## Question

START did nothing in a VS battle (RE-389 left Pause and Unpause as
frozen states nothing entered). What does the pause menu do?

## Source

- **Pausing** (`ifCommonBattleGoUpdateInterface`). START during Go
  pauses the battle before `gcRunAll`. If the player is inside the camera
  bounds, `gmCameraCheckPausePlayerOutBounds` passes and
  `gmCameraSetStatusPlayerZoom` closes in on them:
  - the eye offset is `(0, 0)`;
  - the distance is `closeup_camera_zoom`;
  - the pan is 0.1 and the field of view 29°.
  This is the Default kind. If the player is outside the bounds, the kind
  is PlayerNA and there is no zoom.
- **The menu** (`ifCommonBattlePauseInitInterface`).
  - Every interface `GObj` is hidden.
  - `ifCommonBattlePauseProcDisplay` fills five white rectangles for the
    border (`G_CYC_FILL`, both corners inclusive).
  - "1P" sits at (213, 191).
  - The decals from file 197 (`gGMCommonFiles[5]`) have their own
    primitive and environment colours. There are twelve for Default
    (with the stick and arrows) and ten for PlayerNA.
- **In the menu** (`ifCommonBattlePauseUpdateInterface`).
  - Past a stick dead zone of 8, the view turns by 0.000333 radians per
    unit (`gGMCameraPauseCameraEyeX`/`Y`), within ±50° across and ±20°
    up and down.
  - START unpauses. The camera returns to its previous status and eases
    back for 20 ticks, or 0 ticks for PlayerNA.
  - A+B+R+Z with a tap resets the battle (`is_reset`, then Set).
  - Otherwise the zoom camera runs. `gmCameraPlayerZoomFuncCamera` falls
    back to the battle camera while the player is out of bounds.
- **Resuming** (`ifCommonBattlePauseRestoreInterfaceAll`).
  - Each restore tick moves the turn 10% back toward its value at the
    pause, and runs the battle camera.
  - On the last tick the menu goes, the interface shows again, Go
    resumes and the world runs that same frame. The timer restarts on the
    next Go frame.
- **The turn in the battle camera.** `gmCameraGetAdjustAtAngle` adds the
  pause turn to the battle camera's own eye angles too.

## Implementation

- **`ssb_game::battle`.** Adds `pause`, `unpause(restore)` and `reset`, and
  the Unpause restore in `begin_frame`. Two host tests cover the frozen
  clock, the 20-tick ease, the timer restart and the reset.
- **`ssb_game::camera`.** `Camera::pause_eye`, which `eye_direction` now
  reads, and `Camera::tick_player_zoom` (`gmCameraUpdatePlayerZoom`).
- **`ssb_game::pause`.** The decals, the border, `kind_for`, `steer` and
  `ease_back`, with two host tests.
- **Sprites.** `ssb_rom::sprite::BATTLE_PAUSE` lists file 197's 16 sprites
  from `reloc_data.us.h`, and a ROM test decodes them. The pack is
  28,096,032 bytes, SHA-256
  `b41c195101349351f22c19e698372c08ed4d013bacb03cea723d573b23bbe7b4`.
- **`psp-game`.**
  - `pause_frame` handles START, the stick, the reset and the zoom camera
    before `begin_frame`.
  - `training_frame` eases the camera back during Unpause.
  - `draw_training` draws the pause menu instead of the HUD.
  - A reset returns to the menu, not the results.
  - The new `vspause` capture presses START at tick 500 and captures at
    560.

## Deviations

- **Model detail.** The pausing fighter's high-detail switch
  (`ftParamSetModelPartDetailAll`) is not ported; the port always draws
  one detail.
- **Other pause work.** The rumble stop, the pause sound, the BGM volume
  and the wallpaper's perspective pause are not ported.
- **Bonus pause.** Bonus stages' map zoom and "L: RETRY" are not ported.

## Verification

- **Golden.** The new `f1-vs-pause` shows Mario zoomed in, the white
  border, "1P PAUSE", the stick with its arrows and the "A+B+Z+R: RESET"
  line. It was identical in both captures.
- **Neutral-stick fix.** `vssuddendeath` now has an explicitly neutral
  stick. RE-397 had left it to the fallback, which was -30 on locked
  ticks only; its golden is unchanged.
- **All goldens.** 104 of 104 match.
- **Gates.** Workspace tests, Clippy and both PSP builds pass with no
  `psp-game` warnings. The release `run` is 119,484 bytes.
