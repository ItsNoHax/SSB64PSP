# RE-393 — Fighter emblems and stock icons, and the CPU's fifth emblem colour

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, ROM decode, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: interface, fighter, stage, pack, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-rom/src/{sprite,stage,pack}.rs`, `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/{hud,hud_tests}.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`

## Question

The damage display (RE-392) left out each fighter's series emblem. Where
do a fighter's HUD sprites live, and what colour does each emblem take?

## Source

- **`FTSprites`.** `FTAttributes.sprites` is a pointer to `FTSprites`
  (`fttypes.h`): `stock_sprite`, `stock_luts` (one palette per costume) and
  `emblem`. Its offset is 0x340. That comes from counting on from
  `translate_scales` at 0x324, which romtool already reads, through four
  pointers and three joint ids.
  - **Where it points.** In every playable fighter's main file the
    pointer leads to an intern `FTSprites`. Its sprite pointers lead to
    the fighter's model file. For Mario, `llMarioModelStockSprite` is at
    296+0x72D0 and `llMarioModelFTEmblemSprite` at 296+0x74C8, as
    `reloc_data.us.h` names them.
  - **Stock icons.** They are 8×10 CI4 sprites with 7 or 8 costume
    palettes.
  - **Emblems.** They are I4 sprites, 22–30 texels wide.
- **Placement.** `ifCommonPlayerDamageInitInterface` makes the emblem the
  display's first `SObj`:
  - its origin is `(x - w/2) + 3, (210 - h/2) - 3`, truncated;
  - its scale is 1;
  - its `attr` is `SP_TEXSHUF | SP_TRANSPARENT`;
  - its colour is `gMPCollisionGroundData->emblem_colors[players[].color]`.
  `ifCommonPlayerDamageProcDisplay` draws it before checking
  `is_show_interface`, so the emblems show during the VS countdown and
  the digits only from "Go".
- **Colours.** `emblem_colors` is `SYColorRGB[4]` at `MPGroundData`+0x50,
  after `wallpaper` (0x48) and the fog colour (0x4C).
  - A human's `color` is its port (`mnPlayersVS`). A CPU outside team
    battles, and Training's dummy (`sc1PTrainingModeInitVars`), have
    `color` 4.
  - `emblem_colors[4]` reads the next word, which the decomp names
    `unused`. Every stage sets it to a grey: `0xDCDCDC00` on Peach's
    Castle, `0xC8C8C800` on Zebes. It is the CPU's colour.

## Implementation

- **`ssb_rom::sprite`.** Adds `fighter_sprites`, which follows
  `FTAttributes.sprites` to the three places. It also adds
  `decode_with_tlut`, for a stock icon under each costume's palette.
- **`ssb_rom::stage`.** `GroundData.emblem_colors` reads five colours: the
  four players' and the CPU's grey.
- **Pack v62.**
  - `SpriteDesc` gains `fighter`, `role` (emblem or stock) and `costume`,
    and grows to 28 bytes.
  - `StageDesc` gains `emblem_colors` (120 bytes).
  - romtool converts the twelve emblems and 92 stock icons, 116 sprites
    with the digits.
  - `Pack::fighter_sprite` finds a fighter's emblem, or its stock icon for
    a costume.
  - The pack is 27,403,312 bytes, SHA-256
    `f072a92c463632f97b3d7634eeb3f320df728e424010acf65e2f9422b6864dd0`.
- **`ssb_game::hud`.** Adds `emblem_origin` and `CPU_COLOR`.
- **`psp-game`.** `draw_training` draws each fighter's emblem in its
  colour, then the digits. The player's colour is 0 and the dummy's is 4.
  The HUD pass moved out of `run` and into `draw_training`.

## Deviations

- **Stock icons.** They are packed but not drawn. The default VS rule is
  time, which shows no stocks.
- **Team colours.** Team colours (`dIFCommonPlayerTeamColorIDs`) are not
  used; there are no team battles.

## Verification

- **ROM decode.** Host tests decode all twelve emblems and stock icons.
  Mario's places match the named `reloc_data.us.h` offsets. An image dump
  showed the twelve series emblems and the twelve stock faces.
- **Emblem colours.** All nine VS stages give four distinct player colours
  and a grey fifth.
- **Goldens.** 35 goldens changed inside the HUD band (y 438–498 of the
  capture) and were rebaselined, each captured twice.
  - `f1-vs-countdown` now shows the red and grey mushrooms without
    digits.
  - `f1-training-kirby` shows Kirby's star.
- **Tests.** Workspace tests, Clippy and both PSP builds pass. `run` is
  119,532 bytes.
