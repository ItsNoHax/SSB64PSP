# RE-332 — Gameplay joint transforms for attacks, catches and held fighters

Status: COMPLETE (decomp source, host tests, both PSP builds, PPSSPP grab capture; physical PSP not captured)
Topics: fighter, gameplay, collision, grab, capture, animation, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/attack.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/fighter.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/grab.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/gu.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`

**Question.** How are a fighter's motion-command hit and catch offsets placed,
and how does a held fighter's TopN follow the catcher's heavy-item joint?

**Source.** `ftmain.c` updates each live attack collision by passing its
`offset` through `gmCollisionGetFighterPartsWorldPosition(attack_coll->joint)`.
`gmcollision.c` composes the joint and ancestor matrices and transforms the
offset as a point. The US Mario, Fox and Donkey `MainMotion.c` scripts supply
the `jid` arguments. `ftcommoncapturepulled.c` takes the negative translation
of the held fighter's first child of TopN, scales it by the held fighter's
root scale, then transforms it through the catcher's `joint_itemheavy_id`
matrix. It also extracts that matrix's rotation for the held TopN. The pulled
status writes X and Z and leaves Y to the floor map; thrown statuses write
all three coordinates.

**Implementation.** `psp-runtime` composes the current skeleton once per
fighter tick and samples its model nodes into pack-independent
`JointTransform` values in `ssb-game`; joint IDs 4 onward index the packed
model parts. TopN is sampled as joint 0. Attack and catch checks transform
their authored offsets through the source joint. Repeated move windows reuse
their source joint pattern. The grab snapshot carries the full heavy-item
joint transform. The held fighter subtracts its current first-child
translation through that transform, and the PSP draw path uses the joint's
normalized basis for TopN rotation. Host callers without a skeleton retain a
root fallback for non-root joints.

**Verification.** Host tests cover a Jab1 collision at a displaced hand, a
catch at an animated hand, a rotated and scaled hit offset, and a held child
offset through a rotated catcher joint. `cargo test --workspace` passes 905 tests. Both PSP release builds
pass. The `f1-training-grab` PPSSPP software capture changed 10,908 pixels
after the held pose correction and matched on two consecutive captures after
rebaselining. A separate scripted Training capture completed. No pack format
or generated asset changed.

**Limits.** The target hurtbox remains one root sphere, while the original
uses joint-bound capsules and swept attack positions. Some earlier move data
condensed multiple source hitboxes with identical damage/size/offset but
different `jid` into one; this batch attaches every represented hitbox but
does not restore those omitted boxes. The held-root scale is still treated
as one for the current normal-sized Training fighters; giant or shrunken
capture poses need their own scale input. PSP hardware has not been captured
for this pose.
