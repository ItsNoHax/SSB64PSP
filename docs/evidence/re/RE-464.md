## RE-100 was wrong

RE-100 measured each wipe's photo tiles as 300 x 5 and 300 x 6 and
concluded the wipes show a repeating six-row smear of the screen's top. It
read the strips' `G_SETTIMG` (always offset 0) but not their `G_LOADTILE`
rows: each strip loads a different band of the photo and its
`G_SETTILESIZE` origin matches, so on the RDP a vertex's `t` addresses the
whole picture. Subtracting each strip's origin, as the converter did for
every tile, folded every band onto rows 0-6. Pack v104 keeps the absolute
coordinates; `tests/transitions.rs` requires one 300 x 220 photo texture
and every wipe's photo vertices to span rows 0 to at least 214.

## PSP deviations

- The photo is resampled at the N64's 320 x 240 from the 362 x 272
  pillarboxed PSP frame (pixel centres), in RGB565.
- A fading fighter blends with fixed factors `(a, 1 - a)`: exact for its
  opaque primitives (`COMBINED * ENV_ALPHA` with combined alpha 1). A
  primitive that also carries texel alpha keeps its alpha test but not its
  partial-alpha weighting during the 12-frame fade.
- The steal's battle half runs after every fighter's update in the same
  frame, not inside the thief's update; the stolen fighter's count changes
  a few slots later in that frame. Its sound and the winner's rumble are not
  ported (no audio, no rumble).
