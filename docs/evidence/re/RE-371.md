# RE-371 — Held fighter TopN keeps its opposite facing rotation

Status: COMPLETE (decomp source, PPSSPPHeadless software capture and goldens, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no original-ROM or physical-PSP visual capture)
Topics: grab, animation, rendering, gameplay, visual-regression
Relevant files: `psp-runtime/src/gu.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `tests/golden/f1-training-grab.png`

## Question

After RE-370 made the scripted Training grab connect, why did the caught
yellow Mario appear to face left like the red catcher, rather than toward
him?

## Source and cause

`ftCommonCapturePulledProcCapture` sets the caught fighter's `lr` opposite
the catcher. `ftMainSetStatus` then writes that `lr` as the caught fighter's
TopN Y rotation. Separately, `ftCommonCapturePulledProcPhysics` extracts
the catcher's heavy-item joint rotation and puts it on the caught fighter's
root DObj. Thus the source draws the held model under **both** rotations:
the catcher-joint root and the held fighter's own TopN facing.

`Gpu::model_transform_joint` already loaded the normalized catcher-joint
matrix, but `psp-game` used it in place of the normal fighter facing
transform. The caught fighter's TopN facing rotation was absent. With a
left-facing catcher, the old image made both models point left even though
the held fighter's gameplay `facing` was Right.

## Correction and verification

The held draw now postmultiplies its own facing Y rotation after loading
the catcher's joint matrix. PPSSPP's tick-118 log reports
`player_facing=Left`, `dummy_facing=Right`, `CatchWait`, `CaptureWait`, and
mutual grab links. The corrected image shows the caught Mario's face
toward the camera. That appearance follows the two source rotations at
this frame; it is an inference from the decomp and PSP pose, not a direct
original-ROM screenshot comparison.

The `f1-training-grab` golden changed 4,848 pixels at 2x, confined to
the held Mario. Two repeat captures were identical, and the grab and jab
goldens match twice. Workspace tests, both PSP release builds, rustfmt,
the PPSSPP grab smoke, and documentation checks pass. The asset pack is
unchanged. The viewer retains its five existing warnings.
