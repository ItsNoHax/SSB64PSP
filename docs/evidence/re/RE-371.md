# RE-371 — Held fighter TopN takes only the catcher joint rotation

Status: COMPLETE (decomp source, PPSSPPHeadless software capture and goldens, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no original-ROM or physical-PSP visual capture)
Topics: grab, animation, rendering, gameplay, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/grab.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/gu.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `tests/golden/f1-training-grab.png`

## Question

After RE-370 made the scripted Training grab connect, does the caught
fighter's drawn orientation compose its own facing yaw on top of the
catcher's heavy-item joint rotation?

## Correction to the first version of this record

The first version of RE-371 (commit `b287dfc`) answered yes and added a
`facing_turn(facing)` Y rotation after the held fighter's joint matrix.
That reading was wrong. It treated the TopN facing yaw and the root rotation
from the catcher's joint as two separate DObjs. In the source they are the
same DObj, and the second write replaces the first.

## Source

1. `ftCommonCapturePulledProcCapture` sets `this_fp->lr = -capture_fp->lr`.
2. `ftMainSetStatus` writes
   `fp->joints[nFTPartsJointTopN]->rotate.y = fp->lr * 90°`.
3. `ftCommonCapturePulledProcPhysics` then calls
   `ftCommonCapturePulledRotateScale(..., &DObjGetStruct(fighter_gobj)->rotate.vec.f)`.
4. `ftManagerMakeFighter` creates TopN with
   `topn_joint = gcAddDObjForGObj(fighter_gobj, NULL)` and stores it in
   `fp->joints[nFTPartsJointTopN]`. That first DObj is what
   `DObjGetStruct(fighter_gobj)` returns, so both writes target one DObj.
5. `func_ovl2_800EDA0C` normalizes the catcher joint matrix rows and writes
   all three of x, y and z rotation. The facing yaw from step 2 does not
   survive.

The held fighter's gameplay `lr` stays opposite the catcher. It adds
nothing to the drawn orientation while the fighter is held.

## Implementation

- `ssb_game::grab::held_root_axes` is the portable form of
  `func_ovl2_800EDA0C`. It returns the joint axes with scale removed.
- `Gpu::model_transform_joint` no longer takes `Facing`. It loads the
  normalized axes times the held fighter's model scale, with no further
  rotation.
- Both held-fighter draw sites in `psp-game` pass only position, joint and
  scale.
- `capture_pulled` still sets `f.facing = holder.facing.flipped()`. The
  RE-370 scripted Training grab and the attachment math are unchanged.

  catcher facing, the captured fighter faces the other way. The drawn
  axes equal the normalized catcher joint axes. They differ from the same
  axes with a ±90° facing yaw composed on.
- PPSSPPHeadless `grab` log at tick 118: `player_facing=Left`,
  `dummy_facing=Right`, `CatchWait`, `CaptureWait`, mutual grab links.
- `f1-training-grab` was rebaselined with 4,848 changed pixels at 2x. It is
  byte-identical to the golden before `b287dfc` (the RE-370 image). The grab
  and jab goldens each match twice in two separate verify runs.
- Workspace tests, pinned 1.98.0 Clippy with warnings denied, and both

  The asset pack is unchanged.
