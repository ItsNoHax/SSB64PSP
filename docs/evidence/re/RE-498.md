# RE-498 — A closed-form billboard matrix with `sqrt.s` cuts the billboard span 310 to 182 us

Status: COMPLETE (PSP-2000, 6.61 ARK, PSPLink v3.2.1, `profile,capture_scene_file,no_audio` builds of `vs4@2400`, host0 PRX with a local pack symlink, means over profile reports 3..18, each build relinked with its own searched `link-order.txt` per RE-494/RE-496)
Topics: performance, hardware, precision, billboard, golden
Relevant files: `psp-runtime/src/meshdraw.rs`, `crates/ssb-engine/src/math.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/profile.rs`, `psp-game/link-order.txt`, `tests/golden/scenes.tsv`

## Question

RE-497 left the billboard arm's `load_billboard_matrix` (a `sceGum*`
Translate/MultMatrix/RotateZ/Scale chain) unmeasured. Does it cost
enough to replace, and what does a replacement risk?

## Findings

- `vs4@2400` averages 22 billboard nodes a frame. The `sceGum*` chain cost
  310 us a frame (14.1 us a node). The opening has billboards only in some
  windows (up to 32-36 a frame); `op-run` and `op-clash` have none.
- `ssb_engine::math::billboard_matrix` is the closed form of `T*B*Rz*S`
  as scalar f32 in the order PPSSPP's `vmmul` uses; it is bit-exact against
  a reference chain over 200,000 random cases (test
  `billboard_matrix_matches_the_gum_chain`; reordering one column's
  products makes it fail). cos/sin still come from `vrot`, as gum's
  `RotateZ` did; placement and scale use `sqrt.s` (RE-497).
- Result, each build on its own searched order: 55.31 -> 56.45 FPS
  (+1.14); billboard span 310 -> 182 us (8.3 us a node); frame CPU
  18,090 -> 17,720 us. Only the billboard span's -128 us is attributable to
  the change directly; the rest of the frame gain is partly seed-to-seed
  layout spread (RE-496). Per round FPS: baseline 55.31/55.31/55.32,
  new 56.40/56.47/56.48.
- The remaining cost is instruction-cache misses, not arithmetic.
- The link order was re-searched for the new sizes. A real
  `cargo psp --release` is byte-identical to the `relink.py` relink of the
  chosen order; 312 searched functions keep address and size between
  capture and release builds.

## Golden coverage and blind spot

- 58 of 105 billboard-related goldens observe billboard geometry (scaling x
  by 1.01 changes them); a 1-ULP change in scale matches all 105. The
  goldens cannot see 1 ULP of billboard scale, so a real PSP-2000's VFPU
  rounding in the old gum chain may have differed from the new scalar compose
  by about 1 ULP undetected (same standing blind spot as RE-497).
- Three animated opening goldens close the movie-draw gap
  (`Span::MovieMesh`): `f1-opening-mario` (`op-mario@60`),
  `f1-opening-run` (`op-run@140`), `f1-opening-clash` (`op-clash@80`);
  `verify --twice` gives zero differing pixels. `op-newcomers` frames were
  black or unusable and were not added.

## Setup trap

The `ms0:/PSP/GAME/ssb64` pack on the test PSP is v108-sized (64 MB), not
v109. A hardware profile run with a PRX on host0 needs a local pack
symlink beside it (`ln -s ../../../assets/generated/ssb64.pak`); otherwise
nothing draws (`prims=0`).
