# RE-410 — The VS results screen's wallpaper, text and table

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, `reloc_data.us.h`, ROM sprite decode, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: menus, match, sprites, visual-regression, pack
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/{results,results_layer,results_layer_tests}.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/sprite.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/meshdraw.rs`, `psp-game/src/{main,results_screen}.rs`

## Question

RE-409 drew the results' fighters over black, with the rankings as plain
slots along the top. Which sprites does `mnVSResults` load, where does
each piece of text and each number of the table go, in what colours,
on which tic does it appear, and in what order do the layers draw?

## Source

All in `mn/mnvsmode/mnvsresults.c` unless noted. Sprite offsets are from
`include/reloc_data.us.h`.

- **Files.** `dMNVSResultsFileIDs` loads `MNVSResults` (file 34),
  `IFCommonPlayerTags` (38), `MNPlayersGameModes` (18),
  `IFCommonPlayerDamage` (164), `FTEmblemModels` (35), `IFCommonDigits`
  (36), `IFCommonAnnounceCommon` (37) and `FTStocksZako` (25).
- **Sprites converted.** File 34: `TKOText` 0x358, `PlaceText` 0x990,
  `KOsText` 0xD38, `PtsText` 0x10D8, `1PArrow` to `4PArrow` 0x49E8,
  0x4B08, 0x4C28, 0x4D48, `Wallpaper` 0xD5C8 and `Winner` 0xE2A0. File 18:
  `FreeForAllText` 0x280 and `TeamBattleText` 0x4E0. File 36: `Digits0`
  to `Digits9` (0x68 to 0x698) and `DigitsDash` 0x710. File 38: `1P` to
  `4P` (0x258, 0x4F8, 0x798, 0xA38) and `CP` 0xCD8. Files 37 and 164 and
  the stock icons were already in the pack. The ROM decode gives the row
  labels, arrows, digits and tags as IA8, the wallpaper as 300 × 220 I4,
  the game-mode labels as I4 and the winner plate as RGBA16.
- **Draw order.** Every piece has its own camera. A higher camera
  priority draws first: the default camera's black clear (100), the
  wallpaper (80), `WallpaperTint2` (70), the emblem (60), `WallpaperTint`
  (55), the fighters (50), the player tags (30), the results text (20),
  the tint (17), the table (15) and the wipe (10). All use the viewport
  (10, 10) to (310, 230).
- **Wallpaper.** `mnVSResultsFuncRun` makes it on
  `sMNVSResultsDrawWallpaperTic` (80, or 1 after a no contest), and after a
  contest also `WallpaperTint2`. The sprite is at (10, 10). Its display
  sets `(PRIMITIVE - ENVIRONMENT) * TEXEL0 + ENVIRONMENT` and an opaque
  render mode. The colour pair is the winner's port, the winning team's
  (`team_colors` `{0, 1, 3}`), or `syUtilsRandIntRange(4)` after a no
  contest. That draw comes before the fighters on tic 1.
- **Fades.** `WallpaperTint` is made in `mnVSResultsFuncStart` at 0xFF;
  `WallpaperTint2` at 0xFF with the wallpaper. Each display subtracts 5
  and then fills the viewport black at that alpha. 0xFF is a multiple of 5,
  so the alpha stops at 0 and the eject never runs. The first covers the
  wallpaper and emblem until tic 51, the second the wallpaper until
  tic 130.
- **Results text.** On `sMNVSResultsMakeResultsTic` (120, or 1),
  `mnVSResultsMakeResultsText` and `mnVSResultsMakeLabel` run.
  `mnVSResultsMakeString` places letters from file 37 at a running x
  advanced by `widths[c] * scale`. A digit in the string adds that many
  pixels, a space `10 * scale`, and a period sits 26 lower. `scale` sets
  `sprite.scalex` only. The envcolor is `colors[id].prim` and the
  primitive is `colors[id].env` (white).
  - After a no contest: "NO CONTEST" at (30, 180), colour 4 (black).
  - In a free-for-all: the winner's name at `pos_x[fkind]` in red with
    `scales[fkind]` (0.7 for Captain Falcon and Pikachu, 0.6 for
    Jigglypuff), then `"W1I1N1S1!"` at `x_fkinds[fkind]` in purple. The
    US build uses "WINS!" for fighters too.
  - In a team battle: "RED", "BLUE" or "GREEN" in the team's colour, then
    "WINS!" at `x_teams[team]`.
- **Label.** `mnVSResultsLabelProcDisplay` fills (32, 42) to (282, 44)
  white in fill mode, then draws "FREE FOR ALL" or "TEAM BATTLE" (file 18)
  at (32, 29). The label's process is the kind's `mnVSResultsDrawResults*`
  proc.
- **Rows by tic.**
  - Time, free-for-all or team: tint at 180; header and KOs (y 66) at
    210; TKO (y 81) at 230; bar (y 98) at 250; points (y 104) at 270;
    places (y 124) at 290.
  - Stock, free-for-all or team: tint at 180; places (y 66) at 210; bar
    (y 110) at 230; header and KOs (y 124) at 250.
  - No contest: tint at 30; header and KOs (y 66) at 60; TKO (y 81) at 80.
- **Row pieces.**
  - Each row's label is at (26, y), or (10, y) for "PLACE", white.
  - The TKO row also has a white dash at (90, y + 3), except after a no
    contest.
  - `mnVSResultsGetColumnX` is the column of the player's order among the
    present players: 135 and 215; 125, 175 and 225; or 115, 155, 195 and
    235.
  - `mnVSResultsMakeNumber` puts the hundreds digit at x + 8, the tens at
    x + 16 when it or the hundreds is not zero, and the ones at x + 24. A
    negative number puts the dash 3 lower, just left of its first digit.
    The digits are file 36's.
  - `mnVSResultsSetNumberColor` makes the envcolor black and the primitive
    white. In a team battle it uses the team's colour instead:
    (0xFF, 0x82, 0x82), (0x91, 0xC0, 0xFF) or (0x9F, 0xFF, 0x9F).
  - The place row shows `mnVSResultsGetDisplayPlace`: the place plus 1,
    or 4 in a four-player free-for-all with two players in second.
    First place is the "winner" plate at column + 2. In a team battle,
    first-place players who are neither the winner nor a shared winner get
    the damage display's 1 instead. Other places are file 164's digits at
    column + 15.
  - `mnVSResultsMakeHeader` puts each present player's arrow at
    (column + 17, 49) and the stock icon, in the fighter's costume, 10 to
    the left.
- **Tint and bar.** `mnVSResultsTintProcDisplay` adds 9 to its alpha
  each display, up to 0x80, then fills the viewport black. The table
  camera draws over it. `mnVSResultsBarProcDisplay` adds 10 to
  `sMNVSResultsBarWidth` each display, up to 190, and fills (87, y) to
  (87 + width, y) white in fill mode. That is one row, with both corners
  included.
- **Player tags.** `mnVSResultsInitFighter` makes each fighter's tag:
  "1P" to "4P" for a human and "CP" for a CPU (file 38). The primitive is
  `dIFCommonPlayerTagPrimColors[color]` and the envcolor black
  (`if/ifcommon.c`). `mnVSResultsSetPlayerTagPosition` indexes
  `pos_xy_2p`, `_3p` or `_4p` by distance ID and spot. `pos_y_kinds` is
  all zeros.
- **Emblem.** After a contest, `mnVSResultsMakeEmblem` makes the winner's
  series emblem from file 35. It is a 3D `DObj` with a material animation
  in the player's or team's colour, at (0, 100, -11000) and scale 25.
  From tic 40 it shrinks by 0.15 per tic to 10 and rises by 11 per tic to
  y 1000.
- **Unset envcolor.** `gcAddSObjForGObj` copies the `Sprite` but never
  writes `envcolor`. The row labels, the game-mode label, the TKO row's
  dash and the stock icons never set it.

## Implementation

- **`ssb_rom::sprite`.** `VS_RESULTS`, `GAME_MODES`, `DIGITS` and
  `PLAYER_TAGS` join `FILES`, so `romtool pack` converts 28 more sprites
  (227 in all).
  - The pack format is unchanged: new sprites add rows to the existing
    sprite table (`VERSION` 61), as RE-394 to RE-397 did. So `VERSION`
    stays 64.
  - Pack v64 is now 29,127,792 bytes (617,792 more), SHA-256
    `abb0b38a5e472408faadb64511613e5a6f03ca3e1e826cea3c58b2219946eb66`.
    Most of the growth is the wallpaper in 8888.
- **`ssb_game::results`.** `Results` gains `is_team_battle` (kept after a
  no contest) and `team`.
- **`ssb_game::results_layer`.**
  - `Layer::tick` runs after `Results::tick` and before the fighters are
    made. It makes the wallpaper (and its random draw after a no
    contest), the tint and the bar on their tics. Then it applies the
    display procs' per-frame steps.
  - `Layer::visit` yields `Draw`s back to front: the wallpaper, the two
    fades, a `Fighters` marker, the tags, the results text, the tint, then
    the label and each row made so far.
  - `make_number`, `make_string`, `column_x`, `number_color_id`,
    `display_place` and `player_tag` port their source functions.
  - `results_scene`'s spot and distance helpers are now `pub(crate)` so
    the tags can use them.
- **`psp-runtime` `meshdraw`.**
  - `draw_sprite_xy` separates `scalex` from `scaley`; `draw_sprite` calls
    it with one scale.
  - `fill_rect_n64` is an untextured `gDPFillRectangle` in N64 pixels,
    blended below alpha 0xFF.
- **`psp-game`.** `results_screen::draw_all` walks the layer.
  - Each sprite is looked up by file and offset, or as a stock icon by
    kind and costume. It is drawn with `SP_FASTCOPY` cleared and
    `SP_TRANSPARENT` set on the pack's attr.
  - The wallpaper is a fill in the envcolor, then the white-alpha I4
    texture tinted by the primitive and blended by its intensity. That
    gives `ENV + (PRIM - ENV) * I`, the source's combiner.
  - `draw_results` takes the roster for kinds, costumes, tag colours and
    humanity. The plain ranking slots are gone.
  - The `vsnocontest` capture moves from tick 560 to 640, results tic
    117, so the golden shows the KOs and TKO rows.
  - `run` stays at 20,800 bytes in release.

## Deviations

- **Emblem.** The winner's 3D series emblem and its colour animation are
  not drawn.
- **Confetti and wipe.** `mnVSResultsMakeConfetti` and the wipe are still
  not drawn (RE-409). The fighters still draw opaque instead of fading in.
- **Unset envcolor.** The source leaves it unset on some pieces (see
  Source). The port uses black, the value of a zeroed SObj pool. Each of
  those pieces sets a white primitive, and every IA texel with full
  intensity draws that primitive.
- **Per-frame steps.** The fades, tint and bar step once per tic. The
  source steps them once per drawn frame, which is the same only while
  every tic is drawn.
- **Viewport.** The cameras' (10, 10) to (310, 230) scissor is not
  applied to the 2D pieces. Every piece lies inside it.
- **`scaley`.** The draw uses 1.0 for `sprite.scaley` and does not read
  the ROM `Sprite`'s value.
- **Wallpaper texture.** The I4 wallpaper is stored as 8888, like every
  other sprite. A 4-bit form would save most of the 617,792 bytes. That
  is left to `P5`.

## Verification

- **Host tests** (`results_layer_tests.rs`, 13):
  - `make_number` alignment and the negative dash;
  - `make_string` advances, digits, the x-only scale, the period and the
    space;
  - the columns for two, three and four players;
  - the wallpaper's tic, colour and fades, and that it makes no random
    draw after a contest;
  - the team wallpaper colour;
  - the no-contest random pick on tic 1;
  - the tint's 9-a-frame rise to 0x80 and the bar's growth to 190;
  - the stock table's order (places, bar, header and KOs), positions,
    plate and colours;
  - the winner's name, "WINS!" and the tags;
  - team number colours and the plate going only to the winner;
  - the four-player display place;
  - the time table's rows and dashes;
  - the no-contest table.

  `ssb_rom::sprite` checks the new files' sizes and formats against the
  ROM.
- **Goldens.** Both changed goldens were checked by eye and rebaselined.
  Both captures were identical.
  - `f1-vs-results` differs in 337,000 pixels at 2×. The blue wallpaper
    (Kirby is port 2) is behind the fighters. Luigi has a red "1P" tag and
    Kirby a grey "CP". The header shows each player's arrow with its stock
    icon. The "Place" row shows Luigi's white 2 and Kirby's gold 1 plate.
    The white bar is at y 110 and the "KOs" row reads 0 and 0. "KIRBY" is
    edged in red and "WINS!" in purple, under the tint. "Free-for-all" is
    above its white rule.
  - `f1-vs-no-contest` differs in 338,384 pixels at 2×. Its capture moved
    to results tic 117. The wallpaper is the random pick, here blue. The
    "KOs" and "TKO" rows read 0 and 0 with no dash. "NO CONTEST" is edged
    in black under the two clapping Marios.
  - After the rebaseline, 110 of 110 goldens match. The other 108 were
    unchanged by the new pack.
- **Gates.** Workspace tests, pinned 1.98.0 Clippy with warnings denied,
  rustfmt and both PSP release builds pass.

## Conclusion

The results screen now draws `mnVSResults`' wallpaper, fades, player
tags, winner text, tint and full table with their source timing,
positions and colours. The emblem and confetti remain.
