# RE-450 — PSP 1P campaign binding and its deviations

Status: PARTIAL (select START, scene flow and seven stages' battles on PSP; scene draws, remaining stages and runtime checks remain)
Topics: gameplay, front-end, battle
Relevant files: `psp-game/src/{campaign,main,play}.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/scene.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/spgame/{frontend,session}.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/{appear,kirby,camera,players_1p}.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/{stage,pack}.rs`

## Questions and source evidence

How does `psp-game` run `spgame::Frontend` and its stages' battles without
substituting or skipping what is not ported, and where does it differ?

| Finding | Source | Port |
|---|---|---|
| The select's ready START enters `nSCKind1PGame`; the select saved its data on exit | `mnPlayers1PGameSetSceneData`, `mnPlayers1PGameFuncRun` | `Frontend::start` begins past the select without a second save; `Saved::selection` carries the PSP select's fields |
| Each fighter's `FTDesc` comes from the battle state and `sSC1PGamePlayerSetups`: start map object, trait, copy, entry skip, magnify ignore, camera frame multiplier; `detail` is high below three fighters | `sc1PGameFuncStart`, `sc1PGameGetStartPosition` | `Session::entrants`; spawn by map-object kind, facing by `start_facing`, CPUs at `Behavior::Default` with the stage's trait |
| A Kirby made with a copy keeps it (`is_ignore_losecopy`) and its hat is the default model part | `ftManagerInitFighter` Kirby case | `kirby::init_copy` for every Kirby made, as every `FTDesc` names a copy |
| Team stages show the player's entry and drop the other members in from halfway between the camera and map tops, framed at their start until Go | `sc1PGameWaitStageTeamUpdate`, `ftCommonAppearSetPosition` | `appear::appear_set_position`; the wait events run on the battle clock |
| The common wait's zoom is `gmCameraSetStatusPlayerZoom(fighter, 0, 0, closeup, 0.1, 28)` until `gmCameraSetStatusDefault` | `sc1PGameWaitStageCommonUpdate` | The VS entry focus's zoom, held from `Zoom` to `CameraDefault` |
| A 1P enemy's camera target is held in the team camera bounds in every mode, replacing the normal and dead-up clamps | `gmCameraUpdateInterests`, `gmCameraSetTeamBoundsPosition` | `camera_interest` clamps to `StageDesc::team_camera`; `Interest::own_clamp` |
| The 1P-only stages have no ground controller | `grMainSetupMakeGround` | `StageSetup::new` returns none for them; stages are found by `GRKind` file (`stage::ground_kind`) |
| A fallen enemy sleeps or is destroyed and the next member made in its slot, skipping entry, with `ifCommonPlayerDamageStopBreakAnim` | `ftCommonDeadCheckRebirth`, `sc1PGameSpawnEnemyTeamNext` | `Session::next_enemy`, a fresh `Dummy` at the drop-in point, `set_wait_or_fall` |

## Deviations

- The original replaces a team member inside the fallen fighter's update.
  The PSP makes the newcomer after the frame, so it first runs one frame
  later.
- Yoshi Team members' `shade` and the stage-clear victory zoom
  (`Battle::set_zoom`) are not drawn; VS has no victory zoom either.
- The 1P-only stages' wallpapers are not packed, so Yoshi's Island and
  Dream Land's 1P stages draw no wallpaper.
- Announcements, continue cues and stage music have no audio.
- The intro, continue and stage-clear scenes run their controllers but
  draw only their clear colour.
- Giant Donkey Kong, Metal Mario, Race to the Finish, the Polygon Team,
  Master Hand and every other unported scene (bonus stages, challengers,
  ending) end the campaign at the menu. Nothing substitutes for them.
- The PSP has one controller; a campaign whose player is not on port 0
  ends at the menu.
