## Integration and boundaries

`Frontend::campaign` starts from scene/backup data saved by `players_1p`.
It preserves that PSP select; RE-448's duplicate `spgame::select` needs
reconciliation later. The host builds the roster from BattleState and
PlayerSetup using common ground kinds and authored map-object spawns. It
installs `Battle::new_1p`, item switches, KO configuration, CPU traits, copy
kind, camera multiplier and interface positions. CPU World knows it is 1P.
Initial facing agrees with `sc1PGameGetEnemyStartLR`; replacement facing
comes from EnemySpawn. Configure and Wait/Fall preserve it.

Session temporarily owns the live battle during falls/replacements.
`collect_fall` drains callbacks and samples percent before consuming
`dead.scored` once. Final callbacks are collected before `finish_battle`
transfers time, stocks and records and the frontend synchronizes.

Link, Yoshi Team and Fox are supported. Break the Targets stops explicitly.
Other bonus, boss, ending, challenger and message requests also block;
missing stage/fighter assets block separately. START/B returns to the menu.
No request is skipped or substituted with VS. Metal Mario, Giant DK,
Polygons and Master Hand are absent from the playable-fighter pack.
(Correction, RE-457: Master Hand's model and idle clip were packed; his
motions, cameras and sprites arrived in pack v101.)

## Deviations and deferred work

- Enemy replacement runs after that frame's fall collection, rather than
  inside the dead-status callback. Pending requests are consumed before the
  next fighter update; event-aligned N64 equivalence remains unproven.
- Intro, Continue and stage-clear run portable controllers with interim
  rectangles. Authored sprites/models/cameras remain. The menu stands in
  for the 1P mode menu.
- Yoshi same-costume shade, `is_magnify_ignore`, authored 1P CP/heart tags,
  music and persistent backup writes remain. Generic battle tags still draw.
- Later-stage replacements, Continue and stage-clear PSP paths have source
  and portable host coverage; this run did not exercise them on PPSSPP.
