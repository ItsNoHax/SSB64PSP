# RE-415 — The fighters' effects, the display hit effects and their random draws

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, ROM animation replays, packed-bank runs, host tests broken and restored, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice, RNG-isolation A/B captures, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: effect, particle, fighter, combat, animation, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/{fteffect,fteffect_tests,effect,effect_tests,colanim,combat,attack,reaction,status,fighter,weapon}.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/motion/{mod,scripts}.rs`, `tools/gen-motion-scripts.py`, `crates/ssb-rom/tests/{display_effects,particle_runtime}.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, 27 `tests/golden/f1-*.png`

## Question

RE-413 ran the particle runtime for the hit sparks only, and RE-414 read
the colour animations' `Effect` events and skipped them. How does
`ftParamMakeEffect` place and make the effects the motion scripts, colour
animations and status code ask for, when in the frame are they made, what
do the display hit effects (slash, orbs, sparks) do, and in what order do
they all draw from the one random sequence?

## Source

- **`ftParamMakeEffect`** (`ft/ftparam.c`). `nEFKindChargeSparkle` first
  moves to joint 16 with an x offset (Samus 180, Donkey Kong 100, Kirby
  50). With a joint other than `-1`, the offset (or zero) takes the
  scatter, `(syUtilsRandFloat() - 0.5) * 2s` per non-zero axis in x, y, z
  order, is divided by `attr->size` for `EffectItemHold`, and goes through
  the joint's world matrix. The second switch makes the effect: the damage
  spark, flames and small shock at `ftParamGetEffectJointPosition` (the
  next of the five `effect_joint_ids`, starting from the second), the
  dusts, `DustExpandLarge` with two more draws, the impact wave (rotated
  by `syUtilsArcTan2(-floor_angle.x, floor_angle.y)` on a floor, level in
  the air), the Star Rod spark, the orbs, sparks and metal dust, the white
  sparkles, the quakes (not for a demo fighter), the flashes, Kirby's
  star, the charge sparkle (scale 0.7, alpha 0xC0), the ripple, heal
  sparkles, music note and egg break.
- **When.** `ftMainProcUpdateInterrupt` sets `is_events_forward`; while it
  is set, `ftMainUpdateMotionEventsAll` skips `Effect` events and does not
  copy `motion_scripts[0]` to `[1]`. At the end of `ftMainProcPhysicsMap`,
  outside hitlag and after `proc_map`,
  `ftMainUpdateMotionEventsForwardEffect` runs `[1]`'s flow commands and
  effects, then clears the flag. `ftMainSetStatus` opens with that pass
  while the flag is set; a status set outside it makes its effects at once
  and copies. `ftMainUpdateMotionEventsForward` always copies, so a status
  entered part-way during the passes runs its copy one frame ahead (kept).
  Colour animations make theirs where they run (`ftMainUpdateColAnim`,
  also in hitlag). `ftParamKirbyTryMakeMapStarEffect` follows Kirby's
  `proc_map`.
- **Makers** (`ef/efmanager.c`). `DustLight` (a struct, two draws, its own
  decelerating process), `DustHeavy` (no struct, no first update),
  `DustHeavyDouble` (a struct, no first update, a reversed `DustHeavy`
  126 units lower on its second frame), `DustDash` and `DustExpandLarge`
  (no struct), the flames (a struct, two to four draws), the sparkles,
  flashes and ripple as RE-413's struct-less makers, the music note (its
  script drawn first).
- **Display effects** (`efManagerMakeEffect`). A desc with
  `EFFECT_FLAG_USERDATA` takes a struct (the five-free rule); the slash's
  (`0x4 | 0x1`) takes none. The effect plays its animation once when made,
  gets its process at the next frame's `func_run` (`efManagerFuncRun`),
  and most end on the play at which `anim_frame <= 0`. The small shock
  draws five numbers (one angle is wasted) and ends by its material clock.
  The orb spawner lasts `rand(3) * 4 + 4` frames and throws an orb (four
  draws) every fourth; the spark and metal-dust spawners throw three at
  18, 0 and -18 degrees on their side. The hit's orbs and sparks each pass
  `syUtilsRandIntRange(4) == 0` first. The quake holds a struct for its
  animation; the impact wave fades from 255 by 127/11 a frame.
- **Matrix kinds.** Kind 40 (`func_80010918(.., TRUE)`) turns the `DObj` to
  face the camera's eye at its translation; 0x45 (69) is
  `lbCommonRotScaFuncMatrix`, 0x44 (68) `func_ovl0_800CA024` (scale and
  translate), 73 `func_ovl0_800CB140` (the fire spark's).
- **Weapons.** `ftMainUpdateAttackStatWeapon` makes a set-off for each
  side that stops, `ftMainUpdateShieldStatWeapon` one of `shield_damage +
  damage` at the shield; `wpLinkBoomerangMakeWeapon` sets
  `is_hitlag_victim`, so its hits make their spark.

## Implementation

- **`ssb_game::fteffect`.** `EffectRequest` (the call's arguments),
  `FighterEffect` and the fighter's `EffectQueue` (16). `make` is
  `ftParamMakeEffect`, run by `flush` when the match holds the runtime; it
  reads the fighter as it stands then. `kirby_map_star`.
  `CombatAttrs` gains `effect_joint_ids` and `joint_itemlight_id`
  (`tools/gen-motion-scripts.py`).
- **Motion scripts.** `MotionState::effect_threads` is `motion_scripts[1]`
  and `is_events_forward` its flag; `Effect`/`EffectItemHold` queue a
  request. `Fighter::tick_interrupt` sets the flag;
  `tick_physics_map` ends with `motion::end_physics`;
  `set_any_status_preserve` runs `forward_effect` first on the old clock.
- **Colour animations.** `ColAnim::update_effects` hands each `Effect`
  event to `run_update`, which queues it.
- **Status code.** The down bounce and shield-break down impact waves, the
  level-3 floor bounce's wave and quake, the damage fly's dust interval
  (`ftCommonDamageSetDustEffectInterval`/`UpdateDustEffect`), Mario's
  Tornado dust and the ledge catch's middle flash.
- **`ssb_game::effect`.** The particle makers above; `Display`, a 64-slot
  pool of display effects with their structs, clocks and `effect_vars`;
  `process`, the priority-3 pass (each struct's mover and each started
  display's `proc_update`, in `GObj` make order), which now also moves
  RE-413's sparks; `func_run`; `make_hit` makes the slash, orbs, sparks and
  metal dust. `life` holds each display's play count, replayed from the ROM
  (`crates/ssb-rom/tests/display_effects.rs`): slash 18, impact wave 13,
  common spark 18, metal dust 26, fire spark 11, quakes 19/31/31, small
  shock 20 (its material script loops through `SetAnim`, which sets
  `anim_frame = -anim_wait`, zero).
- **Frame.** `psp-game` flushes every fighter's queue after each
  fighter's interrupt and physics passes, and `finish_frame_with` before
  the hit effects and after each `ftMainProcParams`; `HitEffectSink::process`
  runs between the physics and hit passes.
- **Drawing.** `draw_display_effects` replays each effect's packed
  animation and materials to its clock on one heap scratch and draws the
  manager-effect object under its kinds (kind 40 plus 0x45, `Sca` or
  `RotRpyR` at the root, `Sca`/0x45/0x44 on a flag-1 child), the impact
  wave in its prim colour and alpha.

## Evidence

- **The bank.** Every new maker runs on the packed bank and ends (dust
  light 16 frames, heavy 29, double 31, expand large 55, dash 18, flames
  16–18, sparkles 5–28, flashes 8, ripple 21, Kirby's star 17); none uses
  `VORTEX`, `ATTACH`, `NOISE`, `ALPHABLEND` or `DITHER`.
- **Capacity.** Four fighters trailing dust every four frames, struck
  every eight with a heavy spark, set-off, slash, orbs and sparks, two
  shocked every other frame and a quake: 51 of 112 particles, 38 of 80
  transforms, 34 structs (the five-free rule refuses beyond), 33 of 64
  displays, nothing else refused. Every motion script and colour animation
  of the twelve fighters, flushed each frame, stays under the queue.
- **What moved the goldens.** A build restoring the random seed around
  every particle run, effect process, maker and flush, beside `HEAD` with
  the same isolation, gives identical `f1-vs-four`, `f1-vs-team`,
  `f1-vs-results`, `f1-vs-pause`, `f1-training-cpu-walk` and `-cpu-jump`;
  `f1-vs-cpu` differs only at its hit spark. Those changes are the
  effects' random draws.
- **Order.** A first draft ran the old script's pending effects at the end
  of `ftMainSetStatus`, on the new status's clock; `f1-training-samus-shot`
  then drew the charge loop's impact wave. Running them first, as the
  source does, removed it.

## Deviations

- **When they are made.** An effect is made at the end of the process
  that asked for it (the fighter's interrupt or physics pass, or a hit
  process), not inside it, so a random draw the rest of that fighter's
  process makes comes before it. Colour-animation effects in the
  interrupt pass read the previous frame's joints.
- **Not made.** Kirby's two copy-bank scripts (0x4C, 0x4D; the bank is not
  loaded), Donkey Kong's crate pieces (`itBoxContainerSmashMakeEffect`),
  Yoshi's roll egg (`efManagerYoshiEggEscapeMakeEffect`, which also hides
  him) and `func_ovl2_8010183C`. The fire spark and quakes hold their
  structs but are not drawn; the quake moves no camera.
- **Weapons.** The weapons' own effects (Fireball, Charge Shot, PK Fire,
  PK Thunder and Jolt sparkles, dust and `efManagerImpactShockMakeEffect`)
  and weapon-against-weapon set-offs (`wpProcessProcSearchHitWeapon` has no
  port) are not made. The item-shoot and item-swing dust (no such items)
  and the results scene's fighters' effects are not made.
- **Slash.** `efManagerHaveStructProcUpdate` reads and frees the slash's
  null struct; the port holds none and frees none.
- **Draw.** The children's kinds use the child's animated pose; the impact
  wave's animation drives only its scale. Priority-3 quake processes
  (priority `3 - magnitude`) run with the others.

## Verification

- **Host tests.** Seventeen `fteffect` tests: a status set in the frame
  makes its effects after the physics pass, one set outside makes them at
  once and once, the dash's two clouds, a status change making the old
  script's pending cloud, the scatter's axis order before the maker's
  draws, the effect-joint cycle, the small shock's five draws, deferred
  process and life, the slash without a struct and a refused effect
  drawing nothing, the spark spawner's angles, the orb spawner's schedule,
  the orbs' gate, colour-animation flames queued, Kirby's star, the impact
  wave's turn, every script inside the queue, the dust interval, and the
  display pool's refusal count. ROM tests replay the lives and run the
  makers.
- **Tests fail when broken.** Not skipping effects while
  `is_events_forward`, not copying the scripts, dropping a scatter axis,
  not advancing the joint cycle, dropping the wasted draw, a life off by
  one, starting a display at once, giving the slash a struct, reversing
  the spark angles, an orb every third frame (the first test passed it and
  was tightened), letting Mario make stars, flipping the wave's turn, a

- **Goldens.** 27 rebaselined, identical in both captures, among them:

    his feet.
  - `f1-training-captain`: the Falcon Punch's dust cloud and flames.
  - `f1-training-kirby`: Kirby's landing dust and a small star.
  - `f1-training-samus-shot`: the dash dust behind Samus as she fires.
  - `f1-training-jab`: the light spark in a new place.
  - The battles, CPU scenes and `f1-training-rebirthblast` follow the

  - 114 of 114 goldens match.
- **Gates.** Workspace tests with an absolute `SSB64_ROM` and one thread

  both PSP release builds pass. The release `run` is 19,004 bytes (was
  26,440; `Hud::new` is no longer inlined into it). No pack change.

## Conclusion

Every effect the fighters' motion scripts, colour animations and ported
status code ask for is made by `ftParamMakeEffect` on the match runtime,
at the source's pass and in its random order, and the hit's slash, orbs,
sparks and metal dust draw as display effects holding their structs. The
weapons' own effects and weapon clashes remain.
