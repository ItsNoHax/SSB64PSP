## Question

In what order does the decomp run a fighter's velocity, dead check, whistle and map step, and where does the port still differ?

## Order ported (`ftmain.c:1739-1847`, every status)

| Step | Decomp | Port |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `pos_prev = TopN` (:1747) | `tick_physics_map_procs` after the release sweep and egg-lay sweep |
| 2 | `proc_physics`, velocity, damage-air decay (:1755-1799) | `physics_step`, `own_physics_step` (dead, rebirth, boss, appear), hitlag `smash_di_nudge` (:1800) |
| 3 | moving-floor `vel_speed` (:1804-1812) | `map_window` pre-map TopN, also for held fighters |
| 4 | `ftCommonDeadCheckInterruptCommon` (:1814) | `dead::check` before the map; death returns |
| 5 | altitude whistle (:1817), `public_knockback` clear (:1821-1827) | `after_dead_check(pos_prev.y)` on pre-map TopN |
| 6 | mask roll then `proc_map` (:1830); none when dead or no `proc_map` | `map_window` |
| 7 | Kirby map star after `proc_map` (:1840) | `kirby_map_star`, every status with a `proc_map` |

- Closed deviations: map-before-dead check; whistle and knockback read post-map position; dead/rebirth/boss/appear skipped dead check and whistle; hitlag, held, dokan and egg lay had no mask roll or Kirby star; held on a moving floor lacked `vel_speed`; masks rolled on the death frame; `lag_map` recorded landing contacts only.
- Tests: `physics_map_tests.rs`, `ko_tests.rs`, `audio_battle_tests.rs`; each fails when its ordering is mutated back.

## `f1-vs-team` moved

- 11144 px at 2x (2786 native). Deterministic.
- Cause: Kirby hit grounded becomes `DamageHi2` with `situation = Air` (`ftcommondamage.c:519-525`); the CPU Smash-DIs down in hitlag; `proc_map` lands on a floor (`mpprocess.c:2031`); `ftParamKirbyTryMakeMapStarEffect` runs after `proc_map` (`ftmain.c:1840`). The star alters the battle afterwards.
- Mutation bisect: only the hitlag `Lag` mask roll plus star moves it. No N64 trace of this battle.
