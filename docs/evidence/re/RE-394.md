# RE-394 — The VS countdown's traffic light and "GO!"

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, ROM decode, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: interface, match, pack, psp-ge, visual-regression
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/{countdown,countdown_tests,battle}.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/sprite.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/meshdraw.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`

## Question

The VS battle counted down to "Go" with nothing on screen (RE-389). What
does `ifCommonCountdownThread` draw, and when?

## Source

- **Timing.** `ifCommonEntryAllThread` sleeps 90 ticks, then:
  - makes the countdown (`ifCommonCountdownMakeInterface`);
  - makes the entry focus, whose `syUtilsRandIntRange(3)` draws from the
    shared seed.
  The countdown's timer `t` runs on frame 91 + `t`, so "Go" at `t` = 300
  is frame 391, as RE-389 found.
- **Sprites.** They come from file 82 (`gGMCommonFiles[1]`,
  `IFCommonGameStatus`):
  - the rod, the frame and the rod shadow (IA8,
    `SP_TEXSHUF | SP_TRANSPARENT`);
  - fifteen lamps from nine I-format sprites (dim, contour and light in
    red, yellow and blue), with `dIFCommonTrafficSpriteData` positions
    and colours (`SP_CLOUD | SP_TEXSHUF`);
  - the RGBA32 "G", "O" and "!".
- **The thread.**
  - **Slide down.** It moves every `SObj` down 0.8833 per tick for 60
    ticks.
  - **Lamps.** At 120, 180, 240 and 300 it ejects the current lamp pair
    (`sobj` and `sobj->next`) and appends lamps 6 to 9, then 11 to 14.
    The new first lamp starts at scale 3 and shrinks 0.2 per tick, centred
    on its data position.
  - **Go.** At 300 the rod shadow takes the Go backlight `(6A, 6A, 95)` and
    the shadow env `(12, 12, 2E)`, and moves to the list's end. The same
    tick, `ifCommonAnnounceGoMakeInterface` shows "GO!" for 60 ticks.
  - **Slide up.** From 360 it moves back up for 60 ticks, then ejects
    itself.
- **Sudden death.** `ifCommonSuddenDeathThread` has no traffic light. It
  shows "GO!" after its 90-tick sleep.
- **Prim colour.** `lbCommonPrepSObjAttr` sets the primitive colour, and
  IA's environment colour, on every sprite. Only the combiner waits for a
  format change.

## Implementation

- **`ssb_game::countdown::Countdown`.** Ports the thread over a fixed
  `SObj` list, the "GO!" letters' 60 ticks and the sudden-death variant.
  `Battle::clock` exposes the scene's frame count. Seven host tests cover
  it.
- **Sprites in the pack.** `ssb_rom::sprite::GAME_STATUS` lists file 82's
  24 sprite offsets from `reloc_data.us.h`, and the pack converts them.
  That makes 140 sprites; the pack is 27,770,800 bytes, SHA-256
  `4c1c7045adcb8ea09400e785f3674143527b99f86c2d52bb1cc25c0c332850dd`.
- **`meshdraw::draw_sprite`.** It now takes an `SObjDraw` with an
  environment colour.
  - Tinted sprites use `TextureEffect::Blend`, with the texture
    environment at PRIM and the vertex at ENV. That is IA's
    `(PRIM - ENV) * TEXEL0 + ENV`, and PRIM for the white-baked I
    textures.
  - The white flash is `Blend` with both colours at PRIM.
  - `SP_CLOUD` blends like `SP_TRANSPARENT`.
- **`psp-game`.**
  - A `Hud` holds the damage displays and the countdown.
  - `training_frame` makes the countdown at frame 91, drawing the entry
    focus's random number, and ticks it on every running frame.
  - Sudden death arms "GO!" at the same frame.
  - `draw_training` draws the countdown after the damage displays.

## Deviations

- **Entry focus.** Its camera work (`ifCommonEntryFocusMakeInterface`) is
  not ported; only its random number is drawn.
- **Sudden-death text.** The "SUDDEN DEATH" letters (file 37) are not
  converted yet.
- **Voices.** The announcer voices are not played.

## Verification

- **ROM decode.** File 82's 24 sprites decode: 12 RGBA32 letters, 3 IA8
  parts and 9 I lamps. An image dump showed "GO!", "TIMEUPSAG", the rod,
  the frame and the lamps.
- **Goldens.** 35 goldens were rebaselined, each captured twice.
  - `f1-vs-countdown` (tick 300, `t` 209) shows the light with the red
    lamp and the "3" and "2" yellow lamps lit, and the "2" sparkle.
  - `f1-vs-sudden-death` shows "GO!" over the two 300% displays.
  - `f1-vs-cpu` changed because the entry focus now draws from the RNG,
    which moved the CPU's choices. At tick 690 it pulls the player, at
    10%, into a grab (`CatchPull`, from `PPSSPPHeadless --log`).
  - The Training goldens differ by at most one level per channel in the
    HUD. That is the `Blend` combiner's rounding.
- **Tests.** Workspace tests, Clippy and both PSP builds pass. The release
  `run` is 119,984 bytes. The `golden_capture` build's `run` is 124,692,
  inside MIPS branch range but close to it.
