# RE-513 — Kirby attack100 vulcan puffs and the Link/Captain/Kirby attack100 interrupt

Status: COMPLETE, checked against N64 RDRAM traces (Kirby, Link and Captain rapid jab, with and without an item in reach)
Topics: fighters, effects, rendering, reverse-engineering
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/effect.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/fteffect.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/kirby.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/link.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/captain.rs`, `crates/ssb-tablegen/src/kirby_attack100.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`

## Question

How does `ftCommonAttack100LoopKirbyUpdateEffect` make the vulcan puffs, what do Link, Captain and Kirby do on the attack100 `GetCheckInterruptCommon` else branch, and in what order are link-15 display effects drawn?

## Findings

Kirby vulcan puffs (`ATTACK100_EFFECT`, file 228 at 0x1220, 6x6 f32, generated at build time by `ssb-tablegen`, D-048):

- Puffs on the loop start frame (labelled 1012) and at 1012/1018/1023/1028/1033/1039/1043, rows 0,1,2,3,4,0,1, angles 0,19,-13,10,-10,0,19. Row = flag2 - 1; the TopN-marking part query runs, then flag2 = 0. The puff is made at SetStatus (frame 1012).
- TopN mark frames: 1012,1013,1018,1019,1023,1024,1028,1029,1033,1034,1037,1038,1039,1040,1043,1044.
- Puff proc: `vel += add; t += vel`, lifetime 6, ejected on the 7th update, not paused by hitlag (frames 874-878 keep moving).
- lr +1 from position bits (44fbe846, 435a6667, 41da6667): x/y bits per frame 4506d065/43859927, 450de700/43991dea, 451337f4/43a7c17c, 4516c342/43b183dd, 451888e9/43b6650e (twice); rot z 3ea9c91f.
- lr -1 from (44be7b78, 435a6667, c1da6667): 44acc2f4/43857d4b, 449e95be/4398ebc4, 4493f3d6/43a77e9f, 448cdd3b/43b135dc, 448951ee/43b6117a (twice); rot y 40490fdb, z bea9c91f.
- The 0x46 quad's matrix rows equal `gGCMatrixPerspF` every frame (`func_ovl0_800CA194`, lbcommon.c:1702: a view-space quad); its spin is the node's own rotate.z, always 0. `child->child` is the quad (DObjDesc 0xB20, ids 0 and 1, identity TRS). The PSP draws it as an eye billboard at the root translate.
- NKirby is excluded by the C fkind check and makes no puffs; its flag stays set.

Attack100 interrupt:

- Link flag 1 at 5/12/19/26, loop wraps at 35 (unchanged).
- Captain correction: flag 1 at 6/14/22/30/37 with a 40-frame loop (37 to the next 6 is 9 frames). The old port used 37 for the loop and the WaitAsync frames; each WaitAsync subroutine has a Wait(2) before SetFlag1.
- With an item in reach the else branch (`item_throw::check_get`) gives LightGet (status 100) at update 8 for Kirby, 5 for Link and 6 for Captain. Without an item the loop continues. A held item is unreachable (A uses the item).

Draw order: efManagerMakeEffect gives every effect display priority 2 and `gcDLLinkGObjTail` keeps equal priorities in creation order, so link-15 displays draw in make order (`Effects::displays_in_make_order`, sorted by `seq`), not pool order. ThunderTrail, ThunderShock and VulcanJab now draw inline in that order. Still separate passes: the shield and the particle lists.

Like-for-like N64 comparison (GLideN64 at 320x240, warp ROM Training/Pupupu Kirby vs CPU Mario, PSP tick = el + 8, tick 110 against el 102):

- Puff profile differences are 0-5 levels per channel; at the white-peach-orange transition +-16 with mixed sign; dash-end texels +-20-47, matching the background difference between GLideN64 3-point and PSP bilinear. No texture scale, mirror or matrix error. An earlier "orange starts later on N64" reading came from different scenes and cameras.
- Kirby's body differs: N64 brightest pink is always (255,164,184), PSP highlights reach (255,220-237,241-250) (probe capture with the vulcan disabled has the same pixels). Affects every Kirby golden; suspect `psp-runtime/src/meshdraw.rs:408` `configure_fighter_light`. Not fixed here.
- Shadow under Kirby's feet, rows 164-168, x 210-222: N64 (154,113,62), PSP (247,181,99). The N64 also shows stock icons by the damage display.
