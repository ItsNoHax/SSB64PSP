# RE-417 — Drawing Pikachu's Thunder, Kirby's copy hats and the Egg Lay egg

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, ROM records, packed-asset ROM tests, host tests broken and restored, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice and broken by disabling each draw, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: weapon, effect, fighter, asset-pack, rendering, visual-regression
Relevant files: `tools/romtool/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/{pack,effect,fighter,mobj,scene_deps}.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/tests/{copy_hats_and_thunder,display_effects}.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/{pikachu_weapon,weapon,wpeffect,wpeffect_tests,kirby_copy,capture_yoshi}.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/{meshdraw,scene}.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `tests/golden/f1-training-{pikachu-thunder,kirby-hat,yoshi-egg}.png`

## Question

The game state for Pikachu's Thunder (head, trails and the fading segments
of RE-416), Kirby's copies and the Egg Lay capture exists, but `psp-game`
drew none of them. What does each draw, and from which ROM data?

## Source

- **Thunder's model** (`wp/wppikachu/wppikachuthunder.c`,
  `ef/efmanager.c`). The head and trail `WPAttributes`
  (`llPikachuMainThunderHeadWeaponAttributes`, file 243 + 0x0C, and
  `...TrailWeaponAttributes`, + 0x40) and `dEFManagerPikachuThunderTrailEffectDesc`
  all name file 341's `DObjDLLink` array at 0x95B0 (one list, 0x94F8) and
  the `MObjSub` table at 0x9420, with no transform or material animation.
  The `MObjSub`'s `sprites[]` are 0x9020, 0x8C18, 0x8810 and 0x8408
  (`WPPIKACHUTHUNDER_TEXTURES_NUM` 4); the list sets ENV to (255, 255, 102)
  and loads `sprites[texture_id_curr]` through segment 0x0E.
- **Frames.** `wpPikachuThunderHeadMakeWeapon` sets `texture_id_curr = 3`
  and scale 0.5; a trail (`wpPikachuThunderTrailMakeWeapon`, scale 0.5)
  starts at `gcAddMObjForDObj`'s 0 and each `wpPikachuThunderTrailProcUpdate`
  draws `syUtilsRandIntRange(3)`. A segment
  (`efManagerPikachuThunderTrailMakeEffect`, scale 0.5) starts at texture 3
  (the head's, lifetime 10) or 0 (a trail's, lifetime 6); while not 3,
  `efManagerPikachuThunderTrailProcUpdate` draws a random frame and, on its
  last frame, sets texture 3 and `rotate.z` = 180°.
- **The fade.** `efManagerPikachuThunderTrailProcDisplay` sets
  `G_RM_AA_XLU_SURF` and `G_AC_NONE` and draws; nothing changes a colour or
  alpha over a segment's life. A segment "fades" by its frames: random
  bolts, then frame 3 turned over, then gone. The weapons draw under
  `wpDisplayDrawNormal` (`G_ZBUFFER` cleared, `G_RM_AA_XLU_SURF`).
- **Copy hats** (`ft/ftchar/ftkirby/ftkirbyspecialn.c`, `ft/ftparam.c`).
  `ftKirbySpecialNCopyInitCopyVars` calls
  `ftParamSetModelPartDefaultID(fighter_gobj, 6, copy[copy_id].copy_modelpart_id)`
  and `ftParamResetModelPartAll`, which swaps joint 6's `dl` and `MObj`s
  for `modelparts_container->modelparts_desc[6 - 4]->modelparts[part][detail]`
  (`FTModelPart`: `dl`, `mobjsubs`, `costume_matanim_joints`,
  `main_matanim_joints`, `flags`), played at the costume
  (`lbCommonAddMObjForFighterPartsDObj`). `ftKirbySpecialNLoseCopy` sets
  part 0. `FTAttributes::modelparts_container` is at 0x328 (after
  `translate_scales`); the joints map onto the model's descriptors from
  `nFTPartsJointCommonStart` (4), so joint 6 is node 2, whose list (0xDB0)
  is part 0's high-detail list.
- **The copy table.** `llKirbyMainMotionSpecialNFTKirbyCopy` (file 228 +
  0x0) holds `FTKirbyCopy[27]`; the playable rows' `copy_modelpart_id`s are
  12, 7, 4, 8, 11, 10, 5, 9, 0, 6, 3, 13 (Mario to Ness). The ROM's part
  records (KirbyMain 0xCC, 15 parts × 2 details) resolve through
  relocations; the decomp's `gap_*` labels in `229_KirbyMain.c` name other
  offsets.
- **The Egg Lay egg** (`ef/efmanager.c`, `ft/ftcommon/ftcommoncaptureyoshi.c`).
  `dEFManagerYoshiEggLayEffectDesc` (flags `0x4 | USERDATA | 0x1`) builds
  file 339's tree at 0x960 under a root of kind 0x50 (a translation to the
  fighter's TopN) then `Sca`; `efManagerYoshiEggLayMakeEffect` scales X and
  Y by `dFTCommonYoshiEggDamageCollDescs[fkind].effect_size` and makes the
  tree's child `Tra` then kind 0x2E. Its animations
  (`dEFManagerYoshiEggLayAnimJoints`) are Wait (0xDB0) and Break (0x9F0);
  the `EFDesc`'s own is Throw (0xB90), index 2 at the make. The process
  plays the animation, switches to `force_index` when it differs, and after
  Throw ends forces Wait. `ftCommonYoshiEggProcPhysics` sets the speed to
  5 on a mash and 1 otherwise, and forces Break once the wait runs out;
  `ftCommonYoshiEggProcInterrupt` leans the tree's root ±22 on the ground
  with the stick (range 26).

## Implementation

- **Pack v68** (30,710,256 bytes, +219,040):
  - Thunder: file 341's list converted four times under the weapon seed,
    once per sprite, keyed `(341, sprite)`
    (`ssb_rom::effect::PIKACHU_THUNDER_SPRITES`).
  - Copy hats: for each part the copy table names, joint 6's high-detail
    `FTModelPart` replaces node 2's list and chain (colours from its own
    costume scripts) and the whole graph is converted, as the costumes are;
    the node's mesh is a costume override keyed
    `pack::modelpart_costume(part, costume)` (0x1000 + part × 16 +
    costume), costume 0 always and others where they differ (55 meshes).
    `fighter::model_part`, `mobj::read_chain` and romtool's
    `apply_costume_colors` (shared with `materials_at`) read them.
  - Egg: the Wait and Break tables as `AnimDesc::EFFECT` slots
    `effect::YOSHI_EGG_LAY_ANIM_SLOT` (0x104) and 0x105 on the tree's nodes.
- **Game state (drawing only).** `ThunderTrail::texture`: the pool's flush
  draws each trail's `TextureRand` itself, in the same link order, and
  keeps it on a Thunder trail. `kirby_copy::COPY_MODELPART_IDS` and
  `copy_hat`. `capture_yoshi::EggLayEffect` (made on the egg's first
  update, its process at the next, speed, wiggle, Break forced), with
  `EGG_THROW_PLAYS` and `EGG_EFFECT_SIZES`; the escape still counts
  `EGG_BREAK_FRAMES`.
- **Drawing.** `meshdraw` resolves a model-part key to the part's mesh on
  the nodes it names (this costume, else the part's costume 0) and the
  plain costume elsewhere; `fighter_draw_costume` gives Kirby his hat's key.
  The Thunder head and trails draw after PK Thunder's trails, at scale 0.5
  unturned; the segments (`draw_thunder_segments`) before the display
  effects, under their translation, `rotate` and scale. The egg
  (`draw_egg_effects`, after the fighters: link 10) places its node 1 by
  TopN · `Sca(size, size, 1)` · node 0 (wiggle added to its translate) and
  draws a kind-46 billboard spun by node 1's `rotate.z`, sized by
  `size × node 0's scale × node 1's`; `EffectVisuals::sync_eggs` replays
  each port's animation by epoch and plays at the effect's speed.
- **Scenes.** `pikachuthunder` (dash right from under the top platform,
  Down+B at tick 50, capture at 92, the first `SpecialLwHit` frame),
  `kirbyhat` (the grab scene's route, B at 108 inhales the dummy, B at 170
  copies, capture at 260) and `yoshiegg` (walk under the left platform, two
  jumps, an aerial Egg Lay at 250, the egg on the top platform in Wait at
  360).

## Evidence

- **ROM tests.** `copy_hats_and_thunder.rs`: the copy table equals
  `COPY_MODELPART_IDS`; part 0's high-detail list is node 2's; every hat
  has a costume-0 mesh on node 2 only, from its own list; the four Thunder
  frames are packed and bind four textures. `display_effects.rs`: the
  packed Throw ends on play 17 (`EGG_THROW_PLAYS`), Break on its tenth play
  after the set (`EGG_BREAK_FRAMES`), and the packed Wait and Break scripts
  are the archive's bytes on nodes 0 and 1.
- **Logs.** A capture build's per-tick log shows the head falling 450 a
  frame from 7,350; under the top platform its map test ends it at 1,500
  (a quake and sparkle), so the scene moves Pikachu to x 831. Kirby's copy
  becomes Mario, with `copy_hat` 12, during `SpecialNCopy`; the dummy's egg
  is made as it is laid, turns from Throw to Wait after play 17 and comes
  to rest on the top platform.
- **Images.** The Thunder bolt runs from the top of the screen to Pikachu
  as a chain of frames; Kirby wears Mario's red cap, brim forward; the egg
  (white with green spots) leans in its Wait wobble.

## Deviations

- **Egg timing.** The egg's process runs at the start of the next egg
  update rather than in the effect link the same frame, as the port's
  display effects do. The sync replays skipped plays at the current speed.
  The escape keeps its 10-frame clock, so a Break played at speed 5 (a
  mash on the frame the wait ran out) ends early on screen.
- **Hats.** Only joint 6's base part is drawn: motion-script model-part
  events (`ftParamSetModelPartID`) are not ported, so a status's temporary
  part does not show. High detail only.
- **Not drawn.** Yoshi's roll egg (`efManagerYoshiEggEscapeMakeEffect`,
  reached only from the unported escape statuses) and egg explosion (his
  particle bank is not packed) stay undrawn.

## Verification

- **Host tests.** The Thunder trail keeps each drawn frame (the flush's
  draw, not the sink's); the egg throws, waits from play 18, breaks and
  ends; a mash sets speed 5 and the stick leans the egg on the ground only;
  the copy hat follows `copy_id` and is gone after a lost copy. Breaking
  the Throw threshold (`>`), the mash speed or the trail's stored frame
  fails its test.
- **Goldens.** Three added (`f1-training-pikachu-thunder`,
  `f1-training-kirby-hat`, `f1-training-yoshi-egg`), identical twice. A
  build that skips each draw changes them by 11,504, 2,608 and 4,728
  pixels. All 117 goldens match twice with pack v68; no existing golden
  changed.
- **Gates.** Workspace tests with an absolute `SSB64_ROM` and one thread
  (1,503 passed), pinned 1.98.0 Clippy with warnings denied, rustfmt, the
  `no_std` builds and both PSP release builds pass. Pack v68 is 30,710,256
  bytes, SHA-256
  `a4e979efa307e0d09f6501ddbacbd954ef9110e4f29abaf93604e86430542d57`.
  The release `run` is 20,740 bytes (was 18,480) with an 18,176-byte
  frame; the largest frame is `enter_training` at 159,456 bytes (the
  fighter's egg state); `sync_eggs` takes 46,440, beside `sync_ko`'s
  95,368, both under `draw_frame`.

## Conclusion

Thunder's head, trails and segments draw their frames from Pikachu's
model, Kirby wears each copy's `FTModelPart` on joint 6 in his costume, and
the Egg Lay egg draws around the trapped fighter with its Throw, Wait and
Break animations.
