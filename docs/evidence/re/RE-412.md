# RE-412 — The KO presentation: blast, flash, halo and fades

Status: IMPLEMENTED (decomp source, `reloc_data.us.h`, ROM display-list and attribute reads, host tests, PPSSPPHeadless goldens captured twice, workspace tests, both PSP builds; no physical PSP)
Topics: fighter, effect, animation, visual-regression, pack
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/{colanim,colanim_tests,ko,ko_tests,dead,status,fighter}.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/motion/mod.rs`, `crates/ssb-rom/src/{stage,pack,fighter}.rs`, `psp-runtime/src/{scene,gu}.rs`, `psp-game/src/main.rs`, `crates/ssb-capture/src/lib.rs`, `tests/golden/f1-training-{rebirth,rebirthblast}.png`

## Question

KOs and rebirth run (RE-388), but none of their presentation is drawn:
the blast explosion, the screen flash, the rebirth halo, the star KO's
fade and sparkle. What does each `ftCommonDead*` and `ftCommonRebirth*`
setter make, with which data, placed where, and for how long?

## Source

- **The blast.** `ftCommonDeadDown/Right/LeftSetStatus`
  (`ft/ftcommon/ftcommondead.c`) call `efManagerDeadExplodeMakeEffect(&pos,
  fp->player, type)` with type 0, 1 or 3. `pos` is the death point clamped
  into the camera bounds on the other axis (RE-388).
  - `dEFManagerDeadExplodeEffectDesc` names file 84's four-node tree.
    The reloc header's `llEFCommonEffects2DeadExplodeDefaultMObjSub`
    (0x53E8) sits in the DObjDesc slot and `...DefaultDObjDesc` (0x4F08) in
    the MObjSub slot; the pack key (84, 0x53E8) is the tree. Its
    transform animation is `...DefaultAnimJoint` (0x54D0).
  - The maker sets `o_matanim_joint` to
    `dEFManagerDeadExplodeMatAnimJoints[player]`: `DeadExplode1` (0x58E0),
    `2` (0x5800), `3` (0x5950) or `4` (0x5870). Each table names one
    PRIMCOLOR script for each of the three drawn nodes. Player 0's hold
    (255, 255, 187, 153), white and (255, 180, 61, 102) for 30 or 20 frames,
    then fade to alpha 0.
  - It sets the root's translation to `pos` and `rotate.z` to
    `dEFManagerDeadExplodeRotateD[type]` (0, 90, 180, 270 degrees).
  - It gives `dobj->child` the ENV colour
    `dEFManagerDeadExplodeEnvColorChild*[player]` and
    `child->sib_next->sib_next` `...Sibling*[player]`, setting
    `MOBJ_FLAG_ENVCOLOR`. A ROM dump of the three display lists (0x5218,
    0x52B0, 0x5310) shows each sets its own ENV before calling segment
    0x0E, so the second node keeps its list's (0xFC, 0xF6, 0x90).
  - `efManagerHaveStructProcUpdate` ejects the effect when the transform
    animation ends. Replaying the packed scripts, that is after 36
    `gcPlayAnimAll` calls.
  - The maker also starts an `LBParticle` script
    (`dEFManagerDeadExplodeGenID[(type % 2) * 4 + player]`) under a
    transform with the same rotation.
- **The screen flash.** The three setters and `DeadUpFall`'s landing call
  `ifScreenFlashSetColAnimID(nGMColAnimScreenFlashDeadExplode, 0)`
  (`if/ifscreenflash.c`). `dGMColScriptsScreenFlashDeadExplode`
  (`gm/gmcolscripts.c`, priority 60) sets white at alpha 0, blends to 0x6E
  over 6 frames, then to 0 over 30, and ends. `ifScreenFlashProcUpdate`
  runs `ftMainUpdateColAnim` once a frame. `ifScreenFlashProcDisplay` fills
  (10, 10) to (310, 230) with `G_CC_PRIMITIVE` and `G_RM_AA_XLU_SURF`, on
  link 22, under the damage HUD's 23. `is_allow_screenflash` is always on.
- **The halo.** `ftCommonRebirthDownSetStatus` calls
  `efManagerRebirthHaloMakeEffect(fighter_gobj, attr->halo_size)` and sets
  `is_effect_attach`.
  - `dEFManagerRebirthHaloEffectDesc` names file 85's tree
    (`llEFCommonEffects3RebirthHaloDObjDesc`, 0x2AC0) and its animation
    (0x2B70), which spins the inner node through 2π every 30 frames and
    loops (`gcPlayAnimAll`, no eject).
  - The root's matrix kind 0x50 is `func_ovl0_800C99CC`, a translation to
    the world position of `dobj->user_data.p`, the fighter's TopN. The maker
    sets the child's scale to `halo_size`.
  - `RebirthStand` and `RebirthWait` keep the effect
    (`FTSTATUS_PRESERVE_EFFECT`). The next status change runs
    `ftParamProcStopEffect`, which ejects it: at `Fall`, or at an earlier
    ground action out of `RebirthWait`.
  - `halo_size` is `FTAttributes` + 0xEC. A ROM read of all 27 fighters
    matches `relocData/2xx_*Main.c` (Mario and Ness 1.0, Fox, Link and
    Pikachu 1.1, Donkey Kong 1.7, Luigi 1.02, Kirby 1.14, the others 1.2).
- **The rebirth glow.** `ftCommonRebirthDownSetStatus` also starts
  `nGMColAnimFighterRebirth` (priority 10, unlocked). The script turns the
  fighter's light to (0, -70), sets `color1` white at alpha 0xFF, waits 2,
  blends to alpha 0x0A over 36 and back to 0xB4 over 18, and loops.
  `RebirthStand` and `RebirthWait` preserve it
  (`FTSTATUS_PRESERVE_COLANIM`); `Fall` ends it.
- **The fighter's colour.** `ftDisplayMainProcDisplay` draws with
  `G_RM_FOG_PRIM_A`. With `is_use_color1` and no shade,
  `ftDisplayMainCalcFogColor` makes the fog colour `color1` (every
  fighter's `attr->fog_color.r` is 0xFF, so the alpha is unscaled): each
  pixel is blended towards `color1` by its alpha. With `is_use_light`,
  `ftDisplayLightsDrawReflect` uses `lr * light_angle_x, light_angle_y`.
- **The colour animation.** `ftMainUpdateColAnim` (`ft/ftmain.c`) steps
  the timer, runs events until one waits, then adds each
  `(target - colour) / frames` step, truncated. It returns true at `End`
  or when `length` runs out, and `ftMainRunUpdateColAnim` then runs
  `ftParamResetStatUpdateColAnim`. It runs every frame in
  `ftMainProcUpdateInterrupt`, in hitlag too, before `proc_update`, and
  once in `ftMainSetStatus` for a status that starts at frame 0.
  `ftMainSetStatus` resets an unlocked animation unless the status
  preserves it.
- **The star KO.** `ftCommonDeadUpStarSetStatus` ends with
  `ftParamResetFighterColAnim`. On its first update `...ProcUpdate` sets
  `color1` to `MPGroundData.fog_color` at alpha 0. While it flies it
  writes alpha `128 - wait * 128 / 180`. At the end it clears the colour,
  hides the fighter and calls
  `efManagerSparkleWhiteDeadMakeEffect(TopN, 5.0)`, an `LBParticle` script
  (0x5C). It makes no blast or flash. The fighter shrinks by perspective as
  it moves 15,000 units away (`vel_z = -83.33` for 180 frames).
- **The falling top-out.** `DeadUpFall` makes the flash, a quake and no
  blast when it lands.
- **Also made.** Every dead setter calls `efManagerQuakeMakeEffect(2)`
  (a camera shake played from an animation) and the rumble.

## Implementation

- **`ssb_game::colanim`.** Ports `GMColAnim`: `check_set` with priority,
  `update` event for event, `run_update`, `reset`, and the set-status
  reset. It transcribes the two scripts this batch runs. The hit-status
  animations `ftParamResetStatUpdateColAnim` would start are not ported,
  so an ended animation only resets.
- **`Fighter::colanim`.** Runs after the motion events in
  `status::update`, in hitlag, and in `motion::start` for a frame-0
  status. `Preserve::colanim` carries `FTSTATUS_PRESERVE_COLANIM`.
- **`ssb_game::dead`.**
  - The side and down setters, and `DeadUpFall`'s landing, set
    `DeadState::flash`.
  - `DeadUpStar` resets the colour animation, fades towards
    `StageBounds::fog_color`, and records `DeadState::sparkle`.
  - The rebirth starts the glow; `RebirthStand` and `RebirthWait` preserve
    it.
  - `ExplodeKind::rotate_z_degrees`, `halo_size` and `halo_scale` (the
    halo's scale while the fighter is in a rebirth status) are new.
- **`ssb_game::ko::KoEffects`.** The match takes each fighter's blast and
  flash after the fighters (`observe`), then runs the effect and interface
  processes (`tick`): each blast counts its `gcPlayAnimAll` calls and ends
  at 36, and the flash runs its colour animation. Blasts are kept by port.
- **Pack v65.** `StageDesc` carries `MPGroundData.fog_color` (0x4C). A ROM
  test checks the nine VS stages against the decomp.
- **`psp-runtime`.** `DEAD_EXPLODE_EFFECT_KEY`, the per-player material
  scripts and ENV colours, `REBIRTH_HALO_EFFECT_KEY`, and two `Gpu`
  helpers: a blended rectangle and a constant fog.
- **`psp-game`.**
  - `EffectVisuals::sync_ko` catches each port's blast and halo players
    up to their clocks. It restarts the blast's three material joints on
    the player's scripts (`EffectMaterialAnimator::restart`).
  - `draw_ko_effects` draws each halo at the fighter's TopN, its child
    scaled by `halo_size` about its origin. It then draws each blast at its
    point, rotated, one node at a time with the player's ENV and the live
    PRIM. It draws for all four ports.
  - `draw_screen_flash` fills the scaled (10, 10) to (310, 230) under the
    HUD.
  - `draw_fighter_model` turns the light by the colour animation's angles.
    With `color1` in use, it fogs the fighter with a constant factor: the
    GE fogs by `(far - depth) / (far - near)`, so a window 100,000 units
    wide placed at the fighter's view depth gives `1 - alpha` to within
    1/255 across the model.

## Deviations

- **Not drawn.**
  - The blast's particle half and the star KO's sparkle. Both are
    `LBParticle` scripts, and no match runs particles yet.
  - The quake (`efManagerQuakeMakeEffect`) and the rumble.
  - The halo's rays: its first DObj's second display list (file 85
    0x2A88 → 0x2890) has twelve triangles with `PRIMITIVE` colour and
    `TEXEL0` alpha. The converter classifies that combiner as a flat colour
    and drops the texture, so they would draw as an opaque white box. The
    port hides that node (`REBIRTH_HALO_RAYS_NODE`).
- **Colour.** `FTPARTS_FLAG_NOFOG` parts are fogged too, and the fog is per
  vertex on the GE.
- **Far plane.** The battle projection keeps its far plane at 10,000
  instead of `dGMCameraPerspDefault`'s 39,936, so a star KO is clipped once
  it is 10,000 units from the eye, about a third of the way out. A trial
  with 39,936 changed seven unrelated goldens by 4 to 16 pixels (depth
  precision), so it was left for a projection batch.
- **Other.** `DeadUpFall`'s move to DL link 19 and high detail are not
  modelled.

## Verification

- **Host tests.** Ten new tests: the flash's alpha sequence and its end;
  the rebirth pulse and light; priority; the status-change reset; blast
  placement, rotation, player and its 36-tick life; the falling top-out's
  flash without a blast; the star KO's fade and sparkle; the halo and glow
  lasting through `RebirthStand`/`RebirthWait` and ending at `Fall`; and
  halo sizes. ROM tests read `halo_size` for 27 fighters and the fog
  colours for nine stages.
- **Tests fail when broken.** Each was checked by breaking the code:
  - Rounding the blend step up (`BlendColor1`) fails the flash and pulse
    tests.
  - Dropping `Preserve::colanim` from the rebirth setters fails the halo
    and glow test.
  - Ending blasts at 35 fails the blast test.
  - Removing the `DeadUpFall` flash fails the falling top-out test.
- **Goldens.**
  - `f1-training-rebirth` was rebaselined. Mario crouches in `RebirthStand`
    on the halo, a white disc with a grey and gold rim, faintly lit white.
  - The new `f1-training-rebirthblast` is `rebirth` at tick 168, eight
    ticks after the KO (tick 160, logged from a capture build). The
    orange-white blast column rises from the bottom of the screen with its
    green-white side shards, and the flash lightens the frame inside the
    (10, 10) to (310, 230) border. P1's damage display is breaking.
  - `f1-vs-results` is unchanged: its battle is not captured.
  - 111 of 111 goldens match twice.
- **Pack.** v65, 30,405,584 bytes, SHA-256
  `f0e8c31f06a96e9c4b87a12c973159a103c153976fc14ff3d296c98c47bd9f1d`.
- **Gates.** Workspace tests with an absolute `SSB64_ROM` and one thread,
  pinned 1.98.0 Clippy with warnings denied, rustfmt, and both PSP release
  builds pass. The release `run` is 20,064 bytes.

## Conclusion

The blast, the screen flash, the halo, the rebirth glow and the star KO's
fade are drawn for every fighter. The particle halves (the blast's streaks
and the star sparkle), the quake and the halo's rays remain.
