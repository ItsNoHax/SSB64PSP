# RE-346 — Jigglypuff's moveset, the sleep element and Kirby status ordinals

Status: COMPLETE (decomp, host tests, both PSP builds, one PPSSPP smoke; ledge stops, per-part hit status and physical PSP not validated)
Topics: gameplay, fighter, status, hit-resolution, animation, asset-pipeline
Relevant files: `crates/ssb-game/src/purin.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/purin_attack.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/attack.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/status.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/kirby_copy.rs`, `crates/ssb-game/src/grab.rs`, `tools/gen-anim-table.py`, `tools/extract_purin_moves.py`

**Question.** What does Jigglypuff need beyond the common machinery, how
does Sing's sleep reach a target, and do the ported Kirby copy statuses use
the source ordinals?

**Sources.** `ftpurin.h`, `ftpurinstatus.h`, `ftpurinspecial{n,hi,lw}.c`,
`ftkirbycopypurinspecialn.c`, `ftkirby.h` (`ftKirbyStatus`),
`ftkirbystatus.h`, `ftcommonattack1.c`, `ftcommonattack100.c`,
`ftcommonjumpaerial.c`, `ftcommonattackair.c`, `ftcommonfurasleep.c`,
`ftcommoncapture.c`, `ftcommondamage.c` (`ftCommonDamageUpdateMain`,
`ftCommonDamageGotoDamageStatus`), `ftmain.c` (`MakeAttackColl` decode and
`ftMainSearchFighterAttack`), `mpcommon.c`
(`mpCommonSetFighterLandingParams`), `ftdata.c` (`dFTPurinMotionDescs`,
`dFTKirbyMotionDescs`), US `232_PurinMainMotion.c`, `233_PurinMain.c`,
`228_KirbyMainMotion.c`.

**Findings.**

- **Rapid jab is unreachable.** `ftCommonAttack100CheckFighterKind` lists
  Jigglypuff and counts four A inputs, but `Jab2` never sets flag 1, so
  neither `Attack12ProcUpdate` nor the interrupt starts `Attack100Start`.
  `ftpurin.h` marks the three statuses unused. They are not ported; the
  input count is kept.
- **Aerial jumps.** Five jumps (`jumps_max` 6) with
  `dFTPurinJumpAerialFVelocities` `{60, 40, 20, 0}` and drift ×0.8. `Jump2`
  to `Jump5` set flag 1 at `WaitAsync(25)`; `Jump6` sets none.
- **Pound.** Flags 1 and 2 at frame 12 (boost 65 at
  `(clamp(|y|,50)-10)·20/40` degrees, then ×0.92 per tick), flag 2 = 2 at
  30 (drift with fast fall). Grounded Pound follows root motion and
  switches air on `mpCommonProcFighterOnEdge`; Kirby's copy uses
  `mpCommonProcFighterOnFloor` and joint 16 instead of 15. The Pound counter
  (`passive_vars.purin.unk_0x0`) is cleared by landing and read by nothing.
- **Sing.** One collision slot on joint 6: damage 0, element 6
  (`nGMHitElementSleep`), `ga` 2. `ftmain.c` decodes `ga` bit 1 as
  `is_hit_air` and bit 2 as `is_hit_ground`, so Sing reaches only grounded
  targets. `SetAttackCollSize` alternates 600/60/600/60/760 without a clear,
  so one hit record covers all 98 frames. `ftCommonDamageUpdateMain`
  bypasses the no-knockback flash for sleep and
  `ftCommonDamageGotoDamageStatus` enters `FuraSleep`: breakout wait
  `max(300 − percent, 0) + 75` (US), minus one per frame and four per
  mash. The port adds the element and ground/air mask to `ActiveHitbox`.
- **Rest.** `SetHitStatusAll(3)` from frame 0 until `WaitAsync(30)`, then
  one 20-damage, base-60 box at frame 1. The air/ground switches preserve
  the hit status and frame, so the port models the window as frames 0..30
  of either status.
- **Throws.** Forward: release (flag 2 = 1) at frame 8 into
  `DamageFlyRoll` (status 55), 14 damage, angle 90. Back: release at 24
  into `DamageFlyN` (52), 16 damage.
- **Existing port errors corrected.** `KirbyStatus::CopyPikachuSpecialN`
  was 293/294, which are `CopyPurinSpecialN`/`AirN`; Pikachu's copy is
  252/253. Pikachu's throw descriptors mapped status 52 to `DamageFlyHi`
  instead of `DamageFlyN`. A timed `LandingAirF`/`LandingAirLw` ended on
  the next tick for every fighter except Kirby, so Pikachu's landing hitbox
  never played; any timed landing now runs to its end.
- **Animation.** 30 appended slots: Jigglypuff's 18 attack/landing clips
  (her `LandingAirF` names Kirby's figatree), nine own clips (Sing and Rest
  share one figatree each across ground and air), Kirby's two Pound clips
  (Jigglypuff's figatrees) and a shared `FuraSleep` slot for the ported
  fighters. The pack looks animations up by `(fighter, slot)`, so the pack
  version stays 38.

**Verification.** 1,059 workspace tests with the ROM; pinned Rust 1.98
clippy and rustfmt clean. `romtool anims --verify`: 750 lengths agree, and
the generated table reproduces byte for byte. Tests cover special routing,
the Pound angle, boost and decay, ground/air switches keeping the frame,
jump velocities and the flag-1 gate, Rest intangibility and hit, Sing's
ground-only sleep and mash-out, landing windows, jab chains that never
reach a rapid jab, Jigglypuff's throw frames and the copied Pound. Both PSP
builds pass (`psp-game` also with `strict_render`). PPSSPPHeadless
`r2-purin-fighter` matches its golden with zero differing pixels. That
static capture proves pack/render integration, not playable Jigglypuff.
Pack v38 is 23,511,568 bytes; SHA-256
`7a4a9aa630684576e661d83d90d8da6081d91fa906ac6e6b192582ec66988382`.
ROM-derived output remains untracked.

**Limits.** Jigglypuff is host-only, like the preceding fighter batches.
Pound's and Sing's ledge stops and the aerial jumps' cliff catch wait on
the fighter map solver. Down smash's per-part intangibility and the throws'
`SetHitStatusAll(2)` wait on per-part hurtboxes and hit-status
invincibility. Sing's note effect and the `FuraSleep` colour animation are
not drawn. No physical-PSP test or full golden matrix was run.
