# D-047: CRT Overscan Crop — the N64's Visible Box Fills the PSP's Height

Category: Rendering Architecture

**Decision:** The N64's 320×240 frame is mapped onto the PSP so that its
inset box (10, 10)–(310, 230), the part a CRT showed, fills the PSP's 272
lines. One scale, 272 / 220 ≈ 1.236, applies to both axes. N64 (160, 120)
lands on PSP (240, 136). The box spans PSP x 54.5–425.5, about 371 columns
at the N64's 15:11. The pixels whose centres lie inside it, (55, 0)–(425,
272), are the visible area. Black pillarbox bars fill the columns on either
side. The 10-pixel strip around the box is cropped, as a television's
overscan hid it. Nothing is stretched.

- `ssb_engine::coord` holds the only N64 → PSP screen mapping:
  `n64_to_psp_x`/`_y`, `n64_rect_to_psp(_xywh)`, `n64_scissor` (the pixels
  whose centres a rectangle holds, clipped to the visible area),
  `visible_area`, `n64_pixel_to_psp_column`/`_row` (where a framebuffer
  copy samples an N64 pixel) and `psp_pixel_edge`.
- `Gpu::set_viewport_n64` maps a camera's N64 viewport and scissors it to
  the visible area. Its scale and centre are sent as floats, because
  `sceGuViewport` halves an integer width. `set_viewport_pillarboxed` is
  the whole 320×240 frame as viewport, scissored to the visible area, and
  is what 2D and full-frame content draw under. `set_viewport_fullscreen`
  is only for the port's own diagnostics in PSP pixels. `begin_frame`
  clears the whole screen, then restores the viewport and scissor the last
  `set_viewport_*` call chose.
- The following all go through the mapping: sprites, fills, the depth mask,
  screen and 3D particles, the wallpaper, the pause border, the screen
  flash, the frozen picture, the transition and wallpaper photo copies, the
  staff roll's halved 640×480 screen and the no-pack diagnostic slots.
- The battle camera frames by the N64's
  `gGMCameraStruct.viewport_width / viewport_height` (300 / 220). It used
  the old pillarbox's 362 / 272.

**What the strip held:** The N64 never draws there. libgc clamps every
camera's scissor 10 pixels inside the frame (`dGCCameraScissor*` = 10,
`gcSetCameraScissor` is never called), and N64 captures of the opening,
the title and the menus are black outside (10, 10)–(310, 230) (RE-477).
Sprites placed partly outside the box are clipped there by the N64
itself. Examples are a fighter's tag or an item's arrow at the top edge,
the countdown's zoom, menu slide-ins and the How to Play window's last 4
rows. The port drew backgrounds and those sprites into the strip under
the old mapping. The crop removes that difference.

**Reasoning:** The user's decision. Under D-008 the whole 320×240 frame
was a 362×272 pillarbox (scale 17/15). The strip showed on the PSP as a
border in which the wallpaper or menu background showed but the stage did
not. The crop shows the picture as a CRT did, 9% larger. The GE fills more
pixels: the battle viewport covers 100,640 pixels instead of 84,411.

**Supersedes:** [D-008](D-008.md).

**Status:** Adopted (RE-477).
