# D-009: VPK0 Decompression — Postfix Huffman, Bit-Width Leaves

Category: Asset Pipeline

## Decision

- VPK0: LZ77 + two postfix-encoded Huffman trees
- Leaves hold bit widths, not values

## Why

- Two independent paths agree on all 499 compressed files:
  - walk intern chain through decompressed payload
  - measure ROM gaps (no decompression)
