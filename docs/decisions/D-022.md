# D-022: Fighter Constants — Data-Driven from FTAttributes

Category: Gameplay Model

## Decision
- Extract all 27 characters' `FTAttributes` from relocData
- Verify field-by-field against the decomp
- Invented defaults: never

## Why
- Per-character tuning is data in the original:
  - gravity, terminal velocities, air accel/friction, traction
  - dash/run speed, weight, jump params, shield size
  - SFX IDs, move-availability bitfields
- Invented defaults were 26x off; hid a stick-scaling bug in air drift
