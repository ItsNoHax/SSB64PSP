# D-052: One Portable Fighter Tick in crates/ssb-pose, Run by psp-runtime and the Host Tests

Category: Platform & Toolchain

## Decision
- `crates/ssb-pose` owns `PosedFighter`: a fighter, its skeleton and clip state, ticked as the match runs it.
  - Priority-5 half: input, TransN's sample, `ftMainProcUpdateInterrupt`, the played-spawn placement, a throw's early clip.
  - Priority-4 half: clip, `ftMainProcPhysicsMap`, slope contour, joint sample, accessory, anchors.
  - Settles after the hit phase: catch, capture, status, appear.
  - Joint sampling (`set_joint_parents`, `set_posed_joints`, `part_locals_into`), `fighter_skeleton`, `tick_skeleton_animation`.
- Depends on `ssb-game`, `ssb-engine`, `ssb-rom`; `no_std` without `std`; never on `psp-runtime`.
- `psp-runtime`'s `FighterScene` embeds it (`pose`) and derefs to it.
  - Host-only concerns stay in the runtime and reach the physics half through `PhysicsHooks`: profiler spans, the overlay's airborne count.
  - Stack poisoning, camera, display, stage bounds stay in `FighterScene`.
- Host replays (`crates/ssb-rom/tests/common/posed.rs`) use `PosedFighter` directly; no second copy.
  - `ssb-rom` takes `ssb-pose` as a dev-dependency (cycle accepted by Cargo for integration tests).
- `place_played_spawn`'s save and restore is one `Snapshot` struct.

## Why
- The host copy had drifted from the PSP one (spawn-host research, batch 18):
  - skeleton without animlocks or root scale;
  - pose writes after the slope instead of before;
  - an incomplete played-spawn restore.
- Host tests passed against behaviour the PSP did not run.
- `ssb-game` must not know the pack; `ssb-rom` must not know gameplay: the join needs its own crate.

## Applied
- Batch 18: PSP side a pure move; goldens unchanged.
- `PosedFighter::new` builds out of line into the caller's slot: no skeleton-sized temporary on the 512 KiB game stack.

## Amendment trigger
- The match loop's process order (`tick_pair` vs `psp-game`'s loop) moved into a shared crate.
- A second platform needing different physics-half hooks.
