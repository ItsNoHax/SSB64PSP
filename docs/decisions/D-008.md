# D-008: Aspect Ratio — Pillarboxed 362×272

Category: Rendering Architecture

Superseded by [D-047](D-047.md).

## Decision

- 320×240 (4:3) frame: rendered into centred 362×272 viewport on 480×272 PSP
- Applied to both `sceGuViewport` and `sceGuScissor`

## Why

- Stretching distorts characters
- Pillarbox aspect in projection + 480-wide GE viewport: stretches 1.33x

## Implemented (until D-047)

- `crates/ssb-engine/src/coord.rs::pillarboxed_viewport()`
- `psp-runtime/src/gu.rs::Gpu::init`
