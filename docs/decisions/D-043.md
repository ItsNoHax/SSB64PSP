# D-043: Filtering and Tile Addressing Are Measured Against an N64 Reference Model

Category: Rendering Fidelity

Supersedes: unqualified equivalence conclusions of RE-066, RE-102, RE-124, for the claims below only.
- Those records' own measurements stay valid for what they tested.

## Decision
- Judge texture filtering and tile addressing against host-side N64 reference models, not name equivalence.
  - Models: `ssb_rom::n64_filter`, `ssb_rom::n64_addressing`.
- Measured results:

| Subject | Result |
|---|---|
| `G_TF_BILERP` (RDP 3-point reconstruction) | not PSP bilinear; accepted PSP fixed-function deviation, reduced by build-time filter compensation ([`docs/rendering/textures.md`](../rendering/textures.md)) |
| Mirror+clamp lowering | bakes every mirrored period the drawn rect reaches, authored UVs and texgen |
| PSP power-of-two padding | repeats logical edge; N64 clamp addressing unaffected |
| `G_SETTILE` CI4 palette bank | honoured |
| `mask == 0` axes | always clamped on N64; archive has none; `romtool` fails if one appears |

## Why
- RE-124 measured only that SSB64 selects `G_TF_BILERP`.
  - Not measured: RDP 3-point reconstruction vs PSP four-tap filter.
- RE-066, RE-067, RE-102 fixed their cases without a full addressing model.
- RE-218 reopened all four claims; RE-219–RE-224 measured and closed them.
