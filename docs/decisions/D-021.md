# D-021: Physics — Float, Not Fixed Point

Category: Gameplay Model

## Decision
- Type: `f32` throughout, as the original
- +Y up; gravity subtracts: `ftPhysicsApplyGravityClampTVel`: `vel_air.y -= gravity`
- Z: shallow depth axis, clamped to ±60

## Why
- No fixed-point representation to recover
- Direct port preserves behavior
