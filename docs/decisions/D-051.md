# D-051: PPSSPP Golden Hashes May Move for a Renderer-Path Change Only Under Hardware Proof

Category: Testing & Validation

**Decision:** A PPSSPP golden hash may change for a renderer-path change
(list chunking, stall batching, draw-word writing) only when all three hold:

1. A hardware framebuffer comparison (`frame_hash`, FNV-1a of each 480x272
   frame, PSP before and after) proves the PSP output identical over the
   affected scenes.
2. The new hashes are stable across repeated runs: at least 3 captures,
   including one at `-j 1`.
3. The changed rows are listed, with the native pixel count per row, in the
   commit and in the RE record.

Gameplay or asset changes still need bit-identical goldens or a reviewed
rebaseline, as before.

**Reasoning:** PPSSPP's software renderer bins its work by range, and flush
chunking moves the bin boundaries (RE-534). Display lists that are word for
word identical on the PSP can therefore move PPSSPP pixels (edge pixels and
single sprite-edge rows, in either direction). The hardware comparison is
what shows nothing was lost.

**Applied:** stall batching moved 44 rows (RE-537).
**Amendment trigger:** a PPSSPP change that makes bin boundaries independent
of the list layout, or a hardware difference in a scene whose golden moved.
